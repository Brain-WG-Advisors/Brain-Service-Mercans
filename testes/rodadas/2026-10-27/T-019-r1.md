## Resposta

**Family benefits, maternity and AT/MP: 75,000 FCFA/month. Retirement: 3,375,000 FCFA/month.**

Source: CNPS Directorate-General, *Communiqué à l'attention des employeurs* (January 2023), which implemented Decree n° 2022-986 of 21 December 2022 raising the SMIG from 60,000 to 75,000 FCFA. The communiqué set three values effective 1 January 2023: a monthly contribution floor of 75,000 FCFA across all branches; a monthly retirement-branch ceiling of 3,375,000 FCFA; and a monthly ceiling of 75,000 FCFA for the other branches (maternity insurance, family benefits, and work injury/occupational disease).
PDF: https://www.cnps.ci/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/NOUVEAU-PLAFOND-DES-COTISATIONS-SOCIALES-DE-LA-CNPS.pdf

These values still hold in 2026. The retirement ceiling is 45 × SMIG, and as of April 2026 no new SMIG increase had been announced or adopted, so the SMIG remains 75,000 FCFA. I found no later CNPS communiqué that replaces the 2023 one.

Many sources still show **70,000 FCFA**, which is the pre-2023 figure. That includes CLEISS, whose Côte d'Ivoire contributions page dated 1 January 2025 lists 70,000 FCFA as the monthly ceiling for maternity, family benefits and work accidents. It also includes Mercans' own Côte d'Ivoire page (dsr.mercans.com), which shows per-branch ceilings of 70k / 3.375M. The CNPS communiqué is the primary source and takes precedence over both.
