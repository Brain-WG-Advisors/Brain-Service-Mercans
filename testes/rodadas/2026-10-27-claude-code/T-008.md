# T-008
**Question:** Ukraine: reference period used to compute the average daily salary for the sick-leave benefit?

**Answer:**
The 12 calendar months preceding the month in which the incapacity begins.
- Daily rate = ESV-subject earnings in that window ÷ calendar days of insured employment in it.
- Statutory holidays are not subtracted from the denominator.
- Removed from the window, days and earnings: prior sick leave, maternity, childcare leave, unpaid leave, war suspension, unpaid military service.
- Fewer than 12 months worked → the fully worked months since hire.
- First-day sickness → contractual rate ÷ 30.44.
- Under 6 months of insured record in the last 12 → capped at minimum wage ÷ 30.44.

Our earlier "6 months" answer (HRBS-14331, col. K) was wrong. It was never withdrawn in writing in the ticket.

Legal basis: Poryadok (KMU Resolution) No. 1266 on calculating average salary for insurance benefits. The exact paragraph is not confirmed locally.
