## Resposta

**12 calendar months of employment (counted from the 1st to the 1st) at the employee's last main place of work, immediately preceding the month in which the incapacity began.**

**Source:** Procedure for Calculating Average Salary for Compulsory State Social Insurance Payments, approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1266 of 26.09.2001, as amended (latest change: Resolution No. 533 of 29.04.2026).

- **Clause 25, standard rule:** the reference period is 12 calendar months of employment (from the first to the first day) at the insured person's last main place of work, preceding the month in which the insured event occurred.
- **Clause 26, less than 12 months employed:** the reference period is the calendar months actually worked (from the first to the first day).
- **Clause 27, less than one full month employed:** the reference period is the time actually worked (calendar days) before the insured event.
- **Clause 3, average daily salary formula:** the salary accrued for the reference period on which the unified social contribution (ЄСВ) was charged, divided by the number of calendar days of employment in that period, excluding days not worked for valid reasons. Since Resolution No. 837 took effect on 18.07.2025, months of the reference period in which the person did not work for valid reasons (from the first to the last day of the month) are excluded from the reference period.

Clause 7, which several secondary sources cite for the 12-month rule, governs voluntarily insured persons, not employees. The correct cite for employees is clause 25.
