# T-016

**Pergunta:** Chile: in the Mercans regulation design, how should the Impuesto Único de Segunda Categoría (IUSC) and its reliquidation be structured (calculation steps and accumulators)?

**Resposta:**
**IUSC:** build it in 3 steps, not as one regulation per band:
1. Convert the taxable base from the base currency to UTM (58121). The UTM is keyed in monthly in `$legal_entity_hr.utm`.
2. Look up the band rate and deduction in the Art. 43 LIR UTM table (58122/58123). The bands are 0% up to 13.5 UTM, then 4%, 8%, 13.5%, 23%, 30.4%, 35%, and 40% above 310 UTM.
3. Post the tax to 2552.

Do not use `differential`.

**Reliquidation:**
- Art. 47: a single YTM step (`$58120.ytm`) instead of 12 monthly accumulators.
- Art. 46 (multi-month bonuses): done manually in steps, because the engine has no native support for it.

Design v1.0 (8 regulations by band plus 12 accumulators) was replaced by v1.1.

Open point: Part 2 of the design still lists the 8 IUSC regulations [INCERTO].
