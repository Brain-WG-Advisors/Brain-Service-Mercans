Title: Required Workplace Postings - Planning & Development Services (Bernalillo County)
REQUIRED WORKPLACE POSTINGS
***Effective Jan. 1, 2026, the Bernalillo County minimum wage is $10.95.
However, the State of New Mexico Workforce Solutions minimum wage supersedes Bernalillo County's minimum wage and is the prevailing wage. This wage is $12 per hour worked.
For tipped employees, the minimum hourly wage is the state minimum of $3. If the tipped employee's hourly rate does not equal or exceed $12 per hour with tips, the employer shall pay the employee the difference between the wage received and $12. ***
