## Resposta

**Sector 42 (Groothandel II), small employer, 2026: Whk total = 1.45 %** (WGA 0.91 % + ZW-flex 0.54 %).

The source is UWV's 2026 premium note. Tabel 1.2 (Sectorale premies 2026) lists sector 42 Groothandel II at WGA 0,91 and ZW 0,54. The legal instrument is the *Besluit gedifferentieerde premie Werkhervattingskas 2026* (Stcrt. 2025, 24033). The same figures appear in Cijferbijlage LH-209, table 10.

A correction to the premise: the Handboek Loonheffingen 2026 does not print the sectoral rates. §7.6 only sets out the mechanism: small employers pay the sectoral rate, which the Belastingdienst notifies by *mededeling*. The number itself comes from the Cijferbijlage and the UWV Besluit.

Some practical points:
- Up to 50 % of the WGA component (max 0.455 %) may be recovered from net pay (Handboek §7.6.9). The ZW-flex component can never be recovered.
- "Small" for 2026 means total 2024 premieplichtig loon of € 1,082,500 or less.
- For 2027, sector 42 rises to 0.99 % + 0.56 % = 1.55 % (Besluit Whk 2027, Stcrt. 2026, 24832). Make sure you are using the 2026 row.

There is also a gap in the skill. Sector 42 is outside the ten verified rows in the NL skill's `01-ccg.md` §4.3. This result also resolves the "orphan triple" flagged in QA item U1: 0.98 / 0.49 / 1.47 is sector 40, Uitgeverij. That means the extracted page-2 values sit one line above their sector names, so reading values off the line next to a sector name gives the wrong row. U1 and the §4.3 table should be updated from the UWV note.
