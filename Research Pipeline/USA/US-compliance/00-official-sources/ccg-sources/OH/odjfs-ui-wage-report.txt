Unemployment Services

If you are unemployed or have had your work hours reduced, Ohio’s Unemployment Services can help provide temporary financial assistance while you look for new employment.

In this section, you can find information about filing and managing an unemployment claim, understanding eligibility requirements, and meeting ongoing claim responsibilities. You can also access resources that support your job search while you receive benefits.

Use the options below to start your journey to check your claim status, submit required information, or find help if you run into issues during the process.
