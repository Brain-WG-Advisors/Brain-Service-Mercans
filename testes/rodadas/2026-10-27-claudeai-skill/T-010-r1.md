## Resposta

**4DF income code 157, Mercans wage type 62942 (Payment to Individual Entrepreneur). PIT: No. Military levy: No. ESV: No.**

- **PIT and Military levy: not withheld.** Under PKU p. 177.8, the paying entity does not withhold tax on a FOP's business income once the FOP gives a copy of its state-registration document. For single-tax payers, the related rule is PKU pp. 2 p. 297.1. The 4DF line still has to be filed: columns 3a and 3 show the accrued and paid amounts, and columns 4a, 4, 5a and 5 (tax and levy) carry dashes. The legal basis for code 157 is the Income Features Directory (Довідник ознак доходів), Annex 2 to MinFin Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015.
- **ESV: not charged by the payer.** Law 2464-VI, art. 4, part 1, item 1, second paragraph excludes civil contracts with a FOP when the work or services match the activities recorded for that FOP in the Unified State Register (ЄДР). The FOP pays its own ESV. If the services fall outside its registered activities, the exclusion does not apply and 22% ESV is due under art. 8, part 5.
- **Wage fund:** WT 62942 is excluded from the wage fund.
