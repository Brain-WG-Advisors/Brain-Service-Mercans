Employer Information and Resources

Most businesses with 10 or more employees are required to participate. As a business owner and employer, you’ll have some initial and ongoing responsibilities. Below, you’ll find links to all the documents, forms, and information you’ll need for your role as an employer in Delaware Paid Leave.

This program helps to ensure Delaware workers have financial support if they need to take a leave of absence from their job due to a serious life event. It’s a win-win for employees who need support and for employers who want to attract and retain top-quality personnel. Most businesses with 10 or more employees are required to participate, and those with fewer than 10 employees have the option to voluntarily participate. Get to know the guidelines so that you can fulfill the requirements, since Delaware Paid Leave went into full effect on Jan. 1, 2026.

Am I required to participate?

The following employers are exempt from the program:

  • Employers with nine or fewer employees (these employers may voluntarily participate – learn more here).
  • Federal government employers.
  • Seasonal operations that close to the public for a month or more.

Have questions? Need help? Book a virtual meeting.
One-on-one Zoom video meetings are available with the director of Delaware Paid Leave. These meetings are optional and are NOT required to file a claim. Office hours are on Tuesdays and Thursdays , from 1 to 2:30 p.m.

You can also email general questions to PFML@Delaware.gov.

Not using the state plan?

Private Plans

There are several private insurance companies currently approved to offer this coverage in Delaware. Self-insured plans automatically renew for the next calendar year unless you notify Delaware Paid Leave of a material change by Dec. 1 of each year. To submit claims or for questions about your private plan, please visit the website for your insurance company or contact your plan’s representative.

Key Dates

Mandatory enrollment

Jan. 1, 2025 — Payroll deductions began for employers participating in Delaware Paid Leave, if employees are contributing to the cost of the plan.

April 30, 2025 — Date employers were required to submit the first quarterly Hours & Wage Report.

July 30, 2025 — Date for employers to submit contributions for the first and second quarters.

Jan. 1, 2026 — Employees began submitting claim applications.

Voluntary enrollment

Dec. 1, 2025 — Voluntary open enrollment period ended for the effective date of Jan. 1, 2026.
Private plans must start on Jan. 1, April 1, July 1, or Oct. 1, 2026.
