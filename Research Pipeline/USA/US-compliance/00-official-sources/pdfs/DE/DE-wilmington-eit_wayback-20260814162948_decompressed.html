Earned Income Tax Refunds for Non-resident Employees Guidance and Frequently Asked Questions (Update February 1, 2022)  

The City of Wilmington has published clarifying information for customers who are requesting refunds of earned income taxes (wage taxes) withheld during the calendar year 2021. We are providing guidance and a list of frequently asked questions and examples of the types of situations in which customers would be eligible for refunds.
