R5| SUBMISSION CHANNEL VERIFICATION (v2.7 STEP 3.5)
R6| - Channel: paper or electronic delivery TO THE EMPLOYEE \| Portal: none - this artefact is NOT submitted to any authority \| Source: ET arts. 378-380 and the form's own instructivo, which addresses only the issuing of the certificate \| CONFIRMED.
R7| - This is the one in-scope Colombian artefact with NO government channel at all. It is issued to the employee and retained by the employer. DIAN sees the same data annually through Formato 2276 (CO-DIAN-005), which IS filed - but the two are separate obligations with different field sets and different deadlines.
R8| - The certificate SUBSTITUTES for the employee's income-tax return where the six ET art. 380 conditions hold (DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.6.1.13.2.7), which is why it carries employee-declared sections that payroll cannot populate.
~
R10| INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
R11| - integration_type = OUTBOUND (scope_manifest CO-OUT-F220), to the EMPLOYEE rather than to an authority. There is no inbound leg.
R12| - product_mapping_required = true. The certificate needs per-employee ANNUAL totals by concept, three separate severance buckets, the meal-payment split at 41 UVT, the rolling six-month average labour income, and the AVC deduction which has no dedicated nomina electronica element. None of these can be reconstructed at year end from a single annual gross figure.
R13| - NOT DERIVABLE FROM THE MONTHLY FILINGS (scope_manifest failure check F21). Colombia imposes NO employer year-end tax reconciliation. This certificate is a SEPARATE annual obligation built from the annual accumulation, and it additionally carries data the employer never sees monthly - the employee's own asset, debt and other-income declarations under ET art. 380.
~
R15| RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT (v2.8 control B1)
R16| - RESIDENT: casilla 60 carries the ET art. 383 progressive-table withholding for the year, and casillas 53 to 58 carry the contributions and voluntary savings that depurated the base. The employee may then use the certificate in place of a return under ET art. 380.
R17| - NON-RESIDENT: the same certificate is issued, but casilla 60 carries the FLAT 20% of ET art. 408 applied to the GROSS - so casilla 52 (total gross income) and the withholding in casilla 60 stand in a simple 20% relationship, with NO depuration visible. The contributions in casillas 53 and 54 are still reported because they were still deducted, but they did NOT reduce the withholding base.
R18| - The art. 206 num. 4 severance exemption in casilla 59 is an exemption of the RESIDENT cedula-general regime. It does NOT attach to a non-resident's flat withholding, so for a non-resident the six-month average is reported for completeness but has no effect on casilla 60.
R19| - An employee who ACQUIRES RESIDENCE mid-year receives ONE certificate covering the whole period, with casilla 60 carrying the sum of the 20% months and the art. 383 months. The certificate does not disclose the split; the employee reconciles on their own return. The engine should retain the split internally for audit.
R20| - The employee's own annual return - Formulario 110 - is expressly DESCOPED in scope_manifest: the employer's duty ends with this certificate.
~

R8| Part-time employee | YES | Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
R9| All-inclusive salary employee (salario integral, >= 13 SMMLV) | YES | CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the nomina electronica carries SalarioIntegral = true.
R10| Apprentice (contrato de aprendizaje) | YES | Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decreto 0223 of 5 March 2026. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 4; PILA worker type 12 (lectiva) or 19 (productiva).
R11| Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias) | YES | Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
R12| Teleworkers and remote workers | YES | Ley 1221 de 2008 as amended by Ley 2466 de 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the nomina electronica carries a dedicated Teletrabajo devengado element (NIE158).
R13| Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract | YES | Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
R14| NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract | YES | IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
R15| Employed old-age pensioner in active service | YES | PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
R16| Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract | YES | Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
R17| Independent contractor (contrato de prestacion de servicios) | NO | Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (planilla types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Formulario 350, but under a different casilla.
R18| Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity) | NO | Public-sector only; PILA planilla types T and F are descoped in scope_manifest.
R19| Non-executive director paid honorarios only (junta directiva) | NO | Not employment income. Reported by the payer as honorarios, not as rentas de trabajo of an employee.
R20| Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer | NO | The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
~
R22| Annual salary paid | YES | Casilla 36, on the CST art. 127 definition quoted in full in the instructivo.
R23| Voucher, cheque, card and vale payments | YES | Casilla 37, including meal payments for an employee earning MORE than 310 UVT per month.
R24| Meal payments up to 41 UVT for an employee earning up to 310 UVT | NO | Not income of the employee at all under ET art. 387-1 - income of the food supplier. Only the EXCESS over 41 UVT is reported, in casilla 38.
R25| Statutory benefits other than severance | YES | Casilla 42 - prima de servicios, vacaciones, dotaciones.
R26| Severance savings, split three ways | YES | Casillas 47 (paid to the employee), 48 (traditional pre-1991 regime) and 49 (deposited to the fund). Three separate buckets that must be maintained year-round.
R27| Per diems and representation expenses | YES | Casillas 43 and 44.
R28| Employee mandatory contributions | YES | Casillas 53 and 54. Note that pension and the solidarity fund are COMBINED in casilla 54, unlike the nomina electronica which separates them.
R29| Voluntary savings - RAIS, voluntary pension, AFC, AVC | YES | Casillas 55 to 58, each separately. The nomina electronica has one element for the first two and none for AVC, so the engine must tag them itself.
R30| Rolling six-month average labour income | YES | Casilla 59. Drives the ET art. 206 num. 4 severance exemption and its taper; must be maintained year-round.
R31| Employer social-security contributions | NO | The certificate reports the EMPLOYEE's income and the EMPLOYEE's contributions. The employer's cost is reported only on PILA.
R32| The employee's other income, assets and debts | NO | Casillas 61 to 78 are supplied BY THE EMPLOYEE, not by payroll. They exist so the certificate can substitute for the income-tax return under ET art. 380.
~
R34| Reported on the accrual (causacion) date | NO | Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
R35| Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date | YES | The universal Colombian rule - pago o abono en cuenta. Applies to the nomina electronica (Res. DIAN 000013 de 2021 art. 3), to Formulario 350, to Formulario 220 and to Formato 2276.
R36| A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month | NO | It does not. The nomina electronica is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (art. 3). PILA and Formulario 350 are likewise monthly.
R37| Nil period still filed | VARIES | Formulario 350: NOT required where no operation subject to withholding occurred in the period (ET art. 606 par. 2). Nomina electronica: no document where there was no payment. PILA: a planilla is still required while the employment relationship subsists.
R38| Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period | VARIES | Nomina electronica: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Reemplazar or Eliminar) referencing the original CUNE. Formulario 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a planilla type N (correcciones) or M (mora).
~
R40| Employee who left during the year | YES | Casillas 30 and 31 carry the actual employment window rather than 1 January to 31 December. The certificate is still issued by the last business day of March.
R41| Employee whose withholding was nil all year | YES | A certificate is still issued. Casilla 60 is ZERO - the instructivo directs that a casilla with no value be written as zero, not left blank.
R42| Employee who acquired tax residence mid-year | YES | ONE certificate for the whole period. Casilla 60 sums the 20% months and the art. 383 months; the split is not disclosed on the form but must be retained internally.
R43| Employee with a dependant claimed for the art. 387 deduction | YES | Casillas 79 to 82 identify the dependant. The same dependant is reported in Formato 2276 numeral 34, where DIAN cross-checks a dependant claimed by more than one taxpayer.
R44| Employee under the pre-1991 traditional severance regime | YES | Casilla 48 rather than casilla 49. A shrinking population - employees hired before 1 January 1991 who did not elect the Ley 50 de 1990 regime.
R45| Employee holding a PEP or PPT | PARTIAL | Reported, but the form's six-value document-type list has no code for either. Escalated in column T of casilla 24.
R46| Certificate substituting for the employee's income-tax return | YES | Where all six ET art. 380 conditions hold and the employee signs, the form states that it substitutes 'para todos los efectos legales' for the return.
