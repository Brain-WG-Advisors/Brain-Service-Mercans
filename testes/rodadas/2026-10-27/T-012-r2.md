## Resposta

For 2026 there are two versions of Annex Д6. Which one applies depends on the reporting month.

**July 2026 periods onward: J0510611 (version 11)**
- J0510611 is the code for "Information on grounds for recording service of certain categories of persons in accordance with the law" (Annex Д6). It is filed with the main form J0500111.
- Legal basis: MinFin Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015, as last amended by MinFin Order No. 243 of 07.05.2026.
- Dates: Order No. 243 was published in the Official Gazette of Ukraine No. 49 on 17.06.2026, so it entered into force on 17.07.2026. However, the State Tax Service (DPS) stated that the forms apply from 01.08.2026.
- First use: legal entities first file on the new form for July 2026, with a deadline of 20 August 2026. The DPS confirmation is at cv.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/1032436.html.

**January–June 2026 periods: J0510610 (version 10)**
- The appendices for these periods were J0510110 through J0510610, filed with the main form J0500110.

**Sole proprietors (FOP) and self-employed persons**
These file a separate quarterly package. Their equivalent is F0510611 (Annex ФІЗ-Д6), used from Q2 2026.
