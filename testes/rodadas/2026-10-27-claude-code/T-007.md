# T-007
**Question:** Ukraine 2026: percentages of average salary paid as sick-leave benefit by insurance record (stazh)?

**Answer:**
- Under 3 years: 50%
- 3 to 5 years: 60%
- 5 to 8 years: 70%
- Over 8 years: 100%

Certain privileged categories get 100% regardless of stazh (e.g. Chornobyl categories 1-3). Maternity benefit is always 100%.

If the employee has less than 6 months of insured employment in the last 12 months, the daily rate is capped at minimum wage / 30.44.

Our earlier answer of 60/80/100 (HRBS-13389) was wrong.

Legal basis: Law of Ukraine No. 1105-XIV "On compulsory state social insurance". The brain cites art. 17; I believe the size-of-benefit article is art. 24. The primary text is not available locally, so the exact article is not confirmed.
