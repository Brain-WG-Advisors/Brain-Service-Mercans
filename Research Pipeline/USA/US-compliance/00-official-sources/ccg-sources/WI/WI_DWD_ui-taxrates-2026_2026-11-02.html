Unemployment Insurance 2026 Tax Rates

Back to Most Recent Tax Rates

  • Under Wis. Stat. § 108.18, Schedule D is in effect for 2026.
  • Taxable wage base $14,000 - The first $14,000 in wages paid to each employee in a calendar year is taxed. Wages paid to each employee above the $14,000 limit must be reported but are not taxed.
  • Employers with a zero rate are still required to file quarterly contribution and wage reports.
