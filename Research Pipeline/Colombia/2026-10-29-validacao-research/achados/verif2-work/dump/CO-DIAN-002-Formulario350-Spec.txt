Report Matrix!A22	DETAILED COMPLIANCE NOTES (Colombia-specific)
Report Matrix!A23	SUBMISSION CHANNEL
Report Matrix!A24	- Channel: online_form | Portal: DIAN Electronic Computer Services (Muisca), www.dian.gov.co | Source: DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.2.2.26 para. 1 and the form's own instruction guide, which repeatedly refers to 'el SI de Diligenciamiento' computing casillas automatically.
Report Matrix!A25	- This is a PORTAL ROUND-TRIP, not machine-to-machine. Do not model it on the electronic payroll: DIAN publishes no XSD, no cahier des charges and no import template for Form 350. The engine's deliverable is a reconciled casilla-value set, not a file.
Report Matrix!A26	- The DIAN service computes several casillas itself - 55, 56, 57, 58, 109, 110, 111, 112 from the sheet-2 annex; 130, 134, 136 and 138 as totals; 147 as 145 x 146; and 28 from 27. Payroll must not attempt to supply those.
Report Matrix!A27	- Signature is by the Electronic Signature Instrument (IFE) authorised by DIAN - an authenticated portal credential, not an X.509 document signature as in the electronic payroll.
Report Matrix!A28	- The tax portal is NOT the social-security channel. PILA is filed through a licensed private information operator and never touches Muisca.
Report Matrix!A29	INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
Report Matrix!A30	- Integration: outbound only - the engine delivers the reconciled casilla-value set to the DIAN portal. There is no inbound file: the portal returns an on-screen acknowledgement and a form number (casilla 4), which the engine should capture for the audit trail and for any later corrective return (casilla 26).
Report Matrix!A31	- Product mapping is required: the engine must accumulate withholding BY COUNTERPARTY TYPE - natural person versus legal person - and, within natural persons, must separate resident employment income (casillas 77 / 93) from payments to non-residents (the sheet-2 annex). The 2024 redesign introduced that separation; the pre-2024 form did not have it.
Report Matrix!A32	- Payroll owns six casillas directly - 77 and 93 for resident employees, 78 and 94 for the pensions the employer pays itself, and, for non-residents, the sheet-2 rows that roll into 109 / 111 or 110 / 112 - plus casilla 129 where a prior over-withholding is reversed. Everything else on the form comes from accounts payable or the tax function.
Report Matrix!A33	RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT
Report Matrix!A34	- RESIDENT employees are reported on SHEET 1: the depurated ET art. 388 base in casilla 77 and the withholding under the art. 383 progressive table in casilla 93.
Report Matrix!A35	- NON-RESIDENT foreign natural persons are reported in the 'Pagos al exterior' section: SHEET 2 rows carrying the sheet-2 payment concept and the DIAN country-table code, rolling up to casillas 109 / 111 (no treaty) or 110 / 112 (treaty). The base in casilla 145 is the NOMINAL (gross) payment, because ET art. 408 admits no depuration, and the rate in casilla 146 is 20%. They are excluded from casillas 77 / 93.
Report Matrix!A36	- The sheet-2 concepto de pago (casilla 142) for a non-resident employee is concept 5, 'Prestaciones de servicios y compensacion por servicios personales', for a non-treaty country, and concept 24, 'Rentas de trabajo', for a treaty country. Concept 24's own definition on the form is 'rentas de trabajo de cualquier naturaleza EN RELACION CON UN EMPLEO a favor de personas naturales no residentes' - it is unambiguously the employee concept.
Report Matrix!A37	- The same employee therefore MOVES BETWEEN SHEETS during the year when residence is acquired: sheet 2 while non-resident, sheet 1 from the payment following the date the 183-day test is met. The switch is prospective only; the earlier months are not restated (DIAN Concept 011108 of 29 June 2026).
Report Matrix!A38	- Directors' fees to a non-resident go to concept 25 (Honorarios de directores) and employer-paid pensions to concept 27 (Pensiones) - neither is the employee concept, and mis-selecting them changes the treaty article applied.
Report Matrix!A39	RELATED FILINGS / CROSS-REFERENCES
Report Matrix!A40	- Casilla 93 plus casilla 94 must reconcile to the sum of RetencionFuente (NIE177) across every electronic payroll document for the same month - CO-DIAN-001 - after the nearest-1,000 rounding. The contingent withholding of ET arts. 126-1 / 126-4 is practised by the fund and is in neither casilla; the theoretical and hypothetical wage types (21114, 21619, 25834) are out of scope; wage type 21330 is included only where it carries the withholding actually deducted.
Report Matrix!A41	- Casilla 27 (art. 114-1 exonerated self-withholder) rests on the SAME exoneration test as PILA file tipo 1 field 33 and Record Tipo 2 field 76 - CO-PILA-003.
Report Matrix!A42	- The twelve monthly casilla-93 figures do NOT produce Form 220. Colombia imposes no employer year-end reconciliation; Form 220 (CO-DIAN-004) is a SEPARATE per-employee obligation built from the annual accumulation, not from the monthly returns.
Report Matrix!A43	- Casilla 77 is an AGGREGATE base; the per-employee detail behind it is reported annually in Format 2276 (CO-DIAN-005), which is why the two cannot be derived from one another.
Report Matrix!A44	- Form 350 aggregates every withholding the employer makes, including on supplier payments and VAT. Payroll is one contributor among several; the return is owned by the employer's tax function.

Payroll Scope Summary!A5	═══ WHO GETS REPORTED ═══
Payroll Scope Summary!A6	Indefinite-term employee (indefinite-term contract)
Payroll Scope Summary!B6	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C6	The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.
Payroll Scope Summary!A7	Fixed-term employee (fixed-term contract, max 4 years)
Payroll Scope Summary!B7	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C7	Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.
Payroll Scope Summary!A8	Specific-work or task employee (contract por obra o labor)
Payroll Scope Summary!B8	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C8	An employment contract; CST art. 46. Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).
Payroll Scope Summary!A9	Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity)
Payroll Scope Summary!B9	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C9	Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA return type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.
Payroll Scope Summary!A10	Part-time employee
Payroll Scope Summary!B10	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C10	Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
Payroll Scope Summary!A11	All-inclusive salary employee (all-inclusive salary, >= 13 SMMLV)
Payroll Scope Summary!B11	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C11	CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the electronic payroll carries SalarioIntegral = true.
Payroll Scope Summary!A12	Apprentice (apprenticeship contract)
Payroll Scope Summary!B12	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C12	Law 2466 of 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decree 0223 of 5 March 2026. Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 4; PILA tipo cotizante 19 'Aprendices etapa lectiva Ley 2466 de 2025' for the lectiva phase from the health period of August 2025 (code 12 only for earlier periods). For health periods before August 2025, code 19 covers the productive phase; from the health period of August 2025, AT2 v30 names no code for the productive phase (AT2 v30 §2.1.2.3.1 campo 5, types 12 and 19).
Payroll Scope Summary!A13	Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias)
Payroll Scope Summary!B13	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C13	Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
Payroll Scope Summary!A14	Teleworkers and remote workers
Payroll Scope Summary!B14	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C14	Law 1221 of 2008 as amended by Law 2466 of 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the electronic payroll carries a dedicated Teletrabajo earning element (NIE158).
Payroll Scope Summary!A15	Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract
Payroll Scope Summary!B15	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C15	Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
Payroll Scope Summary!A16	NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract
Payroll Scope Summary!B16	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C16	IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Reported in the 'Pagos al exterior' section - sheet-2 rows with the sheet-2 payment concept and the DIAN country-table code - and excluded from casillas 77 / 93: withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the NOMINAL (gross) value, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concept 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
Payroll Scope Summary!A17	Employed old-age pensioner in active service
Payroll Scope Summary!B17	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C17	PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
Payroll Scope Summary!A18	Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract
Payroll Scope Summary!B18	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C18	Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
Payroll Scope Summary!A19	Independent contractor (service contract)
Payroll Scope Summary!B19	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C19	Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (return types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Form 350, but under a different casilla.
Payroll Scope Summary!A20	Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity)
Payroll Scope Summary!B20	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C20	Public-sector employees are outside the scope of this specification; the public-sector PILA return types T and F are not produced.
Payroll Scope Summary!A21	Non-executive director paid fees only (junta directiva)
Payroll Scope Summary!B21	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C21	Not an employee where no employment contract exists: directors' fees are not employment income and are not produced by payroll. The withholding on them is declared by the employer's accounts-payable or tax function - in the natural-person fees casillas (79 / 95, or 77 / 93 where the fee is withheld under the ET art. 383 table, art. 383 para. 2) for a resident director and under sheet-2 concept 25 (Honorarios de directores) for a non-resident director - never as payroll employment income. A director holding an employment contract is reported as an ordinary employee (see 'Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract').
Payroll Scope Summary!A22	Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer
Payroll Scope Summary!B22	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C22	The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
Payroll Scope Summary!A23	═══ AMOUNTS AND CONCEPTS REPORTED ═══
Payroll Scope Summary!A24	Depurated taxable base of resident employees
Payroll Scope Summary!B24	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C24	Casilla 77. The ET art. 388 base after non-constitutive income, art. 387 deductions and the 25% exempt income - NOT the gross.
Payroll Scope Summary!A25	Withholding on resident employees
Payroll Scope Summary!B25	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C25	Casilla 93. The art. 383 progressive table result, approximated to the nearest 1,000.
Payroll Scope Summary!A26	Gross payments to non-resident employees
Payroll Scope Summary!B26	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C26	Sheet 2 casilla 145, rolling up to casilla 109 or 110. The NOMINAL (gross) value, because art. 408 admits no depuration. Excluded from casilla 77.
Payroll Scope Summary!A27	Withholding on non-resident employees
Payroll Scope Summary!B27	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C27	Sheet 2 casilla 147, rolling up to casilla 111 or 112. The flat 20% of art. 408, or the treaty rate.
Payroll Scope Summary!A28	Employer-paid pension income above 1,000 UVT
Payroll Scope Summary!B28	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C28	Casillas 78 / 94, where the employer itself pays pensions (the retirees' pay group): the taxable excess over 1,000 UVT in 78 and the withholding actually practised on it (WT 21140, WT 21155) in 94 - never in 77 / 93. Casilla 93 plus casilla 94 reconcile to the electronic payroll RetencionFuente.
Payroll Scope Summary!A29	Reversal of excess or undue withholding
Payroll Scope Summary!B29	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C29	Casilla 129, where an employee's withholding is adjusted downward in a later month.
Payroll Scope Summary!A30	Employer social-security contributions
Payroll Scope Summary!B30	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C30	Not a withholding. Reported on PILA. Form 350 is a TAX return only.
Payroll Scope Summary!A31	Employee social-security contributions
Payroll Scope Summary!B31	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C31	Not a withholding by the employer in the ET sense. They reduce the casilla-77 base as non-constitutive income, but are never declared as a retencion.
Payroll Scope Summary!A32	Payments in the 0% band of the art. 383 table
Payroll Scope Summary!B32	PARTIAL
Payroll Scope Summary!C32	The payment IS included in the casilla-77 base; the withholding in casilla 93 is simply nil for that employee. Do not exclude the base.
Payroll Scope Summary!A33	Withholding on supplier and contractor payments
Payroll Scope Summary!B33	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C33	Casillas 29-54 (legal persons) and 79-108 (natural persons). Not produced by payroll: populated by the employer's accounts-payable or tax function. Form 350 aggregates every withholding the employer makes; payroll contributes only casillas 77 / 93, casillas 78 / 94 where the employer pays pensions itself, the sheet-2 rows for non-resident employees and casilla 129 where a prior over-withholding is reversed.
Payroll Scope Summary!A34	VAT and stamp-duty withholding
Payroll Scope Summary!B34	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C34	Casillas 131-135. Owned by the employer's tax function.
Payroll Scope Summary!A35	═══ TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ═══
Payroll Scope Summary!A36	Reported on the accrual (causacion) date
Payroll Scope Summary!B36	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C36	Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
Payroll Scope Summary!A37	Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date
Payroll Scope Summary!B37	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C37	The universal Colombian rule - payment or credit in account. Applies to the electronic payroll (DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2), to Form 350, to Form 220 and to Format 2276.
Payroll Scope Summary!A38	A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month
Payroll Scope Summary!B38	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C38	It does not. The electronic payroll is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2). PILA and Form 350 are likewise monthly.
Payroll Scope Summary!A39	Nil period still filed
Payroll Scope Summary!B39	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C39	A period with no operation subject to withholding is not filed: ET art. 606 para. 2 makes the return non-obligatory for such a period (Population Rules, 'Nil period'). The electronic payroll and PILA have no equivalent exemption, so a month with employees but no withholding still produces those two filings and no Form 350.
Payroll Scope Summary!A40	Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period
Payroll Scope Summary!B40	VARIES
Payroll Scope Summary!C40	Electronic payroll: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Replace or Delete) referencing the original CUNE. Form 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a correction return type N (correcciones) for the period, filed through the same operador as the original return; the C records (correct values) must be equal to or greater than the A records (original values) in IBC and in days, and type N cannot be used for a correction that leads to a refund, which is requested from each administradora outside PILA (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8; Disposiciones comunes a la Planilla N). A return paid after its deadline is filed on type E; type M (mora) applies only to periods before health period March 2014 with a total absence of payment (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8).
Payroll Scope Summary!A41	═══ SPECIAL CASES ═══
Payroll Scope Summary!A42	A month in which no withholding was practised
Payroll Scope Summary!B42	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C42	ET art. 606 para. 2: the return is NOT obligatory for a period with no operation subject to withholding. This is unusual among Colombian filings - the electronic payroll and PILA do not have an equivalent exemption.
Payroll Scope Summary!A43	Employee whose withholding is nil because the base is in the 0% band
Payroll Scope Summary!B43	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C43	The BASE still goes in casilla 77. Only the withholding is nil.
Payroll Scope Summary!A44	Employee who acquires tax residence mid-year
Payroll Scope Summary!B44	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C44	Reported on sheet 2 for the months before residence and on sheet 1 from the payment following the date the 183-day test is met. Prospective only - the earlier months are never restated.
Payroll Scope Summary!A45	Non-resident whose work is performed entirely outside Colombia
Payroll Scope Summary!B45	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C45	Foreign-source income, not taxable in Colombia, no withholding and nothing to declare (DIAN Official letter 28380 of 2019).
Payroll Scope Summary!A46	Filing the return without paying it
Payroll Scope Summary!B46	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C46	ET art. 580-1: a withholding return filed without full payment produces NO legal effect, with no administrative act required. Casilla 980 must equal casilla 138.
Payroll Scope Summary!A47	Correcting a filed return
Payroll Scope Summary!B47	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C47	By a corrective return carrying casilla 25 (correction code) and casilla 26 (the form number being amended). The minimum penalty is 10 UVT of the year the ORIGINAL return was filed.

File Structure!A2	═══ SUBMISSION FORMAT ═══
File Structure!A3	Channel
File Structure!B3	online_form. Data is keyed, or robotically entered, directly into the DIAN Electronic Computer Services (Muisca) form at www.dian.gov.co.
File Structure!A4	Why this is NOT XML
File Structure!B4	DIAN publishes NO XSD, NO cahier des charges and NO import template for Form 350. DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.2.2.26 para. 1 says only that filers must use 'los Servicios Informaticos dispuestos por la entidad, utilizando el Instrumento de Firma Electronica (IFE)', and that DIAN 'pondra a disposicion el Formulario numero 350 en forma virtual en la pagina web'. Contrast the electronic payroll, which has a published XSD and a SOAP contract.
File Structure!A5	Authentication
File Structure!B5	Electronic Signature Instrument (IFE) authorised by DIAN - an authenticated portal credential. NOT an X.509 document signature.
File Structure!A6	Consequence for the model below
File Structure!B6	The structure below is a LOGICAL MODEL - the field list and hierarchy of the return - NOT the wire or submission format. There is no wire format to document.
File Structure!A7	Payment
File Structure!B7	The return must be paid IN FULL. ET art. 580-1: a withholding return filed without full payment produces no legal effect, with no administrative act needed to declare it so. Casilla 980 must equal casilla 138.

Population Rules!A1	POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
Population Rules!A2	Field Name
Population Rules!B2	Source System
Population Rules!C2	Population Logic
Population Rules!D2	Edge Cases
Population Rules!A3	═══ MANDATORY FIELD RULES ═══
Population Rules!A4	Casilla 77 - base employment income
Population Rules!B4	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C4	Accumulate, across every RESIDENT employee paid in the month, the taxable base actually used to compute the withholding: the ET art. 388 DEPURATED base - gross taxable payments less non-constitutive income (mandatory health, pension and solidarity-fund contributions), less the art. 387 deductions elected in writing by the employee, less the 25% exempt income of art. 206 num. 10, subject to the 40% and 1,340-UVT caps. Do NOT report the gross. Non-resident foreign natural persons are not accumulated here: they are reported as sheet-2 rows in the 'Pagos al exterior' section at the nominal (gross) value. Pension income the employer pays itself is not accumulated here: it goes to casilla 78. Round the accumulated total to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577), once, at the end - never round each employee and then sum.
Population Rules!D4	The casilla also sweeps in fees, services and commissions paid to NATURAL persons that are withheld under the art. 383 table (ET art. 383 para. 2, added by Law 2277 of 2022 art. 8). Payroll supplies only the EMPLOYMENT component; the fee, service and commission component is supplied by the employer's accounts-payable or tax function, which also owns casillas 79 / 95.
Population Rules!A5	Casilla 93 - withholding employment income
Population Rules!B5	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C5	Accumulate the withholding at source actually withheld on work income from resident natural persons in the month: wage type 2552 'Income Tax (employee)', plus the withholding deducted on 21129 (terminations) and, where the WTC classifies them as withholding actually deducted, on 25832 (stock-award vesting) and 25833 (IC local), in the month it is practised, across every pay group and run. Exclude the contingent withholding of ET arts. 126-1 / 126-4 - the withholding computed without the AFC and voluntary-pension contributions less the withholding computed with them - which is practised by the fund on an early withdrawal and never by the employer, and exclude the theoretical and hypothetical wage types 21114, 21619 and 25834, which carry no withholding actually practised (wage type 21330 is included only where it carries the withholding actually deducted). Withholding on the pensions the employer pays itself (21140, 21155) goes to casilla 94, not here. Each employee's withholding is itself already approximated to the nearest 1,000 under ET art. 868; accumulate those approximated figures and then apply the art. 577 nearest-1,000 rounding to the total. Reconcile casilla 93 plus casilla 94 to the sum of RetencionFuente (NIE177) across every electronic payroll document for the same month BEFORE filing. Select the runs by their payment date - payment or credit to account, whichever comes first - not by the pay period they settle (ET art. 383; ET art. 27; instruction guide of Res. DIAN 000031/2024).
Population Rules!D5	An employee whose depurated base falls in the 0% band contributes zero here but still contributes their base to casilla 77. Excluding the base is a common and material error.
Population Rules!A6	Casillas 78 / 94 - pension income
Population Rules!B6	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C6	Populate where the EMPLOYER itself pays pensions (the retirees' pay group). Casilla 78: the monthly amount EXCEEDING 1,000 UVT (COP 52,374,000 in 2026) under ET art. 206 num. 5, for the wage types classified as pension income; the pension-income classification of each wage type (casilla 78 rather than casilla 77) comes from the Wage Type Catalogue (WTC) attribute. Casilla 94: the withholding actually practised on that excess - wage type 21140 'Personal Income Tax Withholding-Retirees- 6799' and wage type 21155 'Income Tax Pensioner'. Neither amount enters casillas 77 / 93. Where the pension is paid by a pension fund, the fund is the withholding agent and nothing is reported here.
Population Rules!D6	Colombia has no employer year-end reconciliation, so an error here is amended only by a corrective return, not by a December true-up.
Population Rules!A7	Casilla 12 - regional tax office
Population Rules!B7	Company master data
Population Rules!C7	Copy the code from casilla 12 of the entity's RUT. It is a static configuration attribute; it does not change with the period and must not be re-derived monthly.
Population Rules!D7	A branch structure does not change this casilla - the return is filed for the principal domicile of the legal entity.
Population Rules!A8	═══ CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ═══
Population Rules!A9	Nil period
Population Rules!B9	Engine (control)
Population Rules!C9	Where NO operation subject to withholding occurred in the month, DO NOT file. ET art. 606 para. 2 makes the return non-obligatory for such a period. This is unusual: the electronic payroll and PILA have no equivalent exemption, so a month with employees but no withholding still produces those two filings while producing no Form 350.
Population Rules!D9	Filing an all-zero return is not an error, but it creates a record that must then be amended if a late payment is discovered. Prefer not filing where the exemption genuinely applies, and document the decision.
Population Rules!A10	Payment must accompany the return
Population Rules!B10	Engine (control)
Population Rules!C10	Casilla 980 (payment total) must equal casilla 138 (total withholdings mas sanciones). ET art. 580-1: a withholding return filed WITHOUT full payment produces NO legal effect, and no administrative act is needed to say so. Until the taxpayer files again WITH payment, the original filing is treated as never made.
Population Rules!D10	This is the single most expensive procedural trap on this return: a filed-but-unpaid return accrues late-filing penalties as though nothing had been filed.
Population Rules!A11	Do not key the portal-derived casillas
Population Rules!B11	Engine (control)
Population Rules!C11	The DIAN service derives casillas 28 (from 27), 55, 56, 57, 58, 109, 110, 111 and 112 (from the sheet-2 annex totals 148 to 155), 130, 134, 136, 138 and 147 (145 x 146). Supply only the inputs. Attempting to key a derived casilla either fails or, worse, creates an inconsistency the portal silently overwrites.
Population Rules!D11	For the non-resident block this means payroll supplies sheet-2 ROWS - casillas 141 to 146 - and never the sheet-1 casillas 109 to 112.
Population Rules!A12	Counterparty-type separation
Population Rules!B12	Engine (accumulation)
Population Rules!C12	The 2024 form separates withholding on NATURAL persons from withholding on LEGAL persons across the whole grid. The engine must therefore accumulate withholding by counterparty type from the transaction level, not classify it at reporting time. The pre-2024 form did not require this and a migrated accumulation will not carry the flag.
Population Rules!D12	Payroll is inherently natural-person only, so its own accumulation is unambiguous. The risk lies in a shared withholding ledger where supplier and employee withholding are commingled.
Population Rules!A13	═══ AMOUNT FORMAT RULES ═══
Population Rules!A14	All money casillas
Population Rules!B14	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C14	Whole pesos, then rounded to the NEAREST 1,000 under ET art. 577. Apply the rounding ONCE, to the accumulated casilla value, not to each contributing record. No decimals, no thousand separators in the transmitted value.
Population Rules!D14	Rounding each employee to the nearest 1,000 and then summing produces a total that can differ from the correctly rounded aggregate by several thousand pesos across a large population.
Population Rules!A15	Three conventions must not be blended
Population Rules!B15	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C15	The SAME withholding appears as three different numbers across the Colombian artefacts: nearest-1,000 here; two decimals round-half-to-even in the electronic payroll; and irrelevant in PILA, which carries contributions not tax. Store the unrounded figure once and apply each convention at output.
Population Rules!D15	The reconciliation between casilla 93 plus casilla 94 and SUM(NIE177) must therefore be performed on the UNROUNDED values with a tolerance, not on the rounded ones.
Population Rules!A16	Minimum penalty
Population Rules!B16	Engine (control)
Population Rules!C16	Where casilla 137 is populated, the amount cannot be below 10 UVT. For 2026 that is 10 x 52,374 = COP 523,740. For a CORRECTIVE return the minimum is 10 UVT of the year in which the ORIGINAL return was filed, not of the current year.
Population Rules!D16	A correction of a 2024 return filed in 2026 uses the 2024 UVT for the minimum penalty. Store the original filing year with the correction.
Population Rules!A17	═══ PAYROLL-SPECIFIC POPULATION RULES ═══
Population Rules!A18	Non-resident employees - sheet 2 rows
Population Rules!B18	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C18	For each non-resident foreign natural person, create one sheet-2 row per country and treaty status in the 'Pagos al exterior' section. Set casilla 141 to SIN CONVENIO or CON CONVENIO; casilla 142 to concept 5 (Prestaciones de servicios y compensacion por servicios personales) for non-treaty countries or concept 24 (Rentas de trabajo) for treaty countries; casilla 143 to Persona natural; casilla 144 to the DIAN country-table code of the country of tax residence (the same code used in the RUT and in exogenous information); casilla 145 to the NOMINAL (gross) payment - not a depurated base, because ET art. 408 admits no depuration; and casilla 146 to 20.00, or to the treaty rate. The employee is excluded from casillas 77 and 93. The portal computes casilla 147 and the four totals.
Population Rules!D18	Rows for the same country, concept and person type may be aggregated. Do NOT aggregate across treaty statuses or across concepts - the rate and the treaty article differ. Directors' fees belong in concept 25 and employer-paid pensions in concept 27, never in 24.
Population Rules!A19	Residence switch mid-year
Population Rules!B19	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C19	Carry a residence flag with an effective date on the employee record and re-evaluate it every period against ET art. 10 - presence exceeding 183 calendar days, continuous or not, in any 365-day period. Until the test is met, report the employee on sheet 2 at the flat 20%. From the payment FOLLOWING the date the test is met, report on sheet 1 casillas 77 and 93 under the art. 383 table. Never restate the earlier months.
Population Rules!D19	DIAN Concept 011108 of 29 June 2026 expressly rejects withholding under art. 383 from day one in anticipation of future residence, even on a contract exceeding 183 days. Withholding is determined at the moment of each payment on the status then held. There is no payroll true-up; the employee reconciles on their own return.
Population Rules!A20	Work performed entirely abroad
Population Rules!B20	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C20	Where a NON-RESIDENT performs the work entirely outside Colombia, the income is foreign-source and there is NO Colombian withholding and nothing to report on this return - neither sheet 1 nor sheet 2. Where a RESIDENT performs work abroad, the income IS taxable on the worldwide basis and goes to casillas 77 and 93 as normal.
Population Rules!D20	The distinguishing attribute is the place-of-work country carried on the electronic payroll (NIE050). Keep the two records consistent: a document showing LugarTrabajoPais other than CO for a non-resident should produce no Form 350 line.
Population Rules!A21	Casilla 129 - reversing an over-withholding
Population Rules!B21	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C21	Where an employee's withholding is adjusted downward in a later month - a retroactive change to the art. 387 deductions, a cancelled payment, or an arithmetic error - report the reversal in casilla 129 of the month in which the correction is made, rather than correcting the earlier return. Where the month's withholding is insufficient to absorb the credit, the instruction guide permits carrying it to the immediately following periods.
Population Rules!D21	Casilla 129 also carries reintegros of non-transferable withholding attributable to a resident natural-person beneficiary or a resident foreign investor under DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.2.4.37 - not a payroll case, but the same casilla.
Population Rules!A22	Casilla 27 and the art. 114-1 exoneration
Population Rules!B22	Company configuration
Population Rules!C22	Populate casilla 27 only where the employer is a LEGAL person exonerated from employer contributions under ET art. 114-1. The SAME exoneration test - employees earning less than 10 SMMLV (COP 17,509,050 in 2026) - drives PILA file tipo 1 field 33 and PILA Record Tipo 2 field 76. Configure the test ONCE and consume it in both places.
Population Rules!D22	The exoneration is tested MONTHLY and PER EMPLOYEE for PILA purposes, but the self-withholding activity code here is an entity-level, static attribute. Do not conflate the two granularities.
