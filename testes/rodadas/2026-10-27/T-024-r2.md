## Resposta

**Neither date on its own, if you use a plain "age < 25" test.** The rule works by calendar month, not by date. Under Szja tv. 29/F § (3), the eligible months are those in which the young person has not yet turned 25, and the last eligible month is the month in which they turn 25. NAV confirms the allowance can be claimed for the last time in the month the person turns 25.

This is how each date behaves with a strict "age < 25" test:

- **Period end date:** this wrongly drops the birthday month whenever the birthday falls on or before the last day of the period.
- **Period begin date:** this works except when the 25th birthday falls on the 1st of the month. In that case the employee is already 25 on the begin date, but that month is still eligible.

The test that matches the statute is:

`Eligible if 25th birthday (DOB + 25 years) ≥ period begin date`

You can also compare year and month: YYYYMM of the 25th birthday ≥ YYYYMM of the period. A third equivalent is age < 25 on the day before the period begin date.

**Which month counts:** for employment income, the allowance applies to income for the eligibility months, meaning income accounted for those months (29/F § (2)). The period dates should therefore be the dates of the month the wages relate to, not the payment date. This matters for wages paid in the following month and for retro or back pay.

**Date of birth source:** use the employee's date of birth from the personal/master data. This is the same data the employer holds for NAV reporting, since the 2021 amending act added date of birth as the data needed to apply the 29/F allowance. Don't use a payroll input or an age field, because age has to be derived per period. I don't have the Mercans DD variable list for Hungary, so I can't give you the exact variable name. It should be the employee-level DOB field, not a calculated age.

Sources: NAV, [25 év alatti fiatalok kedvezménye](https://nav.gov.hu/ado/szja/szja-kedvezmenyek/25-ev-alatti-fiatalok-kedvezmenye); Szja tv. 29/F § (2)–(3), as introduced by [T/15395](https://www.parlament.hu/irom41/15395/15395-0009.pdf).
