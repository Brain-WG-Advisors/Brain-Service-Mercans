Occupational license fee: rates and current forms

Online filing

You can file online through our portal for businesses to submit all occupational tax forms. These include:

  • Estimated net profits
  • Net profit extensions
  • Net profit forms
  • Annual returns

Previously, the only way to file was by mail and pay by check. This expands on the online payment options launched in 2025 for net profit payers. The online portal will provide convenience for our business community and greater efficiency for our staff in processing refunds.

Online filing user guides

The Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government assesses an occupational license fee of 2.25%. The fee applies to an individual's compensation and a business's net profits. 

Current year forms

Business License Application

  • Form 228 IP – application for a license fee account

Payroll Withholdings

  • Form 220-221 – Employer's return of license fee withheld is not available online or by fax. Official copies are mailed to registered businesses in January. Failure to use the original forms delays processing and may cause your return to be considered late. If the original form is lost or damaged, call (859) 280-8300 for a replacement.
  • Form 222 – 2025 reconciliation of license fee withheld *Please note the mailing address for this form.
  • Form 211-22 – 2025 employee refund application (valid until February 29, 2028)
  • Form 211-65 – 2025 employee refund application, over age 65 (valid until February 29, 2028)

Net Profits

  • Form 228 ENP – 2026 quarterly estimated net profit payment form
  • Form 228  – 2026 due dates
  • Form 228 – 2025 net profit license fee return
  • Form 228 – 2025 net profit license fee return instructions
  • Form 228 EXT – Request for Extension of time to file – NOTICE: The Division of Revenue will accept copies of federal Form 4868, 8736 or 7004 in lieu of Lexington's Form 228-EXT to request an extension of time to file the Net Profit License Fee Return but all such requests must include the local account number; must be received by the original due date and must be accompanied by the amount reasonably estimated as due.

1099 Contractors

  • Form 1099-LX – 1099 transmittal sheet
