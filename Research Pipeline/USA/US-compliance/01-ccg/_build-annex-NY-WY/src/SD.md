## @labour
- **Minimum wage (SDCL 60-11-3, 60-11-3.2) [US-SDS5-001, US-SDS5-003]:** {{sd.minwage.hourly@2026-01-01}} USD per hour from 2026-01-01 and {{sd.minwage.hourly@2027-01-01}} USD from 2027-01-01 (published).
- **Tipped employees (60-11-3.1) [US-SDS5-001, US-SDS5-003]:** cash wage at least half the minimum wage: {{sd.minwage.tipped.cash_wage@2026-01-01}} USD in 2026 and {{sd.minwage.tipped.cash_wage@2027-01-01}} USD in 2027; the employer makes up any shortfall each pay period and keeps a record of all tips.
- **Opportunity wage** for young workers per FLSA s.6 (60-11-4.1); apprentices, learners and persons with a developmental disability only with a DLR permit (60-11-5) [US-SDS5-001].
- **Local minimum wage:** none located; no preemption clause in the text read (open point below).
- **Overtime:** {{sd.labour.overtime.state_rule}} [US-SDS5-012]. No state break or holiday-pay requirement [US-SDS5-011].
- **Pay frequency and method (60-11-9) [US-SDS5-001]:** {{sd.pay_frequency.rule}}, in lawful money; check, cash or direct deposit unless otherwise agreed. No express consent rule for direct deposit or paycards located (open point below). The wage-payment sections cover employers "employing any person of this state" (60-11-8).

## @income_tax
- **No state income tax and no withholding** (income tax exists: {{sd.pit.exists}}) [US-SD-001]: no state W-4, supplemental rate, rounding rule, state W-2 or reciprocity.
- **No local income tax** (exists: {{sd.local_wage_tax.exists}}) [US-SDP52-001].
- **Resident and nonresident matrix.** Nothing is withheld for South Dakota for any income type, resident or nonresident. A South Dakota resident working in a wage-taxing state is withheld for that state under its rules (Annex MULTI).

## @social
- **Reemployment Assistance (UI), DLR [US-SDS5-002, US-SDS5-018].**
  - Taxable wage base {{sd.sui.wage_base}} USD, fixed in statute and not indexed (61-1-46); wages already reported to another state in the year count toward it: {{sd.sui.wage_base.other_state_credit}}.
  - New employer: {{sd.ui.rate.new_employer.year1}} % in year one, then {{sd.ui.rate.new_employer.later}} % while the account balance is positive until experience-rated; construction new employers {{sd.ui.rate.new_employer.construction.year1}} % in year one (61-5-24).
  - Experience-rated employers: the schedule is set by the fund's average high cost multiplier (61-5-25). Schedule in effect for 2026: {{sd.ui.schedule.in_effect}}; its top rate is {{sd.sui.employer.rate.max}} % (61-5-25.7, SL 2025 ch 199). A DLR page quoting a higher maximum predates 2026; the statute governs [US-SDS5-013].
  - Administrative fee {{sd.ui.admin_fee.rate}} % for every experience-rated employer from 2026 (61-5-28.1); investment fee {{sd.ui.investment_fee.new_employer.rate}} % for employers not yet rated, lower by reserve ratio for rated employers (61-5-29). Neither fee may be deducted from wages. Fund-balance surcharge (61-5-28) dormant.
  - Employee contribution {{sd.sui.employee.rate}} % [US-SDP51-002].
- **UI wages [US-SDS5-013]:** employee pre-tax deferrals are included ({{sd.sui.wages.401k_included}}) and cafeteria-plan amounts are included ({{sd.sui.wages.section125_included}}); employer plan contributions and non-required severance are excluded.
- **No SDI, TDI, PFML, LTC or transit levy** (exists: {{sd.sdi_pfml.exists}}) [US-SDP51-002].

## @payslip
- **No pay-statement statute** (exists: {{sd.payslip.statute.exists}}) [US-SDS5-001, US-SD-003, US-SDL-001]; federal recordkeeping applies (Annex FED). The RA handbook lists the per-employee records to keep [US-SDP51-002]; the retention period is open (below).

## @reporting
- **No state withholding return, reconciliation or state W-2.**
- **Quarterly RA wage report (Form 21) [US-SDS5-013, US-SDS5-014]:** due (received by) {{sd.report.ui.quarterly_due}}; zero reports required; online system or file upload; corrections on Form 21C; domestic employers may elect annual filing [US-SDS5-020].
- **New hire (25-7A-3.3) [US-SDS5-007, US-SDS5-015]:** within {{sd.newhire.report_days}} days, or two monthly electronic transmissions, to the DLR New Hire Reporting Center; covers rehires; a multistate employer may designate one state.
- **Registration and agents:** online registration [US-SDS5-020]; RA power of attorney [US-SDP51-001].

## @leave
- **No paid sick leave, vacation, holiday or PFML statute** (sick leave statute exists: {{sd.leave.sick.state_statute.exists}}) [US-SDS5-011].
- **Voting leave (12-3-5) [US-SDS5-008]:** {{sd.leave.voting.paid_hours}} consecutive hours off while the polls are open, with no deduction from wages, unless the employee already has that much off-duty time.
- **Jury duty:** protection from discharge; pay at the employer's discretion (16-13-41.1). Military: SCRA protections for National Guard; no state duty to pay [US-SDS5-009].

## @termination
- **Final pay (60-11-10, 60-11-11) [US-SDS5-001, US-SDS5-010]:** {{sd.final_pay.rule}}, on discharge or resignation.
- **Accrued vacation:** no statutory payout rule; follows the agreement or policy (case law open, below) [US-SDS5-011]. No severance, separation-notice or WARN duty.

## @bik
- **Income tax:** not applicable. **UI base:** employee deferrals and cafeteria-plan amounts included; employer plan contributions excluded; board, lodging and goods included at cash value [US-SDS5-013].

## @deductions
- **Wage-deduction authorisation:** no SD statute located; federal limits apply (open point below).
- **Ordinary garnishment (21-18-51) [US-SDS5-006]:** the lesser of {{sd.garn.creditor.max_percent}} % of weekly disposable earnings, or disposable earnings minus a protected floor of forty times the minimum wage ({{sd.garn.consumer.floor.weekly}} USD per week in 2026), reduced by {{sd.garn.consumer.dependent_reduction.weekly}} USD per week for each dependent family member living with the debtor. Stricter than the CCPA. Support orders: CCPA support percentages (21-18-52).
- **Child support IWO [US-SDS5-007]:** statutory cap (25-7A-32); first deduction from the first pay after service; remit within the statutory business days; employer fee up to {{sd.garn.iwo.employer_fee.monthly}} USD per month (25-7A-34); current support has priority (25-7A-35).
