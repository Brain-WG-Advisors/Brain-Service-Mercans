## Resposta

For sick leave in Ukraine, the reference period is the **12 calendar months, counted 1st to 1st, that precede the month in which the insured event (the start of incapacity) occurs**, at the employee's last main place of work. This is set in point 25 of the Procedure approved by CMU Resolution No. 1266 of 26.09.2001, and the general rule appears in point 7.

For a worker sick from 24.01.2026, the reference period is January 2025 to December 2025.

Shorter employment follows different rules:
- **Less than 12 months:** the actual full calendar months worked, 1st to 1st (point 26).
- **Less than one month:** the actual calendar days worked before the illness (point 27).
- **Illness on the first day of work:** the benefit is based on the salary or tariff rate set on the day of the insured event (point 28).

To get the average daily salary, the salary accrued in the reference period that was subject to ЄСВ is divided by the number of calendar days of employment in that period. Days not worked for valid reasons are excluded (point 3). Since Resolution No. 533 took effect on 01.05.2026, a month is excluded entirely when the employee was absent for valid reasons on all of its working days. That full-month exclusion was first reinstated by Resolution No. 837, effective 18.07.2025.

The official consolidated text of Resolution No. 1266 is at zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1266-2001-п. I couldn't load it directly, so the paragraph numbers above come from current professional sources (Debet-Kredyt, 7eminar, buhgalter.com.ua) and Pension Fund of Ukraine notices.
