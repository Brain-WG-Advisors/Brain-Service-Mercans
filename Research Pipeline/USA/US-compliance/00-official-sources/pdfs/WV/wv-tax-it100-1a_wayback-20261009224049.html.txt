Search Forms Guidance and Publications Business Individuals Tax.wv.gov Free Tax Workshops &amp; Webinars for New West Virginia Businesses ☰ Open Menu ☰ Open Menu West Virginia Tax Division &gt; Business &gt; Withholding Withholding Tax Forms Page Content Every employer making payment of any wage or salary subject to the West Virginia personal income tax is required to deduct and withhold the tax from such wages or salaries and remit the tax withheld to the Tax Division. Employers are required to remit the withheld tax on or before the fifteenth day of the succeeding month. Quarterly returns are due on or before the last day of the month following the end of the calendar quarter.
