New employers or employers new to filing wages to the state of Colorado start paying Unemployment Insurance (UI) premiums at an introductory rate based on the industry they support. 

New employers are employers who meet the liability requirements but do not qualify for an experience or computed rate because the do not have the required benefit charging history at the time rates are computed.

Current Introductory Rates

The 2027 introductory rate consists of the Base Rate, the employer's Support Rate. The Solvency Surcharge is not in affect for the 2027 calendar year.
