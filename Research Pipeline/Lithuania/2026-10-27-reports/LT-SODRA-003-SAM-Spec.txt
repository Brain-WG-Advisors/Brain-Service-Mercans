R4: P35, P14T, P17T - revision block | Period control | Mark P35 and populate P14T and P17T only where the notification revises a previously submitted period, and note that the corrected period may differ from the submission period. Be clear which route applies: the Rules describe correction of already-accepted data through the PT electronic request annexes PT-SAM3SD, PT-SAM3SD-M and PT-SAM3SDP (points 86.6-86.8), with the corrected period stated in the annex (point 92.4) and only the affected insured persons listed (points 95.4 and 96.4). R5: P20.1-P20.5, P21.1-P21.5, P23 - annex summary grid | Derived from the built annexes | Derive the whole grid from the annexes after they are built, never from configuration. Point 54.1: mark P20.1 and P21.1 with 'x' where the respective annex is submitted. Point 54.2: P20.2 and P21.2 carry the page counts. Point 54.3: P23 is the total, i.e. P20.2 plus P21.2. Point 54.4: P20.3 and P21.3 carry the insured person counts. Point 54.5: P20.4 and P21.4 carry the insurable income sums. Point 54.6: P20.5 and P21.5 carry the contribution sums. Error C2330 fires where an annex is present but its flag is unmarked, and C2338 requires a positive person count. | SAM3SD submitted with no SAM3SDP, leaving the whole P21 row empty; SAM3SDP person count where one person has several period lines; page count arithmetic that assumes 6 rows per page for SAM3SDP when it holds 3; annex built but flag left unmarked.
R6: SAM3SD A1-A4P - line and person identification | Employee master data | Number lines consecutively with no gap in the middle of a page - error C2343 rejects incorrect numbering or unfilled mid-page rows, so a late filter that removes an employee must renumber rather than leave a hole. Use SIX detail rows per page. A2 takes the 11-digit Lithuanian personal code. A3 is a COMPOSITE that must be decomposed: the single printed box maps to two XML elements with identical MXFD comments but different data types, a 2-character Text series and a 7-digit Decimal number, so writing the stored number whole into either element fails type validation. A4V and A4P hold the first name and surname SEPARATELY, in contrast to GPM312 L3/U3 which concatenate them with a mandated blank - the same master data therefore feeds the two filings differently and no shared full-name mapping satisfies both. | Foreign insured person with no 11-digit personal code, against a fixed 11-cell Decimal field; stored insurance number that cannot be split cleanly into series and number; employee removed after line numbering; name exceeding 68 characters; diacritics lost in transliteration, breaking SODRA's match against A2 and A3.
R7: SAM3SD A11, P3, A12 - the whole-month contribution triple | Payroll results | Populate A11 with the income computed for the CALENDAR MONTH on which contributions are calculated (point 55.1), EXCLUDING income from sport and performer activity and under copyright contracts, and EXCLUDING tantiemes and supervisory-board, management-board or loan-committee remuneration paid instead of or with tantiemes. That exclusion list means the SODRA base is NOT the VMI taxable gross: amounts GPM312 reports under codes 44, 51, 52, 60 and 61 are deliberately outside A11. Populate P3 with the general rate applicable at the START of the calendar month (point 55.5) - a point-in-time rate, so a mid-month category change does not alter it. Populate A12 with the contribution computed at P3 on A11 (point 55.2). SODRA enforces A12 = A11 x P3 line by line (error C2344), so the three values cannot be taken from three independent payroll results without reconciliation, and the rounding used must match SODRA's to the cent. | Negative retro adjustment exceeding the month's insurable income, which the rules do not contemplate and the MXFD sets no MinValue for; insured category changing mid-month while P3 stays at the month-start value; half-up rounding difference of one cent tripping C2344; employee paid only excluded income types, leaving A11 at zero; annual leave pay spanning two months, which point 51.4 splits by calendar month.
R8: SAM3SD - population and suppression | Payroll period control | Point 51.1: complete SAM3SD for ALL insured persons except those for whom a 2-SD notification was filed during the calendar month - the annex title itself says 'be atleistuju', excluding leavers. Point 50 removes the persons listed in points 16.9-16.12, 16.16-16.17 and 16.19 from SAM entirely. Point 49 permits the whole SAM to be omitted where no contributions were due for any insured person for the period, excluding those covered by a 2-SD during the month, AND 9-SD and/or 12-SD notifications were filed for the whole reporting period - so suppression depends on other forms having been filed, not on the payroll being empty. Point 49-1 permits one SAM covering several insured categories at different rates, or a separate SAM for part of the population. | Employee who left mid-month with a 2-SD filed, who must be excluded from SAM3SD but may still need SAM3SDP treatment; whole workforce on parental leave with 9-SD filed, permitting suppression; employee in a point 50 category who must not appear at all; decision to file two SAMs for different rate groups under point 49-1, which doubles every page count and cross-foot.
R9: SAM3SDP A5 and A6 - reason code and description | Employee insured-category master data | A5 is what justifies a SAM3SDP line existing at all and is mandatory on every line. THE DOMAIN IS 01-10 AND THERE IS NO 99: the MXFD ListItems enumerate exactly ten values and stop, and points 51.2.1-51.2.10 enumerate exactly ten categories, with point 51.3 repealed from 2019-02-01. Do not offer 99 - SODRA's own form definition will reject it. Two further cautions. Code 04 is expressly limited to insurance periods up to 2021-12-31 and must not be issued for a current period; the office-holder cases it used to cover were replaced by codes 09 and 10 when V-656 added points 51.2.9 and 51.2.10. And points 56.1 and 56.2 cite only 'points 51.2.1-51.2.7' when defining A5 and A6, a cross-reference not updated when 51.2.8, 51.2.9 and 51.2.10 were added, so the rules understate their own live domain by three codes - build to the enumerated points, not to the cross-reference. A6 is only 65 characters against the multi-line statutory descriptions, so agree a standard abbreviation per code rather than truncating the legal text arbitrarily. | Legacy period correction that legitimately needs code 04; office holder whose PSD obligation changes mid-year, moving between codes 09 and 10; a category that appears to fit none of the ten codes, where the absence of a 99 residual leaves no fallback; A6 abbreviation differing between submissions for the same code.
R10: SAM3SDP A21, A22 - within-month insurance period | Employee insurance period data | Each SAM3SDP line is a within-month interval. Point 56.3: A21 is the start of the state social insurance period WITHIN THE CALENDAR MONTH, so a period that began earlier is CLIPPED to the first day of the reporting month rather than reported with its true start. Point 56.5: A22 is the end within the calendar month, so an ongoing period is reported to month end rather than left open. A period crossing a month boundary is therefore split across two monthly notifications. Point 56.4 overrides the derivation for reason code 01: for civil servants receiving compensations the period start within the month is the day AFTER the day of dismissal, not the dismissal date itself. | Period starting in the previous month, requiring clipping to the 1st; ongoing period requiring an artificial month-end close; reason code 01 where the day-after-dismissal rule shifts the start; several discrete days within one month for the same person, needing several lines; period wholly outside the reporting month, which error C2352 rejects.
R11: SAM3SDP A11, P3, A12 - the per-period contribution triple | Payroll results | Populate A11 with the income computed FOR THE A21-A22 PERIOD on which contributions are calculated (point 56.6), P3 with the rate applicable DURING that period (point 56.10), and A12 with the contributions computed during that period (point 56.7). Two differences from SAM3SD must be modelled separately rather than shared. First, P3 here follows the PERIOD, whereas point 55.5 fixes the SAM3SD rate at the month start - so a rate change mid-month splits a SAM3SDP period into two lines but leaves SAM3SD untouched. Second, point 56.6 excludes only sport and performer activity income and copyright-contract income, and does NOT repeat point 55.1's exclusion of tantiemes and supervisory-board remuneration - the two annexes therefore appear to have different base definitions, which is raised as a Query to RA. The identity A12 = A11 x P3 is enforced here too (error C2344 names both annexes). | Rate change mid-period requiring a line split on SAM3SDP but not SAM3SD; tantiemes paid to a SAM3SDP-category person, where the two annexes' exclusion lists diverge; third-party-payer amounts which point 51.6 routes to SAM3SDP in the period computed; court-awarded pay with no determinable period, which point 51.7 spreads proportionally by working days on a 5-day week.
R12: SAM3SD and SAM3SDP P7, P8 - page subtotals | Derived from the detail lines | Compute P7 as the sum of the A11 fields on that PAGE and P8 as the sum of the A12 fields on that page (points 55.3, 55.4, 56.8 and 56.9), in euro to the cent. These are per-page subtotals that coexist with the whole-annex totals in title page boxes P20.4, P20.5, P21.4 and P21.5, so a build must produce both levels. Validation errors C2345 and C2346 check P7 and P8, and their text names both annexes. Because the subtotal is per page, repaginating the annex changes every P7 and P8 as well as PageNumber, PageTotal and the title page grid - so pagination must be settled before totals are computed. | Repagination after totals were computed; a page with fewer than the full six or three rows, whose subtotal covers only the filled rows; rounding differences between the page subtotals and the annex total; page containing only zero-income lines.
R13: Whole submission - channel, signature and timing | Submission control | Points 8 and 249: SD notifications are submitted as an electronic document through EDAS. Point 250: the notification is an FFData-format XML file that must conform to the Electronic SD Notification data structure description PUBLISHED ON THE FUND'S WEBSITE - that description is not in the sources folder, so the FFData envelope must be obtained before an interface is built, and this is the single largest open item on this spec. Point 88 and the signing provision require a secure electronic signature over the file. Point 52 sets the deadline at the 15th of the following calendar month and requires a PT electronic request with correction reason code 01 where SAM is filed late. Point 84.1 notes the employer is informed of acceptance through EDAS, and point 1488 gives SODRA five working days to respond. | FFData structure description not obtainable, blocking the interface; electronic signature certificate expiring before the deadline; deadline falling on a weekend or public holiday, which point 52 does not address for SAM although the NP-SD2 rules do; submission rejected on a C1xxx structural error after the deadline; acceptance notification not received within five working days.
