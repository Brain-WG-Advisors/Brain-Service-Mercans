The Reporting Matrix is not ready for client review. Congo has three statutory payroll filings, all submitted on the authorities' portals (no XML/XSD exists for any of them):

1. **Monthly CNSS bordereau** (nominative; e-déclaration CNSS; before the 15th of M+1) — includes PVID, PF, AT-MP, CAMU (4.55% ER / 2.27% EE / 0.5% solidarité, collected through the CNSS circuit, Décret n°2024-131) and the CNSS share of TUS (5.475%). In the matrix the "CG - CNSS" tab is empty, and CAMU and the TUS CNSS share appear nowhere.
2. **Monthly ITS + TUS declaration to DGID** (aggregate; DGID e-Taxe/SYSTAF online declaration; by the 15th of M+1; TUS DGID share 2.025%, LF 2026 loi n°42-2025). The "CG-DGID-001" tab states submission by XML upload, and its ITS total (279,300 XAF for 120 employees) corresponds to one employee's annual ITS, not the monthly total.
3. **Annual DAS** (DAS1 + DAS2 + DAS3, filed to both CNSS and DGID by 31 March; DAS3 introduced by LF 2026 art. 116 H–I). The "CG-DAS-001" tab is empty.

Content from other countries remains in the file: the "note" tab lists Senegal's reports (État 1024, TRIMF, CFCE, IPRES), the "Payslip" tab is an Estonia example in GHS, the "Global Payment Report" is set to Ghana/GHS, and the "Indemnity Accrual Register" holds another entity's data, including what appear to be employee IDs. "Payslips Local Language" is set to English; the official language is French.

I remain available should there be any discrepancy in this information.
