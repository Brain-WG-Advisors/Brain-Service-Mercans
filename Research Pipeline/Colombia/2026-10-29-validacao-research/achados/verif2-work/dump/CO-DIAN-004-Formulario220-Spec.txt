Report Matrix!A23	SUBMISSION CHANNEL
Report Matrix!A24	- Channel: paper or electronic delivery TO THE EMPLOYEE | Portal: none - this artefact is NOT submitted to any authority | Source: ET arts. 378-380 and the form's own instruction guide, which addresses only the issuing of the certificate.
Report Matrix!A25	- This is the one in-scope Colombian artefact with NO government channel at all. It is issued to the employee and retained by the employer. DIAN sees the same data annually through Format 2276 (CO-DIAN-005), which IS filed - but the two are separate obligations with different field sets and different deadlines.
Report Matrix!A26	- The certificate SUBSTITUTES for the employee's income-tax return where the six ET art. 380 conditions hold (DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.6.1.13.2.7), which is why it carries employee-declared sections that payroll cannot populate.
Report Matrix!A27	INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
Report Matrix!A28	- Integration: outbound only, to the EMPLOYEE rather than to an authority. There is no inbound leg.
Report Matrix!A29	- product_mapping_required = true. The certificate needs per-employee ANNUAL totals by concept, three separate severance buckets, the meal-payment split at 41 UVT, the rolling six-month average labour income, and the AVC deduction which has no dedicated electronic payroll element. None of these can be reconstructed at year end from a single annual gross figure.
Report Matrix!A30	- NOT DERIVABLE FROM THE MONTHLY FILINGS. Colombia imposes NO employer year-end tax reconciliation. This certificate is a SEPARATE annual obligation built from the annual accumulation, and it additionally carries data the employer never sees monthly - the employee's own asset, debt and other-income declarations under ET art. 380.
Report Matrix!A31	RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT
Report Matrix!A32	- RESIDENT: casilla 60 carries the ET art. 383 progressive-table withholding for the year, and casillas 53 to 58 carry the contributions and voluntary savings that depurated the base. The employee may then use the certificate in place of a return under ET art. 380.
Report Matrix!A33	- NON-RESIDENT: the same certificate is issued, but casilla 60 carries the FLAT 20% of ET art. 408 applied to the GROSS - so casilla 52 (total gross income) and the withholding in casilla 60 stand in a simple 20% relationship, with NO depuration visible. The contributions in casillas 53 and 54 are still reported because they were still deducted, but they did NOT reduce the withholding base.
Report Matrix!A34	- The art. 206 num. 4 severance exemption in casilla 59 is an exemption of the RESIDENT ID card-general regime. It does NOT attach to a non-resident's flat withholding, so for a non-resident the six-month average is reported for completeness but has no effect on casilla 60.
Report Matrix!A35	- An employee who ACQUIRES RESIDENCE mid-year receives ONE certificate covering the whole period, with casilla 60 carrying the sum of the 20% months and the art. 383 months. The certificate does not disclose the split; the employee reconciles on their own return. The engine should retain the split internally for audit.
Report Matrix!A36	- The employee's own annual return - Form 110 - is outside the scope of this specification: the employer's duty ends with this certificate.
Report Matrix!A37	RELATED FILINGS / CROSS-REFERENCES
Report Matrix!A38	- Casilla 60 must reconcile to twelve months of electronic payroll RetencionFuente (NIE177) for this employee - CO-DIAN-001.
Report Matrix!A39	- Casillas 53 and 54 must reconcile to twelve months of electronic payroll NIE163 and NIE166 + NIE168 + NIE170, and to the employee portions of PILA campos 55, 47, 51 and 52 - CO-PILA-003.
Report Matrix!A40	- The SUM of casilla 60 across all employees must reconcile to twelve months of Form 350 casillas 93 plus 94 - CO-DIAN-002 - but the certificate is NOT derived from those returns.
Report Matrix!A41	- Casillas 36 to 60 map almost one for one to Format 2276 numerals 5 to 33 - CO-DIAN-005 - but the ORDER OF THE THREE SEVERANCE LINES DIFFERS: Form 220 runs 47 paid / 48 traditional / 49 fund, while Format 2276 runs 18 paid / 19 fund / 20 traditional. A positional copy between the two SWAPS the fund and traditional amounts.
Report Matrix!A42	- Casilla 49 must reconcile to the amount actually deposited under CO-CESANTIAS-006 by 14 February OF THE TAX YEAR: the February deposit is certified in the year of the deposit (ET art. 27 num. 3).

Payroll Scope Summary!A35	═══ TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ═══
Payroll Scope Summary!A36	Reported on the accrual (causacion) date
Payroll Scope Summary!B36	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C36	Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
Payroll Scope Summary!A37	Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date
Payroll Scope Summary!B37	YES
Payroll Scope Summary!C37	The universal Colombian rule - payment or credit in account. Applies to the electronic payroll (DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2), to Form 350, to Form 220 and to Format 2276.
Payroll Scope Summary!A38	A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month
Payroll Scope Summary!B38	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C38	It does not. The electronic payroll is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2). PILA and Form 350 are likewise monthly.
Payroll Scope Summary!A39	Nil period still filed
Payroll Scope Summary!B39	N/A
Payroll Scope Summary!C39	Form 220 is an annual certificate, not a periodic return, so there is no nil period. One certificate per employee per tax year is issued by the last business day of March following the tax year (DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.6.1.13.2.40) whether or not any withholding was practised; casilla 60 is written as zero where nil (see 'Employee whose withholding was nil all year').
Payroll Scope Summary!A40	Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period
Payroll Scope Summary!B40	VARIES
Payroll Scope Summary!C40	Electronic payroll: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Replace or Delete) referencing the original CUNE. Form 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a correction return type N (correcciones) for the period, filed through the same operador as the original return; the C records (correct values) must be equal to or greater than the A records (original values) in IBC and in days, and type N cannot be used for a correction that leads to a refund, which is requested from each administradora outside PILA (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8; Disposiciones comunes a la Planilla N). A return paid after its deadline is filed on type E; type M (mora) applies only to periods before health period March 2014 with a total absence of payment (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8).

The instruction guide (Res. 000022/2024) says this number is 'asignado por la natural person o juridica que expide la certificacion' - the EMPLOYER assigns it, not DIAN. On Form 350 casilla 4 is DIAN-assigned and opaque. The engine must therefore maintain its own certificate numbering series and keep it stable, because the employee quotes it. FORMAT: DIAN prescribes no format for this number and the value is alphanumeric (instruction guide, Res. 000022/2024). COMPOSITE KEY - position breakdown. Because DIAN prescribes NO structure, the series is Mercans-designed and is fixed here so that it is stable and auditable: positions 1-4 = accrual year AAAA, source PayPeriod.Fiscal_Year, the year the income was earned and NOT the year of issue; position 5 = literal hyphen; positions 6-11 = a six-digit zero-filled consecutive, source Mercans.Certificate_Sequence, restarting at 000001 for each accrual year and unique per employer per year. PERSISTENCE: assigned once and NEVER reused, including after a reissue - a replacement certificate takes the NEXT consecutive and cites the superseded number, because ET art. 378 makes the employee's copy the document that proves the withholding and two certificates may not share a number.

File Structure!A2	═══ SUBMISSION FORMAT ═══
File Structure!A3	Channel
File Structure!B3	Issued TO THE EMPLOYEE, on paper or electronically as agreed, and RETAINED BY THE EMPLOYER. There is NO government channel: Form 220 is a certificate, not a return, and is not submitted to DIAN.
File Structure!A4	Source
File Structure!B4	ET arts. 378, 379 and 380; DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.6.1.13.2.40 (deadline) and art. 1.6.1.13.2.7 (substitution for the income-tax return); the form's own instruction guide, which addresses only issuing.
File Structure!A5	Why there is no file format
File Structure!B5	DIAN prescribes the FORM but not a transmission format, because nothing is transmitted. The structure below is therefore a LOGICAL MODEL - the casilla list and its hierarchy - not a wire format.
File Structure!A6	Where DIAN does see this data
File Structure!B6	Annually, through Format 2276 (CO-DIAN-005), which IS filed. The two obligations overlap in content but differ in field set, ordering and deadline, and neither is derived from the other.
File Structure!A7	Retention
File Structure!B7	The employer retains the issued certificate as proof of the withholding. Where the employee signs the six ET art. 380 declarations, the certificate substitutes for their income-tax return, so its probative value runs beyond the employer.

File Structure!A26	Rounding
File Structure!B26	The instruction guide (Res. 000022/2024) is explicit: values 'deben ser expresados en pesos colombianos y aproximados al multiplo de mil (1.000) mas cercano'. The SAME convention as Form 350, and NOT the electronic payroll convention of two decimals round-half-to-even.

File Structure!B27	The instruction guide (Res. 000022/2024) directs: 'En el evento en que no haya valor a registrar en alguna casilla escriba cero (0)'. Write ZERO, do not leave blank. This differs from the electronic payroll, where an inapplicable element is OMITTED rather than zero-filled.

File Structure!A29	═══ THE THREE SEVERANCE LINES - AND THE ORDERING TRAP ═══
File Structure!A30	Form 220
File Structure!B30	47 = paid to the employee | 48 = traditional pre-1991 CST regime | 49 = deposited to the fund
File Structure!A31	Format 2276
File Structure!B31	18 = paid to the employee | 19 = deposited to the fund | 20 = traditional pre-1991 CST regime
File Structure!A32	The trap
File Structure!B32	THE SECOND AND THIRD LINES ARE TRANSPOSED BETWEEN THE TWO ARTEFACTS. An implementation that maps positionally - 47 to 18, 48 to 19, 49 to 20 - will swap the fund deposit and the traditional-regime amount in the information return. Map by MEANING, never by position.
File Structure!A33	Recorded defect
File Structure!B33	The instruction guide for casilla 42 cross-refers severance deposited to the fund to 'la casilla 48'. The form's own printed label puts the fund deposit at casilla 49 and the traditional regime at casilla 48. The form layout is authoritative; the cross-reference is wrong.

Population Rules!A1	POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
Population Rules!A2	Field Name
Population Rules!B2	Source System

Population Rules!D2	Edge Cases
Population Rules!A3	═══ MANDATORY FIELD RULES ═══
Population Rules!A4	Casilla 4 - Numero de formulario
Population Rules!B4	Engine sequence
Population Rules!C4	Assign the certificate number from the ENGINE's own series - the instruction guide says it is 'asignado por la natural person o juridica que expide la certificacion'. Keep the series stable and unique per employee per tax year, because the employee may quote it when filing or when asking for a duplicate. The number is alphanumeric: DIAN prescribes no format for it (instruction guide, Res. 000022/2024).
Population Rules!D4	SHARP CONTRAST WITH FORM 350, where casilla 4 is DIAN-assigned. Do not reuse the Form 350 numbering logic here.
Population Rules!A5	Casillas 30 and 31 - Periodo de la certificacion
Population Rules!B5	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C5	Set casilla 30 to 1 January or the hire date, whichever is later, and casilla 31 to 31 December or the termination date, whichever is earlier. Write year, month, day in that order, as the instruction guide directs.
Population Rules!D5	A leaver certificate covers only the employment window. It is still due by the last business day of March following the tax year, not on termination.
Population Rules!A6	Casilla 36 - Salary payments
Population Rules!B6	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C6	Accumulate salary payments ACTUALLY MADE in the period, on the CST art. 127 definition the instruction guide quotes in full - ordinary fixed or variable remuneration plus everything received in money or in kind as direct consideration for the service: primas, sobresueldos, habitual bonuses, overtime, work on mandatory rest days, sales percentages and commissions. Then apply the exclusion rule: remove anything belonging in casillas 38 to 46.
Population Rules!D6	The exclusion rule is the trap. Commissions under an employment contract are salary and stay in casilla 36; commissions outside an employment relationship go to casilla 41. Habitual bonuses are salary and stay in casilla 36; non-habitual bonuses go to casilla 46. Vacation pay goes to casilla 42. Per diems ALWAYS go to casilla 43, even where the permanent portion is salary for contribution purposes. The certificate's classification and the contribution base's classification are NOT the same.
Population Rules!A7	Casillas 47, 48 and 49 - the three severance lines
Population Rules!B7	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C7	Maintain THREE separate severance buckets from the outset, not one: (47) amounts and interest ACTUALLY PAID to the employee, (48) amounts RECOGNISED under the pre-1991 traditional CST regime, and (49) amounts DEPOSITED to the fund in the tax year - the deposit made by 14 February is certified in the year of the deposit (ET art. 27 num. 3). Casillas 48 and 49 are mutually exclusive for the same employee, because an employee is in one regime or the other.
Population Rules!D7	They CANNOT be split retrospectively out of a single annual severance savings figure. And when mapping to Format 2276, map by MEANING: 47 to numeral 18, 48 to numeral 20, 49 to numeral 19. The second and third lines are transposed between the two artefacts.
Population Rules!A8	Casilla 59 - Ingreso laboral promedio de los ultimos seis meses anteriores (numeral 4 art. 206 E.T.)
Population Rules!B8	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C8	Maintain the monthly salary history YEAR-ROUND and store it monthly, so that the six-month average can be taken for the event that applies. The average is the monthly average SALARY ('salario mensual promedio', ET art. 206 num. 4) of six months: on termination or on a withdrawal of severance, the last six months of employment or the six months before the withdrawal; while the employment continues, the six months of the immediately preceding year at the time of the deposit to the fund; for employees outside the Ley 50 de 1990 regime, the six months before the employer recognises the severance (ET art. 27 num. 3) (DIAN Concepto 4443 de 2025). The average drives the ET art. 206 num. 4 severance exemption: full exemption where it does not exceed 350 UVT (COP 18,330,900 per month in 2026), tapered above.
Population Rules!D8	This is the single field on the certificate that cannot be reconstructed at year end. A moving six-month window is not derivable from twelve annual totals, and getting it wrong changes the exemption on the severance, not merely the disclosure.
Population Rules!A9	Casilla 60 - Valor de la retencion
Population Rules!B9	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C9	Accumulate the retencion en la fuente actually practised on this employee's work and pension income during the certified period. For a RESIDENT this is the ET art. 383 table result under Procedure 1 or 2; for a NON-RESIDENT it is the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the gross. Where the employee changed residence status mid-year, sum both. Only withholding actually practised is accumulated: theoretical, hypothetical or contingent withholding amounts are excluded (instruction guide, Res. 000022/2024).
Population Rules!D9	Reconcile to twelve months of electronic payroll NIE177 for this employee BEFORE issuing. A certificate that disagrees with the transmitted documents counts against the employer, because the XML is the tax support for the cost.
Population Rules!A10	═══ CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ═══
Population Rules!A11	Write zero, never blank
Population Rules!B11	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C11	The instruction guide (Res. 000022/2024) directs: 'En el evento en que no haya valor a registrar en alguna casilla escriba cero (0)'. Every money casilla is populated, with zero where there is nothing.
Population Rules!D11	This INVERTS the electronic payroll rule, where an inapplicable element is OMITTED rather than zero-filled. Two artefacts, two opposite conventions, from the same authority.
Population Rules!A12	Round to the nearest 1,000
Population Rules!B12	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C12	All money casillas are expressed in Colombian pesos and approximated to the nearest 1,000, as the instruction guide (Res. 000022/2024) directs and exactly as on Form 350. Apply the rounding to each casilla value, then check that the printed sums still tie - casilla 52 = sum of 36 to 51, casilla 75 = 60 + 74.
Population Rules!D12	Rounding sixteen casillas independently can make casilla 52 differ from the rounded sum of the unrounded parts by up to 8,000 pesos. Compute casilla 52 from the ROUNDED casillas so the arithmetic printed on the form holds.
Population Rules!A13	RUT consistency before issue
Population Rules!B13	Engine (control)
Population Rules!C13	Casillas 5 to 11 must match casillas 5, 6, 31, 32, 33, 34 and 35 of the RUT. The instruction guide repeats for each that where they do not match, THE RUT MUST BE UPDATED BEFORE THE CERTIFICATE IS ISSUED.
Population Rules!D13	Do not let the certificate diverge from the register in order to meet the March deadline - the register governs.
Population Rules!A14	Casilla 37 versus casilla 38 - the meal-payment fork
Population Rules!B14	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C14	Where the employee's monthly salary EXCEEDS 310 UVT (COP 16,235,940 in 2026), the WHOLE meal payment goes to casilla 37 and casilla 38 stays nil. Where it does not exceed 310 UVT, the payment is not income up to 41 UVT (COP 2,147,334) and only the EXCESS goes to casilla 38, with nothing in casilla 37 for that concept.
Population Rules!D14	Two thresholds, applied in sequence, and the outcome routes the amount to a DIFFERENT casilla. Both tests are MONTHLY, so an employee can fork one way in some months and the other way in others within the same certificate.
Population Rules!A15	Casilla 48 and casilla 49 are mutually exclusive
Population Rules!B15	Engine (validation)
Population Rules!C15	An employee is either in the pre-1991 traditional severance regime or in the Ley 50 de 1990 annual-deposit regime, not both. Assert that at most one of the two is non-zero for any employee.
Population Rules!D15	The only exception is a transition year in which an employee formally elected the new regime - rare, and worth an explicit override rather than a silent allowance.
