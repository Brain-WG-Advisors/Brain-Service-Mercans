0. Purpose, Scope & Audit Compliance
This register is the single statutory-integration record for Colombia. It states, per authority, whether a machine-to-machine channel exists, how it authenticates, what it carries, how it fails and when it is due. Discovery verdict: HAS_INTEGRATIONS. Of four authorities in the Colombian payroll landscape, one carries a genuine system-to-system channel and three are portal or operator-mediated. Of the six in-scope filings, one is carried system-to-system and five are manual.

0.2 Direction & product-mapping rule
Direction is stated from the payroll engine outward: OUTBOUND is a submission by the engine, INBOUND is a response or file the engine must consume. Product mapping is required only where the engine itself builds or reads the payload. A portal round-trip operated by a human is recorded for completeness and marked "No", because there is no interface for the product to map.
0.3 DERIVED / confidence disclosure
Every fact in sections 3 to 6 for DIAN-NE is traced to a downloaded artefact: Resolución DIAN 000227 de 2025 (Resolución Única, which compiles Resolución 000013 de 2021), the Anexo Técnico DSPNE v1.0 (269 pp.), the XSD set v1.0.6 and the live WSDL retrieved on 2026-09-04. The three manual authorities are recorded from their published portals and operator documentation; they have no technical specification to cite because none is published. Where the Anexo and the WSDL disagree, the WSDL is treated as authoritative and the conflict is stated in the open (see 3.1).
1. Inbound Integrations (data pulls from government)
1.1 Finding: one mandatory inbound leg, and it is a business dependency
Unlike most jurisdictions, Colombia has an inbound leg that cannot be treated as a receipt. The NE is the supporting document for costs and deductions: Res. DIAN 000227/2025 art. 1.5.3.2.1 (formerly art. 2 of Res. DIAN 000013/2021), ET art. 771-2 inc. 3, ET art. 616-1 par. 6 and DUR art. 1.6.1.4.27; the ApplicationResponse must be retained. A payroll cost never validated by DIAN is not supportable. The ApplicationResponse is therefore evidence the employer must hold, not an acknowledgement it may discard.

3. Authentication Requirements
3.1 DIAN — Nómina Electrónica (the specification conflict, resolved)
The two published artefacts do not agree, and the choice made here is deliberate. The Anexo Técnico (§9.3, §9.4) describes mutual TLS with client certificates. The live WSDL declares the opposite: sp:HttpsToken RequireClientCertificate="false". Server-authenticated TLS 1.2 is therefore used for transport, and the client identity is carried at message level, not transport level. Where the two disagree the WSDL governs, because it is the contract the service actually enforces.
Layer
	Setting
	Transport
	HTTPS, TLS 1.2, server-authenticated. No client certificate at transport level.
	Message security
	WS-Security 1.0 with the X.509 Certificate Token Profile 1.1; algorithm suite Basic256Sha256Rsa15; strict layout; timestamp included.
	Addressing
	WS-Addressing required (wsaw:UsingAddressing). The To header carries the environment endpoint and must be signed.
	Document signature
	XAdES-EPES enveloped signature over the XML, with a signature policy identifier. SHA-1 digests are rejected.
	Certificate
	Digital certificate from a certifying body accredited under the ONAC regime referenced by the signature policy.
	Onboarding gate
	Habilitación in the DIAN portal: register the software or a proveedor tecnológico, obtain SoftwareID and secret PIN, pass the test set. Global rule 92 rejects every document from a non-enabled issuer.
	Third-party transmission
	Where Mercans generates or transmits for a client, Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.8.1 requires the third party to be enabled by DIAN, that capacity registered in the RUT, and both parties to retain the mandate document.
	

3.2 DIAN — Muisca portal (Form 350)
Browser session with the Instrumento de Firma Electrónica (IFE). No machine-to-machine credential exists and none is published. The return is prepared and filed in the portal; the payroll engine supplies the figures, not the transmission.
3.3 PILA — licensed operador de información
Credentials are issued by the operator, not by the government: each operator (Aportes en Línea, Enlace Operativo, MiPlanilla, Asopagos, SOI and others) runs its own registration and its own portal login. The employer chooses the operator; the credential set, session model and any file-validation service therefore vary per operator and must be confirmed per client before go-live. No authority-published authentication exists.
3.4 Fondos de Cesantías — operator portal
Portal login at the chosen operator, with the fund selected per employee. As with PILA, there is no authority-published credential model. One operator, ARUS / Enlace Operativo, markets an "APIS PILA" developer portal; fetched on 2026-09-04 it returned only a welcome shell, with no API catalogue, no endpoints and no authentication description. It is a private commercial offering, not a government channel, and is not relied on here.

5. Error Handling
5.1 Validation & rejection
DIAN applies 437 rules to the electronic payroll document: 408 are rejecting (R) and 29 are notifying (N). An N rule does not stop validation; the document is accepted and the warning is logged. An R rule rejects the document, and nothing is filed until it is resolved. Failures fall into seven classes, and they need different owners:
Class
	Raised by
	Typical cause
	Remediation
	Container
	DIAN, before schema
	Empty or corrupt ZIP, more than one entry, over 500 KB, not UTF-8
	Fix packaging. No consecutive is consumed if the rejection precedes registration — confirm with GetStatus.
	Signature
	DIAN
	Missing ds:Signature, SHA-1 digest, wrong signature-policy hash, wrong ClaimedRole, expired certificate
	Fix the signing configuration. This is the highest-likelihood early failure: no signed reference instance is published.
	Schema
	DIAN
	Element order, missing required attribute, wrong datatype
	Fix the mapper. XSD sequence order is strict — validate locally against v1.0.6 before transmitting.
	Business (R)
	DIAN, 408 rules (payroll documents)
	Invalid code-table value, totals not reconciling, negative value, date inconsistency
	Fix the source data or the mapping and resubmit under a new consecutive.
	Business (N)
	DIAN, 29 rules (payroll documents)
	Data-quality warnings
	The document validates. Log and trend.
	Global rule 90
	DIAN
	A document number reused for the same worker
	Allocate a new consecutive. A number may be transmitted only once per worker.
	Global rule 92
	DIAN
	Issuer not enabled on the platform
	Habilitación is incomplete. This blocks all traffic, not one document.
	

5.2 Retry & idempotency
There is no idempotent retry. Global rule 90 makes the document number single-use per worker, so a blind resend of a registered document is rejected as already processed. The safe sequence is to query GetStatus by CUNE first: if the document registered, do not resend; if it did not, resubmit under a new consecutive. The CUNE, returned as XmlDocumentKey, is the only reliable identity oracle.
5.3 Correction & reconciliation
Corrections are made with a nota de ajuste (NominaIndividualDeAjusteElectronica), not by editing the original. The engine must persist the CUNE, the ApplicationResponse and the validation status per employee per period, and reconcile them before the period is closed. ApplicationResponse code 02 is "documento validado por la DIAN"; code 04 is "documento rechazado".

6.3 Acknowledgement & response-time rules
The DianResponse carrying the validation outcome returns on the same call, and there is no asynchronous queue to poll for the transmit leg. GetStatus exists for reconciliation, not for waiting. DIAN publishes no response-time SLA for either operation.
SendNominaSync is synchronous; no published SLA; contingency: transmit within 48 hours counted from the day after the service is restored (Res. 227/2025 art. 1.5.3.5.1.5); keep the supporting documents (soportes) of the payroll document. The engine must therefore hold unsent documents and drain them on recovery, rather than failing the period.
The three manual authorities publish no acknowledgement SLA. The operator portals return a settlement or deposit confirmation in-session; the timing is the operator's, not the government's.
