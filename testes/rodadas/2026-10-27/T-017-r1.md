## Resposta

**IRPP:** The transport allowance is exempt only if it is paid to **all staff** of the company, and only up to **30% of each employee's monthly base salary**. Any amount above that cap is taxable. If the allowance is paid to only some employees, the whole amount is taxable.
*Source:* CGI Tchad, Art. 45, 13°, as amended by Art. 6 of the Loi de finances 2019 (Loi n°037/PR/2018 du 31 décembre 2018). Art. 45 (nouveau) states that transport allowances are exempt from tax when they benefit all of the company's personnel, within the limit of 30% calculated on each employee's monthly base salary. Before 2019, the 2016 CGI exempted the allowance with no cap, provided it applied to all staff.

**CNPS:** The monthly transport allowance is excluded from the contribution base because the CNPS treats it as a reimbursement of expenses. Unlike the IRPP rule, no percentage cap applies.
*Source:* CNPS Tchad, *Guide de l'employeur*, section on the contribution base. The base consists of base salary, bonuses and various allowances, and the "indemnité mensuelle de transport" is listed among the items, treated as expense reimbursements, that are deducted from the contribution base. The underlying rule is the Code de prévoyance sociale. It sets contributions on all salary and salary accessories, except the elements excluded from the calculation of the leave allowance.
