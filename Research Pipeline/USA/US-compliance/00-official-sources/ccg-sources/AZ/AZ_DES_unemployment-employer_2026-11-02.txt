2027 Unemployment Insurance Reporting Changes
Beginning January 1, 2027: Arizona law establishes new reporting requirements, including the introduction of the Unemployment Insurance Operating Fund (UIOF) and Employer Contribution Reductions. Experience-rated employers are encouraged to review these changes, which includes sample tax calculations, filing platform updates, and FUTA reporting notes to ensure full compliance.
