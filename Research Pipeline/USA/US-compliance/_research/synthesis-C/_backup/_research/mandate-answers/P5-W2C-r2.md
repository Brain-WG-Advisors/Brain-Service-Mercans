## MO - Missouri (round 2 open: 2, 3, 4, 6)
2. ANSWERED (US-MO-P5-2-006, -007). State has no local wage tax on the state return; two city earnings taxes exist. St. Louis City: 1% earnings tax withheld by employers on employees living or working in the city, plus 0.5% payroll expense tax paid by the employer, filed quarterly on W-10/P-10 with the Collector of Revenue (page snapshot 2026-08-08). Kansas City: city FAQ refers to the one percent earnings tax, nonresident employers may withhold voluntarily (RD-100); city-administered. Confidence H (St. Louis), M (Kansas City rate wording comes only from FAQ question text; the page on mandatory withholding was not read).
