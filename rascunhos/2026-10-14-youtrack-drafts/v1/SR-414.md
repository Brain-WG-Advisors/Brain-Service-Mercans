1. REASON_KOD takes Derzhstat's no-data reason code (34, 37, 39, 40, 42). The DD keys are now these codes (DD V4.2, Country Option Mappers rows 83–87), so REASON_KOD takes the key with no conversion; Field Options updated (spec v5.1).
Derzhstat does not publish the values of ZERO_ZVIT or the content of REASON: XSD S0301016 defines them only as an optional integer and an optional text, and neither the technical field list nor the form controls describe them.

2. Row 11 (r.1030) is the PIT actually withheld from the row-1020 wage fund, not row 1020 × a rate. It excludes only PIT on temporary disability, without recalculations for the previous year; wage types outside row 1020 do not count. If the 5% is the military levy (військовий збір), it is not PIT and is not included.

Source: Derzhstat "Список причин відсутності даних"; XSD S0301016; Роз'яснення № 19.1.2-12/24-24, section II, item 2; form 4DF (J0510411) reports PIT and military levy separately.

I remain available should there be any discrepancy in this information.
