Worker Rights & Protections

The Department of Labor's Wage & Hour Program oversees the initiatives involved with workplace rights and wages for employees and employers, including state law and adopted rules concerning minimum wage, overtime, wage payments, employee benefits, and child labor.

For more information on Vermont's Wage & Hour Program click the links below. 

Wage & Hour Program

  • View all Laws and Rules

  • Effective 01/01/2023: $13.18 per hour worked / $6.59 for tipped employees (Poster)
  • Effective 01/01/2022: $12.55 per hour worked / $6.28 for tipped employees 
  • Effective 01/01/2021:  $11.75 per hour worked / $5.88 for tipped employees
