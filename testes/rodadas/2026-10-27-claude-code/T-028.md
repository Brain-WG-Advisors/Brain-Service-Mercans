# T-028

**Pergunta:** Egypt: does the Martyrs' Fund contribution (0.05%) apply to private-sector employees' payroll? If so, on what base and who bears it?

**Resposta:**
Yes. 0.05% of the employee's total monthly salary, deducted from employees of private legal entities under the Labour Law (except irregular/daily workers); borne by the employee (employer withholds and remits). The skill's earlier "not a private-sector payroll item" was wrong; skill corrected (v2, 22/10/2026).
