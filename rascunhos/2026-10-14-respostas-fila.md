## SR-162 (UA, D5) — pronto

Spec v3.3: row 49 and the XML sample updated (light blue).
Row 51 (SOFTWARE) is the one sent as `<SOFTWARE xsi:nil="true"/>` when empty.
Is the main Tax Calculation (J05 001) generated by the system? There is no spec for it, and the LINKED_DOCS values come from that file.

Source: наказ Міндоходів № 729/2013, Додаток 1 (items 3–4, LINKED_DOCS); form J05 001, section 06; form D5 (J0510511), note 7.
