Hi @lli,

Data Dictionary V4.3, Country Fields rows 86–88 (light blue).

**Rows 38–39 (C_REG, C_RAJ):** no. `dps_region_code` / `dps_district_code` hold the ДПС tax office codes (directory SPR_STI). In the 1-PV header, C_REG and C_RAJ are the region and district of the statistics body the report is submitted to, from the Derzhstat directory SPR_SSCU (Derzhstat Format 3.1, Table 1). The code lists differ: Kyiv is 26 in SPR_STI and 80 in SPR_SSCU, where 26 is Ivano-Frankivsk oblast. New fields: `$legal_entity_hr.derzhstat_region_code` and `$legal_entity_hr.derzhstat_district_code`.

**Row 44 (OBL):** yes, if `katottg_administrative_territory_code` holds the КАТОТТГ code of the address where the activity is carried out (the 1-PV uses that address, not the registered one): characters 3–4, UA80000000000093317 → 80.

**Row 45 (RAY):** no. RAY has 3 digits (XSD type STATRAY), while the КАТОТТГ district level has 2 (characters 5–6; 00 for Kyiv). I found no Derzhstat rule converting one into the other; the technical-field list states RAY is filled automatically from the enterprise card. New field: `$legal_entity_hr.statistical_district_code`.

**Row 46 (NOMER):** yes, fixed 1 for an enterprise without separate structural units (ГУС у Львівській області, 1-ПВ 2026, slide 5).
