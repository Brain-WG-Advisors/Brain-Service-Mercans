R72| * Payslip disclosure: Colombia's Codigo Sustantivo del Trabajo (CST) has no single formatted-payslip article; the binding duty to disclose social-security contributions paid on the worker's behalf is Ley 1393 de 2010, Art. 32. The desprendible de nomina must itemise devengados, deducciones and neto for each pay period. DIAN Nomina Electronica (Resolucion 000013 de 2021) requires a structured monthly payroll support document, transmitted within the first 10 days of the following month.
R73| * Social Security (Ley 100 de 1993): Health/Salud 12.5% (employee 4% + employer 8.5%); Pension 16% (employee 4% + employer 12%). Contribution base (IBC) floor 1 SMMLV, ceiling 25 SMMLV (2026 = COP 43,772,625). FSP (Art. 27 Ley 100, mod. Art. 8 Ley 797 de 2003): employee-only surcharge 1% for IBC >= 4 SMMLV, rising 1.2%-2.0% above 16 SMMLV. Auxilio de transporte is excluded from the IBC.
R74| * Occupational Risk / ARL (Decreto-Ley 1295 de 1994; Decreto 1607 de 2002): employer-only, five risk classes by CIIU activity code - I 0.522%, II 1.044%, III 2.436%, IV 4.350%, V 6.960%. Nothing is deducted from the employee.
R75| * Parafiscales (employer): SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, Caja de Compensacion Familiar 4%. Exoneration under Art. 114-1 Estatuto Tributario (originated Ley 1607 de 2012): income-tax-paying employers are exonerated from SENA, ICBF and the 8.5% employer health contribution for each employee earning under 10 SMMLV; the 4% Caja de Compensacion, pension and ARL are never exonerated.
R76| * Income Tax Withholding / Retencion en la fuente (Arts. 383, 388, 206 & 336 Estatuto Tributario; UVT 2026 = COP 52,374 per DIAN Resolucion 000238 de 2025): progressive marginal table 0/19/28/33/35/37/39%, starting above 95 UVT (approx. COP 4,975,530/month). Depuration allows 25% exempt income capped at 790 UVT/year (Art. 206 num. 10, mod. Ley 2277 de 2022) subject to a 40% global cap of deductions/exemptions, max 1,340 UVT/year (Art. 336).
R77| * Minimum wage SMMLV 2026 = COP 1,750,905/month (Decreto 1469 de 2025, reinstated by Consejo de Estado auto of 9-Jul-2026 after provisional suspension). Auxilio de transporte 2026 = COP 249,095/month (Decreto 1470 de 2025), payable to workers earning up to 2 SMMLV (Ley 15 de 1959); excluded from the IBC but included in the base for prima and cesantias.
R78| * Leave: annual leave 15 working days/year (CST Art. 186); sick leave (enfermedad general) - employer pays days 1-2, EPS pays from day 3 at 66.67% for the first 90 days then 50% for days 91-180 (Decreto 780 de 2016 Art. 3.2.1.10; CST Art. 227); maternity 18 weeks funded by EPS (CST Art. 236, Ley 1822 de 2017); paternity 2 weeks (CST Art. 236A, Ley 2114 de 2021).
R79| * Prestaciones sociales (employer): prima de servicios one month's salary/year (CST Art. 306, paid half by 30 Jun and half by 20 Dec); cesantias one month's salary/year deposited to the fund by 14 Feb (CST Art. 249); intereses a las cesantias 12%/year paid to the employee by 31 Jan (Ley 52 de 1975). Identifiers: employer NIT within the RUT (DIAN, Art. 555-1 ET); employee documento de identidad - Cedula de Ciudadania (C.C.) or Cedula de Extranjeria (C.E.). Currency COP (ISO 4217). Art. 8 Ley 797 de 2003): aporte adicional solo del trabajador, 1% para IBC >= 4 SMMLV, aumentando de 1,2% a 2,0% por encima de 16 SMMLV. El auxilio de transporte se excluye del IBC.

R75| * Parafiscales (empleador): SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, Caja de Compensacion Familiar 4%. Exoneracion del Art. 114-1 del Estatuto Tributario (origen Ley 1607 de 2012): los empleadores contribuyentes del impuesto de renta estan exonerados de SENA, ICBF y del aporte patronal de salud del 8,5% por cada trabajador que devengue menos de 10 SMMLV; la Caja de Compensacion del 4%, la pension y la ARL nunca se exoneran.
