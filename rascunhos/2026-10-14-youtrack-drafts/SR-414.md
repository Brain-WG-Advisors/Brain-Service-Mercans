1. REASON_KOD takes Derzhstat's no-data reason code (34, 37, 39, 40, 42). The DD keys are now these codes (DD V4.2, Country Option Mappers rows 83–87), so REASON_KOD takes the key with no conversion; Field Options updated (spec v5.1).
For ZERO_ZVIT and REASON, Derzhstat's XSD S0301016 defines only an optional integer and an optional text; its technical field list and form controls do not describe their values.

2. Row 11 (r.1030) is all PIT actually withheld from the row-1020 wage fund, at any rate, not row 1020 × a rate. It excludes only PIT on temporary disability, without recalculations for the previous year; wage types outside row 1020 do not count. If the 5% is the military levy (військовий збір), it is not PIT and is not included.

Source: Derzhstat directory of no-data reasons ("Довідник причин неподання або відсутності даних"); XSD S0301016; Роз'яснення № 19.1.2-12/24-24, section II, item 2; form 4DF (J0510411) reports PIT and military levy separately.

I remain available should there be any discrepancy in this information.
