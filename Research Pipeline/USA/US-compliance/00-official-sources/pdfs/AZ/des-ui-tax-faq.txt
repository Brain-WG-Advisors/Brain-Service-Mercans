Reporting Wages When are the wages for the services of a minor (under age 21) child or spouse subject to Arizona state and federal unemployment taxes? I am a Member/Member-Manager of my LLC. Do I have to report my wages and pay taxes? Are those who receive commissions exempt from reporting? My business is a corporation, and I am a corporate officer. Why do I have to report the wages that I earn from my corporation? I have a domestic employee in my home. Do I need to pay unemployment tax on these wages? Do I report part-time or temporary employees on my quarterly reports? Once I have met the taxable wage base of $8,000 ($7,000 before January 1, 2023) for each worker, do I have to continue reporting each employee’s wages? What is the difference between gross, total and taxable wages? What if I no longer have any employees but expect to in the future? Unemployment Insurance Tax Do I have to pay state unemployment tax if I already pay federal unemployment tax? Is unemployment tax withheld from an employee’s paycheck? Can I get unemployment insurance coverage for my workers even if I’m not required to provide coverage? Should wages be reported in the quarter they are earned or the quarter they are paid? What should an employer do if they cannot make the full payment of their unemployment taxes? Filing Wage Reports and Paying Taxes The IRS has recently advised me to contact the state because of a discrepancy between the federal and state wages reported. What form do I need? Can I file my quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report and/or Report of Changes online? How do I correct an error on a report that I have already filed? How do I compute excess wages as needed for line C2 on the quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report? How do I correct names or social security numbers on a report I have already filed? How do I change the address on my account? How do I file my quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report without an Arizona unemployment account number? Can I file one report for the whole year? Does the online Unemployment Tax and Wage Report System calculate Job Training Tax? Can I pay my past due unemployment taxes online? I was unable to print out a copy of my quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report when I filed it online. How do I get a copy? Late Filings/Payment and Penalties If I do not receive my preprinted quarterly report forms in the mail, will I still be charged penalty and interest if I am late in submitting the reports? What are the penalties for not submitting timely quarterly wage reports? My report was late, but the taxes due were only a few dollars. Why do I owe a penalty of $200? My report was late, but I paid all the taxes due, including Job Training Tax; why did I get a notice saying I still owe Job Training Tax? Appeals I disagree with a determination sent to me by your department. How do I appeal?
