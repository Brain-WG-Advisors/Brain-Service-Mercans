## Resposta

Yes. The employer must withhold **1,000 XAF per month** from every salaried employee, in both the public and private sectors. This is an advance (*acompte*) on the annual TOL due on the employee's residential premises (*locaux à usage d'habitation*).

**Legal basis:** Article 14 (*article quatorzième*) of Loi n°34-2013 du 30 décembre 2013 (Loi de Finances 2014), which created the TOL. That article was rewritten by Loi n°48-2014 du 31 décembre 2014 (Loi de Finances 2015), article 8, Section 6, §6.3, titled "Recouvrement par retenue à la source sur les salaires et modalités diverses". It is published in the Journal officiel, Édition spéciale n°1-2015, p. 17. For salaried individuals in the public and private sectors, the TOL on residential premises is collected as an advance through withholding at source by the employer, at 1,000 FCFA per month.

The same provision also sets out how the advance is settled:
- By the 20 April deadline, certain employees file a regularisation declaration at their fiscal residence. These are married employees sharing the same dwelling, sub-lodged employees, employees taxable at the city-centre rate, and employees occupying exempt premises.
- Any erroneous withholding that the tax administration confirms, based on documents the taxpayer provides, gives rise to a tax credit offset against future withholdings.

The TOL is a local tax. It is listed in CGI tome 1, art. 250(1)(f), among the taxes levied for the budgets of the decentralised authorities (départements, communes). The LF 2015 also confirms that its proceeds go to local authorities and are allocated under CGI art. 250.
