## Resposta

**WT 79432 Parental Leave Payout → Clave L, subclave 27.** Subclave 27 covers "Prestaciones públicas por maternidad o paternidad exentas del IRPF". L.08 explicitly excludes these: the exempt art. 7.h) benefits it covers do not include the exempt public maternity or paternity benefits, which go in subclave 27.

**WT 79429 Breastfeeding Risk Payout and WT 79430 Pregnancy Risk Payout → Clave B, subclave 01.** In the B.01 benefit-type field (positions 390–394), set position 394 to "1".

- **B.03 is no longer available for these.** From ejercicio 2025, B.03 is limited to benefits received by beneficiaries of the compulsory civil-servant mutual societies, colegios de huérfanos and similar entities. This change comes from Orden HAC/1431/2025, which applies for the first time to the Modelo 190 for ejercicio 2025 and later years.
- **B.01 is the right home.** The new benefit-type field is mandatory for B.01 only. Its option 05 is "Resto de prestaciones del artículo 17.2.a).1ª de la Ley 35/2006, de 28 de noviembre, no exentas, distintas de las anteriores". So non-pension, taxable Social Security benefits under art. 17.2.a).1ª, such as pregnancy-risk and breastfeeding-risk benefits, are reported in B.01.
- **B.99 is for other Clave B items.** It covers Clave B benefits other than those that belong under the earlier subclaves. That means 17.2.a) items not covered by 01–04, not Social Security benefits.

**Outside the request:** WTC V4.4 (current as of 2026-10-27) still maps 79429/79430 to B.03. That mapping was valid only up to ejercicio 2024.

**Who reports it:** Clave B and L.27 amounts are reported by whoever pays them. These three benefits are normally paid directly by the INSS or the Mutua (pago directo). They belong on the employer's 190 only if the employer is the actual payer.
