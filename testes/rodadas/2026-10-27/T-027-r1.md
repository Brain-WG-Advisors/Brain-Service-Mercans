## Resposta

The consolidated text is linked; let me pull the actual chapter 6 wording.

**Consequence for GPM313:** If the corrected GPM312 changes payment amounts and/or income tax amounts that were already reported in aggregate on the monthly GPM313s, then the GPM313 forms for the corresponding months of the same tax period (calendar year) whose declaration is being corrected must also be corrected. The two have to stay aligned, because VMI matches them: taxable payment amounts in GPM312 must match the taxable amounts declared in the GPM313s, with payments reported in the period they were actually paid, and the A or B income class shown for taxable income in GPM312 must also match what was declared in the GPM313s.

**How GPM312 is corrected:** The payer can correct the data of a submitted GPM312 and its L and U annexes by filing with VMI a newly completed declaration, with its L and U annexes, for the same tax period (calendar year) whose data is being corrected. In other words, you submit a complete replacement for that year, not a delta for the affected lines. Like the original, it is submitted electronically only, through VMI's e-declaration system (deklaravimas.vmi.lt).

**Source:** The governing instrument is VMI head order No. VA-9 of 2018-02-06, which approves the GPM312 / GPM312L / GPM312U completion and submission rules. Correction is covered in Chapter 6, "Deklaracijos tikslinimas", points 63–66 (current consolidated version in force from 2026-03-28): https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/a310caa00c4a11e88a05839ea3846d8e/asr. The GPM313 rules are a separate instrument, VMI order No. VA-121 of 2017-12-20.
