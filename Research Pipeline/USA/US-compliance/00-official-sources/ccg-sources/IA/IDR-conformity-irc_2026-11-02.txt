Iowa has also adopted rolling conformity, meaning the state will automatically conform with any changes made to the Internal Revenue Code in the calculation of federal taxable income, except as specified by Iowa law. The calculation of Iowa taxable income is the same as the calculation for federal taxable income, with certain exceptions which are described in the income tax instructions. Examples of the differences between federal and Iowa taxable income include but are not limited to: depreciation for certain assets placed in service before tax year 2021, section 179 special election deductions, and the business interest expense limitation.
