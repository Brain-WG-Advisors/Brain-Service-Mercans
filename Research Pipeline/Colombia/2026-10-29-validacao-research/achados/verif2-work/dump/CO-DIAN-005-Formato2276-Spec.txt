Report Matrix!A22	DETAILED COMPLIANCE NOTES (Colombia-specific)
Report Matrix!A23	SUBMISSION CHANNEL
Report Matrix!A24	- Channel: file_upload (XML) into the DIAN third-party (exogenous) information service | Portal: www.dian.gov.co, Electronic Computer Services, with the Electronic Signature Instrument (IFE) | Source: DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 arts. 1.3.7.1.1 and 1.3.10.1 | The XML schema is defined by the published technical specifications of Format 2276 version 4.
Report Matrix!A25	- This is NOT machine-to-machine. Unlike the electronic payroll there is no SOAP service, no document signature and no synchronous ApplicationResponse - the employer uploads a file through an authenticated portal session and reads the outcome on screen.
Report Matrix!A26	- The word 'XML' is used here because Res. 227 art. 1.3.10.1 lists Format 2276 among the formats with published technical specifications, and the exogena service accepts XML. The resolution itself prescribes no XSD: the element names, ordering and lengths are fixed by the technical specifications of Format 2276 version 4, so every element path in this specification is a logical name marked DERIVED, mapped to the element names of the published technical specifications. The physical file is an XML document in ISO-8859-1 encoding named Dmuisca_ccmmmmmvvaaaacccccccc.xml (cc = 01 insertion / 02 replacement, mmmmm = format 02276, vv = version 04, aaaa = year of sending (four digits; Anexo T3.45), cccccccc = consecutive file number), with at most 5,000 records per file.
Report Matrix!A27	- The tax portal is not the social-security channel and not the information operator. Format 2276 goes to DIAN; PILA never does.
Report Matrix!A28	INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
Report Matrix!A29	- Integration: outbound file generation only. No inbound file: the portal returns an on-screen validation outcome, which the engine captures for the audit trail.
Report Matrix!A30	- product_mapping_required = true. The format needs three separate severance savings lines, the meal-payment split at the 41-UVT threshold across TWO numerals, the rolling six-month average labour income, the RAIS-versus-voluntary-pension split that the electronic payroll cannot make, the AVC amount that has no electronic payroll element at all, and each dependant's identity document. None of these is derivable at year end from annual totals.
Report Matrix!A31	- NOT DERIVABLE FROM THE MONTHLY FILINGS. It has its own field list, its own validation and its own deadline. It also carries concepts Form 220 does not - numeral 6 (ecclesiastical emoluments), numeral 32 (meal payments up to 41 UVT, informative) and numerals 30, 31, 35 and 36.
Report Matrix!A32	RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT
Report Matrix!A33	- RESIDENT: numeral 29 carries the ET art. 383 progressive-table withholding, and numerals 23 to 28 carry the contributions and voluntary savings that depurated the base.
Report Matrix!A34	- NON-RESIDENT: numeral 29 carries the FLAT 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS. Numerals 23 and 24 still report the contributions actually deducted, but they did NOT reduce the base - art. 408 admits no depuration. Numeral 33 is reported where severance was paid but has no effect, because the art. 206 num. 4 exemption belongs to the resident ID card-general regime.
Report Matrix!A35	- The format has NO tax-residence field: the country in numeral 4 is the country of the beneficiary's address (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025), not a tax-residence marker. Tax residence is therefore invisible to DIAN in this return except through the arithmetic relationship between numerals 22 and 29 - a non-resident's numeral 29 is close to exactly 20% of numeral 22, which is itself a useful internal control.
Report Matrix!A36	- Res. 227 par. 5 draws a SECOND distinction that is easy to confuse with residence: payments to natural persons receiving work income NOT from an employment relationship are reported HERE where they were withheld under ET art. 383, and in the ordinary payments-and-withholdings formats where they were not. That test is about the WITHHOLDING REGIME, not about residence.
Report Matrix!A37	- Where a non-resident's work was performed ENTIRELY outside Colombia the income is foreign-source, was not withheld, and is not reported here at all (DIAN Official letter 28380 of 2019).
Report Matrix!A38	RELATED FILINGS / CROSS-REFERENCES
Report Matrix!A39	- Numerals 5 to 33 map almost one for one to Form 220 casillas 36 to 60 (CO-DIAN-004) - but THE SECOND AND THIRD SEVERANCE LINES ARE TRANSPOSED: numeral 19 is Form 220 casilla 49 and numeral 20 is casilla 48. Map by meaning, never by position.
Report Matrix!A40	- Numeral 29 summed across the population reconciles to twelve months of Form 350 casillas 93 plus 94 (CO-DIAN-002), and per employee to twelve months of electronic payroll NIE177 (CO-DIAN-001).
Report Matrix!A41	- Numerals 23 and 24 reconcile to the employee portions of PILA Record Tipo 2 campos 55, 47, 51 and 52 (CO-PILA-003).
Report Matrix!A42	- Numeral 19 must reconcile to the deposit made under CO-CESANTIAS-006 in February of the reported year - the deposit is counted in the year it is made (ET art. 27 num. 3; Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025, element ceco).
Report Matrix!A43	- Numeral 34 must agree with Form 220 casillas 79 and 80 - DIAN checks a dependant claimed by more than one taxpayer.
Report Matrix!A44	- Numeral 16 is mutually exclusive with Format 5247 concept 5102 for the same payment (Res. 227 para. 7): employer-paid educational support is reported here, third-party administered support there. Format 5247 is not a payroll filing.
Report Matrix!A45	- Numerals 30, 31, 35 and 36 are NOT payroll fields - VAT and collaboration-contract identifiers, owned by the employer's tax function outside payroll.

Payroll Scope Summary!A37	Reported on the accrual (causacion) date
Payroll Scope Summary!B37	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C37	Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
Payroll Scope Summary!A38	Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date
Payroll Scope Summary!B38	YES

Payroll Scope Summary!A39	A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month
Payroll Scope Summary!B39	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C39	It does not. The electronic payroll is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2). PILA and Form 350 are likewise monthly.
Payroll Scope Summary!A40	Nil period still filed
Payroll Scope Summary!B40	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C40	Format 2276 is annual and carries one record per beneficiary to whom work or pension income was paid or credited in account in the tax year (Res. 227 art. 1.3.7.1.1). A beneficiary whose withholding was nil still gets a record, with zero in every value numeral (par. 2 - see 'Employee whose withholding was nil'). Where no work or pension income was paid or credited in account to any person in the tax year there is no beneficiary and payroll generates no Format 2276 record; the format has no nil record. A monthly nil period does not arise for this return: every amount is an annual accumulation by payment date.
Payroll Scope Summary!A41	Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period
Payroll Scope Summary!B41	VARIES
Payroll Scope Summary!C41	Electronic payroll: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Replace or Delete) referencing the original CUNE. Form 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a correction return type N (correcciones) for the period, filed through the same operador as the original return; the C records (correct values) must be equal to or greater than the A records (original values) in IBC and in days, and type N cannot be used for a correction that leads to a refund, which is requested from each administradora outside PILA (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8; Disposiciones comunes a la Planilla N). A return paid after its deadline is filed on type E; type M (mora) applies only to periods before health period March 2014 with a total absence of payment (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8).

File Structure!A1	FILE STRUCTURE AND TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
File Structure!A2	═══ SUBMISSION FORMAT ═══
File Structure!A3	Channel
File Structure!B3	file_upload into the DIAN third-party (exogenous) information service at www.dian.gov.co, in an authenticated portal session signed with the Electronic Signature Instrument (IFE).
File Structure!A4	File format
File Structure!B4	XML prepared to the DIAN technical specifications for Format 2276 version 4: an XML document in ISO-8859-1 encoding, named Dmuisca_ccmmmmmvvaaaacccccccc.xml (cc = 01 insertion / 02 replacement, mmmmm = format 02276, vv = version 04, aaaa = year of sending (four digits; Anexo T3.45), cccccccc = consecutive file number), with at most 5,000 records per file. DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.3.10.1 lists the format in its formats-y-especificaciones-tecnicas table as entry T3.45, version 4.
File Structure!A5	Technical specifications
File Structure!B5	The technical specifications annex of Format 2276 version 4 fixes the XML ELEMENT NAMES, the field ordering and the column lengths; it is a SEPARATE document from Res. 227, which prescribes WHAT to report, not how the file is shaped. Every element path in this specification is therefore a logical name marked DERIVED. The productive layout is configured from the published technical specifications.
File Structure!A6	Why this is NOT machine-to-machine
File Structure!B6	There is no SOAP service, no XAdES document signature and no synchronous ApplicationResponse. Contrast the electronic payroll (CO-DIAN-001), which has all three. Here the employer uploads through an authenticated portal session and reads the outcome on screen.
File Structure!A7	What the model below is
File Structure!B7	A NEUTRAL FIELD MODEL - the resolution's own thirty-six numerals in their published order, plus the envelope attributes the deadline depends on. It is a LOGICAL MODEL, not the wire format.
File Structure!A8	═══ LOGICAL MODEL - ONE RECORD PER BENEFICIARY ═══

File Structure!A26	═══ AMOUNT AND ROUNDING CONVENTION ═══
File Structure!A27	Rounding
File Structure!B27	Positive integers in whole pesos - no decimals, no sign, no thousand separators - zero-filled where nil (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025; art. 1.3.7.1.1 par. 2). This is the fourth Colombian convention encountered across the six filings, alongside the electronic payroll (2 decimals round-half-to-even), PILA (round UP to the next 100) and Form 350 and 220 (nearest 1,000). File Structure!B28	Write ZERO. Res. 227 para. 2 is explicit, and the technical specification (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025) takes positive integer values only. This matches Form 220 and INVERTS the electronic payroll, where an inapplicable element is omitted.

File Structure!A30	Grandes Taxpayers
File Structure!B30	By the LAST digit of the NIT: 1 = 17th business day of April; 2 = 18th business day of April; 3 = 1st business day of May; 4 = 2nd; 5 = 3rd; 6 = 4th; 7 = 5th; 8 = 6th; 9 = 7th; 0 = 8th business day of May.

File Structure!B31	By the LAST TWO digits of the NIT, in five-number bands: 01-05 = 9th business day of May, rising one business day per band through 51-55 = 19th business day of May, then 56-60 = 1st business day of June, through 96-00 = 9th business day of June.
File Structure!A32	Category tested at filing
File Structure!B32	Art. 1.3.9.1.3 para. 1: the Gran Taxpayer, legal-person or natural-person status is the one held AT THE MOMENT OF FILING, not at the year end being reported.
File Structure!A33	Three different deadline rules
File Structure!B33	Form 350 uses the LAST digit and the 7th-16th business day of the following MONTH. PILA uses the LAST TWO digits and the 2nd-16th business day of the following MONTH. This return uses the LAST digit for Grandes Taxpayers and the LAST TWO for everyone else, in MAY or JUNE of the following YEAR. Do not generalise one rule to another filing.

Population Rules!A3	═══ MANDATORY FIELD RULES ═══
Population Rules!A4	Numeral 1 - Reporting entity
Population Rules!B4	Configuration
Population Rules!C4	For an ordinary employer reporting employment and pension income, set the code to 1 - Informante general - and leave numerals 35 and 36 BLANK. Res. 227 para. 4 states this in terms. Depart from concept 1 only where the income was genuinely paid out of trust assets (concept 2, plus numeral 35) or under a collaboration contract (conceptos 3 to 8, plus numeral 36).
Population Rules!D4	The three cases are mutually exclusive and each drives a different pair of numerals. Getting the concept wrong changes which identifier fields DIAN expects and produces a validation failure rather than a silent misstatement.
Population Rules!A5	Numerals 5 to 21 - the income concepts
Population Rules!B5	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C5	Accumulate each concept separately for the whole tax year on the PAYMENT date. Then apply the two exclusion rules the resolution imposes: para. 6 forbids numeral 7 from repeating anything reported in another numeral, and para. 7 sends third-party-administered educational support to Format 5247 rather than to numeral 16.
Population Rules!D5	Note two concepts that Form 220 does NOT separate: numeral 6 (ecclesiastical emoluments), which Form 220 folds into casilla 39, and numeral 32 (meal payments up to 41 UVT), for which Form 220 has no casilla at all.
Population Rules!A6	Numeral 22 - Total gross income
Population Rules!B6	Engine (computed)
Population Rules!C6	Sum numerals 5 to 21. DO NOT include numeral 32: para. 1 makes it informative, because meal payments below the threshold are income of the food supplier and not of the employee. Assert that numeral 22 equals Form 220 casilla 52 for the same employee and year.
Population Rules!D6	Including numeral 32 in the total overstates the employee's gross income and breaks the reconciliation to Form 220 - and it is an easy mistake because every other value numeral does enter the total.
Population Rules!A7	Numeral 33 - Average employment income
Population Rules!B7	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C7	Populate ONLY for employees to whom ET art. 206 num. 4 applies - that is, where severance or its interest was paid or deposited in the year, so that numerals 18, 19 or 20 are non-zero. The resolution says 'UNICAMENTE'. Maintain the rolling six-month average year-round; on a termination use the last six months OF EMPLOYMENT.
Population Rules!D7	The average cannot be reconstructed from annual totals at year end. Populating it for every employee regardless is also wrong: the resolution restricts it, and an unnecessary value invites an enquiry.
Population Rules!A8	Numeral 34 - Economic dependant
Population Rules!B8	Employee master data
Population Rules!C8	Populate the document type and number of each dependant claimed under ET art. 387 para. 2 during the year. Keep it byte-identical to Form 220 casillas 79 and 80.
Population Rules!D8	THIS IS THE FIELD DIAN USES TO CROSS-CHECK a dependant claimed by more than one taxpayer - typically both parents claiming the same child. A divergence between this return and the certificate the employee holds produces an enquiry to the employee, not to the employer.
Population Rules!A9	═══ CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ═══
Population Rules!A10	Write zero, never blank
Population Rules!B10	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C10	Res. 227 para. 2: 'Las casillas de valores son de obligatorio diligenciamiento. En caso de no tener ningun valor, se deben diligenciar con cero (0).' Every value numeral is populated, as a positive integer (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025).
Population Rules!D10	Matches Form 220 and INVERTS the electronic payroll, where an inapplicable element is omitted rather than zero-filled. Two DIAN artefacts, two opposite conventions.
Population Rules!A11	Numeral 7 must not double-report
Population Rules!B11	Engine (validation)
Population Rules!C11	Par. 6 forbids numeral 7 from containing anything reported in another numeral. Before filing, assert that no amount is counted both in numeral 7 and in numerals 5, 8, 12, 17 or elsewhere.
Population Rules!D11	The risk is real because voucher and card payments can plausibly be classified as salary (numeral 5), as a meal benefit (numerals 8 or 32) or as other payments (numeral 17). Fix the classification on the wage type, once.
Population Rules!A12	The meal-payment fork
Population Rules!B12	Payroll engine
Population Rules!C12	Apply the two ET art. 387-1 tests MONTHLY and in sequence. First: does the employee's monthly salary exceed 310 UVT (COP 16,235,940 in 2026)? If YES, the whole meal payment goes to numeral 7 and both numeral 8 and numeral 32 stay nil for that month. If NO, split the payment at 41 UVT (COP 2,147,334): the part up to the threshold goes to numeral 32 (informative), and the EXCESS goes to numeral 8 (taxable). ET art. 387-1; Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025, elements pabop, vaex and pagahuvt.
Population Rules!D12	Three possible destinations for one payment, decided monthly. An employee can fork one way in some months and the other way in others within the same annual record, so the accumulation must be per month and then summed - not decided once on the annual figures.
Population Rules!A13	Numerals 35 and 36 are mutually exclusive
Population Rules!B13	Engine (validation)
Population Rules!C13	Numeral 35 is populated only where numeral 1 = 2; numeral 36 only where numeral 1 is 3 to 8; both blank where numeral 1 = 1. Never both.
Population Rules!D13	Res. 227 par. 4 spells out each of the three cases explicitly.
Population Rules!A14	Numerals 19 and 20 are mutually exclusive per employee
Population Rules!B14	Engine (validation)
Population Rules!C14	An employee is in the Ley 50 de 1990 fund-deposit regime or in the pre-1991 traditional regime, not both. Assert that at most one of numerals 19 and 20 is non-zero for any beneficiary.
Population Rules!D14	The only exception is a formal regime election in the transition year - rare enough to warrant an explicit override rather than a silent allowance.
Population Rules!A15	═══ AMOUNT FORMAT RULES ═══
Population Rules!A16	Whole pesos, no separators
Population Rules!B16	Engine (formatting)
Population Rules!C16	Amounts are positive integers in whole Colombian pesos with no decimals, no sign and no thousand separators; zero when there is no value (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025; Res. 227 para. 2). Round once, at the annual accumulation, not per month.
Population Rules!D16	This is the fourth rounding convention across the six filings. Store the unrounded value once and apply each convention at output; never derive one artefact's figures from another's rounded output.
Population Rules!A17	Numeral 24 combines two concepts
Population Rules!B17	Engine (accumulation)
Population Rules!C17	Numeral 24 carries mandatory PENSION contributions AND the solidarity-fund surcharge in one figure, exactly as Form 220 casilla 54 does. The electronic payroll reports three separate elements and PILA three separate campos.
Population Rules!D17	Sum them only at this point. Combining them earlier makes the monthly artefacts impossible to produce correctly.
Population Rules!A18	Numerals 25 and 26 split one electronic payroll element
Population Rules!B18	Engine (accumulation)
Population Rules!C18	Numeral 25 is voluntary RAIS contributions under ET art. 55; numeral 26 is voluntary contributions to VOLUNTARY pension funds under ET art. 126-1. The electronic payroll has ONE element for both (PensionVoluntaria, NIE198).
Population Rules!D18	Carry the distinction on the wage type from configuration. It cannot be recovered from the XML at year end.
Population Rules!A19	Numeral 28 has no electronic payroll element
Population Rules!B19	Engine (accumulation)
Population Rules!C19	AVC contributions to the Fondo Nacional del Ahorro have no dedicated electronic payroll element and fall to OtraDeduccion (NIE197). Tag the wage type so the amount can be recovered here and at Form 220 casilla 58.

Population Rules!A21	Numeral 29 - RESIDENT
Population Rules!B21	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C21	Accumulate the withholding actually practised under the ET art. 383 progressive table, Procedure 1 or Procedure 2, on the art. 388 depurated base - the withholding actually practised, never a hypothetical or contingent amount (Anexo T3.45 of Res. 000227/2025, element vare). Population Rules!B22	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C22	Accumulate the flat 20% of ET art. 408 applied to the GROSS. Numerals 23 and 24 still report the contributions actually deducted, but they did NOT reduce the base - art. 408 admits no depuration. Population Rules!A23	Mid-year residence change
Population Rules!B23	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C23	Produce ONE record. Numeral 29 sums the months withheld at 20% under art. 408 and the months withheld under the art. 383 table. The format has no field to disclose the split.
Population Rules!D23	Retain the split internally. It also breaks the 20%-of-numeral-22 control above, so document the reason before the control fires.
Population Rules!A24	Non-employment work income under par. 5
Population Rules!B24	Payroll engine / accounts payable
Population Rules!C24	Where the employer pays a natural person work income that does NOT arise from an employment, legal or statutory relationship, report it HERE if it was withheld under ET art. 383 (art. 383 para. 2, added by Law 2277 of 2022 art. 8, using the payments mensualizados method). Population Rules!D24	The test is about the WITHHOLDING REGIME, not about residence and not about employment status. It is the most commonly misread paragraph of the article, and it determines which FORMAT the payment appears in, not merely which numeral.
Population Rules!A25	Work performed entirely abroad by a non-resident
Population Rules!B25	Payroll engine (tax)
Population Rules!C25	Foreign-source income, not withheld, and NOT reported in this return at all.
Population Rules!D25	The corresponding field is the electronic payroll place of work (NIE050). Keep the two consistent: a document showing a non-CO place of work for a non-resident should produce no 2276 record.
