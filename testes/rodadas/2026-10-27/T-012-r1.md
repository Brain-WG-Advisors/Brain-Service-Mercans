## Resposta

For 2026 reporting periods there are two versions of Annex D6. Which one applies depends on the month being reported.

**July 2026 onward: J0510611 (version 11).** This is in force for the J0500111 package.
- **Legal basis:** Ministry of Finance Order No. 243 of 07.05.2026, amended by Order No. 284 of 26.05.2026. Order No. 243 amends MoF Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015 and was registered with the Ministry of Justice on 26.05.2026 under No. 751/46145.
- **Entry into force:** both orders were published in the Official Gazette of Ukraine on 17.06.2026 (issue 49), and Order No. 243 took effect 30 days after publication (para. 6), so the new forms entered into force on 17 July 2026.
- **First period:** the State Tax Service applies the forms from 1 August, and for tax agents other than sole proprietors and independent professionals the first period is July 2026. Under para. 4(1) of Order No. 243, legal entities file the new form starting with the month the order took effect.
- **Package:** J0500111, with annexes J0510111, J0510211, J0510311, J0510411, J0510511 and J0510611.

**January–June 2026: J0510610 (version 10).** This was part of the earlier J0500110 package, which included annexes J0510110, J0510210, J0510310, J0510410, J0510510 and J0510610.

The sole-proprietor equivalent is ФІЗ-Д6, code F0510611. Annex D6 is only filed if the employer has positions that count toward special service (спецстаж). If there are none, it is not prepared or filed (para. 6, section IV of Order No. 4).
