A1	'PAYROLL SCOPE SUMMARY - ELECTRONIC PAYROLL SUPPORT DOCUMENT'
A2	'PURPOSE: Tell the developer WHO gets a Electronic Payroll Support Document each month and what happens to a borderline case.'
A4	'SCENARIO'
B4	'REPORT?'
C4	'NOTES / REASONING'
A5	'═══ WHO GETS REPORTED ═══'
A6	'Indefinite-term employee (indefinite-term contract)'
B6	'YES'
C6	'The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.'
A7	'Fixed-term employee (fixed-term contract, max 4 years)'
B7	'YES'
C7	'Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.'
A8	'Specific-work or task employee (contract por obra o labor)'
B8	'YES'
C8	'An employment contract; CST art. 46. Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).'
A9	'Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity)'
B9	'YES'
C9	'Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA return type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.'
A10	'Part-time employee'
B10	'YES'
C10	'Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.'
A11	'All-inclusive salary employee (all-inclusive salary, >= 13 SMMLV)'
B11	'YES'
C11	'CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the electronic payroll carries SalarioIntegral = true.'
A12	'Apprentice (apprenticeship contract)'
B12	'YES'
C12	"Law 2466 of 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decree 0223 of 5 March 2026. Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 4; PILA tipo cotizante 19 'Aprendices etapa lectiva Ley 2466 de 2025' for the lectiva phase from the health period of August 2025 (code 12 only for earlier periods). For health periods before August 2025, code 19 covers the productive phase; from the health period of August 2025, AT2 v30 names no code for the productive phase (AT2 v30 §2.1.2.3.1 campo 5, types 12 and 19)."
A13	'Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias)'
B13	'YES'
C13	'Electronic payroll TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.'
A14	'Teleworkers and remote workers'
B14	'YES'
C14	'Law 1221 of 2008 as amended by Law 2466 of 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the electronic payroll carries a dedicated Teletrabajo earning element (NIE158).'
A15	'Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract'
B15	'YES'
C15	'Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.'
A16	'NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract'
B16	'YES'
C16	'IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concept 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.'
A17	'Employed old-age pensioner in active service'
B17	'YES'
C17	'PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.'
A18	'Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract'
B18	'YES'
C18	'Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.'
A19	'Independent contractor (service contract)'
B19	'NO'
C19	'Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (return types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Form 350, but under a different casilla.'
A20	'Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity)'
B20	'NO'
C20	'Public-sector only. The PILA return types T and F of public-sector entities are outside the scope of this payroll, so worker type 47 is never emitted.'
A21	'Non-executive director paid fees only (junta directiva)'
B21	'NO'
C21	'Fees of a non-executive director (honorarios de junta directiva) are not derived from an employment relationship, so they are outside the document, which supports only payments derived from an employment or statutory relationship and pensions borne by the employer (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.1). They are paid and withheld outside payroll and reported by the payer in the third-party information return, as for an independent contractor (see the contractor row). A director who also holds an employment contract is reported for that contract only (see the executive-director row).'
A22	'Pensioner: pension paid by a fund, or paid directly by the employer'
B22	'VARIES'
C22	"Paid by a fund: NO - the paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer. Paid directly by the employer (pensioner 'a cargo del empleador'): YES - the document supports the payments 'que se desprenden de una relación laboral o legal y reglamentaria y los pensionados a cargo del empleador', the employer that makes payments 'a los pensionados a cargo del empleador' is obliged to issue it, and the pensioner is a beneficiary of the payment (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 arts. 1.5.3.2.1, 1.5.3.2.3 and 1.5.3.1.1 num. 5)."
A23	'═══ AMOUNTS AND CONCEPTS REPORTED ═══'
A24	'Basic salary for the days worked'
B24	'YES'
C24	'SueldoTrabajado (NIE070). The single largest input to every downstream filing.'
A25	'Statutory transport allowance'
B25	'YES'
C25	'AuxilioTransporte (NIE071), where the employee earns up to 2 SMMLV. Not salary for contributions, but IS in the base of cesantias, prima and the severance indemnity.'
A26	'Overtime and premium hours, itemised by type with times and percentages'
B26	'YES'
C26	"Seven separate hour-type blocks (table 5.5.5), each with start time, end time, quantity, percentage and amount. A single aggregated 'overtime' figure is NOT admissible."
A27	'Vacation taken and vacation compensated in cash, as separate elements'
B27	'YES'
C27	'VacacionesComunes and VacacionesCompensadas. They must not be merged. Vacation paid in cash (during employment or on termination) is NOT part of the IBC for health, pension and occupational risk (Consejo de Estado, Sección Cuarta, exp. 26941 of 2024-04-04; unification ruling exp. 25185 of 2021, rule 1), while for enjoyed vacation days health and pension are contributed on the IBC of the month before the vacation starts (DUR 780 de 2016 art. 3.2.5.1; Consejo de Estado, Sección Cuarta, exp. 25958 of 2022). Both enjoyed vacation and vacation paid in cash ARE part of the parafiscal base for CCF, SENA and ICBF (Ley 21 de 1982 art. 17; Ley 89 de 1988 art. 1 par. 1; Consejo de Estado, Sala de Consulta, concept 2027 of 2010).'
A28	'Service bonus, severance savings and interest on severance savings'
B28	'YES'
C28	'Primas, Severance savings and Cesantias/@PagoIntereses. Reported here even though severance savings are excluded from the monthly withholding base.'
A29	'Incapacity, maternity / paternity leave and paid leave'
B29	'YES'
C29	'Each with its own dates, quantity and amount, and - for incapacity - its type code.'
A30	'Unpaid leave and lawful strike'
B30	'YES'
C30	'Reported as days ONLY. Both blocks deliberately carry no amount element.'
A31	'Benefits in kind (salary in kind)'
B31	'YES'
C31	'Through the paired OtroConcepto S / NS elements or the specific allowance elements. Valued contractually or by expert appraisal; Colombia has no notional valuation scale.'
A32	'Employer social-security contributions'
B32	'NO'
C32	"The document reports what the EMPLOYEE accrued and what was DEDUCTED from the employee. The employer's 12% pension, 8.5% health, occupational-risk premium and 9% levies are an employer cost reported on PILA, never a deduction here."
A33	'Employer payroll levies (SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, CCF 4%)'
B33	'NO'
C33	'Employer cost, reported on PILA. Never an employee deduction.'
A34	'Documented business-expense reimbursements'
B34	'NO'
C34	"A reimbursement of an expense the employee incurred on the employer's behalf, supported by the supplier's document, is not remuneration (CST art. 128: amounts received not for the employee's benefit but to carry out the job, such as transport, work tools and similar, are not salary) and is not an accrual of the employee; it is settled through accounts payable, not through this document. Per diems for meals and lodging ARE reported, in ViaticoManuAlojS (permanent, salary) or ViaticoManuAlojNS (accidental, non-salary) - NIE072 / NIE073."
A35	'Amounts in a currency other than the Colombian peso'
B35	'NO'
C35	'Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.8.5 makes the peso mandatory. A foreign currency may be ADDED to a graphical representation but never substituted; the TRM element (NIE200) exists in the schema but is out of scope for a Colombian payroll.'
A36	'═══ TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ═══'
A37	'Reported on the accrual (causacion) date'
B37	'NO'
C37	'Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.'
A38	'Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date'
B38	'YES'
C38	'The universal Colombian rule - payment or credit in account. Applies to the electronic payroll (DIAN Resolution 000227 of 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2), to Form 350, to Form 220 and to Format 2276.'
A39	'A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month'
B39	'NO'
C39	"It does not. The electronic payroll is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 art. 1.5.3.2.2). PILA and Form 350 are likewise monthly."
A40	'Nil period still filed'
B40	'NO'
C40	'The document is the support of the payments or credits in account made to the employee in the month (Res. DIAN 000227 de 2025 arts. 1.5.3.2.1 and 1.5.3.2.2); it is not a periodic return. Where no payment or credit in account is made to an employee in the month (for example a full-month unpaid leave or suspension), no document is generated for that employee, and there is no nil document at employer level. Where payments exist but the deductions absorb them, a document IS generated with ComprobanteTotal 0.00 (see the zero-net-pay row).'
A41	'Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period'
B41	'VARIES'
C41	'Electronic payroll: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Replace or Delete) referencing the original CUNE. Form 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a correction return type N (correcciones) for the period, filed through the same operador as the original return; the C records (correct values) must be equal to or greater than the A records (original values) in IBC and in days, and type N cannot be used for a correction that leads to a refund, which is requested from each administradora outside PILA (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8; Disposiciones comunes a la Planilla N). A return paid after its deadline is filed on type E; type M (mora) applies only to periods before health period March 2014 with a total absence of payment (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8).'
A42	'═══ SPECIAL CASES ═══'
A43	'Employee with zero net pay (deductions equal accrued)'
B43	'YES'
C43	'A document is still required. ComprobanteTotal (NIE189) is 0.00; the accrued and deducted blocks are fully itemised.'
A44	'Employee whose withholding falls in the 0% band'
B44	'YES'
C44	'The payment is still reported. RetencionFuente (NIE177) is simply omitted rather than zero-filled.'
A45	'Mid-month hire or mid-month leaver'
B45	'YES'
C45	'DiasTrabajados (NIE069) is reduced; FechaIngreso or FechaRetiro is set; TiempoLaborado is computed to the settlement end on the 360/30 convention.'
A46	'Second employer of a multi-employer employee'
B46	'YES'
C46	'Each employer issues its own document for its own payments. There is no aggregation across employers at document level.'
A47	'Employee paid semi-monthly'
B47	'YES'
C47	'ONE monthly document carrying PeriodoNomina = 4 (Fortnightly) and TWO FechaPago (NIE203) occurrences. Not two documents.'
A48	'Correction of a document already validated by DIAN'
B48	'YES'
C48	'By adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste), never by re-sending the original number - global rule 90 forbids reuse.'
A49	'Employee holding a PPT (Temporary protection permit)'
B49	'YES'
C49	'Reported. Annex table 5.2.1 has no code for the PPT (Decree 216 of 2021 post-dates the annex); a PPT holder is reported with code 47, the residence-permit code of the table, and the PPT number in NumeroDocumento; a PEP holder is reported with code 47, the PEP code of the table (see NIE044).'
