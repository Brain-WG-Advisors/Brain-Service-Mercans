## Resposta

**Neither date as a simple age test. The rule is month-based, so check the payroll month against the month of the 25th birthday.**

**Date to use:** The allowance covers every month in which the employee has not yet turned 25, up to and including the month in which they turn 25. The source is Szja tv. 29/F. § (3): eligibility months are the months in which the young person has not reached age 25, the last one being the month in which they reach it. NAV says the same: the allowance can be claimed for the last time in the month in which the person turns 25.

- **Period end date is wrong.** It would make the employee ineligible in their birthday month.
- **Period begin date works in most cases.** But it fails when the birthday falls on the 1st of the month: the system may count the employee as already 25 on that day and wrongly exclude the month.
- **The most robust check:** eligible if YYYYMM(DOB + 25 years) ≥ YYYYMM(payroll period). This never depends on the day.

**Which month counts for back pay or retro payments:** The test runs against the period the pay belongs to, not the payment date. For employment income, the allowance applies to income accounted for the eligibility months. So arrears for an eligible month keep the allowance even if they are paid after the birthday month.

**DOB source:** Use the employee's statutory date of birth from the employee master data, not a stored age field. Ideally use the same DOB that goes to NAV, since the law introducing 29/F made date of birth one of the data items required to apply the 29/F allowance. I don't have your regulation's variable dictionary, so I can't confirm the exact Mercans field name. Map it to whatever field holds the employee's master-data date of birth.

Source: [NAV – 25 év alatti fiatalok kedvezménye](https://nav.gov.hu/ado/szja/szja-kedvezmenyek/25-ev-alatti-fiatalok-kedvezmenye); Szja tv. (1995. évi CXVII.) 29/F. § (2)–(3).
