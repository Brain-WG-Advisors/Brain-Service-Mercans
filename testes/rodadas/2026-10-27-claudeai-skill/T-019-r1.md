## Resposta

**Family benefits (PF), maternity (AM) and AT/MP:** 75,000 XOF per month (840,000 per year).

**Retirement:** 3,375,000 XOF per month (40,500,000 per year).

**Source:** CNPS, *Communiqué à l'attention des employeurs – Nouveau plafond des cotisations sociales* (11 January 2023), issued under Décret n° 2022-986 of 21 December 2022, which raised the SMIG to 75,000. The communiqué sets, from 1 January 2023, a monthly contribution floor of 75,000 FCFA for all branches, a monthly retirement ceiling of 3,375,000 FCFA, and a monthly ceiling of 75,000 FCFA for the other branches (maternity, family benefits, AT/MP). No SMIG revision or new CNPS ceiling has been issued since, so these values still apply in 2026. As of April 2026, no new SMIG increase had been announced or adopted.

Because the floor and the PF/AM/AT-MP ceiling are both 75,000, those three branches are fixed amounts for any salary at or above the SMIG.

The figure 70,000 for PF/AM/AT-MP is still published in several places, and it is the pre-2023 value. CLEISS's 2025 table lists 70,000 FCFA for maternity, family benefits and AT/MP. Mercans' own page at dsr.mercans.com also shows it. That page describes applying a 70,000 XOF family/maternity/AT ceiling.
