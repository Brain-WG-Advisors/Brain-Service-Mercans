# T-013

**Pergunta:** Colombia 2026, withholding tax (retención en la fuente): are payments for temporary incapacity (common illness from day 3, work-related/ARL incapacity) and maternity/paternity/parental leave exempt income under Estatuto Tributario art. 206, or taxable?

**Resposta:**
Taxable. They are not exempt income under ET art. 206:
- Num. 1 exempts only *indemnizaciones* for work accidents or illness.
- Num. 2 exempts only *indemnizaciones* that protect maternity.

Incapacity payments (common illness and work accident/ARL) and maternity and paternity leave are not indemnities. They are therefore not exempt, and retención en la fuente applies. Parental leave follows the same reasoning (it is a leave payment, not an indemnity), but the DIAN ruling names only maternity and paternity explicitly.

In the WTC these lines use tax code CO-TX-01 (Subject to Tax = Yes). CO-TX-07 is reserved for genuine indemnities.

**Fontes:**
- DIAN Oficio 27408 of 01/11/2019, items 7.1–7.4 (local copy `raw/docs/oficiais/CO/2026-10-13-DIAN-Oficio-27408-2019.htm`):
  - incapacity: "no se encuentran como rentas exentas las incapacidades por enfermedad general y accidentes de trabajo";
  - maternity and paternity leave: "no se consideran rentas exentas";
  - 7.4: "debe practicarse retención en la fuente".
