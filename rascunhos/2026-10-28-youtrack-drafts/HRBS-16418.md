@asaber @mjain @rfusana Yes, the rule applies. Art. 12 bis of Law 89-114 sets a minimum tax of 45% of the IRPP computed without the exemptions (Law 2017-8, art. 2-6; unchanged by LF2025/LF2026). It covers deductions granted "sous réserve" of art. 12 bis (life insurance, art. 39-I-2; CEA, art. 39-VIII; CEI, art. 39 bis), not those granted "nonobstant" it. Since withholding is the annual tax divided by the number of pays (art. 53-I), it applies in payroll too. The example is correct: 45% × 9,550.000 = 4,297.500, above 3,250.000. Nothing extra is needed from the client.
