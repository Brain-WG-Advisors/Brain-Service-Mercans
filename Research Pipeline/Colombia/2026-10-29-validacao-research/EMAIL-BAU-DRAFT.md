Hi Heidy,

We have finished validating the Colombia research against an independent calculation and the September payroll. The updated documents are in the Colombia folder on Drive: CCG v1.6, WTC v1.8, DD v1.5, report specs v1.2 and SIR v1.3.

Three changes affect the payroll calculation:

1. Withholding: the 25% exempt income (790 UVT), the 40% / 1,340 UVT cap and the AFC/AVC limit (3,800 UVT) are annual limits, applied on the year-to-date amount, not 1/12 per month. The Consejo de Estado annulled the monthly limit in 2021 (rad. 24047), and DUR 1.2.4.1.6 par. 3 now refers only to annual UVT. This replaces the 1/12 approach we accepted in HRBS-13627.
2. Cesantías and intereses are not subject to withholding, including for non-residents (Ley 100, art. 135 par. 3).
3. ET 114-1 exemption: the test is salary below 10 SMMLV and health IBC below 10 SMMLV, so non-salary payments do not count (DIAN Conceptos 8380/2024 and 7196/2025).

In the September payroll we found:

- 113 employees with the exempt income or the cap limited to 1/12. Under the annual rule, September withholding is higher than needed by at least COP 155 million in total. Since we had accepted 1/12 before, please let us know how you want to align this.
- 5 employees with no exempt income or deduction applied at all. For 4 of them the over-withholding is at least COP 7.7 million; the exact amount depends on each employee's year-to-date balance.
- 8 employees who lost the 114-1 exemption because non-salary payments were counted, which means about COP 16.7 million in extra employer contributions. One employee goes the other way: salary below 10 SMMLV but health IBC above it, and the exemption was applied (about COP 2.3 million not paid).
- Dismissal indemnity (CST art. 64): the fraction of the year is truncated to whole days instead of prorated, so 150 employees get up to one day less. In the 9 cases we could recompute, that is about COP 10.6 million underpaid.

Could you review and tell us whether this matches your understanding? We are happy to go through the cases with you.
