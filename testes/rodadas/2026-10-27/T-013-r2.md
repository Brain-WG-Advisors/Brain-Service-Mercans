## Resposta

**Taxable.** None of these payments qualifies as exempt income under art. 206, so withholding applies to them like ordinary salary.

**Temporary incapacity (common illness from day 3 via EPS, and work-related via ARL):** Taxable. Art. 206 num. 1 exempts only *indemnizaciones*. DIAN Oficio 027408 of 2019, item 7.1, states that incapacities for general illness and work accidents are not exempt income, because they do not have the character of indemnities. DIAN Oficio 000321 of 2015 had already held that the num. 1 exemption applies only to the indemnity payment and cannot be extended to incapacity payments to the employee. It also found that incapacity payments substitute salary while the worker is off work, and that the exemption does not depend on who pays (EPS or ARL) but on whether the payment is an indemnity. That oficio expressly revoked the earlier contrary doctrine: Oficio 53358/2014, Oficio 012642/2008 and Concepto 006728/1998.

**Maternity leave:** Taxable. Art. 206 num. 2 exempts only *indemnizaciones que impliquen protección a la maternidad*. Oficio 027408/2019, item 7.2, concludes that num. 2 does not cover maternity leave, since it is not an indemnity, so maternity leave is not exempt income.

**Paternity leave:** Taxable. Item 7.3 of the same oficio reaches the identical conclusion: paternity leave is not an indemnity and is not exempt income.

**Withholding:** Item 7.4 says since these are not exempt, withholding must be applied to the related payments. DIAN Oficio 901191 of 2020 reaffirmed this for maternity and paternity leave paid through payroll. It also notes that art. 369 num. 2 excludes from withholding only payments that are exempt for the beneficiary under special provisions; otherwise the withholding agent must withhold.

**Parental leave (shared or flexible, Ley 2114 of 2021):** I found no DIAN doctrine specifically on this. It is carved out of the maternity and paternity leave entitlements and is not an indemnity, so the same reasoning leads to taxable treatment.

**What remains exempt:**
- All of these payments form part of total labour payments, so they enter the base for the general 25% exemption under art. 206 num. 10. Since Ley 2277 of 2022, that exemption is capped at 790 UVT per year, which is the rule fully applicable for tax year 2026.
- True indemnities stay exempt under num. 1 and num. 2. Examples are ARL compensation for permanent partial incapacity (Ley 776/2002 art. 5) and the CST indemnity for dismissing a pregnant worker or one on leave without authorization.
