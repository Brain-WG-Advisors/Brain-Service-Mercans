## Resposta

**Yes, it applies to the private sector.** The employee bears it. The employer withholds it from the employee's salary each month and remits it.

**Legal basis:** Article 8 of Law No. 16 of 2018, as replaced by Law No. 4 of 2021, which took effect on 4 March 2021.

**Scope:** A monthly deduction of 5/10,000 of the salary applies to employees of public authorities, public sector bodies and companies, and also to employees of private legal persons covered by the labor law. Irregular and daily-paid workers are excluded.

**Who bears it:** It is an employee deduction, not an employer cost. PwC says employers must deduct 0.05% of the employee's gross salary each month, for public and private sector employees alike, and remit it to the fund.

**Base:** There is one nuance here.
- The statute only uses the word "salary" (راتب) and doesn't define it.
- PwC and Eurofast both apply it to **gross salary**. Eurofast describes the Executive Regulations, issued by the Minister of Finance on 10 May 2021, the same way.
- A narrower official reading exists. State Council Fatwa No. 244 (file 3027/1/17, 13 Feb 2023) concluded that the salary subject to the Article 8 deduction is the monthly salary only, not everything the employee receives monthly. The Ministry of Finance enforces this through Instruction Circular No. 61 of 2024. That circular is addressed to financial officers in government bodies. I found no equivalent guidance aimed specifically at private employers.

**Pending change:** A government bill published on 21 Oct 2026 would amend Articles 7, 14, 10 and 15 of the law. It does not touch Article 8, so the payroll deduction is unaffected.
