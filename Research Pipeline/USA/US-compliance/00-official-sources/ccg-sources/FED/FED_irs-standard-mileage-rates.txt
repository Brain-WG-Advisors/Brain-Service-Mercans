 If you use your car for business, charity, medical or moving purposes, you may be able to take a deduction based on the mileage used for that purpose. 2026 mileage rates (July 1 – Dec. 31) The standard mileage rates for 2026 are: Self-employed and business: 76 cents/mile Charities: 14 cents/mile Medical: 23.5 cents/mile Moving ( military only ): 23.5 cents/mile Find out when you can deduct vehicle mileage Mileage rates for all years (cents/mile) Period Business use Charity use Medical or military moving Source 2026 (July 1 – Dec. 31) 76 14 23.5 IR-2026-29 2026 (Jan. 1 – June 30) 72.5 14 20.5 IR-2025-128 2025 70 14 21 IR-2024-312 2024 67 14 21 IR-2023-239 2023 65.5 14 22 IR-2022-234 7/1/2022-12/31/2022 62.5 14 22 IR-2022-124 1/1/2022-6/30/2022 58.5 14 18 IR-2021-251 2021 56 14 16 IR-2020-279 2020 57.5 14 17 IR-2019-215 2019 58 14 20 IR-2018-251 2018 TCJA 54.5 14 18 IR-2017-204 IR-2018-127 2017 53.5 14 17 IR-2016-169 2016 54 14 19 IR-2015-137 2015 57.5 14 23 IR-2014-114 2014 56 14 23.5 IR-2013-95 2013 56.5 14 24 IR-2012-95 2012 55.5 14 23 IRB-2012-02 7/1/2011-12/31/2011 55.5 14 23.5 IR-2011-69 1/1/2011-6/30/2011 51 14 19 IR-2010-119
