# T-030

**Pergunta:** Turkey 2025: how is the payroll income tax deduction for an employee's private life insurance premium limited? Which limits apply, and which of them are cumulative over the year?

**Resposta:**
No Turkey hub in the brain (contexto_pais: hub not found). Per correcoes.md, the deduction is the lowest of three limits: (1) eligible premium of the month: 50% for life insurance with savings, 100% for pure risk cover; (2) 15% of the month's gross wage (monthly test, not carried over); (3) remaining balance of the annual cap = annual gross minimum wage (TRY 312,066 in 2025). Only the annual cap is cumulative over the year.
