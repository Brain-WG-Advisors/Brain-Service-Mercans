# T-021

**Pergunta:** Republic of Congo: is there an occupancy tax (TOL) that the employer must withhold from employees' salaries? If so, what amount and on what legal basis?

**Resposta:**
Yes. The TOL (taxe d'occupation des locaux) on residential premises is withheld at source by the employer from employees' salaries: XAF 1,000 per month, as an advance on the annual tax (employee regularises by 20 April where applicable; annual residential tariff XAF 60,000 centre-ville / 12,000 periphery). Effective 2015-01-01. Configured as code 2540 (HRBS-14055, published 12/10/2026). Note: our earlier answer of 02/09 ("not a payroll deduction, only TOL Prof") was wrong and was corrected on 09/10.
