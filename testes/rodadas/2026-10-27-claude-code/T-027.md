# T-027

**Pergunta:** Lithuania: when an annual GPM312 declaration has to be corrected after submission, what are the consequences for the monthly GPM313 declarations already filed, and how is the GPM312 correction submitted?

**Resposta:**
The affected monthly GPM313 declarations must also be corrected (one GPM312 correction can trigger up to 12 GPM313 corrections). The GPM312 correction is filed as a complete new declaration (no delta form). Rule 64.2 bars correcting over-withheld GPM and NPD errors on resident income this way.
