## Resposta

**TRL = Tipo de Relación Laboral** (Type of Employment Relationship).

It's a 3-digit TGSS code from **table T-52** that flags special employment relationships which are excluded from certain contingencies or benefits, or which contribute on fixed bases or fixed quotas. TGSS identified these exclusions through the TRL data item, table T-52. Examples include training or apprenticeship contracts (TRL 087), training programmes (TRL 986) and scholarship holders (TRL 933), and TRL 950 for shipowners treated as employees.

**Why it matters at tramo level.** In the Sistema de Liquidación Directa, a tramo is a period within the settlement month where the contribution conditions stay the same. A worker with more than one tramo is one whose contribution conditions changed in the Fichero General de Afiliación, for example a CNAE change, a change in a contribution peculiarity, a change of TRL, or temporary incapacity. So in each TrabajadoresTramos row, TRL is one of the attributes that defines that tramo.

**Caveat on current usage.** TRL was originally a property of the CCC. Since the TGSS CCC-reduction process, TRL is no longer a factor that requires a separate CCC. The exclusion now sits on the worker's alta, in the field **Exclusión de Cotización**. That field takes the same values as the CCC's Tipo de Relación Laboral field (table T-52). Depending on how the source system populates the field, a TRL value in the report can therefore come from the CCC (legacy setup) or from the worker-level Exclusión de Cotización.

I couldn't find a public TGSS schema that defines a node literally named "TrabajadoresTramos." That name appears to come from the ES-AFI-001 spec, so the meaning above is based on how TGSS uses TRL in Afiliación and SLD documentation (Boletín Noticias RED 2/2018 and 4/2019).
