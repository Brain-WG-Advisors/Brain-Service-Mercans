The Employer/TPA Info page requires your Employer/TPA UI Tax Account Number (10 digit number), Employing Unit (3 digit number), if applicable, Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) and registration code. Most of this information can be found on the Registration letter sent by this department. If you have not received this letter or have misplaced it, call 603-223-6100 to request the registration code.

If your company does not have a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN), please select the Our Company does not have an FEIN checkbox to the right of the Federal Tax ID (FEIN) entry field.

All fields with a red asterisk (*) are mandatory.

Once the information is entered, select the Next button to continue with the registration process. Select the Cancel button to exit from the registration process.

If you are unable to provide this information or you are receiving a validation message indicating the information provided does not match our records, contact NHES at 603-223-6100 for assistance.

Please note, the information provided during the registration process will be utilized for future verification. You are responsible to retain your registration information.
