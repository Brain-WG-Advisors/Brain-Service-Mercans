Title: Reporting Wages - Completing the Reports | Arizona Department of Economic Security
Form Availability and Instructions
Instructions for completing the quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report (UC-018) can be found on the reverse side of the paper form that DES custom-generates and mails to each employer with an active Unemployment Insurance (UI) tax account every quarter.
Blank UC-018 and UI Tax Wage Listing Continuation (UC-020) forms are also available, with completion instructions on the UC-018 Instructions page and on the UC-018 itself.
How to Prepare Your Report: List employees in alphabetical order or by Social Security number. Report the total wages paid to each employee for the quarter. Report wages in the quarter they are paid, not when earned. Example: If a pay period ends September 30 but wages are paid October 1, report those wages in the fourth quarter (October through December).
Completing Section A (Monthly Employment Data): Enter the count of all your full-time and part-time employees who performed services during the payroll period that includes the twelfth of the month. If you do not have any employees in the payroll period, enter zero.
Reporting on Multiple Pages: computer-generated or plain white paper listings; the format must match Part B (Wage Listing) of the UC-018 form.
Avoid Penalties for Incomplete Reports - each page must include: Employer name. Employer account number. Calendar quarter and year. Employees' Social Security numbers and names (last name first). Total amount of wages paid to each employee during the quarter. Page total amount of wages paid to all employees (listed on the page) during the quarter.
Online Filing Option: You may also file your quarterly wage report online (Arizona Unemployment Tax and Wage System).
