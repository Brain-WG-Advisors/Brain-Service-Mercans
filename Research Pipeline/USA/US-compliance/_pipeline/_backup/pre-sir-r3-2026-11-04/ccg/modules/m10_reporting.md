# Module 10: Reporting, Compliance & Finalisation

*Purpose: To define all statutory reporting obligations and final processing rules.*

## 10.1 Statutory Reporting

Field-level content of every return lives in the Report Specifications (04-reports-spec). Channels and authorisations live in the Statutory Integration Research (03-statutory-integrations). This module states the obligations.

### Describe the country's primary digital payroll reporting system.

There is no single national payroll reporting system. Each layer is reported separately:
- **Federal (IRS):** Form 941 quarterly ([[FACT:fed.form941.frequency=quarterly; due last day of the month after the quarter; amounts in cents]] (partial source)) [US-FEDS5-031], Form 940 annual ([[FACT:fed.form940.due=January 31 (10 more days if all FUTA deposited on time)]] (partial source)) [US-FEDS5-031], and deposits on the schedule set by the lookback liability (monthly up to [[FACT:fed.deposit.monthly_max_lookback_liability=50,000]], semiweekly above it, next day from [[FACT:fed.deposit.next_day_threshold=100,000]]) [US-FEDS5-004].
- **Federal (SSA):** Forms W-2/W-3 filed electronically in the EFW2 format ([[FACT:sir.fed.w2.format=EFW2 Publication 42-007 (W-2) / 42-014 (W-2c) via SSA Business Services Online]]) [US-FIL-002], fixed records of [[FACT:fed.efw2.record_length=512]] bytes [US-FEDS5-032]. Tax-year 2026 changes: [[FACT:fed.efw2.ty2026_changes=TY2026 changes: RE adds Employer Signature PIN (319-328); RW adds Box 14b Primary/Secondary Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (490-492 / 493-495); RO/RU split Codes A and B into separate money fields (combined A+B field valid through TY2025 only), add money fields for existing Codes J, K, L, P and new Codes TA, TP, TT; SS wage base 184,500; FIRE replaced by IRIS]] [US-FEDS5-032].
- **State:** income-tax withholding returns to every State where withholding applies, and the UI quarterly wage report to the State of UI coverage only ([[FACT:multi.ui_wage_report.routing=UI quarterly wage report to the sui_state only (localization); State income-tax withholding returns to every State where withholding applies]]) [US-MULTIS5-017].
- **Local:** separate local returns where a locality collects its own tax (Module 6 and the PA, OH, MI, KY, MO, AL, CO, DE, OR, WV and NJ annexes).

**Filing channel for this build.** Federal and State filings are submitted through a third-party filing provider; there is no ADP integration (owner decision). The employer authorises the agent on Form 8655 ([[FACT:fed.reporting_agent.authorization=Form 8655 (Rev. Jan 2024); agent may sign/file 940/941 and deposit; taxpayer stays liable]]) [US-FED-008]. The employer remains liable for the tax. Third-party payer arrangements are described in Pub. 15 ([[FACT:sir.fed.third_party_payer.rules=Pub. 15 (2026) third-party payer arrangements (reporting agents, sec. 3504 agents, CPEOs) - liability allocation per arrangement]]) [US-FEDP51-002].

### What pay elements are required as intermediates from payroll for reporting?

Per employee and per period, the payroll engine must keep (and accumulate per quarter and per year):
- gross wages; wages subject to FIT; FIT withheld;
- OASDI wages (capped at the annual base) and tax, employee and employer;
- Medicare wages and tax, employee and employer; wages above the Additional Medicare threshold and that tax;
- FUTA taxable wages (first [[FACT:fed.futa.wage_base=7,000]]);
- per State: State wages, State tax withheld, SUI gross and taxable wages, PFML/SDI premium wages and premiums;
- per locality: local wages and local tax;
- the 2026 W-2 box 12 amounts [[FACT:fed.w2.box12.new_codes=TP (cash tips reported to employer); TT (qualified overtime compensation); TA (employer Trump-account contributions)]] [US-FEDS5-007], and the tipped-occupation code;
- hours worked and occupation where a State UI or PFML report requires them (e.g. Washington: [[FACT:wa.report.pfml_ltc.fields=Combined Paid Leave + WA Cares quarterly report; per employee: SSN or ITIN, last and first name (middle initial optional), hours associated with wages paid (rounded up to a whole number; salaried full-time 40 h/week; no hours for leave cash-outs or severance), wages paid in the quarter excluding tips, WA Cares exemption status, date of birth; employer totals of Paid Leave and WA Cares premiums withheld]] [US-WAB5-003]).

## 10.2 Integrations

### Is there any mandatory inbound integration?

- **Income withholding orders (child support):** the employer must act on IWOs received from State agencies (paper or e-IWO). For interstate orders the employer applies the law of the employee's principal place of employment ([[FACT:multi.iwo.governing_law=When an employer receives an income withholding order issued by another State, the employer shall apply the income withholding law of the State of the obligor's principal place of employment in determining the employer's fee for processing an income withholding order; the maximum amount permitted to be withheld from the obligor's income; the time periods within which the employer must implement the income withholding order and forward the child support payment; the priorities for withholding and allocating income withheld for multiple child support obligees; and any withholding terms or conditions not specified in the order]]) [US-MULTIS5-007]. It remits within [[FACT:multi.iwo.remittance_business_days=7]] business days [US-MULTIS5-007]. The current federal IWO form is approved until [[FACT:fed.iwo.omb_expiry=2029-08-31]] [US-FEDS5-028]. Not configured in this release [[GAP:RQ-528]]: The dates on which States go live with the revised IWO / e-IWO (gig-worker fields) within the one-year extension are not known.
- **IRS levies** on wages follow Pub. 1494 exemption amounts (Module 7). Order of priority: [[FACT:fed.iwo.priority=Withholding for support has priority over any other legal process under state law against the same income (section 466(b)(7) of the Social Security Act); if a federal tax levy is in effect, notify the sender]] [US-FEDP6-002].
- No system-to-system inbound feed from a tax authority (such as a tax-code notice) is mandatory. Withholding certificates come from the employee (W-4 and State forms).

### Is there any mandatory outbound integration beyond standard reporting?

- **New-hire reporting** ([[FACT:fed.newhire.fields=employee name, address, SSN, date services first performed; employer name, address, FEIN]]) [US-FEDS5-024]. Electronic filers transmit [[FACT:fed.newhire.electronic_interval=Electronic/magnetic filers: two monthly transmissions, not less than 12 nor more than 16 days apart]] [US-FEDB5-001].
- **Certified payroll** on public works: federal Davis-Bacon weekly [US-FEDS5-027]; California electronic upload ([[FACT:ca.prevailing_wage.certified_payroll=electronic to Labor Commissioner via DIR eCPR (XML upload spec available)]]) [US-CAS5-017]. Prevailing wage (public works) is an optional informational flag [US-OWN-009]: this guide documents prevailing-wage laws, thresholds and certified-payroll duties as an overlay, and the payroll build does not produce certified payroll reports.
- All statutory filings go through the third-party filing provider (owner decision). The SIR documents the handoff.

## 10.4 Other Compliance

### Is payroll software certification mandatory?

No general federal certification of payroll software is established from an official source. A filing agent is authorised per employer on Form 8655 [US-FED-028]. That is an authorisation, not a software certification. Agent-list changes go through the IRS channel described in Pub. 1474 ([[FACT:sir.fed.reporting_agent.tech_spec=Pub. 1474 (Rev. 4-2026): reporting agent = accounting service, franchiser, bank, service bureau or other payroll-services entity; agent list additions/deletions electronic above 100 submissions a week via SLFT B2B; deposits via EFTPS or FTA]]) [US-FIL-001]. State example: Wisconsin ([[FACT:sir.wi.provider_registration=Payroll service providers must register and test their software with DOR]]) [US-WIS5-016]. Not configured in this release [[GAP:RQ-529]]: A census of State software-certification or provider-testing duties (beyond Wisconsin) is not established from an official source.

## 10.5 Year-End Processing and Finalisation

### Describe the mandatory year-end finalisation process.

There is no employer-side income-tax regularisation. The employee settles at assessment (Form 1040 and State returns). The employer's year end is a reconciliation:
1. Reconcile the four quarterly Forms 941 to the W-2/W-3 totals [US-FEDS5-031].
2. File Form W-2/W-3 with SSA by [[FACT:fed.w2.due_date=2027-02-01]] [US-FEDS5-007].
3. File Form 940 ([[FACT:fed.form940.due=January 31 (10 more days if all FUTA deposited on time)]] (partial source)) [US-FEDS5-031].
4. File each State's annual withholding reconciliation and State W-2 copies (Report Specifications; e.g. Michigan [[FACT:mi.report.annual.5081=Form 5081 annual reconciliation for 2026 due 2027-02-28 (every filer)]] [US-MIS5-021]).
5. Collect new W-4 exemption claims for the next year ([[FACT:fed.w4.exempt.validity=2026 only: the exemption checkbox certification lapses; a new W-4 is required for 2027]]) [US-FEDS5-006].
6. Reset the annual accumulators (OASDI, FUTA, SUI and PFML bases, Additional Medicare threshold) at the first payment date of the new year. The 2027 OASDI base is [[FACT:fed.fica.oasdi.wage_base=192,300]] [US-FEDB5-401].

TY2027 federal indexation is not yet published: Not configured in this release [[GAP:RQ-530]]: The tax-year 2027 IRS inflation adjustments, 2027 retirement-plan limits and 2027 Pub. 15-T are not yet published.

### What documentation or annual statements must be provided to employees?

- **Form W-2** by [[FACT:fed.w2.due_date=2027-02-01]] [US-FEDS5-007]. For a terminated employee: [[FACT:fed.w2.terminated_employee.furnishing=Terminated employee: W-2 no later than January 31 of following year (earlier on request)]] [US-FED-016]. State examples: Delaware within [[FACT:de.report.w2.employee_on_termination_days=30]] days of termination on request [US-DES5-005]; Mississippi within [[FACT:ms.term.w2_after_termination.days=30]] days [US-MSS5-004].
- The SSN may be truncated to its last four digits on the employee copies of Form W-2 only, never on Copy A; the EIN is never truncated [US-FEDS5-022].
- **Form 1042-S** for treaty-exempt wages of nonresident aliens [US-FEDS5-016].
- **Form 1095-C** for employees of an applicable large employer [US-FEDS5-012].

### Is there a formal process for correcting errors discovered after the fiscal year has been closed?

Yes. Wage statements are corrected on Form W-2c, filed electronically in the EFW2C format ([[FACT:sir.fed.w2.format=EFW2 Publication 42-007 (W-2) / 42-014 (W-2c) via SSA Business Services Online]]) [US-FIL-002]. Quarterly returns are corrected on Form 941-X [US-FEDS5-031]. Excess OASDI withheld because of multiple employers is refunded to the employee at assessment, not by the employer. States have their own amendment routes; for example Alabama UI ([[FACT:al.report.sui.quarterly=Quarterly Contribution and Wage Report via eGov only (online_form or file_upload); due last day of month after quarter; corrections UC-10C]]) [US-ALS5-007] and Massachusetts W-2 ([[FACT:ma.report.w2=due Jan 31; 50+ W-2s electronic via MassTaxConnect/Bulk File Portal; <= 49 paper with M-3; corrections paper W-2c; EFW2 RA/RE/RS/RT, RS MA wages 276-286, MA tax 287-297, RT = sum RS]]) [US-MAS5-015].
