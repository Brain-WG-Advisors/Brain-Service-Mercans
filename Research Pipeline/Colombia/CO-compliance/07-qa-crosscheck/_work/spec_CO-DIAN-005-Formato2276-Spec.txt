R8| - The word 'XML' is used here because Res. 227 art. 1.3.10.1 lists Formato 2276 among the formats with published especificaciones tecnicas, and the exogena service accepts XML. R9| - The tax portal is not the social-security channel and not the operador de informacion. Formato 2276 goes to DIAN; PILA never does.
R11| INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
R12| - integration_type = OUTBOUND (scope_manifest CO-OUT-2276). No inbound file: the portal returns an on-screen validation outcome, which the engine should capture for the audit trail.
R13| - product_mapping_required = true. The format needs three separate cesantias lines, the meal-payment split at the 41-UVT threshold across TWO numerals, the rolling six-month average labour income, the RAIS-versus-voluntary-pension split that the nomina electronica cannot make, the AVC amount that has no nomina electronica element at all, and each dependant's identity document. None of these is derivable at year end from annual totals.
R14| - NOT DERIVABLE FROM THE MONTHLY FILINGS. It has its own field list, its own validation and its own deadline. It also carries concepts Formulario 220 does not - numeral 6 (ecclesiastical emoluments), numeral 32 (meal payments up to 41 UVT, informative) and numerals 30, 31, 35 and 36.
R16| RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT (v2.8 control B1)
R17| - RESIDENT: numeral 29 carries the ET art. 383 progressive-table withholding, and numerals 23 to 28 carry the contributions and voluntary savings that depurated the base.
R18| - NON-RESIDENT: numeral 29 carries the FLAT 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS. Numerals 23 and 24 still report the contributions actually deducted, but they did NOT reduce the base - art. 408 admits no depuration. Numeral 33 is reported where severance was paid but has no effect, because the art. 206 num. 4 exemption belongs to the resident cedula-general regime.
R19| - The format has NO residence field. Residence is therefore invisible to DIAN in this return except through the arithmetic relationship between numerals 22 and 29 - a non-resident's numeral 29 is close to exactly 20% of numeral 22, which is itself a useful internal control.
R20| - Res. 227 par. 5 draws a SECOND distinction that is easy to confuse with residence: payments to natural persons receiving work income NOT from an employment relationship are reported HERE where they were withheld under ET art. 383, and in the ordinary payments-and-withholdings formats where they were not. That test is about the WITHHOLDING REGIME, not about residence.
R21| - Where a non-resident's work was performed ENTIRELY outside Colombia the income is foreign-source, was not withheld, and is not reported here at all (DIAN Oficio 28380 of 2019).

R1| PAYROLL SCOPE SUMMARY - THIRD-PARTY INFORMATION RETURN ON WORK AND PENSION INCOME - FORMAT 2276 VERSION 4
R2| SCENARIO ~ REPORT? ~ NOTES / REASONING
R3| === WHO GETS REPORTED ===
R4| Indefinite-term employee (contrato a termino indefinido) ~ YES ~ The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.
R5| Fixed-term employee (contrato a termino fijo, max 4 years) ~ YES ~ Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.
R6| Specific-work or task employee (contrato por obra o labor) ~ YES ~ An employment contract; CST art. 46. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).
R7| Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity) ~ YES ~ Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA planilla type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.
R8| Part-time employee ~ YES ~ Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
R9| All-inclusive salary employee (salario integral, >= 13 SMMLV) ~ YES ~ CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the nomina electronica carries SalarioIntegral = true.
R10| Apprentice (contrato de aprendizaje) ~ YES ~ Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decreto 0223 of 5 March 2026. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 4; PILA worker type 12 (lectiva) or 19 (productiva).
R11| Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias) ~ YES ~ Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
R12| Teleworkers and remote workers ~ YES ~ Ley 1221 de 2008 as amended by Ley 2466 de 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the nomina electronica carries a dedicated Teletrabajo devengado element (NIE158).
R13| Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
R14| NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
R15| Employed old-age pensioner in active service ~ YES ~ PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
R16| Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract ~ YES ~ Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
R17| Independent contractor (contrato de prestacion de servicios) ~ NO ~ Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (planilla types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Formulario 350, but under a different casilla.
R18| Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity) ~ NO ~ Public-sector only; PILA planilla types T and F are descoped in scope_manifest.
R19| Non-executive director paid honorarios only (junta directiva) ~ NO ~ Not employment income. Reported by the payer as honorarios, not as rentas de trabajo of an employee.
R20| Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer ~ NO ~ The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
R21| === AMOUNTS AND CALCULATIONS ===
R22| Annual salary and work income by concept ~ YES ~ Numerals 5 to 17, itemised in thirteen separate concepts.
R23| Ecclesiastical emoluments as a separate concept ~ YES ~ Numeral 6 - a concept Formulario 220 does NOT separate, folding it into casilla 39.
R24| Meal payments up to 41 UVT ~ YES, but informative only ~ Numeral 32. Res. 227 par. 1: 'se considera de caracter informativo'. It does NOT enter the gross total in numeral 22, because it is income of the food supplier, not of the employee. Formulario 220 has no counterpart casilla.
R25| Meal payments above 41 UVT ~ YES ~ Numeral 8, as taxable income of the employee.
R26| Severance, split three ways ~ YES ~ Numerals 18 (paid to the employee), 19 (deposited to the fund) and 20 (traditional pre-1991 regime). Note the ordering difference against Formulario 220.
R27| Employee mandatory contributions ~ YES ~ Numerals 23 and 24. Pension and the solidarity fund are COMBINED in numeral 24.
R28| Voluntary savings, split four ways ~ YES ~ Numerals 25 (RAIS), 26 (voluntary pension funds), 27 (AFC) and 28 (AVC). The nomina electronica has one element for the first two and none for the fourth.
R29| Rolling six-month average labour income ~ YES ~ Numeral 33, but ONLY for employees to whom ET art. 206 num. 4 applies.
R30| Withholding practised ~ YES ~ Numeral 29, resident or non-resident alike.
R31| Employer social-security contributions ~ NO ~ The return reports the BENEFICIARY's income and the BENEFICIARY's contributions. The employer's cost appears only on PILA.
R32| VAT as a higher cost and VAT withholding ~ NO ~ Numerals 30 and 31 exist in the format but are not payroll figures - Colombian employment income carries no VAT. Owned by the client's tax function.
R33| Trust and collaboration-contract identifiers ~ NO ~ Numerals 35 and 36. Res. 227 par. 4: for an ordinary employer reporting under concepto 1 they 'no se deben diligenciar'.
R34| === TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ===
R35| Reported on the accrual (causacion) date ~ NO ~ Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
R36| Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date ~ YES ~ The universal Colombian rule - pago o abono en cuenta. Applies to the nomina electronica (Res. DIAN 000013 de 2021 art. 3), to Formulario 350, to Formulario 220 and to Formato 2276.
R37| A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month ~ NO ~ It does not. The nomina electronica is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (art. 3). PILA and Formulario 350 are likewise monthly.
R38| Nil period still filed ~ VARIES ~ Formulario 350: NOT required where no operation subject to withholding occurred in the period (ET art. 606 par. 2). Nomina electronica: no document where there was no payment. PILA: a planilla is still required while the employment relationship subsists.
R39| Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period ~ VARIES ~ Nomina electronica: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Reemplazar or Eliminar) referencing the original CUNE. Formulario 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a planilla type N (correcciones) or M (mora).
R40| === SPECIAL CASES ===
R41| Employee who left during the year ~ YES ~ One record covering the payments actually made in the year. There is no partial-year marker; the amounts simply reflect the shorter period.
R42| Employee whose withholding was nil ~ YES ~ A record is still required. Res. 227 par. 2: 'Las casillas de valores son de obligatorio diligenciamiento. En caso de no tener ningun valor, se deben diligenciar con cero (0)'.
R43| Natural person paid work income OUTSIDE an employment relationship ~ YES ~ Res. 227 par. 5: reported here WHERE the payment was withheld under ET art. 383. Where art. 383 did not apply, it goes to the ordinary payments-and-withholdings formats instead - not here.
R44| Work income paid out of trust assets ~ PARTIAL ~ Reported with numeral 1 = 2 (Fiduciarias) and the trust identified in numeral 35. Not an ordinary corporate payroll case; where it arises, the reporting party is the fiduciaria, not the employer.
R45| Educational support administered by a third party ~ NO ~ Res. 227 par. 7 sends it to Formato 5247 concepto 5102. Only support paid DIRECTLY by the employer goes to numeral 16.
R46| Employee with a dependant claimed under art. 387 par. 2 ~ YES ~ Numeral 34. Must agree with Formulario 220 casillas 79 and 80 - this is where DIAN cross-checks a dependant claimed twice.
R47| Employee who acquired residence mid-year ~ YES ~ ONE record. Numeral 29 sums the 20% months and the art. 383 months; the format has no field to disclose the split.

R3| === MANDATORY FIELD RULES ===
R4| Numeral 1 - Entidad informante ~ Configuration ~ For an ordinary employer reporting employment and pension income, set the code to 1 - Informante general - and leave numerals 35 and 36 BLANK. Depart from concepto 1 only where the income was genuinely paid out of trust assets (concepto 2, plus numeral 35) or under a collaboration contract (conceptos 3 to 8, plus numeral 36). ~ The three cases are mutually exclusive and each drives a different pair of numerals. Maintain the rolling six-month average year-round; on a termination use the last six months OF EMPLOYMENT. ~ The average cannot be reconstructed from annual totals at year end. R10| Write zero, never blank ~ Engine (formatting) ~ Res. 227 par. 2: 'Las casillas de valores son de obligatorio diligenciamiento. R12| The meal-payment fork ~ Payroll engine ~ Apply the two ET art. 387-1 tests MONTHLY and in sequence. First: does the employee's monthly salary exceed 310 UVT (COP 16,235,940 in 2026)? If NO, split the payment at 41 UVT (COP 2,147,334): the part up to the threshold goes to numeral 32 (informative), and the EXCESS goes to numeral 8 (taxable). ~ Three possible destinations for one payment, decided monthly. An employee can fork one way in some months and the other way in others within the same annual record, so the accumulation must be per month and then summed - not decided once on the annual figures.

R27| Numeral 22 arithmetic ~ Engine (validation) ~ Numeral 22 must equal the sum of numerals 5 to 21, EXCLUDING numeral 32. Assert it, and assert equality with Formulario 220 casilla 52 for the same employee. ~ The exclusion of numeral 32 is the trap. Every other value numeral enters the total.
R28| Numerals 8 and 32 are a pair ~ Engine (validation) ~ Where either is non-zero the other is normally non-zero too, because the same underlying payment is split at 41 UVT. The exception is an employee earning over 310 UVT, where both are nil and the payment sits in numeral 7 instead. ~ Three-way interlock between numerals 7, 8 and 32, driven by two monthly UVT tests.
R29| Numerals 18, 19, 20 and 33 travel together ~ Engine (validation) ~ Where any of numerals 18, 19 or 20 is non-zero, numeral 33 should be populated, because ET art. 206 num. 4 applies. Where all three are zero, numeral 33 should be blank. ~ The resolution restricts numeral 33 with the word 'unicamente', so populating it unnecessarily is as wrong as omitting it when required.
R30| Numeral 1 drives numerals 35 and 36 ~ Engine (validation) ~ Concepto 1 - both blank. Concepto 2 - numeral 35 populated, numeral 36 blank. Conceptos 3 to 8 - numeral 36 populated, numeral 35 blank. ~ Implement as a three-branch rule, not as two independent nullable fields.
R31| Cross-report reconciliation ~ Engine (reconciliation) ~ Before filing, assert per beneficiary: numeral 22 equals Formulario 220 casilla 52; numeral 29 equals casilla 60 and twelve months of nomina electronica NIE177; numerals 23 and 24 equal casillas 53 and 54 and the employee portions of PILA campos 55, 47, 51 and 52; numeral 19 equals the deposit made under CO-CESANTIAS-006; numeral 34 equals Formulario 220 casillas 79 and 80. Across the population, assert that the sum of numeral 29 equals twelve months of Formulario 350 casilla 93. ~ MAP THE SEVERANCE LINES BY MEANING: numeral 18 to casilla 47, numeral 19 to casilla 49, numeral 20 to casilla 48.
