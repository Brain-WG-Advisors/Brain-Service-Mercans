These three fields fill the "Seconded" group of the FPS, which HMRC only expects with the starter details of an employee seconded to work in the UK (RTI Data Item Guide 2026-27, items 28 to 31A). A seconded employee is someone sent to work in the UK by an overseas employer who keeps the contract with that employer, including anyone in an expatriate scheme or an EPM6 scheme.

- Seconded Employees Declaration: one option per seconded employee. In the GB FPS configuration (export of May 2025), key 1 reports Stay183DaysOrMore (will live in the UK 183 days or more), key 2 StayLessThan183Days (less than 183 days) and key 3 InOutUK (works both in and outside the UK but lives abroad).
- European Economic Area Citizen: Yes if the employee is from an EEA country (EEACitizen).
- EPM6 Applicable: Yes only if the employee is in an EPM6 modified PAYE scheme for tax-equalised expatriates (EPM6).

The configuration reports them as "yes" only in the hire month, so they need to be set before the first payroll.

The same answers set the starting tax code (HMRC Expat starter checklist 12/25, employer guidance, and "New employee coming to work from abroad"):
- Key 1 (183 days or more): current personal allowance, cumulative.
- Keys 2 and 3: current personal allowance on a week 1/month 1 basis, unless the employee is an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen, who gets it cumulative.
- No information before the first payday: 0T week 1/month 1.
- EPM6: only with HMRC's agreement to a modified PAYE scheme, following PAYE Manual PAYE82008.

What we need from the client, for each seconded employee at hire: which of the three stay options applies, whether the employee is an EEA citizen, and whether they are in an EPM6 scheme. For other employees the fields stay blank. Seconded employees don't fill the starter declaration (A, B or C).
