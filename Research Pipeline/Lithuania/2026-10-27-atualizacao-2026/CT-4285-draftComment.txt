The Lithuania 2026 statutory alert has been reviewed against the official sources and corrected: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Pgcq2dzvafolRi7vvYpNA7WEzOVvoMP4XPfx7Pdyks/edit

1. Annual tax brackets: 20% up to €83,237.40, 25% from €83,237.40 to €138,729, and 32% above (36 and 60 times the 2026 average wage of €2,312.15). The alert had €82,962 / €138,270 and 0% for the first bracket.
2. Without a request from the employee, each payment is taxed by reference to its own amount (in practice 20%). The 25%/32% rates are applied to the total annual payments from the employer only at the employee's request.
3. Sodra annual ceiling: €138,729 (60 times the average wage); it does not apply to health insurance. The 2025 figure was removed, as the alert covers 2026.

The same 2026 rules are in the updated compliance documents (changes highlighted in light blue):
- Payroll Regulation Guide 2026 v1.1: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kHmUGNPbV81jegbNaiBw_JPNgMYDkDyytzP_ByM2G7g/edit

Sources: Law on Personal Income Tax, arts. 6(1) and 23(7); Law XV-632, art. 4; Law on State Social Insurance I-1336, art. 10(9).
