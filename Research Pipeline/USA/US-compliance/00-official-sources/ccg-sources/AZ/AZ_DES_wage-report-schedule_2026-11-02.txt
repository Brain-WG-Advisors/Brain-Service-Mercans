Title: Reporting Wages and Paying Taxes - Schedule | Arizona Department of Economic Security
Reporting Due Dates and Deadlines
Reports are due on the scheduled date. If the due date falls on a weekend or state holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day.
Unemployment Insurance Tax Wage Report Schedule
For Wages Paid During | Calendar Quarter Ends | Report Due By
January, February, March | March 31 | April 30
April, May, June | June 30 | July 31
July, August, September | September 30 | October 31
October, November, December | December 31 | January 31
Late Penalties and Interest
Late Report Penalty: 0.1% of total wages for the quarter; Minimum penalty: $35; Maximum penalty: $200
Interest On Unpaid Taxes: 1% of taxes owed for each month, or part of a month, that the payment is late.
Can a single report be filed for the entire year? No. You must file a quarterly Unemployment Tax and Wage Report for every quarter, even if no wages were paid. ...
