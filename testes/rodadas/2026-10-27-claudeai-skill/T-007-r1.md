## Resposta

Under **Art. 17(1) of the Law of Ukraine No. 1105-XIV** (On Compulsory State Social Insurance, as restated from 1 January 2023; the same scale sat in Art. 24 under the pre-2023 numbering), the temporary incapacity benefit is paid as a percentage of average salary based on insurance record:

| Insurance record (страховий стаж) | Benefit, % of average salary |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 years | 50% |
| 3 to 5 years | 60% |
| 5 to 8 years | 70% |
| Over 8 years | 100% |

This scale applies throughout 2026.

The scale has two kinds of exceptions:

- **100% regardless of stazh.** This applies to categories such as Chornobyl categories 1–3, war veterans, and people rehabilitated under Law No. 962-XII. The donor category lost force from 26 January 2026. People who qualified before 25 January 2026 keep the entitlement for one year after their donation. Employees and gig specialists of Diia City residents receive benefit equal to the salary on which contributions were actually paid, regardless of stazh.
- **Caps.** The monthly benefit cannot exceed the maximum ESV contribution base, which is 8,647 × 20 = UAH 172,940 in 2026. If the person has less than six months of insurance record in the 12 months before the insured event, the benefit cannot exceed the amount calculated from the minimum wage at the time of the event.
