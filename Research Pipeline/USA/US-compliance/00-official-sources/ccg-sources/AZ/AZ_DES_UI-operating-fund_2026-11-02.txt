Title: Unemployment Insurance Operating Fund | Arizona Department of Economic Security
Arizona law establishes the new Unemployment Insurance Operating Fund (UIOF), which goes into effect January 1, 2027. Managed by the Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES), this dedicated fund will manage administrative costs associated with Arizona's Unemployment Insurance (UI) program.
Key Provisions
Applies only to experience-rated employers. Does not apply to new employers and reimbursable employers.
In each quarter of Calendar Year 2027, an experience-rated employer shall:
Pay an amount equal to 3.15% of their UI tax liability to be deposited into the UIOF.
Have their UI tax liability reduced by 3.15%.
Quarterly Unemployment Insurance (UI) Tax Calculation Example
Description | Calculation | Amount
UI Tax Liability (taxable wages multiplied by tax rate) | $8,000.00 x 1.78% | $142.40
Special Operating Fund (calculate 3.15% of UI Tax liability) | $142.40 x 3.15% | $4.49
UI Contribution Reduction (calculate 3.15% of UI Tax liability) | $142.40 - $4.49 | $137.91
Amount Reportable for Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA) | None | $137.91
Total Amount Due for Quarter | None | $142.40
Filing Your UI Tax and Wage Reports
Paper Section B. Wage Summary, in the paper UI Tax and Wage Report (UIT-0018F) has been updated to include the calculation for the UIOF and the Employer Contribution Reduction. Instructions on how to calculate them are provided.
Online: The Tax and Wage System (TWS) will identify the new calculation of the UIOF and Employer Contribution Reduction.
Bulk Filers: Azure / File Transfer Submission will identify the new UIOF line item.
FUTA Reporting Notes
IRS Form 940 (FUTA Credit): Only contributions to a state unemployment compensation fund can be reported to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) on Form 940. Any amount contributed to the UIOF cannot be reported for FUTA credit.
