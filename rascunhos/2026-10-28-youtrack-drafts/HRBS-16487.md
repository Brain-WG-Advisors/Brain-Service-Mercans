The calculation steps and rates (35.75% / 37.56% / 49.50%) are the same. The only difference is in the credits: for a resident of another EU/EEA country, Switzerland or a BES island (Belgium has its own rule), the general credit is given only for its premium part (factor 0.77343 below AOW age), while the labour credit stays in full.

2026 values for this employee, instead of a Dutch resident's:
- Below AOW age: general credit €2,410 instead of €3,115, phase-out factor 0.04948 instead of 0.06398 (same thresholds).
- From AOW age: general credit €850 instead of €1,556, elderly credit €1,130 instead of €2,067, single elderly credit €295 instead of €540 (lower phase-out factors, annex pages 11–12).

Source: Belastingdienst, Rekenvoorschriften voor de geautomatiseerde loonadministratie 2026 (January 2026, version 2), chapter 8 and annex pages 4–6 (Dutch residents) and 10–12 (landenkring, not Belgium).
