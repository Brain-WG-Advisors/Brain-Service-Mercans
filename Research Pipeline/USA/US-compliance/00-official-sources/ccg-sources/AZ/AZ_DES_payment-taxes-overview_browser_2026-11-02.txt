Note: This section does not apply to:
Nonprofit and governmental employers
Indian tribes
Churches or religious organizations
These parties have been chosen and approved to reimburse DES for benefit payments charged to their accounts in lieu of paying quarterly taxes.

Payment Deadlines and Penalties
Your tax payment is due on the same date your report is due. See the schedule for details. If quarterly reports and payments are filed late, penalties and interest are assessed.

Calculating Taxable Wages
State unemployment taxes pay for unemployment benefits only. You cannot take these taxes out of employee pay. Calculate taxable wages carefully using the form instructions.

Wage Limits
Taxable wages are the first $8,000 of gross wages paid to each employee each year. (For wages paid before January 1, 2023, the limit was $7,000.) Check your calculations carefully and watch the decimal point in the tax rate when figuring the amount of taxes to pay.

Gross versus Taxable Wages
You must report the total gross wages paid to all employees each quarter. However, you only pay unemployment taxes on the first $8,000 of wages paid to each employee each year. (For wages before January 1, 2023, the limit was $7,000.)

Determining Excess Wages
To find taxable wages, subtract excess wages (amounts over the yearly wage limit) from total wages.

Successor Employers
If you are a successor employer, include wages paid earlier in the year by the previous owner when calculating taxable wages for employees you kept.

Online Filing and Payment Options
Once you have an Arizona unemployment tax account number, you are encouraged to submit quarterly wage reports and pay taxes by using our Unemployment Tax and Wage System. Do not submit a paper report if you already filed online.
You can:
Pay online at the same time you file your report
Pay online separately from filing
Choose the paper check option, which creates a payment voucher to print and mail with your check
