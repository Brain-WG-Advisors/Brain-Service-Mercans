State Posters
The Mississippi Department of Employment Security provides the Unemployment Insurance poster at no charge. You may download it using the links below.

The only other state-required poster is the Mississippi Worker's Compensation poster. This poster is available by visiting the Mississippi Workers' Compensation website.

Federal Posters
Federal posting requirements vary by statute. Not all employers are covered by each of statutes and thus may not be required to post a specific notice. For example, some small businesses may not be covered by the Family and Medical Leave Act and would not be subject to those posting requirements.
