|  | Why the field set is legal rather than technical | Labour Code art. R1221-1 enumerates the mandatory mentions - 1° employer, including the occupational health service (SPST, C_MT_DCL), 2° employee, 3° date and time of hire, 4° nature, duration and trial period of the contract, 5° agricultural employees (out of scope for this build) - and binds whichever channel is used (online form, file deposit or API) and whichever body receives it; art.
