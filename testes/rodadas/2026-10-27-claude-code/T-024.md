# T-024

**Pergunta:** Hungary, under-25 PIT exemption in the regulation design: on which date should the employee's age be evaluated (period begin date or end date), and which variable should provide the date of birth?

**Resposta:**
Not at end_date (design of 2026-05-27, now superseded): the exemption applies per month up to and including the month in which the employee turns 25. Rule: eligible if period month <= month of the 25th birthday. In practice that is age < 25 at the period begin date (edge case: birthday on the 1st must still count), never end_date, which wrongly drops the birthday month. Date of birth: $person.birth_date (not $employee.years_worked).
