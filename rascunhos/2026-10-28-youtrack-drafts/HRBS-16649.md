@dbalogun Income Tax Act 24 of 1981, Schedule 2:

1. Method (questions 1, 2, 4 and 5): the Act does not set a monthly formula. Employees' tax is determined by the deduction tables prescribed by the Minister and "the manner in which such tables shall be applied" (para 9(1)); any other basis needs the Minister's agreement with the employer (para 10). For an employee employed since the start of the tax year, the employer must adjust during the year so that the total withheld equals the amount under the tables (para 12A). The Act itself does not address projection, once-off payments, adjustment codes or mid-year salary changes; that detail sits in the instructions issued with the tables.
2. Mid-year hire (question 3): para 12A applies only to employees employed since the start of the tax year (1 March). For a hire on 01-01-2026 there is no statutory year-end adjustment by the employer; the final liability is settled on the employee's assessment.
3. Missing TIN (question 6): until the employee provides the Certificate of Registration as a Taxpayer, or the employer receives a directive, employees' tax is withheld at the maximum rate (para 12(2)).
