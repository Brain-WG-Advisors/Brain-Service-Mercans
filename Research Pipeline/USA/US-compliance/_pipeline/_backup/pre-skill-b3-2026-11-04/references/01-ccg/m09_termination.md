# Module 9: Termination & Irregular Payments

*Objective: To detail calculation and tax rules for non-standard payments.*

## 9.1 Termination Payments

### What are the different types of termination payments?

| Payment | Rule | Tax treatment | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

### How do termination rules and calculations differ by residential status and employer type?

- **Federal resident vs nonresident alien.** Severance and final wages are wages for both. For a nonresident alien the nonresident add-on increases the wage used in the regular withholding computation only. It is not applied to supplemental wages withheld at the flat rate (the add-on is not applied to the flat-rate method) [US-FEDS5-002]. Treaty-exempt wages claimed on Form 8233 go on Form 1042-S, not on Form W-2 (Treaty-exempt wages claimed on Form 8233 are reported on Form 1042-S (not W-2 box 1); 2026 Forms 1042-S due 2027-03-15 and must be e-filed via IRIS) [US-FEDS5-016].
- **State residence.** Final-pay timing follows the work State (Table 9.1.1). State withholding on a termination payment follows the same residence/work-State routing as regular wages (Module 6 and the MULTI annex).
- **Employer type and size.** WARN applies to covered plant closings and mass layoffs. Maine's statutory severance applies to establishments of the size stated in the first table of 9.1. Household and agricultural employers follow the special thresholds of Module 11.

### Detail the calculation and tax withholding rules for unused leave paid out on termination.

- **When it is due:** only where Module 8, Table 8.1.3 makes the balance wages due, and on the final-pay date of Table 9.1.1.
- **Federal withholding:** where due, the payout is paid with the final wages. Classification: Vacation pay is withheld on as regular wages; when it is in addition to regular wages for the vacation period (e.g. an annual lump-sum payment for unused vacation leave) it is a supplemental wage payment; vacation pay for longer than the usual payroll period is spread over the pay periods for which it is paid. [US-FEDQ1-002]. A lump-sum payout of unused leave is withheld on with the supplemental-wage methods of Module 6 §6.2 (optional flat rate or aggregate).
- **State example:** Louisiana withholds vacation pay like regular wages, with no flat supplemental rate (withheld like regular wages; no flat supplemental rate (vacation pay likewise)) [US-LAP51-002].

### Describe the rules for statutory severance/redundancy payments.

Federal law sets a notice duty, not a severance entitlement. WARN requires 60 days' notice of a covered plant closing or mass layoff [US-FEDS5-025]. Pay in lieu of the notice is wages. Statutory severance established from official sources: Maine (establishments with 100+ employees: one week pay per year of service, within one pay period after last day; 90 days notice) [US-MES5-010]; New Jersey 1 week per year of service on a covered mass layoff [US-NJS5-020]. Severance is withheld as supplemental wages (9.2).

### Provide the statutory formula for any tax-free portion.

**Federal:** none. Severance is wages for FIT, FICA and FUTA in full [US-FEDS5-029].

**Alabama:** severance pay up to 50,000 is exempt from Alabama withholding [US-ALS5-001].

```
AL_exempt_part  = min(severance, AL_SEVERANCE_EXEMPT_MAX)
AL_taxable_part = severance - AL_exempt_part      # withheld under the AL method
FED_taxable     = severance                       # no federal exclusion
```

### Detail the rules for any applicable concessional tax cap.

No federal concessional cap applies to termination payments. Supplemental wages paid to one employee above 1,000,000 in the calendar year must be withheld at 37% [US-FEDS5-003]. This is a mandatory higher rate, not a concession. Alabama's severance exemption (50,000) is the only State cap established from official sources.

### Provide a detailed worked example of a redundancy termination payout.

**Scenario.** An employee whose position is eliminated receives severance pay of 18,000.00 in a separate payment, after 60,000.00 of 2026 wages from this employer. Severance is wages for FIT, FICA and FUTA [US-FEDS5-029]. Paid separately, it is withheld as supplemental wages at the optional flat rate. FUTA is already exhausted for the year at this wage level. State withholding and final-pay timing follow the work State (Table 9.1.1). Alabama, for example, exempts severance up to 50,000 from its withholding [US-ALS5-001].

*Redundancy severance payout (federal layer)*

| Line | Calculation | Result (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Supplemental payment (paid separately from regular wages) | — | 18,000.00 |
| FIT, optional flat rate (supplemental wages up to 1,000,000 in the year) | 18,000.00 × 22% | 3,960.00 |
| OASDI base remaining before this pay | 184,500 − 60,000.00 | 124,500.00 |
| OASDI base for this pay (lesser of remaining base and pay) | min(124,500.00, 18,000.00) | 18,000.00 |
| OASDI employee | 18,000.00 × 6.2% | 1,116.00 |
| Medicare employee | 18,000.00 × 1.45% | 261.00 |
| Net payment | 18,000.00 − 3,960.00 − 1,116.00 − 261.00 | 12,663.00 |

## 9.2 Irregular and Retrospective Payments

### Describe the specific tax calculation method required for irregular payments.

Bonuses, commissions, severance and similar payments are **supplemental wages**. Three federal rules apply [US-FEDS5-003]:
1. **Optional flat rate** of 22%, when the supplemental payment is identified separately from regular wages.
2. **Aggregate method:** the supplemental payment is withheld together with regular wages under the regular tables (procedure detail: Module 11.1, gap).
3. **Mandatory rate:** above 1,000,000 of supplemental wages in the year, 37% applies to the excess, whatever the W-4.

### How are backdated payments and retrospective salary adjustments handled?

Federal payroll taxes follow the **payment date**, not the period the pay relates to (calendar year (W-2 on calendar-year basis)) [US-FED-006]. A retroactive increase paid in 2026 is 2026 wages. It takes the 2026 tables and the 2026 wage bases, even when it relates to 2025. Massachusetts PFML is the exception: its rate follows the wages earned date [US-MAS5-012]. Not configured in this release (open gap RQ-525): The federal classification of back pay and retroactive increases (supplemental or regular wages) is not established from an official source.

### For arrears, how are contributions and deductions computed?

- Arrears paid now are wages of the payment period. OASDI is due on the lesser of the arrears and the remaining annual wage base (184,500 for 2026) [US-FEDS5-005]. Medicare is due on the full amount. Additional Medicare is due on the part above 200,000 of wages paid in the year [US-FEDS5-005].
- FIT uses the supplemental method if the arrears are paid separately (9.2), or the aggregate method if they are paid with regular wages.
- Child-support arrears withheld under an income withholding order follow the CCPA limits (add 5 percentage points when arrears exceed 12 weeks) [US-FEDS5-023]; see Module 7.

### Provide a worked example of a bonus calculation.

**Scenario.** An annual bonus of 5,000.00 is paid on its own check to an employee with 40,000.00 of 2026 wages. Supplemental wages identified separately from regular wages may be withheld at the optional flat rate [US-FEDS5-003]. FICA applies in full.

| Line | Calculation | Result (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Supplemental payment (paid separately from regular wages) | — | 5,000.00 |

| OASDI base for this pay (lesser of remaining base and pay) | min(144,500.00, 5,000.00) | 5,000.00 |
