Downloading JEDD/JEDZ Income Tax Rate Database Table
The JEDD/JEDZ Rate Database Table contains the income tax rate for each Ohio JEDD/JEDZ, if one exists.
Fields: 1 Beginning Effective Date; 2 Ending Effective Date; 3 JEDD/JEDZ ID; 4 JEDD/JEDZ Name; 5 Rate. Example: 20170101,29991231,1,Berkshire Delaware,0.01

Address-level files: one per municipality, named by the 5-digit Municipal FIPS Code; fields Beginning/Ending Effective Date, Street Address, City Name, State, 9-digit ZIP.
