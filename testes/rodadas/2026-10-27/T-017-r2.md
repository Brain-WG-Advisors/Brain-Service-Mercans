## Resposta

**IRPP:** Exempt only if the allowance is paid to all staff of the company. The exemption is capped at 30% of each employee's monthly base salary. Any amount above the cap is taxable, and so is the full allowance if it is not paid to all staff. Source: CGI Art. 45, 13°, as amended by Art. 6 of Loi n°037/PR/2018 (Loi de finances 2019), effective 1 January 2019: transport allowances are exempt when they benefit all company staff, up to 30% of each employee's monthly base salary.

**CNPS:** Excluded from the contribution base, with no cap stated. Contributions are assessed on gross pay, meaning all salaries, gains, indemnities (except those that reimburse expenses), bonuses and other benefits. The monthly transport allowance is listed among the expense-type items deducted from the contribution base. Source: CNPS Tchad, *Guide de l'employeur*, section II "Les cotisations sociales", heading "L'assiette des cotisations sociales".
