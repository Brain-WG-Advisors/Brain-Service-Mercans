URL 2: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/covid-19-recovery-assessment-rates
"The COVID-19 Recovery Assessment was created in 2021 to help recover funds paid during the COVID-19 pandemic. Experience-rated employers (employers who have been paying into unemployment for more than 3 years) are required to pay the Recovery Assessment." "New rates go into effect on January 1 each year." Exemptions: reimbursable employers; governmental employers; new employers (less than 3 years) who pay new employer rates; employers with assigned rates and zero 3-year average wages; non-profits that changed to contributory within the last 3 years.
