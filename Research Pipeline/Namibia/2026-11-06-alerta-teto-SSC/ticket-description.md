Namibia - Effective 1st September 2026, the Social Security (MSD Fund) contribution ceiling increases as under:

* Maximum monthly basic wage: NAD 11,000 - NAD 12,500
* Maximum employee contribution (0.9%): NAD 99.00 - NAD 112.50
* Maximum employer contribution (0.9%): NAD 99.00 - NAD 112.50
* Contribution rates: 0.9% employee / 0.9% employer - no change

Next steps: NAD 14,000 from 1st March 2027, NAD 15,000 from 1st March 2028, NAD 16,000 from 1st March 2029.

Official source - Government Notice No. 236 of 2026, Government Gazette No. 8975: https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2026/8975.pdf
