=== URL: https://www.revenue.nh.gov/taxes-glance/business-taxes ===
"The Business Enterprise Tax (BET) was enacted in 1993. The tax is assessed on the taxable enterprise value tax base, which is the sum of all compensation paid or accrued, interest paid or accrued, and dividends paid by the business enterprise at the rate of .55% for taxable periods ending on or after December 31, 2022. For taxable periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025, every business enterprise with more than $298,000 of gross receipts from all activities, or an enterprise value tax base of more than $298,000, must file a BET return."
"The BET paid may be used as a credit against the Business Profits Tax (BPT)."
BPT: 7.5% for taxable periods ending on or after December 31, 2023.

Observation: BET is an annual entity-level business tax whose base includes compensation paid; it is filed on the business return (not through payroll, no per-employee calculation, creditable against BPT). Classified as outside payroll scope (business tax), flagged for the owner.
