## Resposta

**Yes, it applies to private-sector employees.** It is an employee deduction of EGP 5 per EGP 10,000 (0.05%). The employer withholds it and remits it.

**Legal basis.** Law 16/2018 art. 8(1), as replaced by Law 4/2021 art. 1 (Official Gazette No. 8 bis (هـ), 3 March 2021, in force 4 March 2021). It provides for a monthly deduction of *"خمسة من عشرة آلاف من راتب ... العاملين لدى الأشخاص الاعتبارية الخاصة الذين تسرى بشأنهم أحكام القانون الخاص بالعمل"*. In plain terms, it covers employees of private legal persons governed by the Labour Law, **except irregular or daily labour** (*العمالة غير المنتظمة أو باليومية*).

**Who bears it.** The employee bears it. The employer pays the salary net of the contribution and remits the amount through ETA. The ETA payroll system issues it as its own Core Taxation claim (*مطالبة بالمساهمة في صندوق الشهداء*), separate from the salary-tax claim (ETA Payroll-System FAQ v4, March 2026, salary-items Q12). Per MoF General Circular 14/2021, item 3 (part ثانيا), payment is due monthly by the 10th of the following month. The contribution does **not** reduce the salary-tax base.

**Base.** The sources give two readings:
- MoF General Circular 14/2021 (10 May 2021), item 3, defines the salary as *إجمالي الراتب الشهري*, the total monthly salary.
- ETA FAQ v4, Q110, says the system computes it *"على الصرفيات الأساسية فقط"*. That means basic disbursements only, as opposed to additional disbursements (*صرفيات إضافية*). It applies to all labour except **Egyptian** daily labour.

Two points diverge. The circular's base is the total monthly salary, while the ETA system uses basic disbursements. The law excludes all daily and irregular labour, while the system excludes only Egyptian daily labour. The practical rule is to follow the ETA system's computation, since that is what generates the claim, and disclose the divergence.
