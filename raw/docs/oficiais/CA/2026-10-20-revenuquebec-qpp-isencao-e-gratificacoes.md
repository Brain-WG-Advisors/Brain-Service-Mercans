# Revenu Québec: isenção básica do QPP e gratificações (cópia de leitura, 2026-10-20)

Lido pelo Claude in Chrome em 2026-10-20 (janela Gmail), para o comentário da Ruchi na planilha "K2 Split into Regular and Bonus Income".

## Fonte 1
https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/source-deductions-and-employer-contributions/calculating-source-deductions-and-contributions/qpp-contributions/basic-quebec-pension-plan-exemption/continuous-employment/
Trecho (literal):
> If you pay pensionable salary or wages to the same employee more than once in the same pay period (for example, salary and a gratuity), you can use the tables (and take the exemption into account) for only one such payment. For subsequent payments of pensionable salary or wages in the pay period, simply withhold the lesser of the following amounts: [the employee's contribution rate × pensionable salary or wages up to the MPE (without taking the exemption into account) + second additional rate × portion between MPE and AMPE] or [the employee's maximum contributions for the year, minus the amounts already withheld].

## Fonte 2
https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/source-deductions-and-employer-contributions/calculating-source-deductions-and-contributions/special-cases-source-deductions-and-employer-contributions/gratuities-and-retroactive-pay/
Trecho (literal, seção "Québec Pension Plan contributions"):
> If you pay an employee a gratuity or retroactive pay during a pay period and you pay the amount with the employee's salary or wages for the period, add the amount to the salary or wages to calculate the employee's Québec Pension Plan (QPP) contributions.
> If you pay the amount separately from the employee's salary or wages and the pay period exemption has already been taken into account, withhold the lesser of the following amounts: [rate × amount without the exemption, capped at the remaining annual maximum].
