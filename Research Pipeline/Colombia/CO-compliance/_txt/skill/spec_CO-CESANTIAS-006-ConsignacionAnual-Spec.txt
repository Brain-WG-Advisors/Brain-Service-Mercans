R7| - THE RECIPIENT IS NOT A GOVERNMENT AUTHORITY. The money goes to a private fondo de cesantias, or to the state-owned FNA, in an individual account in the employee's name. There is no authority portal, no fiscal receipt and no tax-authority validation - which is exactly why no XSD exists and why this filing must never be labelled XML.
R8| - THE OPERATOR IS THE SAME KIND OF INTERMEDIARY AS FOR PILA, BUT THE SERVICE IS NOT PILA. Cesantias is a Pago Complementario a la Seguridad Social (PCSS) and is selected as a distinct service inside the operator's portal, with its own planilla number series and its own period concept - a YEAR, not a month. Simple S.A. notes that the login is the same one used for PILA, which is the source of the frequent error of building a PILA planilla and expecting the cesantias to be in it.
R9| - The DIAN portals play no part in this filing. Formulario 350, Formulario 220 and Formato 2276 go to DIAN; the cesantias deposit never does. What DIAN sees of cesantias is the reporting of the amounts on Formulario 220 casillas 48-49 and Formato 2276 numerals 19-20, which are separate filings with their own Specs (CO-DIAN-004 and CO-DIAN-005).

R19| RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT (v2.8 control B1)
R20| - RESIDENT AND NON-RESIDENT ARE TREATED IDENTICALLY IN THIS FILING, AND THAT IS THE POINT. Cesantias is a LABOUR entitlement under the CST, not a tax concept, so it attaches to the employment relationship and not to tax residence. A non-resident foreign national employed under a Colombian contract accrues cesantias on the same base, at the same 360-day rate, is deposited to a fund of their own choosing and is reported in this planilla on exactly the same record as a resident. There is no non-resident variant of the layout and no withholding on the deposit.
R21| - WHERE RESIDENCE DOES BITE IS THE TAX TREATMENT, WHICH IS NOT THIS FILING. For a RESIDENT, ET art. 206 num. 4 exempts the severance savings and their interest in full where the average monthly income of the last six months is at or below 350 UVT (COP 18,330,900 in 2026), then tapers 90 / 80 / 60 / 40 / 20 per cent across the 350-410, 410-470, 470-530, 530-590 and 590-650 UVT bands, with nothing exempt above 650 UVT. For a NON-RESIDENT the art. 206 num. 4 exemption is unavailable - it belongs to the resident cedula general - and the payment is taxed at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the gross with no art. 388 depuration. Neither outcome changes a single character of the record sent to the fund.
R22| - THE DOCUMENT TYPE IS WHERE THE FOREIGN EMPLOYEE ACTUALLY BREAKS. The layout admits CE, PA, CD, SC, PE and (in the XLS variant) PT, and SOI records that a Permiso Especial de Permanencia number must be exactly 15 digits. A regularisation that converts a PEP or PPT into a Cedula de Extranjeria changes the join key (T03 + T04) that the fund holds, so the change must reach the fund or the following February's deposit lands on an orphan account. See T03 and T04.
R23| - A Colombian employee posted abroad but retained on a Colombian contract remains in scope; an employee of a foreign entity with no Colombian employment contract is out of scope entirely, because there is no CST relationship to accrue cesantias on.

=== SHEET: Population Rules (dims A1:D21, max_row 21, max_col 4) ===

R1| POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
R2| Field Name | Source System | Population Logic | Edge Cases
~
R4| Population of the planilla | Payroll master data + service history | Select every employee IN SERVICE AT 31 DECEMBER of the accrual year who is on the annual cesantias regime (Ley 50 de 1990 art. 99 - all employees hired from 1 January 1991, plus pre-1991 hires who migrated). EXCLUDE, in this order: (1) salario integral employees, whose cesantias is absorbed by CST art. 132 num. 2; (2) employees who LEFT during the year, whose cesantias was settled and paid in the final settlement; (3) pre-1991 non-migrated employees on the retrospective regime, for whom there is no fund deposit at all; (4) employees discharged by proven direct payment under CST art. 57 par. 4; (5) records whose liquidation falls below one day of one SMMLV, which the channel rejects. Do NOT build the population from the December PILA planilla, however convenient the portal makes it (E08): PILA is the population PAID in a month, not the population ENTITLED for the year - it contains every salario integral employee and omits every mid-year leaver. | A leaver included = double payment, and the money is then in a fund account the ex-employee must claim. A salario integral employee included = an unrecoverable deposit. A missed employee = one day's salary per day of delay, uncapped, running until it is found.
R5| Fund per employee | Employee master data | Read Employee.Cesantias_Fund_Code. It is the EMPLOYEE'S statutory choice under Ley 50 de 1990 art. 99 num. 3 ('en el fondo de cesantia que el mismo elija'), so it is employee master data and never a company default. Where a mid-year change of fund has been notified, use the fund in force at the date of the deposit. Where the field is empty the record cannot be built - escalate, because affiliation is compulsory even where the cesantias is paid directly (CST art. 57 par. 4 in fine). Set E07 to N so that this per-employee value governs. | An empty fund code is not a payroll defect to be defaulted - it is an unfulfilled affiliation obligation. Answering YES to the portal's single-fund question to get past it deposits other people's money into the wrong fund and does not discharge the obligation.
~
R7| T14 Valor de las cesantias | Payroll engine - annual liquidation | Compute, do NOT let the portal compute. (a) Determine the BASE under CST art. 253: the LAST monthly salary if it has not varied in the last three months; otherwise, and for every variable salary, the AVERAGE of the last year of service, or of the whole period served if shorter. (b) ADD THE AUXILIO DE TRANSPORTE where it is payable - CST art. 253 requires it and it is payable where the salary does not exceed 2 SMMLV (COP 3,501,810 in 2026). (c) Multiply by days of service over 360. (d) Round to the whole peso - not up to the next 100 as PILA requires, and not to the nearest 1,000 as Formulario 350 requires. (e) Validate against the floor of one day of one SMMLV (COP 58,363.50 for 2026). (f) Where a lawful partial settlement was made during the year under CST art. 254, deduct it. (g) Where the monthly advance regime is ever switched on, deposit only the difference (MA05). Write the result into positions 128-136, right-aligned and zero-filled, with '0000' in positions 124-127. | THE TRAP: the layout's own formula is 'Salario basico * (dias trabajados / 360)' and campo 12 is named 'salario basico'. Follow the portal and a minimum-wage employee is deposited COP 1,750,905 instead of COP 2,000,000 - a COP 249,095 short deposit per employee, penalised as non-deposit at one day's salary per day of delay. The floor also bites in reverse: a residue below COP 58,363.50 cannot be deposited and must be settled with the employee instead.
R8| T12 Salario basico del ultimo mes | Payroll - December of the accrual year | Report the DECEMBER basic salary of the accrual year, exactly as the layout specifies ('el ultimo salario basico reportado en el mes de diciembre del ano causado de cesantias'), in 9 integer positions, zero-filled, with '0000' in positions 111-114. This is a REPORTING field for the fund's records - it is NOT the statutory base, and the two will legitimately differ for a variable-salary employee whose base is a twelve-month average. Do not reverse-engineer T12 from T14 to make the portal's calculator agree. | For an employee who was on unpaid leave in December, or who was hired on 20 December, the December basic is still the contractual monthly salary, not the amount actually earned that month. For an employee whose salary changed in November, T12 carries the December figure while the CST art. 253 base is the twelve-month average - so T14 will not equal T12 * T10/360, and that is correct.
R9| T10 Numero de dias trabajados | Payroll - service calendar | Count the days of service in the accrual year on the 360-day COMMERCIAL convention: each complete month is 30 days, a full year is 360. Include paid leave, paid holidays and recognised incapacity, all of which count as service. Exclude unpaid leave (licencia no remunerada) and suspension. For an employee in service the whole year write 360, not 365 and not 366. The field admits 1 to 360 only, so any computation that can produce 361 or more is wrong before it reaches the file. | A leap year does not change the divisor. An employee hired on 31 January accrues 330 days, not 335. Where unpaid leave straddles a month end, count the calendar days of the leave on the 30-day convention and subtract - do not subtract whole months.

R20| IN01-IN03 Intereses de cesantias | Payroll engine - paid to the EMPLOYEE | Compute 12% per annum on the 31 December balance, pro-rated on days over 360, and PAY IT TO THE EMPLOYEE DIRECTLY DURING JANUARY of the following year - never to the fund and never in February.
