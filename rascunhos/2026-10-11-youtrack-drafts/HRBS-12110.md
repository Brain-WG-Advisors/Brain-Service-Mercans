The Reporting Matrix is not ready for client review. The statutory payroll filings for Congo are:

1. **CNSS** — monthly nominative declaration on the CNSS e-déclaration portal (edeclaration.cnss.cg), including the CNSS share of TUS. TUS is 7.5% employer-borne (loi n°8-2012, art. 6), split 73% CNSS / 27% State from 2026 (LF 2026, loi n°42-2025, art. 47, point 23), i.e. 5.475% / 2.025%. The "CG - CNSS" tab is empty.
2. **CAMU** — paid by the employer **directly to the CAMU** within 5 days after the end of each month, with a nominative declaration sent 10 days before the due date (Décret n°2024-131, arts. 15 and 16). Rates 4.55% employer / 2.27% employee (Décret n°2024-133, art. 2). CAMU does not appear anywhere in the matrix.
3. **DGID** — monthly ITS and TUS (State share) return via e-Tax (CGI art. 461 bis, as introduced by LF 2026). The "CG-DGID-001" tab shows a monthly ITS of 279,300 XAF for 120 employees; on its own figures (taxable base 27,648,000 XAF, i.e. 230,400 per employee, 1 part, LF 2026 art. 116 G scale), 279,300 XAF is one employee's annual ITS, and the monthly total is about 2,793,000 XAF.
4. **Annual salary declaration (DAS1, DAS2, DAS3)** — due during the month of January (CGI art. 176); DAS3 introduced by LF 2026, art. 116 I, model fixed by Arrêté n°1560 of 10/07/2026. The "CG-DAS-001" tab is empty.

Content from other countries remains in the file: the "note" tab lists Senegal's reports (État 1024, TRIMF, CFCE, IPRES), the "Payslip" tab is an Estonia example in GHS, the "Global Payment Report" is set to Ghana/GHS, and the "Indemnity Accrual Register" holds another entity's data, including what appear to be employee IDs. "Payslips Local Language" is set to English; the official language is French (Constitution 2015, art. 4).

I remain available should there be any discrepancy in this information.
