# T-014

**Pergunta:** Colombia, Nómina Electrónica (DIAN): for an employee who is already a pensioner and keeps working (worker subtype 01), what health (Salud) percentage is deducted from the employee, and is a pension (FondoPension) deduction due?

**Resposta:**
- **Salud:** still deducted, at the 4% employee share on the salary (IBC). Report it as Salud/@Porcentaje = 4.00.
- **FondoPension:** not due. The obligation to contribute ends once the member has been granted a pension, so FondoPension = 0 (no deduction).

The earlier spec note that both Salud and FondoPension go out at 0.00 was wrong; only pension is 0.

This is not the same as a pensioner whose pension (mesada) is paid by the employer. That case is not subtype 01 salary: health is charged on the mesada at 4% (up to 1 SMLMV), 10% (over 1 up to 3 SMLMV) or 12% (over 3 SMLMV), since 2024.
