> @lli The withholding rate is calculated under RIRPF (RD 439/2007) art. 82: base for the rate (art. 83), personal and family minimum (art. 84), withholding cuota from the art. 85.1 scale, and the rate = cuota ÷ total annual remuneration × 100, expressed with two decimals (art. 86.1). In a mid-year regularisation, the new rate is the remaining cuota ÷ the remuneration still to be paid until year-end × 100, also with two decimals (art. 87.3.c). The AEAT 2026 withholding algorithm (version applicable from 10 September 2026, "Tipo de retención aplicable") truncates the rate at the second decimal.
