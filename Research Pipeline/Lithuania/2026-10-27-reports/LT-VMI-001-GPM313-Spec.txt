R6: 6 / 7 - GPM on employment income, split at the 15th | Payroll results | Split withheld GPM by the date the individual was actually paid: box 6 for payments up to and including the 15th, box 7 for payments after the 15th to month end. This is a payment-date split, not an accrual split, and it drives two different GPM remittance deadlines, so it must come from the payment ledger rather than the payroll period. Where the employee has requested application of the GPMI art. 6 progressive rates to periodic payments under rule 9.6, the employer applies those rates by reference to total payments already made and planned for the whole tax period, so the rate used in a given month depends on cumulative-to-date figures and cannot be computed from the month alone. | One payroll run paid in two tranches straddling the 15th; off-cycle bonus paid on the 16th; payment date moved across the 15th boundary because it fell on a weekend; rule 9.6 request received mid-year, re-banding later months only; NPD applied such that GPM is zero while box 5 is non-zero.
R7: 8 - Non-employment class A gross | Payroll results and accounts payable | Sum the taxable non-employment class A payments made in the month, stated before GPM, VSD and PSD. Note the deliberate absence of NPD from rule 10.1: NPD is an employment relief and is not deducted here. Payroll typically contributes board and supervisory-board remuneration, tantiemes, employer-paid royalties and small-partnership manager service fees, while other categories arrive from accounts payable. The rate is driven by payment type and amount under rule 10.4: 15 % flat for rule 10.4.1 types, 20/25/32 % for rule 10.4.2 types, 5/20/25/32 % for non-business waste sales, and 15/20/25/32 % for anything else. R9: 11 / 12 - Class B payments and GPM | Accounts payable (out of payroll scope) | Populate only where the payer withheld GPM from, or paid GPM out of its own funds on, class B payments during the month despite having no GPMI obligation to do so. The second paragraph of rule 11.2 overrides the natural cash basis: where the payer pays that GPM out of its own funds in a later month, both the payment and the GPM must be declared in the return of the month the payment was made, which means amending an already-filed return rather than reporting it currently. | GPM paid from own funds in a later month, requiring a retrospective amendment; class B payment where withholding was obligatory after all, which belongs in boxes 8-10; class B payment with no withholding at all, which is not declarable here.
