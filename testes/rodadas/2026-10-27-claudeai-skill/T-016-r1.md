## Resposta

The monthly IUSC structure is settled and holds up. The Art. 46 reliquidation, as recorded in v1.1 (one YTM step), has an accuracy problem I'd raise with Ruchi and Mohit before freezing it. Note that I'm working from the decisions recorded as of 2026-10-27, not the v1.1 design file itself. So 58121, 58122 and 58123 are the real accounts, and any other 581xx/582xx/585xx number below is a placeholder.

## 1. Monthly IUSC (LRE 3161 → F29 cód. 48)

The design works in UTM units. The brackets are fixed in UTM (13.5 / 30 / 50 / 70 / 90 / 120 / 310), while SII's CLP table changes every month. If you convert the base once, the bands never need editing; only `$legal_entity_hr.utm` changes each month.

| # | Account | Type / Tax Rule Group | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | 582xx regime flag | flag | 1 = IUSC, 2 = Impuesto Adicional 35% (non-resident, F50). Route with GREATER/LESS, never EQUALS 0 |
| a | 581xx BTM CLP | Flat | Matrix of the tributable series, **excluding the reliquidable WTs** so they aren't taxed twice. EE contributions and the APV-B intermediate go on the credit side as negatives. Art. 55 bis stays outside the engine |
| b | 58121 BTM in UTM | Flat, `_post_formula` | `DIVIDE($band_amount_raw;$legal_entity_hr.utm)` |
| c | IUSC in UTM | Differential, `simple_tax_monthly` | Bands in UTM at 0 / 4 / 8 / 13.5 / 23 / 30.4 / 35 / 40% |
| d | IUSC CLP → pay element 3161 | Flat, `_post_formula` | `FLOOR(ADD(MULTIPLY($band_amount_raw;$legal_entity_hr.utm);0.5))`, GL 2610/2552 |

Two points on step (c):

- **Differential vs. lookup.** v1.1 records step (c) as a rate/rebaja lookup (58122/58123), "without differential", per dev guidance in June. The rulebook rule "one Differential per progressive tax" came later, on 2 July. The two methods are numerically identical: the SII rebajas (0.54 / 1.74 / 4.49 / 11.14 / 17.80 / 23.32 / 38.82 UTM) are exactly the cumulative differential amounts. Across 0–40M CLP the largest difference I found was 1 peso, from rounding. So this is an engine/style choice to confirm with Ruchi. If you go with Differential, 58122 and 58123 disappear.
- **Band edges.** Don't close the UTM bands at ,99. A 0.01 UTM gap is about CLP 692. Use contiguous edges, or confirm how the engine treats equal edges (still open in rulebook §8.1).

## 2. Art. 46 reliquidation (LRE 3164/3165)

This should be a separate family with three inputs:

- **A lump intermediate (581xx).** It holds only the reliquidable WTs: 62200–62203, 62108, and the arrears/adjustment codes. 62160 aguinaldo is excluded. 62182 (indemnización sobre tope) needs its own path, because it is spread over years of service and posts to 3162.
- **n, the number of attributable months.** This is a non-monetary selector, so it goes in a flag and a formula, never in a band.
- **The original BTM in UTM for each attributable month.**

The calculation, in UTM units: l = L ÷ UTM_payment ÷ n; Δᵢ = T(bᵢ + l) − T(bᵢ); result = ΣΔᵢ × UTM_payment.

- **Pay elements.** Post the result to its own pay elements (3164 for past months, 3165 for the current month), GL 2610/2552. Keep them separate from 3161, because F29 cód. 48 must equal 3161 + 3162 + 3163 + 3164 + 3165.
- **DJ 1887 base.** Accessory income from the prior year that is paid January–April feeds DJ 1887 Box 2 (C-03). That needs a `_period` base crediting 99998.

**The problem with the single YTM step.** A YTM accumulator only holds year-to-month totals, so it can't see each month separately. I ran both methods with a constant UTM of 69,265 and a bonus of 6M:

| Scenario | Exact Art. 46 | YTM average |
|---|---|---|
| CCG example (12 equal months) | 240,000 | 240,000 |
| Raise in July (1,472,760 → 3,200,000) | 438,126 | 480,000 |
| 8 zero months, then 4 at 2.5M | 160,000 | 191,163 |

There's also a calendar problem. YTM resets on 1 January, but the CCG says bonos anuales are typically paid January–March of the following year. In that case the YTM would see the wrong months entirely.

Options to put to the dev team:

1. **`retro_` on the IUSC family**, attributing L/n to each month. This is literally Art. 46, and it fits the 10 July "tax 100% on retro" decision. It is also open item §8.3 in the rulebook.
2. **Keep the YTM step with a documented restriction**: same-year, homogeneous months only, and everything else handled manually.
3. **Per-month accumulators.** This was the v1.0 approach, which was rejected as too heavy.

## Research gaps for compliance

1. Neither the DD nor the WTC has a field for n or the accrual period. The WTC 622xx slots also mix monthly and multi-month bonuses under the same codes. The LRE codes 2116–2122 do encode accrual length (2, 3, 4, 6 or 12 months).
2. **UTM convention for the lump.** CCG v1.0 converts at the payment-month UTM. The current 01-ccg.md §6.3 doesn't say.
3. **Rounding.** The current CCG says "rounded to integer peso"; the CCG v1.0 example truncates.
4. **DD Income Tax Type mapper.** `IUSC_RELIQUIDADO` is a per-payment event, not a worker-level regime. The 35% non-resident option exists only in the LRE COD-1170 mapper. The label `EXENTO_LEY_18156` is misleading, since that worker still pays IUSC.
5. The report spec describes 3164 vs. 3165 only in generic terms.

**What I validated:** I ran the formulas in Python. The three CCG IUSC examples match (21,507 / 44,417 / 296,500), as does the Art. 46 example (240,000). **Not validated:** loading into the engine, the 10-scenario matrix, special categories, or a UTM that varies month to month. Not production-ready.

Outside the request (not included): the F29 spec's Scenario 2 reliquidates a "bono navidad" over 12 months, which contradicts the CCG and the WTC (aguinaldos don't qualify).
