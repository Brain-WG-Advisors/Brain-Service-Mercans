3. **Where nrs. 53, 58, 60 and 62 do appear — Modules 1, 6 and 9 — they cite Annex III to the Royal
   Decree implementing ITC 92**, the FPS Finance withholding-tax scales annex, which genuinely is
   published in numbered points. The research pass searched NSSO/ONSS instructions, circulars and
   annexes. That is the wrong authority, which is why it found nothing: this is a **tax** instrument,
   not a social-security one.

**Confirmed live against an independent source** that Annex III points 58–63 carry the withholding
scale for arrears and notice indemnities, and that point 62 sets the notice-indemnity percentage on
*the remuneration that served as the basis for setting the indemnity* — word for word what CCG
Module 9 says and what its worked example does.

**Why the proposed replacement would have been wrong.** nrs. 53/58/62 govern the withholding
*percentage*. DmfA remuneration codes 3 and 9, worker-contribution code 812 in block 90001, and the
quarter-allocation and prescription chapters govern how severance is *declared and contributed for
social security*. They are two different subjects. Swapping one for the other attaches a
social-security authority to a tax rule.

### How it was done

Not as a patch blob. Each drifted sheet's content was extracted from the delivered workbook back
into **the data structure the existing builder API already consumes** — `sheet_field_options`,
`sheet_version_control`, `sheet_spec`, `sheet_report_matrix` and the rest — and stored in versioned
`_backport/` modules the builders import. Row *kind* (payroll / system / constant / excluded) is
recovered from the delivered row fill, so the amber/blue/green/red convention survives exactly.

Four genuine generator bugs were fixed at source rather than patched around:

1. **Version Control layout** — the builder inserted a two-row gap before `REMARKS`. All nine
   delivered files have `REMARKS` immediately after the changelog: the release passes that appended
   the v1.1–v1.3 rows consumed the gap. Fixed in `spec_builder.py`; it was shifting whole sheets.
2. **Field Options sub-headers** — the sheets carry two header levels (`═══ … ═══` and `--- … ---`)
   and the builder knew only the first, so sub-headers were emitted as unfilled data rows. Text
   matched; fill did not. This is why fills were checked at all.
3. **WTC column A** — written as `None` for all 359 rows on the reading that an orange column stays
   empty, against the workbook's own recorded decision. This one had teeth: column A is the join key
   Stage 10 Check 5 depends on, so a rebuild would have broken that check.
4. **Limosa structures** — the descope banner, the custom scope-summary headers and the
   already-suffixed title could not be expressed at all. Three additive optional parameters.

### The durable fix

**`_pipeline/verify_generators.py`.** Runs all 13 generators into a temporary directory, diffs values
and fills against the delivered artefact, exits non-zero on drift. It cannot write to a delivered
file. **Run it after every change.** This is failure pattern F29 made mechanical: the ledger records
intent, the gate records effect.

**One documented tolerance.** BE-DD-001 Country Option Mappers: 14 of the 86 **empty** separator rows
