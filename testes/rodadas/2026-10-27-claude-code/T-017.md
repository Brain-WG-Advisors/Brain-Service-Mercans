# T-017

**Pergunta:** Chad: what is the income tax (IRPP) and CNPS treatment of a transport allowance paid to employees?

**Resposta:**
- **Up to 30% of the base salary:** exempt from both IRPP and CNPS, provided the allowance is granted uniformly to all staff.
- **Above 30%:** the excess is taxable for IRPP and subject to CNPS. It is also subject to TFE and TAFP.

It is not a full exemption. WTC v1.1 treated it as fully exempt, and this was corrected.
