A2	'Field Name'
B2	'Source System'
C2	'Population Logic'
D2	'Edge Cases'
A3	'═══ MANDATORY FIELD RULES ═══'
A4	'Version (NIE022)'
B4	'Configuration'
C4	"Emit the constant string 'V1.0: Documento Soporte de Pago de Nómina Electrónica' exactly, including accents and the colon-space. Never build it from a version variable, never trim, never transliterate. Validation rule NIE022 compares the whole literal."
D4	"A configuration file saved as ASCII will silently strip the accent on 'Nomina' and every document will be rejected."
A5	'Numero (NIE012)'
B5	'Engine sequence + configuration'
C5	'Concatenate the DIAN-authorised Prefix (NIE010) with the next unused Sequence number (NIE011), with no separator. Persist the last-used consecutive per prefix range in a transactional store, and increment it BEFORE transmission, never after, so that a failed transmission does not leave a reusable number. Global rule 90 permits a given number only once per employee.'
D5	'A rejected document consumes its number. On re-transmission the replacement document must carry a NEW consecutive. Never re-send a rejected number.'
A6	'CUNE (NIE024)'
B6	'Engine (computed)'
C6	'Compute SHA-384 over the eleven inputs concatenated with no separators, in the published order: Numero, FechaGen, HoraGen (with the -05:00 offset), DevengadosTotal, DeduccionesTotal, ComprobanteTotal, employer NIT (no DV), employee document number, TipoXML, the secret software PIN, and Ambiente. The three money inputs must be TRUNCATED to two decimals - not rounded - and written with a decimal point, no thousand separators and no currency symbol. Emit the result as 96 lower-case hexadecimal characters.'
D6	'The four classic failures: (1) rounding instead of truncating the money inputs; (2) omitting the -05:00 offset from HoraGen; (3) including the employer DV in NitNE; (4) using the SoftwareID instead of the PIN. All four produce a well-formed but wrong CUNE.'
A7	'SoftwareSC (NIE020)'
B7	'Engine (computed)'
C7	'Compute SHA-384 over SoftwareID + PIN + document number. Recompute for EVERY document, because the document number is an input. Do not cache it at software level.'
D7	'Caching SoftwareSC as if it were a constant is a common error - it is constant only for a single document.'
A8	'TiempoLaborado (NIE006)'
B8	'Engine (computed)'
C8	"Compute cumulative service from FechaIngreso to FechaLiquidacionFin on the Colombian commercial convention: 1 year = 360 days, 1 month = 30 days. Decompose the elapsed period into whole years, whole months and residual days, then evaluate (years x 360) + (months x 30) + days. Emit with two decimals. The annex's own example is 5 years + 3 months + 18 days = 1908.00."
D8	"Do NOT use a calendar day-count. A naive (date2 - date1).days gives 1913 for the annex's own example instead of 1908, and DIAN will not flag it - the error surfaces only in a seniority dispute."
A9	'DiasTrabajados (NIE069)'
B9	'Payroll engine'
C9	'Days actually worked and paid in the period on the 30-day commercial month. Start at 30 (or the number of days from a mid-month hire), then subtract unpaid-leave days (LicenciaNR), suspension days and lawful-strike days. Do NOT subtract incapacity, maternity or vacation days - those are paid and appear in their own devengado blocks.'
D9	'February always counts 30 days in Colombian payroll, rather than 28. A 31-day month also counts 30.'
A10	'═══ CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ═══'
A11	'DevengadosTotal / DeduccionesTotal / ComprobanteTotal (NIE187-NIE189)'
B11	'Engine (computed)'
C11	'DevengadosTotal is the sum of every populated element in the Earnings block; DeduccionesTotal is the sum of every populated element in Deductions; ComprobanteTotal is DevengadosTotal minus DeduccionesTotal, adjusted for the Rounding residual. Each element is first rounded half-to-even to two decimals, then summed - not summed and then rounded. Where a residual remains, disclose it in Rounding (NIE186) rather than absorbing it into a concept. Validation tolerance is +/- 2.00.'
D11	'Summing unrounded values and rounding the total produces a figure that ties to the cent but does not match the sum of the printed lines on the payslip. Round first, then sum.'
A12	'Salary / non-salary paired elements'
B12	'Wage-type configuration'
C12	'Every paired element (ViaticoManuAlojS / NS, BonificacionS / NS, AuxilioS / NS, ConceptoS / NS, PagoS / NS, PagoAlimentacionS / NS, Primas Payment / PagoNS) is driven by the salary / non-salary attribute of the wage type in the Wage Type Catalogue (WTC). Never infer the split at reporting time from the amount, the description or the wage-type name.'
D12	'The 40% non-salary clamp of Ley 1393 de 2010 art. 30 depends on this split, as does the contribution base. An inference error propagates into PILA and into the employer cost.'
A13	'Employer contributions must never appear in Deductions'
B13	'Wage-type configuration'
C13	"The Deductions block reports only what was withheld FROM THE EMPLOYEE. The employer's 12% pension, 8.5% health, occupational-risk premium and the 9% payroll levies (SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, CCF 4%) are employer costs reported on PILA. Configure them on a cost, not a deduction, wage-type class so they cannot leak into this block."
D13	'A single mis-classified employer wage type inflates DeduccionesTotal and understates ComprobanteTotal, which then disagrees with the actual bank payment.'
A14	'Solidarity fund must not be merged into pension'
B14	'Wage-type configuration'
C14	'FondoSP carries FOUR attributes - the solidarity rate and amount, and the subsistence rate and amount. They are validated independently of FondoPension. Configure the solidarity surcharge as its own wage type from the outset.'
D14	"This is the single most common Colombian mapping error. Merging produces a 5% 'pension' line that fails reconciliation against PILA fields 47, 51 and 52."
A15	'Overtime cannot be aggregated'
B15	'Payroll engine'
C15	'Emit one element per contiguous block of hours of a single type. Split a block that crosses 21:00 or midnight, because the applicable code changes. Populate quantity, percentage and amount for every block, plus start and end times where the time register carries them.'
D15	'A block that crosses the 21:00 boundary but is emitted as a single HED element misstates both the code and the premium.'
A16	'═══ AMOUNT FORMAT RULES ═══'
A17	'All monetary elements'
B17	'Engine (formatting)'
C17	'Two decimals, decimal POINT, no thousand separator, no currency symbol, no leading plus sign. Rounding is round-half-to-even (NTC 3711 / JIS Z 8401), not half-up. Negative values are not used in the accrued or deducted blocks; only Rounding (NIE186) may legitimately be negative.'
D17	'A locale-aware formatter will emit a comma decimal separator in an es-CO locale and the document will fail schema validation.'
A18	'Three rounding conventions coexist'
B18	'Engine (formatting)'
C18	'The XML uses two decimals round-half-to-even. PILA rounds the base UP to the peso and each contribution UP to the next multiple of 100. Form 350 rounds to the nearest 1,000. The same health contribution therefore legitimately appears as three different numbers in three artefacts. Store the unrounded value once and apply each convention at output time.'
D18	'Never display the PILA figure on the payslip as if it were the deduction. Where the PILA figure is shown on the payslip, it must be a separate, labelled line (CCG Module 10.3).'
A19	'Percentage attributes'
B19	'Configuration'
C19	'Rate attributes (NIE077 and the other six @Porcentaje, NIE161, NIE164, NIE167, NIE169, NIE121, NIE171) carry the rate itself, expressed to two decimals - 4.00 rather than 0.04, 25.00 rather than 0.25.'
D19	'Emitting 0.04 for the 4% health rate passes schema validation and produces a document that is internally inconsistent with @Deduccion.'
A20	'═══ PAYROLL-SPECIFIC POPULATION RULES ═══'
A21	'RetencionFuente (NIE177) - RESIDENT'
B21	'Payroll engine (tax)'
C21	'Build the ET art. 388 depurated base: gross taxable payments, less the mandatory health, pension and solidarity-fund contributions (ingresos no constitutivos de renta), less the art. 387 deductions elected by the employee (dependants at 10% of gross capped at 32 UVT per month, prepaid health up to 16 UVT per month, housing-loan interest up to 100 UVT per month), less the 25% exempt income of art. 206 num. 10. Apply the two caps: deductions plus exempt income may not exceed 40% of gross less non-constitutive income, nor 1,340 UVT per year (the annual limit is apportioned at 111.6667 UVT per month, a configurable apportionment rule). Convert the resulting base to UVT at 52,374, read the art. 383 table, convert back to pesos, and approximate to the nearest 1,000 under ET art. 868. Under Procedure 2 apply instead the fixed percentage recomputed each June and December on the preceding twelve months divided by 13.'
D21	'Exclude severance savings and the interest on them from the monthly base under BOTH procedures (art. 206 num. 4). Under Procedure 1 the statutory minimum service bonus gets its OWN separate read of the table (art. 385) and does not merge into the ordinary base. For a sub-30-day period apply the art. 385 rule: gross the payments to 30 days, read the table for a PERCENTAGE, then apply that percentage to the actual smaller payment - never read the table for an amount on the grossed-up figure.'
A22	'RetencionFuente (NIE177) - NON-RESIDENT'
B22	'Payroll engine (tax)'
C22	'Apply a FLAT 20% to the GROSS payment under ET art. 408. Do not depurate: no deduction of the mandatory contributions, no art. 387 deductions, no 25% exempt income, no tax-free band, no Procedure 1 or 2 election. This applies to salary, to the service bonus, to severance savings, to dismissal indemnity, to equity income and to benefits in kind alike. Carry a residence flag with an effective date on the employee record, re-evaluate it EVERY period against the art. 10 test (more than 183 days in any 365-day period), and switch to the art. 383 table PROSPECTIVELY from the payment following the date the test is met.'
D22	'Never apply the switch retroactively and never true up the earlier months through payroll - DIAN Concept 011108 of 29 June 2026 expressly rejects anticipating future residence. The employee reconciles on their own return. Where the work is performed ENTIRELY outside Colombia the income is foreign-source and there is no withholding at all; LugarTrabajoPais (NIE050) is the field that signals this. Treaty relief is applied ONLY on written client instruction with a residence certificate.'
A23	'AuxilioTransporte (NIE071)'
B23	'Payroll engine'
C23	"Emit only where the employee's salary does not exceed 2 SMMLV (COP 3,501,810 in 2026). The 2026 amount is COP 249,095 per month, prorated by days worked. Where the employee is above the threshold, OMIT the element rather than emitting zero."
D23	"The allowance is NOT salary for contribution purposes but IS included in the base of cesantias, prima and the severance indemnity (Ley 1 de 1963 art. 7). Carrying a single 'transport' wage type with one base flag will get one of the two treatments wrong."
A24	'Cesantias (NIE120) and PagoIntereses (NIE122)'
B24	'Payroll engine'
C24	"Cesantias accrue at one month's salary per year of service, on salary plus the transport allowance. Interest accrues at 12% per annum on the 31 December balance and is paid TO THE EMPLOYEE during January. Where the employer has adopted the Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 64 option, the 8.33% is deposited monthly and the 1% interest paid monthly, by written agreement - a configurable engine option."
D24	'Severance savings are EXCLUDED from the monthly withholding base but must still be reported here. They split THREE ways downstream - paid to the employee, deposited to the fund, and under the pre-1991 traditional regime - for Form 220 casillas 47-49 and Format 2276 numerals 18-20. Maintain the three buckets from the outset.'
A25	'Meal payments (NIE153 / NIE154)'
B25	'Payroll engine'
C25	'Maintain the ET art. 387-1 split independently of the S / NS split: the amount UP TO 41 UVT per month (COP 2,147,334 in 2026) where salary does not exceed 310 UVT (COP 16,235,940) goes to Format 2276 numeral 32, and the EXCESS goes to numeral 8. Both are still reported in the XML as PagoAlimentacionS or NS.'
D25	'Two orthogonal splits apply to the same amount - salary / non-salary for the XML and contributions, and under-41-UVT / excess for the information return. Model them as two independent attributes, not one.'
A26	'Incapacity (NIE123-NIE127)'
B26	'Payroll engine'
C26	'Emit one Incapacity element per episode with its dates, day count, type code and amount. For type 1 (comun) the employer bears days 1-2 and the EPS funds from day 3; for types 2 and 3 the ARL funds from day one at 100%. The employer ADVANCES the benefit and recovers it from the institution, so the amount here is what the employee received.'
D26	'The first-two-days rate is a configuration constant, default 66.67% (CST art. 227: two-thirds of salary for the first 90 days); the primary source does not state the rate. The one-minimum-wage floor on incapacity pay follows settled practice and is a configuration constant of the incapacity calculation.'
A27	'AltoRiesgoPension (NIE043)'
B27	'Employee master data'
C27	'Set true where the employee performs an activity listed in Decree-Law 2090 of 2003. The flag is always emitted (1-1); write the literal true or false, rather than 1 or 0.'
D27	'The ADDITIONAL employer pension contribution for high-risk work - ten percentage points above the standard rate, borne by the employer (Decree-Law 2090 of 2003 art. 5; the Resolution 2388 rate table shows 26% total against the standard 16%) - and its base are held in the contribution-rate configuration table, maintained from the primary source in force for the year.'
A28	'═══ CROSS-FIELD VALIDATIONS ═══'
A29	'NIE014 / NIE015 and NIE051 / NIE052'
B29	'Engine (validation)'
C29	'The five-digit municipality code must begin with the two-digit department code. Validate this for both the generation location and the place of work before transmission.'
D29	'DIVIPOLA codes are re-used across departments in their last three digits; only the full five-digit code is unique.'
A30	'NIE065 and NIE066 / NIE067 / NIE068'
B30	'Engine (validation)'
C30	'Where the payment method is a bank-mediated medium (42, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49), the bank, account type and account number must all be present. Where it is 10 (Efectivo), all three must be absent.'
D30	'Colombia does NOT use IBAN. Account numbers are numeric, of bank-specific length, with no national check digit, so no structural validation is possible - only a bank-specific rule or pre-notification.'
A31	'NIE199 and NIE204'
B31	'Engine (validation)'
C31	'Where the Change event element is present, CUNENov is mandatory inside it; where Change event is absent, CUNENov must not appear. Bidirectional.'
D31	'A Change event without a referenced CUNE breaks the cross-reference chain for the employment relationship.'
A32	'NIAE214 and the Replace / Delete blocks'
B32	'Engine (validation)'
C32	'TipoNota = 1 requires the Replace block and forbids Delete; TipoNota = 2 requires Delete and forbids Replace. Mutually exclusive.'
D32	'The Delete CUNE uses ValDev = ValDed = ValTol = 0.00 and DocEmp = 0 (Annex s.8.1.1.4). A shared CUNE routine that reads the real totals will produce a wrong hash for cancellations.'
A33	'NIE041 and NIE042 and NIE164'
B33	'Engine (validation)'
C33	'Where SubTipoTrabajador (NIE042) = 01 (employed old-age pensioner), the pension rate NIE164 must be 0.00 and no pension deduction may be emitted.'
D33	'The PILA equivalent (subtipo 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 or 12 forces field 46 to zero) is broader; map, do not copy.'
A34	'NIE157 and NIE061 and NIE041'
B34	'Engine (validation)'
C34	'Where the apprentice maintenance support (NIE157) is populated, the contract type must be 4 (Aprendizaje) and the worker type must be 12 or 19.'
D34	'Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 made the apprenticeship a labour contract from 2026, so an apprentice now also generates a full PILA settlement - not only the support payment.'
A35	'Cross-report reconciliation'
B35	'Engine (reconciliation)'
C35	"Monthly: SUM(NIE177) over all employees must equal Form 350 casillas 93 plus 94 after the nearest-1,000 rounding. SUM(NIE163), SUM(NIE166) and SUM(NIE168 + NIE170) must equal the PILA totals for health, pension and the solidarity fund, allowing for PILA's round-up-to-100. Annually: the accumulation of each Earnings element must equal the corresponding Form 220 casilla and Format 2276 numeral."
D35	'Run the reconciliation BEFORE transmission. A mismatch discovered after validation can only be put right by an adjustment note, which consumes a new document number.'
