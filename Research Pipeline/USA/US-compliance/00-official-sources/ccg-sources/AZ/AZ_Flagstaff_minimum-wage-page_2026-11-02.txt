2026 Minimum Wage
Effective January 1, 2026 Flagstaff's minimum wage is $18.35 per hour. All employees are required to make the full minimum wage, regardless of whether or not they regularly earn tips.
Each year on January 1, the hourly minimum wage will be adjusted according to the Consumer Price Index.
2027 Minimum Wage
Effective January 1, 2027 Flagstaff's minimum wage will be $18.95 per hour. All employees will be required to make the full minimum wage, regardless of whether or not they regularly earn tips.
Background
At the 2016 General Election, Flagstaff voters passed Proposition 414 - "The Minimum Wage Act". This ballot measure established Title 15 of the Flagstaff City Code, which contains all laws for minimum wage within the City of Flagstaff. This law covers all individuals who work or are expected to work 25 hours or more in any given calendar year within the city limit. All covered individuals are to be paid or to earn no less than the current hourly minimum wage.
