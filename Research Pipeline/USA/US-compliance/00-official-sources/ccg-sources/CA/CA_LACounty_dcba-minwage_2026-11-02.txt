 Fair Chance Ordinance 
 Fiscal Year 2026-27 Notice: Pursuant to Los Angeles County (County) Code Section 8.100.040, Minimum Wage Ordinance, the Chief Executive Office (CEO) has determined that a minimum wage increase from $17.81 to $18.47, within the unincorporated areas of the County, is required effective July 1, 2026. The change equates to a 3.7 percent increase and is derived by using the November 2025 Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers for the Los Angeles metropolitan area. This is the fifth increase calculated by the CEO after the original 2015 Minimum Wage Ordinance established scheduled rate increases from July 2017 to July 2021.
