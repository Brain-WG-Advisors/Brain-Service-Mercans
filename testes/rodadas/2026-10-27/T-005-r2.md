## Resposta

Per **Orden PJC/297/2026, de 30 de marzo, Article 3** (BOE núm. 79, 31 March 2026, BOE-A-2026-7296), the contribution bases apply from 1 January 2026. Groups 1–7 are monthly and groups 8–11 are daily:

| Group | Minimum base | Maximum base |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | €1,989.30/month | €5,101.20/month |
| 2 | €1,649.70/month | €5,101.20/month |
| 3 | €1,435.20/month | €5,101.20/month |
| 4–7 | €1,424.40/month | €5,101.20/month |
| 8–11 | €47.48/day | €170.04/day |

Desde el 1 de enero de 2026, la cotización al Régimen General de la Seguridad Social por contingencias comunes estará limitada para cada grupo de categorías profesionales por las bases mínimas y máximas siguientes, which are the figures in the table above.

The order was published late, in March, but it applies retroactively from 1 January. The legal basis is Article 3 of Real Decreto-ley 3/2026, de 3 de febrero. Under that article, minimum bases are fixed automatically at the 2026 minimum wage (Real Decreto 126/2026, de 18 de febrero) increased by one sixth.

Official text: https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7296 (PDF: https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/03/31/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-7296.pdf)

Some secondary sites still show wrong figures, such as €1,381.20 for groups 4–7 or €46.04/day. Those are the 2025 values, so check against the BOE, not those sites.
