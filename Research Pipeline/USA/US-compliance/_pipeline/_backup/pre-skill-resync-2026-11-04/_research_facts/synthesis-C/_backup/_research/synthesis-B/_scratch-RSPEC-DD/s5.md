
#### AK :: 5. reporting_instruments
- **No state withholding return, deposit schedule, annual reconciliation or state W-2 filing**, because there is no wage tax [US-AK-WT-001].
- **Quarterly UI report: TQ01C (2/26)** with wage schedule [US-AKS5-013, US-AKS5-005]:
  - Due by the last day of the month after the quarter (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31), and within 10 days of ceasing business.
  - A zero report is required. The minimum late penalty is 10 USD.
  - Wages are reported in the quarter **paid**.
  - Per employee: SSN, name, reportable wages, **occupational code (6- or 8-digit) and 2-digit geographic code**. These are Alaska-specific DD fields.
  - Employer level: monthly counts of workers in the pay period that includes the 12th; excess wages; a "wages reported to other states" Y/N flag.
  - Corrections go on Form TADJ.
- **Channel:** `file_upload`, a CSV with no headings [US-AKS5-014, A.2], or online keying through the ES Tax portal, or paper.
  - Online filing is **mandatory** with 50+ employees in a quarter, 1M+ taxable wages in the current or prior year, or when a payroll agent files [US-AKS5-005].
  - The CSV layout (rev. 2016-05-17): a header record of 11 columns and a wage detail record of 12 columns (see the row).
- **Registration:** online, by mail or in person with UI Tax. Form TREG; the new-employer rate depends on the declared industry. The blue "Notice to Employees" placard must be posted (Form 07-1012) [US-AKS5-015]. Agent access: POA [US-AK-P5-1-006].
- **New-hire reporting** (AS 25.27.075) [US-AKS5-007, A.3]:
  - Report within **20 days** of hire, rehire or return to work.
  - Fields: employee name, address and SSN; employer name, address and FEIN.
  - Channel: online through myAlaska. Fax is no longer accepted.
  - Multistate election goes through HHS.
  - Penalty: 10 USD per employee, 100 USD if conspiracy.
  - The child-support agency is now named **CSED**. Its ACSESS system with a Business Portal went live 2025-10-06 [US-AKS5-008, A.2].
  - The statute text itself was not read (ACCESS gap).

#### AR :: 5 — reporting_instruments

  - registration on AR-1R; there are three account types: wage, pension, pass-through;
  - new registrations file monthly: **AR941M by the 15th of the following month**, with a zero voucher when nothing was withheld;
  - DFA reclassifies an account to annual (**AR941A by 31 Jan**) when deposits are under USD 1,000;
  - **EFT is mandatory** at an average of USD 20,000 or more per month (ATAP, ACH credit CCD+TXP, ACH debit);
  - the annual reconciliation **AR3MAR is due 28 Feb**, or within 30 days of business termination;
  - penalties: 5% per month up to 35%; interest 10% a year.

  - due **31 Jan** with ARW-3;

  - **mandatory electronic filing at 125 or more employees**;
  - records required: RA, RE, RW, RS, RT, RF and RCS; RO and RU optional; **RV not allowed**;
  - 512-byte records, CR/LF.
- **1099:** combined Fed/State programme (Pub. 1220, state fields 663–748). AR filing threshold USD 2,500 (USD 100 for DIV/INT), or any amount withheld.
- **Quarterly UI wage report** [US-ARP9V-001, A.3]:
  - filed through Tax21 online or on paper (DWS-ARK-209B / 209BR / 209BS);
  - **electronic filing is mandatory at 250 or more employees**;
  - due **30 Apr, 31 Jul, 31 Oct and 31 Jan**, even when no wages were paid;
  - late penalties: the greater of USD 10 or 5%, rising to USD 30 or 15%;
  - liability registration on DWS-ARK-201 by the last day of the second month after liability arises.
- **New hire** [US-ARP9V-001]:
  - report **within 20 days** via Tax21;
  - covers all employees living or working in AR, including out-of-state employers;
  - fields: employee name, address and SSN; employer name, address and FEIN;
  - multistate employers may choose one state (with the HHS letter).
- **Child-support verification of employment:** answer within 30 days [US-ARS5-008].


#### AZ :: 5 — reporting_instruments
- **Withholding returns** (ADOR Employer Withholding Filing Obligations, existing US-AZ-P5-2-005, A.2; read in full this pass):
  - **Deposit schedule**, by average withholding over the prior 4 quarters:
    - under 200: annual, on A1-APR, due 31 January;
    - 200 to 1,500: quarterly;
    - over 1,500: on the same schedule as the federal deposit (monthly, semi-weekly or next business day).

  - **EFT** is mandatory at a liability of 500 or more. No general e-file waiver from 2022.
  - **A1-QRT** is filed quarterly, even with zero liability. Due 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January. Timely payers get 10 extra days (10 May, 10 Aug, 10 Nov, 10 Feb).
  - **A1-R** is the annual reconciliation with the W-2s, due 31 January. A 30-day extension is available for good cause (WTP 11-1).
  - Statute: 43-412(B), annual return due 31 January [US-AZS5-022, A.1].
- **State W-2 format** (ADOR Pub 701, rev. Jan 2025) [US-AZS5-025, A.2, high on text, medium on edition]:
  - Electronic filing is mandatory, as a .txt upload through AZTaxes or AZFSET, or by a transmitter. Optical media is allowed only for attachments.
  - The SSA EFW2 layout is used. The RS record is required for each employee, the RV record is optional, and the AZ state code is 04.
  - 1099s with AZ withholding are filed directly with ADOR (Pub 1220 format, one form type per file).
  - SSNs and EINs must not be truncated.
  - A1-R and A1-APR go electronically through AZFSET (.csv), AZTaxes data entry or a transmitter.
- **Quarterly UI wage report** (DES UC-018): not re-read, because des.az.gov returns 403. Existing rows cover only the tax pages; format and deadline are open.
- **New-hire reporting** (23-722.01) [US-AZS5-012, A.1, high]:
  - Report to DES every hire, rehire or return to work within 20 days. Electronic filers send two transmissions a month, no more than 16 days apart.
  - Report on a W-4 or equivalent with employee name, address and SSN; employer name, address and FEIN; and the first day of paid work.
  - A multistate employer may designate one state. There is no employer penalty.
- **Registration:** Form JT-1 registers both the ADOR withholding account and the DES UI account (existing US-AZ-P5-2-005).
- **POA:** Forms 821-PSC and UIT-1146A (existing US-AZ-P5-2-001/-002).

- **New hires.** DE 34 within 20 days of the start-of-work date; a rehire is reported after a separation of 60 days or more. Fields:
  - employer: EDD account number, FEIN, name, address, contact;

  - Independent contractors go on the DE 542 (paid or contracted 600 or more, within 20 days).

#### FL :: 5 — reporting_instruments
- **Withholding return, deposit schedule, annual reconciliation, state W-2:** not applicable, because there is no income tax [US-FL-001]. There is no state W-2 filing.
- **Quarterly UI report: RT-6 (R. 07/23, on Rule 73B-10.037 F.A.C.).**
  - **Due dates:** 30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, moved to the next business day when the date falls on a weekend or holiday. A report is required even when no tax is due.
  - **Electronic payment** must be initiated by 5 pm ET on the business day before the due date.
  - **Lines:**
    - Line 1: covered employees in the pay period that includes the 12th of each month.
    - Line 2: gross wages.
    - Line 3: excess wages over USD 7,000.
    - Line 4: taxable wages.
    - Line 5: tax.
  - **Wage detail:** name, SSN and gross/taxable wages per employee.
  - **Corrections:** on RT-8A.
  - [US-FLS5-021, US-FLS5-020, A.2/A.3]
- **Electronic mandate.** Employers with 10+ employees in any quarter of the prior state fiscal year (July-June) must e-file and e-pay.
  - Non-electronic report: USD 25 + USD 1 per employee, max USD 300.
  - Non-electronic remittance: USD 25 each.
  - Erroneous or incomplete report, including a bad SSN: the greater of USD 50 or 10% of tax, max USD 300, waivable once in 12 months.
  - An ITIN is not accepted in place of an SSN.
  - [US-FLS5-006 A.1; US-FLS5-024 A.2]
- **Channel: `file_upload` (fixed-length text) or `xml`.** The prior register said the RT-6 layout was not obtained [US-FIL-015]; it is now obtained.
  - **Flat File Web Import, Version 7 (Dec 2023).**
    - Fixed-length text with record types 00 Header (FEIN, RT account), 01 Employer, 02 Employee (optional for RT-6, required for RT-8A), 03 Totals (monthly counts) and 99 Trailer.
    - Currency fields have 2 implied decimals; numeric fields are zero-padded; zip files are refused.
    - Files with 8,000+ records should be sent in the first two weeks of the due month.
    - [US-FLS5-022, A.2, inspected]
  - **XML package `FL_RT_XML_Schemas_v2026 1.0`.**
    - Contents: 9 XSD, a guide, XPath, samples and 4 test cases. It uses the IRS efile Transmission envelope.
    - It is **required above 20,000 employee records**.
    - The header has an optional E-Verify certification block.
    - **G-SPOT:** the archive was extracted and `RT-6 Sample.xml` validates against `RT_FLTransmission.xsd` (lxml: True).
    - [US-FLS5-023, A.2, inspected]
- **Other quarterly forms:** BLS-3020 (Multiple Worksite Report) for multi-location employers; RT-6EW for staff contracted to public or nonprofit educational institutions; RT-6NF for out-of-state taxable wages; RT-7 annual report for domestic-only employers, due 1 January and late after 31 January [US-FLS5-020, US-FLS5-004].
- **New-hire reporting** (State Directory of New Hires, DOR) [US-FLS5-008, A.1]:
  - **Deadline:** within 20 days of the hire date, which is the first day of work for which pay is owed. Electronic filers may instead send two monthly transmissions 12-16 days apart.
  - **Fields:** employee name, address, SSN and date of hire; employer name, address and FEIN; date of birth optional.
  - **Multistate employers** may designate one state.

**Quarterly Employer Tax and Wage Report (NHES)** [US-NHS5-013, A.3, medium-high; NHES page US-NH-005]
