A + A Listen Withholding Tax Forms for 2025 Filing Season Withholding Tax Forms for 2025 Filing Season (Tax Year 2024/2025 and Prior Years) File the FR-900A if you are an annual wage filer whose threshold is less&nbsp;than $200 per year. Note: The 2024 FR-900A is due January 31, 2025. Deposits are due by January 20, 2025, for the preceding calendar year. File the FR-900Q if you are required to pay&nbsp;monthly or quarterly. Beginning January 1, 2019, if you have a household employee, you must obtain an FEIN and report such withholding using the FR-900Q. File the FR-900P if you are making payments other than electronically and less than $5,000. Please indicate, by shading the oval on the payment voucher, if you are a non-wage filer. &nbsp; Form # Title Due Date 2025 FR-900Q MyTax.DC.gov (Instructions) Employer/Payer Withholding Tax Quarterly Return Beginning TY2023, FR-900Q must be filed electronically on MyTax.DC.gov .
