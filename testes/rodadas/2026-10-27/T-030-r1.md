## Resposta

**Turkey (2025): payroll deduction for an employee's private life insurance premium (GVK Art. 63(1)(3))**

**1. Which part of the premium counts**

This applies before any cap is tested. The insurer must be resident and headquartered in Turkey, and the policy must cover the employee, the spouse or minor children.

- **Savings-type life insurance (birikimli hayat):** 50% of the premium paid by the employee counts.
- **Non-savings life (risk/term) and other personal insurance:** death, accident, health, illness, disability, unemployment, maternity, birth and education premiums count at 100%.

**2. Two caps, both applied to the combined total of all deductible premiums**

| Cap | Basis | Cumulative? |
|---|---|---|
| 15% of wage | The total cannot exceed 15% of the wage earned in the month the premium is paid. | No. It is tested month by month against that month's wage, and unused room does not carry over. |
| Annual minimum wage | The total also cannot exceed the annual gross minimum wage, which is 312,066 TL for 2025. | Yes. It is a year-to-date running total. |

- **Wage used for the 15% cap:** the gross total of the continuous taxable payments to the employee, such as salary, premiums, bonuses, social benefits and raises.
- **How the two caps interact:** GİB's own example shows it. The month is capped at 15% of gross wage. Because the January–October deductions plus the November amount stay under 312,066 TL, the annual limit has no effect that month.

**3. Shared cap with employer BES contributions**

Employer contributions to the private pension system (BES) and the Art. 63(1)(3) premiums count together against the same 15% monthly and 312,066 TL annual limits. However, BES contributions paid by either the employer or the employee can never be deducted from the payroll tax base.

**4. Timing**

- **Normal case:** the premium must relate to the month of the wage and be paid by the end of that month.
- **Prepaid amounts:** premiums paid in advance for later months cannot be deducted in the month of payment, even if they fit within 15%.
- **Lump-sum payments:** once the receipt is given to the employer, the premium is divided over the remaining months, counting the month of submission. Each monthly share is then subject to that month's maximum and the annual limit.

**5. Employer-paid premiums**

These are a taxable benefit. They are treated as a net benefit, grossed up and included in the monthly payroll. After that, they are deductible within the same two caps.
