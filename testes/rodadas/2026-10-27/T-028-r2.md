## Resposta

Yes. It applies to private-sector employees, it's calculated on monthly salary (treated as gross in practice), and the employee bears it.

**Scope.** It covers employees of private legal persons who are subject to the Labour Law. Irregular and daily-paid workers are excluded. The law requires a monthly deduction equal to five per ten thousand of the salary of public-sector staff and of employees of private legal persons governed by the Labour Law, except irregular or daily workers.

**Rate and base.** The rate is 5/10,000, which is 0.05%, applied monthly. The statute itself says only "salary" (راتب) and does not define it further. In practice, and per the guidance on the May 2021 Executive Regulations, the base is gross monthly salary: employers deduct 0.05% of the employee's gross salary each month for both public and private sector employees and remit it to the fund. Some older secondary sources write the rate as "0.0005%". That is a typo; the decimal 0.0005 equals 0.05%.

**Who bears it.** The employee bears it as a withholding, and there is no employer-side contribution. The paying entity pays the salary after deducting the percentage, and the Ministry of Finance collects it and remits it to the Fund's account.

**Source.** Law No. 16 of 2018 (Martyrs and Victims Fund), Article 8(1), as replaced by Article 1 of Law No. 4 of 2021, published in Official Gazette No. 8 bis (H) on 3 March 2021.
