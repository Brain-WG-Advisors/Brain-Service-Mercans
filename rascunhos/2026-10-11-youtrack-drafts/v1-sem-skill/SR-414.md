**2. Column V, row 11 (r.1030):** r.1030 is the **PIT actually withheld** from the wage fund of all workers (r.1020), **excluding the PIT on temporary-disability (sick) pay**. It is not rate × base: it is whatever PIT was withheld — 18% general rate, or 5% for Diia City gig specialists if the client is a Diia City resident. Military levy is not included. U11 currently sums the *amounts of wage types subject to PIT* (the base); it should sum the **PIT withheld** wage type(s) for categories 1, 2 and 3, net of the PIT on sick pay. Official controls: 1030 < 1020 (mandatory) and 1030/1020×100 ≤ 18 (warning).

**3. Row 9 (no-data):** the existing answer stands; in the XML it is `ZERO_ZVIT` + `REASON_KOD`. When the "V" is ticked, no other indicator is filled and the reason is mandatory (Derzhstat controls 11–13).
