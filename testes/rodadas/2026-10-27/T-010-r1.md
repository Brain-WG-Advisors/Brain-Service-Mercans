## Resposta

**4DF income code: 157** (income paid to a self-employed person). Per the Income Codes Reference (Annex 2 to the Procedure approved by MinFin Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015, as amended), income paid to a self-employed person is reported under code "157". Columns 3a and 3 (accrued and paid income) are filled with code 157 in column 6. Columns 4a, 4, 5a and 5 (PIT and military levy) are left blank. The STS (DPS Knowledge Base, category 103.25) still requires these payments to be reported with code "157", even though no PIT or military levy is withheld.

**PIT: not due.** Under Tax Code art. 177.8, the payer does not withhold PIT when the FOP provides a copy of the document confirming its registration as a business entity. For single-tax FOPs, art. 165.1.36 also excludes this income from the PIT base.

**Military levy: not due.** The levy's tax base follows the art. 163 PIT base (Tax Code, Section XX, subsection 10, item 16-1, subitem 1.2). An STS letter confirms that a business entity paying income to an FOP does not withhold military levy, provided the FOP supplies a copy of the document confirming its registration. The FOP pays its own levy. For Group 3 single-taxpayers, this is 1% of income under subitem "2" of subitem 1.3, item 16-1, subsection 10 of the Transitional Provisions.

**ESV: not due, subject to one condition.** Law No. 2464-VI, art. 4(1)(1), paragraph 15 excludes the payment from the employer's ESV base only if the works or services match the FOP's activities registered in the Unified State Register (ЄДР). This is confirmed by the DPS Dnipro brochure citing paragraph 15 of art. 4(1)(1) of Law No. 2464. If the services fall outside the registered activities, 22% ESV applies to the civil-contract remuneration under art. 8(5), paragraph 1, of Law No. 2464. The payment also does not go into Annex D1.
