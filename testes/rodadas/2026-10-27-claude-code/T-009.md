# T-009
**Question:** Ukraine: is an employer-paid life insurance premium subject to ESV/USC?

**Answer:**
Yes. Life insurance premiums paid by the employer are subject to ESV (22%). The exclusion list of KMU Resolution No. 1170 (list of payments not subject to ESV, section II, item 2) covers only voluntary medical insurance and non-state pension provision, not life insurance. Our earlier HRBS-12181 position (ESV = No) was wrong.
