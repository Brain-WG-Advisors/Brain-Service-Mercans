Tax Rates
See Line 10 in the TC-40 Line by Line Instructions.
Multiply line 9 by 4.5 percent (.045). If the result is zero or less, enter "0."
Utah has a single tax rate for all income levels, as follows:
Date Range Tax Rate
January 1, 2025 - current 4.5% or .045
January 1, 2024 - December 31, 2024 4.55% or .0455
January 1, 2023 - December 31, 2023 4.65% or .0465
January 1, 2022 - December 31, 2022 4.85% or .0485
January 1, 2018 - December 31, 2021 4.95% or .0495
January 1, 2008 - December 31, 2017 5% or .05
Tax years prior to 2008 Contact us for assistance
