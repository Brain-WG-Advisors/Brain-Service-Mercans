Unemployment Tax Information

As an employer, understanding unemployment insurance (UI) taxes is essential for maintaining compliance and managing your financial obligations. WorkForce West Virginia is here to provide you with the guidance and information you need to navigate UI tax requirements effectively. WorkForce West Virginia offers comprehensive resources and support to help you understand and manage your UI tax obligations. From determining your tax rate to filing quarterly reports and making tax payments, WorkForce West Virginia is here to assist you every step of the way.

Employer Services Hub

Navigate the Unemployment Process

WorkForce West Virginia supports employers through the UI process, promoting compliance and operational continuity.

Electronic Response: SIDES

SIDES streamlines your UI claim management with secure, efficient data exchange with state unemployment agencies.

Disqualification and Appeals

Challenge unfavorable rulings by appealing so you have the opportunity to present evidence and protect your rights.

Claim Audit: BAM

Mandatory claim audits ensure accurate unemployment benefit distribution and protect business interests.

Report UI Fraud

Reporting UI fraud protects your business and ensures benefits reach those in need, maintaining system integrity.

Short-Time Compensation

STC is an alternative to layoffs — employers can retain skilled workers while adjusting to economic downturns.

Employer Services Hub

Work Refusal

If you suspect an individual is receiving unemployment benefits after refusing work, it is your duty to report it.

Tax Filing & Reporting

Submitting accurate wage and contribution reports timely is a crucial aspect of your employer responsibilities.

Defaulted Accounts

Prevent UI tax issues by responding to notices, paying taxes timely, and maintaining accurate records.

Violators List

Employers on the UI Violator’s List may face penalties, legal action, and reputational damage for non-compliance.

Employer Resources

Access tools and resources to simplify workforce management and ensure compliance to keep your business on track.

Unemployment Tax Information FAQ

Visit the FAQ page for answers to common questions about unemployment tax responsibilities and compliance.

File Quarterly Reports or Pay Quarterly Taxes

Visit our Online Unemployment Compensation Employer Contribution System to file your quarterly reports or pay your quarterly taxes.
