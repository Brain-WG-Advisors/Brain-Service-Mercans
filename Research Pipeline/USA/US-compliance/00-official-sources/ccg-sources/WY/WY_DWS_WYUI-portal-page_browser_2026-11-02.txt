Inside the Wyoming Unemployment Insurance (WYUI) Portal, employers have access to a variety of tools and services to manage their accounts efficiently. Here's what you can do:
File and Amend Reports: File your quarterly reports, amend any previously filed reports, and view all submitted quarterly reports, including wages, rates (current and past), payments, balances due, and credits. This includes any necessary Workers' Compensation reporting.
Manage Taxes: Pay your quarterly taxes online or print voucher coupons to pay by check.
Access Correspondence: View all communications related to your accounts, including rate notices and claims documents.
Update Account Information: Easily update your addresses and contact details and manage third-party access, such as adding or removing a CPA or payroll company.
Manage User Access: Create up to five additional logins for other staff members to access the account.
Print Certifications: Request and print your own 940 re-certifications directly from the portal.
Appeal Processes: Appeal claims, benefit charges, and rates online.
Account Closure: If needed, you can also request the closure of their account.
This streamlined portal helps employers manage their unemployment insurance and/or workers' compensation responsibilities more effectively.
