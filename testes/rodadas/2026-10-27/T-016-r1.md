## 1. Statutory anchors

- **Rate table, Art. 43 N°1 LIR.** It is a monthly table expressed in UTM, with eight brackets:
  - up to 13.5 UTM: exempt
  - 13.5–30 UTM: 4%, minus 0.54 UTM
  - 30–50 UTM: 8%, minus 1.74 UTM
  - 50–70 UTM: 13.5%, minus 4.49 UTM
  - 70–90 UTM: 23%, minus 11.14 UTM
  - 90–120 UTM: 30.4%, minus 17.80 UTM
  - 120–310 UTM: 35%, minus 23.32 UTM
  - over 310 UTM: 40%, minus 38.82 UTM

  The SII circular converts the table to pesos by multiplying the UTM figures by the UTM of the relevant month. The 310 UTM / 40% bracket comes from Ley 21.210 and applies from 1 January 2020.
- **Accessory pay accrued in a single period** (Art. 45 inc. 2). It is added to the remuneration of the month in which it is paid, and the tax is computed on that month's total.
- **Art. 46 reliquidation.** This covers differences, arrears, or accessory/complementary pay accrued over more than one period and paid late. These amounts are converted to UTM and placed in the periods they belong to, and each period is reliquidated at that period's UTM. The resulting tax balances are expressed in UTM and paid at the UTM of the month the remuneration is paid. The detailed procedure is in Circular 37/1990.
- **Who performs it.** The paying company performs the Art. 46 reliquidation.
- **Art. 47 annual reliquidation.** This is done by the taxpayer in their annual return (F22) when they had several employers or other income. The employer's role is limited to issuing the certificate the taxpayer needs for that return. It is not a payroll calculation.

## 2. Calculation steps

**A. Regular monthly IUSC (every pay run)**
1. Start with Art. 42 N°1 taxable gross. Exclude:
   - non-income items (asignación familiar, colación, movilización and similar)
   - exempt income
   - DL 889 zona extrema amounts
   - representation expenses (these are not part of the IUSC base)
2. Subtract the worker's mandatory and/or voluntary pension and health contributions. Within that:
   - The mandatory contributions are those on the capped imponible base.
   - APV/depósitos convenidos are deductible within the DL 3.500 Art. 20 limits. Depósitos convenidos above 900 UF per year are treated as taxable accessory income.
3. The result is the renta líquida imponible (RLI) for the payment month.
4. Compute IUSC = RLI × rate − rebate, using the bracket limits and rebate valued at the payment month's UTM.
5. Treat the Art. 88 voluntary extra withholding as a separate wage type, reported separately.
6. Special populations each need their own branch, to be confirmed in or out of scope: agricultural workers, eventual/discontinuous workers paid per shift, and Art. 52 bis authorities.

**B. Art. 46 reliquidation (triggered when an accessory payment, difference or arrears relates to more than one month)**
1. Allocate the amount across its accrual months. SII Ord. 452/2008 does this proportionally, as an equal split.
2. Convert each monthly share to UTM at the payment month's UTM.
3. Reconvert that UTM amount to pesos at each accrual month's UTM.
4. Add the reconverted amount to that month's original RLI.
5. Recompute IUSC for each accrual month using that month's table.
6. For each month, take the difference between the recomputed tax and the tax already withheld.
7. Convert each difference to UTM at that accrual month's UTM, then sum the UTM amounts.
8. Tax due = total UTM × the payment month's UTM. It is withheld in the payment month and declared in that month's F29.

This is exactly the worked example in Ord. 452/2008 (Anexo N°1).

**C. Reporting attribution**
- On the Certificado N°6, the reliquidated income and tax are recorded in the accrual months, added to the tax already withheld in those months, without any readjustment.
- On DJ 1887, accessory pay accrued in year N and paid up to April of N+1 belongs to year N. Its IUSC goes in a separate box from the IUSC on income accrued and paid within the year.
- Payment after April of N+1: **NOT LOCATED** in this pass.

## 3. Accumulators (proposed structure)

| Scope | Accumulator | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Payment month | Taxable gross; deductible contributions; RLI; IUSC regular; IUSC Art. 46 difference (UTM and CLP); Art. 88 extra withholding | F29, payslip |
| Payment month | Renta no gravada; renta exenta; DL 889 rebate | DJ 1887 annex columns |
| **Accrual month** (keyed by period, not by pay date) | RLI attributed (original + Art. 46 additions); IUSC attributed (original + Art. 46 differences) | Art. 46 recalculation base; Cert 6 |
| Reference tables | UTM per month; SII updating factors per month | Steps A4, B2–B8; annual updating |
| YTD (calendar year) | Renta neta pagada, IUSC, Art. 88, no gravada, exenta and DL 889, each held historic and updated | DJ 1887 Section B |
| YTD split | IUSC accrued and paid in year N vs. IUSC accrued in N and paid Jan–Apr N+1 | DJ 1887 unadjusted-totals box |
| Monthly, unadjusted | Monthly income amount = renta neta + DL 889 per month (C3 + C9, not updated) | DJ 1887 monthly column |

The structurally critical point is the accrual-month layer. Art. 46 needs each month's original RLI and the tax actually withheld in that month. A design that keeps only payment-month totals cannot perform the recalculation.

**Open item (not verified in this pass):** whether the amount allocated in Art. 46 should be taken net of its own contributions, with the cap applied per accrual month. That depends on the pension-side treatment of accessory pay. It needs a separate source check before it goes into the design.
