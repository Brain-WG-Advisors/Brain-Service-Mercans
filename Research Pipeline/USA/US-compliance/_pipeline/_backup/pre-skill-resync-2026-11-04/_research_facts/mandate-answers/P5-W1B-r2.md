## PA - US-MQ-58 (open: part 1 rounding)
1. Wage income tax: rounding of the income tax withholding amount is ABSENT from the whole PA PIT Guide withholding chapter (120,252 chars, both segments read; US-PAP52-001), from 61 Pa. Code 113.1 and 113.4 (US-PAP52-002) and from REV-415 (US-PA-009). The only rounding rule PA publishes for payroll withholding is the UC employee contribution: 0.07% from 2023, three decimals, excess dropped, rounded to the nearest whole cent (US-PAP52-003). ## OH - US-MQ-55 (open: 1, 2, 5, 6)
1. Rounding: the official computation documents (Optional Computer Formula Rev. 09/25 eff. 2025-10-01, 2026 Filing Guidelines Rev. 11/25, 2026 percentage-method PDF) contain no rounding instruction (US-OHP52-001). Figures from the formula: TW = pay x PP - 650 x exemptions; WD = TW x 0.01775 / PP up to 26,050; ((TW-26,050) x 0.02990 + 462.39)/PP to 100,000; ((TW-100,000) x 0.03640 + 2,673.50)/PP above (effective 2025-10-01; 2026 tables effective 2026-08-01 per US-OH-002). Municipal documents: rounding to the nearest dollar is OPTIONAL, ORC 718.25 (effective 2015-03-23; US-OHP52-002). STATUS: absence_proven for the state withholding amount; answered for municipal returns; confidence medium. Residual risk: OAC 5703-7-10 (codes.ohio.gov unreachable) not read.
2. Local: municipal wage tax is levied under ORC ch. 718, withheld by an employer located or doing business in the taxing municipality on qualifying wages earned there at the municipal rate (718.03(A), eff. 2016-09-14), with the 20-day occasional-entrant rule (718.011, US-OH-008); remitted to the municipal tax administrator (CCA, RITA or the city), monthly if prior-year withholding exceeded 2,399 USD or a month of the preceding quarter exceeded 200 USD, else quarterly; municipalities may require semimonthly above 11,999 USD and electronic payment (US-OHP52-003). Rate limit 1% without voters' approval, higher pre-2015 rates grandfathered (718.04). School district income tax is withheld on the state side (SD 101 / SD 141, US-OH-009). Not the state return: municipal tax is administered by each municipality or agent; net-profit filers may elect the Tax Commissioner (718.80). Individual municipal rates NOT listed (hundreds of municipalities; not in scope of a state dossier; The Finder / city pages). STATUS: answered (structure, administrator, thresholds); confidence high for the statute, rate catalogue out of scope.
5. Multistate: employer does not withhold for residents of MI, IN, KY, WV, PA working in Ohio (US-OHP52-006, chrome-read, plus US-OHP51-002). Resident credit (IT RC) only for tax paid to another of the 50 states or DC; no credit for wages of IN, KY, MI, PA, WV earned while an Ohio resident; nonresident credit (IT NRC) allocates compensation by location of the services or by Ohio days / total days (R.C. 5747.20) (US-OHP52-004, TY2025 booklet). No employer-side day or dollar threshold exists in the Ohio guidelines. STATUS: answered; confidence medium-high (ORC 5747.05 text itself not read).
6. POA / third-party filer: TBOR 1 Rev. 11/23 read in full (US-OHP52-005): per tax type and account, up to three years (one if undated), cannot sign taxpayer-only forms. Pay statement ORC 4113.14 and prevailing wage ORC ch. 4115 as round 1. ODJFS UI power of attorney JFS 20107 and WT-8655: STILL NOT opened from an official page (jfs.ohio.gov URL 404; thesource.jfs.ohio.gov blocked by Chrome domain check); only third-party sites name JFS 20107, which is not accepted as evidence. STATUS: partial.
