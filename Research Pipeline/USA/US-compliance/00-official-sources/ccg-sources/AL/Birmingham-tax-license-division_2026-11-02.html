Tax and License Division

File Taxes OnlineRenew Business Licenses OnlineSchedule Of Business Licenses

The Tax and License Administration Division is responsible for collecting license fees and other revenue for the City of Birmingham and ensuring compliance with all license and tax requirements pursuant to the City of Birmingham’s Tax Ordinances.
 

Office Hours

Office hours are from 8:00 a.m. until 5:00 p.m., weekdays, Monday through Friday

Office location

Room TL-100 (First Floor) City Hall
710 North 20th Street, Birmingham, AL 35203

Applications For Business licenses

Applications for new, “non-controlled” business licenses are taken daily during office hours at the Division’s office located in Room TL-100, City Hall.

Applications for “controlled” licenses, such as alcoholic beverage, pool table, and dance licenses, are taken by appointment only. For a list of documents required to process an application for a new business, please see the Supplemental Documentation Checklist.

 

Additional Information On Controlled Or Regulated License Applications

The City of Birmingham’s new business tax and license system

As the City of Birmingham continues to strengthen its systems of engagement with local businesses, the Tax and License Division is introducing a new, more user-friendly online experience. We are pleased to announce our partnership with HdL Companies, Inc. to provide a new tax management process for the taxpayer community.

Online options are provided to file returns, make payments, and submit account updates for the following taxes:

  • Franchise Tax
  • Diesel Tax
  • Lease/Rental Tax
  • Liquor Tax (Wine and Non-Wine)
  • Lodging Tax
  • Occupational Tax
  • Sales Tax
  • Use Tax (Consumers/Sellers)

File And Pay These Taxes Online

This is the City’s preferred method of receiving your tax filings and payments.

We are here to help you! If you have any questions, please e-mail us at BhamTLSupport@birminghamal.gov.
