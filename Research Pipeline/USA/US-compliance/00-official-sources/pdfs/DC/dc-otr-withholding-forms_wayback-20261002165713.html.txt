Note: The 2026&nbsp;FR-900A is due January 31, 2027. Deposits are due by January 20, 2027, for the preceding calendar year. File the FR-900Q if you are required to pay&nbsp;monthly or quarterly. Beginning January 1, 2019, if you have a household employee, you must obtain an FEIN and report such withholding using the FR-900Q. File the FR-900P if you are making payments other than electronically and less than $5,000.
