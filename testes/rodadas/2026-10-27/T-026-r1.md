I can't confirm the code from what I have access to here. The Bulgaria Regulation Design file isn't attached, and your Mercans project chats can't be searched from outside that project.

The closest thing I found is the BG WTC V2.0 review from April. That review did not identify a standalone TZPB wage type. The SS elements it referenced were 2556 (EE DOO), 2566 (ER DOO), 2517 (EE DZPO) and 2537 (ER DZPO). So the TZPB rate is either inside the ER composite (most likely 2566) or in a separate ER element that wasn't part of that review. I'd rather not guess at a 95%+ standard.
