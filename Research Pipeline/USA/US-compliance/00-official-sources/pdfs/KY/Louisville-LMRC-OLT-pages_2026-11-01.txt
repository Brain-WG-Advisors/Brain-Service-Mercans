Withholding Calculation:
"Line 1: Enter the gross wages paid to all employees for work that was performed within Louisville Metro, Kentucky. (Excludes amounts earned by ordained ministers)
Line 1 (a): Multiply Line 1 by .0145 (Louisville Metro Tax 1.25% (.0125) and Transit Authority Tax .2% (.0020))
Line 2: Determine what portion of the total Louisville Metro, Kentucky wages (entry on Line 1) were paid to employees who did not live in Louisville Metro ...
Line 3: Subtract Line 2 from Line 1. ... total wages paid to employees who lived in Louisville Metro ...
Line 5: ... This is the total payroll subject to the School Boards Tax.
Line 5 (a): Multiply wages subject to the school boards tax (Line 5) by .0075.
Line 6: Add Line 1(a) and Line 5(a). This represents the total tax due."
Line 8 (tips): "Resident Tax Rate = 2.2%, Non-resident Tax Rate = 1.45% ... This tax should be paid by the employee with tip income. They are required to file and pay a Form OL-3 ... for the tax shortage owed."

=== URL: https://louisvilleky.gov/government/revenue-commission/forms-and-publications ===
"Employers are required to withhold Occupational License Fees/Taxes from the gross salaries, wages, commissions, and other forms of compensation earned by employees for work performed within Louisville/Jefferson County."
"Form OL-3 ... to file and pay the Occupational Tax Rate of 2.2% (resident rate) or 1.45% (non-resident rate)."
Forms: W-1 Quarterly Withholding Return (due last day of month after quarter); W-1D Monthly Deposit (withholding > $3,000 in any quarter; due 15th after month); W-3 Annual Reconciliation + W-2 data (due February 28); W-1KJC (KY jobs credit); I-2; W-1REE.

"Employers are subject to a penalty of five percent (5%) per month ... to a maximum of twenty-five percent (25%) of the total license tax liability, plus interest at a rate of twelve percent (12%) per annum ..." (W-1 late)
"... if you have employees ... you are required to withhold and remit the occupational license tax on wages, which is filed quarterly on form W-1."

Finding: the Louisville Metro occupational license tax on wages = Metro 1.25% + Transit Authority (TARC) 0.20% (all workers in Louisville Metro, 1.45%) + School Boards 0.75% (residents only) = 2.2% resident. All components are withheld from employee compensation; no employer-paid part is stated.
