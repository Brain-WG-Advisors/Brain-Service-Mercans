DECEMBER 23, 2025

2026 Employer Tax Rates Available Now

The 2026 Annual Tax Rate Notices, DOL-626, are now available on the Employer Portal. An email will be sent to the email addresses on record. The notices will NOT be mailed.

The Employer Portal is the preferred method for employers to manage their unemployment insurance (UI) tax accounts. Employers may view employer account information (such as tax rates, account status, and payment details), file Quarterly Tax and Wage Reports, submit payments, update employer addresses, file employer-filed claims, and email the Field Tax Representative assigned to their area.

Employers who are not already registered should select Employer Portal, follow the instructions to register, and begin using the preferred method to manage the employer account. Employers can download the Administrator Guide from the Employer Portal login page for instructions on completing the registration.
