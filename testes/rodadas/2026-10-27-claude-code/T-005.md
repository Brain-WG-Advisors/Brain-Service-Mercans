**Question:** Spain 2026, General Regime: minimum contribution bases for groups 1, 2, 3, 4-7 (monthly) and 8-11 (daily) from 1 January 2026, and legal source?

**Answer (from 2026-01-01):**
- Group 1: EUR 1,989.30/month
- Group 2: EUR 1,649.70/month
- Group 3: EUR 1,435.20/month
- Groups 4-7: EUR 1,424.40/month
- Groups 8-11: EUR 47.48/day

Legal source: Orden PJC/297/2026, de 30 de marzo (BOE-A-2026-7296), art. 3 (bases by group). Art. 2.2 sets the AT/EP minimum at SMI + 1/6, not below 1,424.40.
