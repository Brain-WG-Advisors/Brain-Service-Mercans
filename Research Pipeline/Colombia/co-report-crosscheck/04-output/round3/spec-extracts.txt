--- row 21 | None | ═══ payroll withholding - the two casillas that payroll owns(sheet 1, 77 and 93) ═══
--- row 22 | 77 | Base - work income (natural persons)
   Format: N (whole pesos, nearest 1,000)
   System Mapping: SUM(Payroll.Taxable_Base_Art388)
   Field Explanation: THE PRINCIPAL PAYROLL CASILLA ON THE BASE SIDE. The instruction guide defines it as 'los payments gravables (base sujeta a withholding) efectuados a natural persons por concept de salaries, commissions, prestaciones sociales, travel allowances, representation expenses, fees, emolumentos eclesiasticos, compensation received por el trabajo cooperative associate, payments en especie, payments en acciones y, en general, las compensation for personal services'. It is the DEPURATED taxable base of RESIDENT natural persons, not the gross: the ET art. 388 base after non-constitutive income, art. 387 deductions and the 25% exempt income - the base on which the casilla-93 withholding is computed. Non-resident foreign natural persons are NOT in this casilla: their payments are reported in the 'Pagos al exterior' section as sheet-2 rows (casillas 141-146) with the sheet-2 payment concept and country, at the nominal (gross) value, at the 20% rate of ET art. 408 and with no depuration; the employee's tax-residence flag decides the section. Pension income paid by the employer is not in this casilla either: it goes to casilla 78. Note also that the casilla sweeps in fees paid to natural persons under the art. 383 table, which are not employment income - payroll supplies only the employment component.
   Rounding: Whole pesos per employee; round only the aggregated monthly casilla total to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577) - applies to every money casilla of the return.
--- row 23 | 93 | Withholding - work income (natural persons)
   Format: N (whole pesos, nearest 1,000)
   System Mapping: SUM(Payroll.Income_Tax_Withheld)
   Field Explanation: THE PRINCIPAL PAYROLL CASILLA ON THE WITHHOLDING SIDE. Sum of the income-tax withholding at source actually practised on work income paid to RESIDENT natural persons in the calendar month, across EVERY pay group and EVERY payroll run of the entity in that month - regular, weekly or exceptional-advance, termination and settlement runs alike - in whole pesos, then rounded once to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577). Source: wage type 2552 'Income Tax (employee)' (Deductions), plus wage type 21330 'Personal Income Tax Withholding (Theoretical)' where it carries the withholding actually deducted from the employee's pay (the case in which the WT 2552 line is a zero-value Non Cash item). The amount is the withholding actually deducted from the employee's pay, computed with the employee's AFC and voluntary-pension contributions (ET arts. 126-1 and 126-4) taken into account; never the figure computed without those contributions. Contingent withholding (ET arts. 126-1 / 126-4) - the difference between the withholding computed without and with those contributions - is practised by the AFC or voluntary-pension fund when the savings are withdrawn before the permanence requirement is met; it is not practised by the employer and does not enter this return, and the wage types that carry a theoretical or hypothetical amount never deducted - 21114 'Theoretical income Tax-5011', 21619 'Theoretical Income Tax - Ajuste - 5035' and 25834 'Hypothetical Witholding Tax  - IC Foreign Country' - are out of scope: a theoretical or hypothetical amount is not withholding actually practised. WT 2552 withholding deducted in advance runs (WT 61100 'Weekly advance', WT 62838 'Exceptional Advance') and in termination or settlement runs (WT 62116, 62110, 62203, 62115) enters in the month it is practised, whatever the run. Withholding deducted on WT 21129 (Terminations), 25832 (Stock awards vesting) and 25833 (IC Local Country) enters in the month it is practised. Whether a wage type carries withholding actually practised, and therefore feeds casilla 93, follows from the withholding classification of each wage type, which comes from the Wage Type Catalogue (WTC) attribute; the wage types named here are the ones so classified. Withholding on the pension income the employer pays itself (WT 21140 'Personal Income Tax Withholding-Retirees- 6799', WT 21155 'Income Tax Pensioner') is not reported here: it goes to casilla 94, with its base in casilla 78. Withholding at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on a NON-RESIDENT foreign natural person is not reported here: it is supplied as sheet-2 rows (casillas 141-146) in the 'Pagos al exterior' section, with the sheet-2 payment concept and country, on the nominal (gross) value and with no depuration, and rolls into casillas 111 / 112; the employee's tax-residence flag, not the withholding rate or the tax-procedure code, decides the section. Accumulate by the payroll engine's run key (pay group and run), not by a report-level identifier. Casilla 93 plus casilla 94 must reconcile to the sum of RetencionFuente (NIE177) across every electronic payroll document for the same month, after the nearest-1,000 rounding. It is one of the forty-two casillas summed into casilla 130.
   Rounding: Whole pesos per employee; round only the aggregated monthly casilla total to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577) - applies to every money casilla of the return.
--- row 24 | 78 | Base - pension income (natural persons)
   Format: N (whole pesos, nearest 1,000)
   System Mapping: SUM(Payroll.Pension_Taxable_Base)
   Field Explanation: Taxable base of the pension income the employer pays itself: the part of the monthly pension EXCEEDING 1,000 UVT (COP 52,374,000 in 2026) under ET art. 206 num. 5, for retirement, invalidity, old-age, survivor and occupational-risk pensions and for substitute indemnities and returns of pension savings. Only the excess over 1,000 UVT is taxable, so the casilla is nil for a pension at or below that amount. It is the base on which the casilla-94 withholding (WT 21140, WT 21155) is computed; the pension wage types behind it are those classified as pension income, and the pension-income classification of each wage type (casilla 78 rather than casilla 77) comes from the Wage Type Catalogue (WTC) attribute. Pension income is not part of casilla 77, and a pension paid by a fund is reported by the fund, not here.
   Rounding: Whole pesos, then rounded to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577) - applies to every money casilla of the return.
--- row 25 | 94 | Withholding - pension income (natural persons)
   Format: N (whole pesos, nearest 1,000)
   System Mapping: SUM(Payroll.Pension_Tax_Withheld)
   Field Explanation: Withholding actually practised on the pension income the employer pays itself, on the taxable excess over 1,000 UVT: wage type 21140 'Personal Income Tax Withholding-Retirees- 6799' and wage type 21155 'Income Tax Pensioner', in the month it is deducted, in whole pesos, then rounded once to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577). Summed into casilla 130.
   Rounding: Whole pesos, then rounded to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577) - applies to every money casilla of the return.
