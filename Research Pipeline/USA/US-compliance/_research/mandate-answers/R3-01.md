## AK - part 2 local wage taxes: ABSENCE PROVEN (confidence Medium-High)
- Exist? No. Alaska Const. art. X sec. 2 vests all local-government powers in boroughs and cities and lets the State delegate taxing authority only to them; the Legislative Research Services report lists only property tax (AS 29.45.010) and sales tax (AS 29.45.700) as municipal taxing powers [US-AKR3-002, US-AKR3-003].
- The Office of the State Assessor (DCRA) page enumerates every type of local tax levied (property, general sales, raw fish, bed, severance, liquor/tobacco, gaming, fuel transfer) and adds "There is no personal state income tax"; no wage or payroll tax is listed [US-AKR3-001].
- Who collects: not applicable (nothing levied).
- Limits: no express statutory prohibition on municipal income tax was found (AS 29.35.865 bars an income tax only for an "authority", not a municipality); the evidence is a closed list of the taxes levied plus the delegated-powers tables. Home-rule municipalities hold residual powers (LRS tables), so the absence is "none levied", not "none permitted".
- register_id: US-AKR3-001, US-AKR3-002, US-AKR3-003 (all `active`).

## AL - part 4 SDI/TDI/PFML/LTC/other levies: ABSENCE PROVEN (confidence Medium)
- Employee side: "no unemployment compensation taxes - federal or state - are withheld from employees in Alabama. The employer pays all taxes involved." [US-ALR3-001, handbook p. 67].
- Employer side: the UC Employer Handbook ch. 2 lists the state employer taxes as the UC tax (new employer 2.70%) with the 0.06% Employment Security Enhancement Assessment (ESA) folded into the computed rate; nothing else [US-ALR3-001].
- Scope pages that would list a disability/leave programme and do not: Workforce employers program list [US-ALR3-002]; ADOR tax types and Business and License division [US-ALR3-003]. Federal DOL TDI list (CA, HI, NJ, NY, RI) [US-FEDP52-007] corroborates.
- Limits: the handbook is rev. 2021 (Wayback copy 2026-01-13); the Workforce page was read by WebFetch only (Chrome denied for workforce.alabama.gov), summariser output; no Alabama statute-chapter index was reachable on an official host. Rate figures (2.70%, ESA 0.06%) are the handbook's, not re-verified for 2026.
- register_id: US-ALR3-001, US-ALR3-002, US-ALR3-003.

## AZ
### Part 2 local wage taxes: ABSENCE PROVEN (confidence Medium-High)
ADOR Pub 10 "Summary of Arizona Taxes" (Feb 2022) lists the taxes administered: personal income tax, small business income tax, TPT (state/county and city), use taxes, general property tax, estate tax, vehicle license tax. The only city-level taxes named are TPT and use tax; no city, county or school-district wage or income tax [US-AZR3-006]. Collected by: n/a. ### Part 3 SUI: ANSWERED (confidence High)
- Agency: Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES), Unemployment Tax Office [US-AZR3-001, US-AZR3-003].
- 2026 taxable wage base: 8,000 USD (7,000 before 2023-01-01) [US-AZR3-001, US-AZR3-003, UIT-0603A form rev. 12/25].
- New-employer rate: 2.00% for at least 2 calendar years [US-AZR3-002, US-AZR3-003]. 2026 experience rates: positive groups 0.03% to 4.18%, negative groups 4.41% to 8.36% (ARS 23-730) [US-AZR3-003].
- Employee share: none. "State unemployment taxes pay for unemployment benefits only. You cannot take these taxes out of employee pay." [US-AZR3-001]; "workers in Arizona do not make any payments into the UI Trust Fund" [US-AZR3-005].
- Watch item: Unemployment Insurance Operating Fund effective **2027-01-01** (3.15% of UI liability, offset by an equal reduction, experience-rated employers only) - not a 2026 parameter [US-AZR3-004].

### Part 4 SDI/TDI/PFML/LTC/other levies: ABSENCE PROVEN (confidence Medium)

- Supplemental wages: no flat supplemental rate in the statute or in OTR FR-230 (2015, 2016, 2018 read); "bonuses and back pay are considered as wage payments for the purpose of withholding", i.e. regular method [US-DCR3-002, US-DCR3-001].
