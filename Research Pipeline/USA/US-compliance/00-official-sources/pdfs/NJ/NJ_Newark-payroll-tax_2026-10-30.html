Payroll Tax

Newark’s Payroll Tax is equal to 1% of wages for services performed within the City of Newark. This applies to services supervised from Newark or for wages of employees who primarily report to a Newark location.

Employers whose workforce is more than 50% Newark residents may qualify for a reduced payroll tax of 0.50%, provided documentation is submitted verifying employee residency.

Payroll tax returns are filed quarterly and are due by the 30th day following the end of each quarter (April, July, October, and January).
