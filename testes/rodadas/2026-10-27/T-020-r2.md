## Resposta

**Total Whk premium for a small employer in sector 42 (Groothandel II), 2026: 1.45%**, made up of 0.91% for the WGA component and 0.54% for the ZW-flex component.

In the Handboek Loonheffingen 2026, these rates appear in Tabel 10 Sectorale premies voor de gedifferentieerde premie Whk (voor kleine werkgevers), on p. 518. The rules themselves are in §7.6, under the heading "U bent een kleine werkgever".

The governing instrument is the UWV's Besluit gedifferentieerde premie Whk 2026 (BWBR0051245). Its Bijlage "Sectorale premies 2026" lists sector 42, Groothandel II: WGA 0,91, ZW 0,54.

A small employer is one whose total premieplichtig loon over 2024 was no higher than € 1.082.500.

Source: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0051245/2026-01-01/0 (Bijlage Sectorale premies 2026)
