## Statutory basis

- **Base and scale:** Art. 42 N°1 and Art. 43 N°1 LIR. The scale is set in UTM. It is converted to pesos by multiplying the bracket limits and the deduction amount by the UTM value of the month in question.
- **Single-period accessory pay:** Art. 45 inc. 2, first part. This covers pay earned within one normal pay period, such as monthly bonuses, overtime and monthly gratificación. It is added to the pay of the month in which it is received, and the tax is calculated on the total paid that month.
- **Multi-period accessory pay (reliquidación):** Art. 46 inc. 2, with the procedure in Circular N° 37/1990. Differences or balances of pay, or accessory pay earned over more than one period and paid late, are converted into UTM and placed in the periods they belong to. They are reliquidated using the UTM value of each of those periods. The resulting tax balances are expressed in UTM and paid at that unit's value in the month the pay is made.
- **Who does it:** the paying company is responsible for the Art. 46 reliquidation.
- **Annual reliquidation (Art. 47):** this is not the employer's job. Workers with simultaneous income from more than one employer must reliquidate annually. They can also ask any employer for a higher withholding under the final paragraph of Art. 88, which counts as a voluntary provisional payment and is declared on F29 code 48.

## Parameters (monthly, with full history)

Store these as time-dependent tables keyed by month. Reliquidation reaches back into past months, including prior calendar years. In the SII example, a July 2007 payment was reliquidated across May 2006 to July 2007.

- **UTM per month.** For example, October 2026 is 72,151 and September 2026 is 71,721.
- **IUSC scale in UTM:** exempt up to 13.5; 13.5–30 at 4% minus 0.54; 30–50 at 8% minus 1.74; 50–70 at 13.5% minus 4.49; 70–90 at 23% minus 11.14; 90–120 at 30.4% minus 17.80; 120–310 at 35% minus 23.32; above 310 at 40% minus 38.82. SII also publishes fortnightly, weekly and daily versions, so the table should be keyed by pay frequency.
- **Separate Art. 52 bis table.** Keep it as its own table and never default to it. It applies only to the President, ministers, undersecretaries, senators and deputies above 150 UTM, and its top bracket starts at 150 UTM.
- **Contribution cap by month, in UF.** The cap changed during 2026: 89.9 UF in January, then 90 UF from 1 February. A reliquidation that touches January must use 89.9.
- **APV Regime B cap:** payroll-deducted APV comes off the IUSC base up to 50 UF per month.

## Accumulators (per employee, per tax month M = devengo month)

| Accumulator | Content |
|---|---|
| `CL_IUSC_GROSS[M]` | Taxable pay attributed to M (ordinary + single-period accessory + Art. 46 portions allocated to M). Excludes non-renta items. |
| `CL_IUSC_SS_DED[M]` | Worker's mandatory contributions for M, capped at M's limit: AFP, legal 7% health, 0.6% cesantía. |
| `CL_IUSC_APVB[M]` | APV-B deducted via payroll (≤ 50 UF) |
| `CL_IUSC_RLI[M]` | GROSS − SS_DED − APVB |
| `CL_IUSC_TAX_CALC[M]` | Tax per M's table on RLI[M] |
| `CL_IUSC_TAX_WHT[M]` | Tax already withheld for M, cumulative across the original run and all prior reliquidations |
| `CL_IUSC_UTM[M]` | UTM snapshot used for M |
| `CL_IUSC_RELIQ_UTM[P]` | Sum of UTM differences settled in payment month P |
| `CL_IUSC_ART88_EXTRA[P]` | Voluntary extra withholding. Kept separate because it goes to F29 code 48, not the IUSC line. |

## Calculation steps

**A. Ordinary month (Art. 43 N°1 + Art. 45 inc. 2)**
1. Sum the period's taxable pay plus any single-period accessory pay.
2. Deduct mandatory worker contributions (capped) and APV-B.
3. Calculate tax as RLI × rate − (deduction in UTM × UTM of the month), using the table for the pay frequency.
4. Write GROSS, RLI, TAX_CALC and TAX_WHT for M.

**B. Art. 46 reliquidation (payment month P, amount A earned over M1…Mn)**
1. Allocate A to its devengo months. SII's worked example splits the amount evenly across the months.
2. Convert each portion to UTM at UTM(P).
3. Convert back to CLP at UTM(Mi).
4. Add to RLI(Mi). Recalculate the portion's contributions under Mi's cap first. On the contribution side, differences in both contributions and tax are paid in the month the reliquidated pay is made, without fines.
5. Recalculate tax(Mi) using Mi's table.
6. Diff(Mi) = new tax(Mi) − TAX_WHT(Mi).
7. Convert Diff(Mi) to UTM at UTM(Mi).
8. Sum the UTM differences, multiply by UTM(P), and withhold in P. In the SII example, the 4.65 UTM difference was converted at the payment month's UTM and paid on that month's F29.
9. Write back GROSS, RLI, TAX_CALC and TAX_WHT for each Mi. A later reliquidation on the same month must start from the updated withheld amount, not the original one.

P's own ordinary tax (step A) stays separate. If P is also a devengo month, its portion goes through step B like any other month, with a conversion factor of 1.

**C. Year-end reporting (no employer recalculation)**
DJ 1887 and Certificado N° 6 report amounts by devengo month. Accessory pay earned over more than one normal pay period is reported in those periods, adjusted under the Art. 46 inc. 2 procedure. This only works if the accumulators are keyed by M, not by P.

## Design guardrails

- **Classification:** each accessory wage type in the WTC needs a single-period or multi-period flag. Multi-period wage types need devengo-from and devengo-to as mandatory inputs. If those are missing, the run should flag it rather than silently fall back to Art. 45. Getting the classification wrong is the most common error; the SII has reversed itself on exactly this question (Ord. 452/2008 reconsidered Oficio 2.595/2007).
- **Rounding:** SII's example carries UTM to 2 decimals per month. I'd treat that as the reference behaviour, not a legal rule, and confirm it with the client or vendor before hard-coding.
