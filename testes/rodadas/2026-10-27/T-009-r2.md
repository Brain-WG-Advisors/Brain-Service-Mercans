## Resposta

**Yes. An employer-paid life insurance premium for an employee is subject to ESV.** This includes long-term life insurance contracts.

**Basis:**

- **Instruction on Wage Statistics No. 5 (Derzhkomstat order of 13.01.2004), subpara. 2.3.4 of para. 2.3, section 2:** employer insurance premiums paid for employees under voluntary insurance (personal or property) count as "other incentive and compensation payments". That makes them part of the payroll fund, which is the ESV base. They enter the payroll fund in the month the payment is transferred to the insurer.
- **Para. 3.5 of Instruction No. 5:** the only insurance premiums carved out of the payroll fund are employer contributions under voluntary medical and pension insurance contracts for employees and their family members. Life insurance is not covered by this carve-out.
- **CMU Resolution No. 1170 of 22.12.2010 (List of payments not subject to ESV), Section II, item 2:** only payments under voluntary medical and pension insurance contracts for employees and their families are excluded. Life insurance is not on the List.
- **State Tax Service position, ZIR category 201.04.01:** premiums the employer pays under long-term life insurance contracts for employees form part of salary and are subject to ESV, while employer contributions under non-state pension insurance are not. The same answer was published by GU DPS in Ternopil region in April 2024 (official PDF): https://tr.tax.gov.ua/data/material/000/652/774460/CHi_bazoyu_15.04.pdf
