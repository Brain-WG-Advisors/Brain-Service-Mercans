  • New Jersey Earned Sick Leave (MW-565)

In addition to the above posters required by the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, employers are also required to post information made available by the New Jersey Office of the Attorney General, Division on Civil Rights, and the US Department of Labor. Please see the below links for additional information.

  • NJ Office of the Attorney General materials: Division on Civil Rights Anti-Discrimination Posters
  • US Department of Labor materials: Workplace Poster Requirements for Small Businesses and Other Employers

Employers must also post a Workers’ Compensation notice, which can be obtained through the employer’s insurance carrier.
