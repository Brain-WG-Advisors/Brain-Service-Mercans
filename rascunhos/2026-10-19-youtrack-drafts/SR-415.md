Hi @lli,

**Rows 38–39 (C_REG, C_RAJ):** no. `dps_region_code` / `dps_district_code` hold the ДПС tax office codes (ДПС directory SPR_STI). In the 1-PV header, C_REG and C_RAJ are the region and district of the statistics body the report is submitted to, from the Derzhstat directory SPR_SSCU.XML (Derzhstat Format 3.1, Table 1). The code lists differ: Kyiv is 26 in SPR_STI and 80 in SPR_SSCU, where 26 is Ivano-Frankivsk oblast. No DD field holds the SPR_SSCU codes.

**Row 44 (OBL):** yes. OBL is the oblast code per КАТОТТГ of the activity address (Derzhstat technical-field list for S0301121). It is the КАТОТТГ first level, characters 3–4 of the code: UA80000000000093317 → 80. The XSD list STATOBL uses the same codes.

**Row 45 (RAY):** no. RAY has 3 digits (XSD type STATRAY), while the КАТОТТГ district level has 2 (characters 5–6; 00 for Kyiv). Derzhstat publishes no rule converting one into the other; the technical-field list states RAY is filled automatically from the enterprise card in the Respondent's Cabinet.

**Row 46 (NOMER):** structural unit number, mandatory and ≥ 1 (Derzhstat control 1 for S0301121). It is 1 when the enterprise has no separate structural units (ГУС у Львівській області, 1-ПВ 2026, slide 5). A separate structural unit gets a number > 1, which makes N9, N1 and N2 mandatory (control 3), and the number must match the one in the monthly 1-PV (control 2).
