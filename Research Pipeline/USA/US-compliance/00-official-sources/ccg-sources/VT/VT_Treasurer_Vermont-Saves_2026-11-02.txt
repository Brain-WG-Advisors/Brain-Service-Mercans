Title: Vermont Saves | Office of the State Treasurer
Vermont Saves is a state-sponsored retirement savings program for workers whose employers do not offer a workplace retirement plan ... automatic payroll deductions into their own Roth IRA. ... There is no cost for employers to facilitate the program ...
Program Administrator: Vestwell. Employer Assistance 1-844-599-4911.
Who Must Register? Under Vermont law, employers with two or more W-2 employees must register for Vermont Saves if they do not already offer a qualified workplace retirement savings plan. Employers that already offer a qualified retirement plan (401(k), 403(b), SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA or another qualified employer-sponsored retirement plan) ... do not need to participate, but they do need to certify their exemption.
Compliance & Enforcement: covered employers must either register and facilitate payroll deductions for eligible employees, or certify exemption.
Penalty Schedule (failure without reasonable cause, maximum per covered employee):
Through September 30, 2026: $20 per covered employee
Beginning October 1, 2026: $75 per covered employee, per calendar year
Paying a penalty does not satisfy the obligation ... Read the compliance and penalty provisions in 3 V.S.A. 535.
FAQ: Participation is voluntary for employees. Eligible employees are automatically enrolled but may opt out at any time. Employers must register even if all employees do not want to participate. Accounts default to an individual Roth IRA (Traditional IRA option). Employees may increase, decrease, pause or stop contributions at any time.
