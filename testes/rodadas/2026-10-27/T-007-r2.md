## Resposta

Trying the primary text directly.

Under **Article 17(1) of Law of Ukraine No. 1105-XIV "On Compulsory State Social Insurance"** (dated 23.09.1999), the sick leave benefit is paid as a percentage of average salary based on the employee's insurance record:

| Insurance record (stazh) | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 years | 50% |
| 3 to 5 years | 60% |
| 5 to 8 years | 70% |
| Over 8 years | 100% |

The Pension Fund of Ukraine (PFU) restated this schedule in its 2026 guidance. The same article also gives 100% regardless of record to: Chernobyl victims in categories 1–3; a parent caring for a sick child under 14 affected by Chernobyl; war veterans, injured Revolution of Dignity participants, and families of deceased veterans and Defenders; victims of Nazi persecution; qualifying blood donors; and persons rehabilitated under Law No. 962-XII. For the donor category, that provision lost force on 26 January 2026, but donors who earned the right before 25 January 2026 keep it for one year after the donation.

A few related rules for 2026:

* **Diia City:** Employees and gig specialists of Diia City residents receive the salary on which contributions were actually paid, regardless of insurance record.
* **Monthly cap:** The benefit cannot exceed the maximum ЄСВ (unified social contribution) base, which for 2026 is UAH 172,940 (8,647 × 20).
* **Short recent record:** If the person has less than 6 months of insurance record in the 12 months before the illness, the benefit is capped at the amount calculated from the minimum wage.

Many older sources cite this as **Art. 24**. That was the article number before the law was renumbered; the current article is **Art. 17**.

Official source: https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1105-14 (Art. 17, part 1). The government site wouldn't load for me directly, so the wording above comes from PFU's 2026 clarification as republished by kadroland.com and dtkt.ua.
