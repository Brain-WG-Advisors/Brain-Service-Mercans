## Resposta

**WT 79432 – Parental Leave Payout → Clave L, subclave 27**

The Social Security birth and childcare benefit (prestación por nacimiento y cuidado de menor) is reported with clave L, subclave 27 (Prestaciones públicas por maternidad o paternidad exentas del IRPF), because it is only a formal renaming of the former maternity/paternity benefits. The exemption is capped: the exempt amount is limited to the maximum benefit Social Security recognises for that concept, and any excess is taxed as employment income under clave A. Source: AEAT, Modelo 190 *Preguntas frecuentes*, and Diseño de registro 2025, field SUBCLAVE (pos. 79–80), clave L, item 27.

**WT 79430 – Pregnancy Risk Payout and WT 79429 – Breastfeeding Risk Payout → Clave B, subclave 01, with flag 05 (pos. 394) = 1**

- **Taxable, not incapacity.** Pregnancy-risk benefit is not exempt because it falls under neither letter h) nor letter z) of art. 7 LIRPF, and it is distinct from incapacity benefit. Breastfeeding-risk benefit is granted on the same terms and conditions as the pregnancy-risk benefit (arts. 188–189 TRLGSS).
- **Why B.01 and not B.03.** The AEAT FAQ still points to clave B, subclave 03, but that text predates the current structure. Orden HAC/1431/2025 (art. 2, Tres), applicable from ejercicio 2025, redefined B.03 as benefits paid to beneficiaries of mandatory civil-servant mutual funds, orphans' colleges and similar entities. The same Order added a new field to B.01 identifying the type of benefit paid by Social Security or Clases Pasivas, with type 05 (position 394) covering other non-exempt benefits under art. 17.2.a).1.ª LIRPF. AEAT has not yet published an updated FAQ naming this subclave for these two benefits.

**Who reports these amounts**

Clave L and clave B report only benefit amounts that the declarant itself pays. Any employer-funded top-up of these benefits is ordinary salary. The AEAT FAQ treats voluntary complements to incapacity benefits this way: they go under clave A, in the field for monetary (or in-kind) payments not derived from incapacity.
