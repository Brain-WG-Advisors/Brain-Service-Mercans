2026 Voluntary Plan Employee Contribution and Benefit Rate
Effective January 1, 2026
Employee Contribution Rate	1.3%
Taxable Wage Ceiling (per employee per year)	$NA
Maximum Contribution (per employee per year)	$NA
Maximum Weekly Benefit Amount (WBA)	$1,765
Maximum Benefit Amount (WBA X 52 weeks)	$91,780
