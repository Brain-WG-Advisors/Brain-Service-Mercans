2026: employers with 25+ covered individuals send 0.88% of eligible wages (family 0.18%; medical 0.28%, employer 0.42%). Employers with fewer than 25 covered individuals must send an effective contribution rate of 0.46% of eligible wages. 2027: shifts to family leave (Chapter 101 of the Acts of 2026); under-25 rate stays 0.46%.
