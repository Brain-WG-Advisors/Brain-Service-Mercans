Granite State Paid Family and Medical Leave Plan Tax Credit

On July 1, 2021, RSA 77-E:3-e came into effect allowing a credit against the Business Enterprise Tax for an amount equal to 50 percent of the premium paid by a sponsoring employer for family and medical leave insurance coverage offered to employees pursuant to RSA 21-I:103 for the taxable period in which the premium is paid.
