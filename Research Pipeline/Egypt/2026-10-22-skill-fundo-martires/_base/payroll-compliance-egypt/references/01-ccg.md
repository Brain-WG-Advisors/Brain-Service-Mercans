# 1. What Egypt actually charges on a private-sector payslip

Egypt's payroll withholding stack has **three** statutory legs plus a small annual levy:

1. **Social insurance** (تأمينات اجتماعية) under Law 148/2019 — five branches plus a mandatory individual savings account, levied on a *capped, statutorily-defined* contribution wage. Employee **11%**, employer **18.75%**.
2. **Salary tax** (ضريبة كسب العمل / ضريبة المرتبات وما فى حكمها) under Law 91/2005 — a progressive charge whose rate table is a **matrix**, not a simple band ladder, and which is computed by **annualising cumulative net income** and then apportioning back to the period.
3. **Universal health insurance** (التأمين الصحي الشامل) under Law 2/2018 — employee **1%** (+ 3% for a non-working spouse, + 1% per dependant), employer **4%**, **but only in governorates where the system has been rolled out**.
4. **Training and Rehabilitation Fund levy** under Labour Law 14/2025 Article 21 — a small **annual** per-worker amount, employer-borne, for establishments with 30 or more workers.

There is **no** payroll-level income-tax surcharge, no municipal tax, no employer "unemployment fund" beyond the 1% inside social insurance, and — for private-sector employers — **no payroll stamp duty and no martyrs'-fund deduction** (both are confined to government and public-business-sector disbursements; see §8).

# 2. The gross-to-net calculation sequence

The order below is the order the **ETA payroll system itself** applies, and it is the order a payroll engine must reproduce. The single most common modelling error in Egypt is treating the salary tax as a monthly band lookup; it is not.

```
STEP 1  Build the SI contribution wage
        الأجر الشامل  (comprehensive wage per Form 2 – تأمينات)
        −  بدلات غير خاضعة تأمينيا  (allowances excluded by Law 148/2019 art. 1(8)(7))
        =  أجر الاشتراك  (contribution wage)
        then FLOOR at the annual minimum (2,700 for 2026)
             and CAP at the annual maximum (16,700 for 2026)

STEP 2  Social insurance
        Employee  = 11.00%  × capped contribution wage
        Employer  = 18.75%  × capped contribution wage

STEP 3  Universal health insurance — ONLY where the employee's
        "حالة التأمين الصحي الشامل" flag = 01 (subject)
        Employee  = 1% (self) + 3% (non-working spouse) + 1% × dependants
        Employer  = 4%, minimum EGP 50/month

STEP 4  Build the salary-tax revenue base for the period
        + all cash entitlements for work done (art. 9(1))
        + amounts from a foreign source for work performed in Egypt (art. 9(2))
        + cash benefits  (employer-borne employee SI share; employer-borne salary tax)
        + in-kind benefits, valued per Executive Regs art. 11
        − nothing yet
        (exempt COLLECTIVE in-kind benefits are NOT entered at all — see §7.3)

STEP 5  Deduct the exemption block to reach NET INCOME for the period
        − employee social-insurance share
        − employee universal-health-insurance share
        − private insurance fund contributions (Law 54/1975 and Law 155/2024)
        − life / health / pension-entitlement premiums, capped (§6.5)
        − employees' profit share within the statutory limit
        − special allowances exempt up to 2013 and tax-approved
        − amounts exempted by special laws
        − the personal exemption, 20,000 / 12 per month, PRORATED if the
          work period in the month is under 30 days
        = صافي دخل الفترة  (net income of the period)

STEP 6  Cumulate, annualise, tax, apportion back  (Law 91/2005 art. 10)
        cumulative net income = net income of this period
                              + net income of all previous periods in the year
        annual pool  = cumulative net income × 360 ÷ (days worked in the period(s))
        ROUND the annual pool DOWN to the nearest EGP 10
        annual tax   = Article 8 matrix applied to the annual pool
                       (pick the COLUMN by the size of the annual pool)
        period tax   = annual tax × (days worked) ÷ 360
        tax due now  = period tax − tax already computed for previous periods

STEP 7  Net pay
        cash entitlements
        − employee social insurance
        − employee universal health insurance (if applicable)
        − salary tax due for the period
        − non-tax-affecting deductions (loan instalments, alimony, penalties,
          union/club subscriptions, insurance-policy instalments)
        = NET PAY
        In-kind benefits never reduce net pay — they enter the tax base only.

STEP 8  Employer-only costs
        + employer social insurance 18.75%
        + employer universal health insurance 4% (min 50) where applicable
        + Training and Rehabilitation Fund levy (annual, per worker)
```

**Recomputation on every disbursement.** ETA FAQ v4, general Q15 and Q76: the tax is recalculated *"عند تقديم كل صرفية عن المدة من بداية الفترة الضريبية حتي تاريخ الصرفية"* — at each disbursement, for the period from the start of the tax year to the date of that disbursement. Two disbursements in one month therefore produce two recomputations and the second claim is the *difference*. This is why the design "has no year-end true-up": FAQ Q56 states the system was designed *"على اساس عدم وجود فروق بين ضريبة التسوية السنوية والضريبة المستقطعة"* — the cumulative method is intended to make the annual settlement difference nil.

## 2.1 Worked example — mid-level Cairo employee, January 2026

Assumptions: Egyptian national, private-sector company in Cairo (**not** a universal-health-insurance governorate), permanent staff, tax treatment **1** (this is the original employer), full month worked (work period = 30 days), January 2026 so there are no prior periods.

**Monthly package**

| Element | Amount (EGP) | In SI base? | In tax base? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| المرتب الأساسي — basic salary | 25,000.00 | Yes | Yes |
| مكافآت وحوافز — incentives/bonus | 8,000.00 | Yes | Yes |
| بدل سكن — housing allowance (cash, in lieu of an in-kind benefit) | 5,000.00 | **No** — art. 1(8)(7)(b) | Yes |
| بدل انتقال — transport allowance (flat cash, not a reimbursement) | 2,000.00 | **No** — art. 1(8)(7)(a) | Yes (ETA FAQ Q17) |
| **Cash gross (الأجر الشامل)** | **40,000.00** | | |
| Company car — company-borne fuel + insurance + periodic maintenance in the month = 5,000 | — | No | **20% = 1,000.00** |

**Step 1 — contribution wage**
```
الأجر الشامل                40,000.00
− بدلات غير خاضعة تأمينيا     7,000.00      (5,000 housing + 2,000 transport)
= أجر الاشتراك              33,000.00
capped at the 2026 maximum  16,700.00      ← binding
```

**Step 2 — social insurance**
```
Employee   11.00% × 16,700.00 =  1,837.00
Employer   18.75% × 16,700.00 =  3,131.25
```

**Step 3 — universal health insurance** — not applicable (Cairo is not yet in the rollout).

**Step 4 — tax revenue base for the period**
```
cash entitlements                        40,000.00
in-kind benefit: car (20% × 5,000)        1,000.00
= إجمالي الإيرادات                        41,000.00
```

**Step 5 — exemption block → net income of the period**
```
employee SI share                        − 1,837.00
personal exemption 20,000 ÷ 12           − 1,666.67
= صافي دخل الفترة                          37,496.33
```

**Step 6 — annualise, tax, apportion**
```
annual pool = 37,496.33 × 360 ÷ 30      = 449,955.96
rounded DOWN to nearest EGP 10          = 449,950
column: annual pool ≤ 600,000 → COLUMN A

  1 – 40,000            @  0.0%  =         0.00
  > 40,000 – 55,000     @ 10.0%  =     1,500.00
  > 55,000 – 70,000     @ 15.0%  =     2,250.00
  > 70,000 – 200,000    @ 20.0%  =    26,000.00
  > 200,000 – 400,000   @ 22.5%  =    45,000.00
  > 400,000 (49,950)    @ 25.0%  =    12,487.50
  annual tax                     =    87,237.50

period tax = 87,237.50 × 30 ÷ 360 =      7,269.79
tax computed for previous periods  =         0.00
TAX DUE FOR JANUARY 2026           =      7,269.79
```

**Step 7 — net pay**
```
cash gross                               40,000.00
− employee social insurance             −  1,837.00
− salary tax                            −  7,269.79
= NET PAY                                 30,893.21
```
The EGP 1,000 car benefit is **not** deducted from net pay. ETA FAQ Q55 is explicit: the 20% is not paid to the employee, it is a cost already borne in the company's accounts, added *for tax purposes only*, and *"لا تظهر في معادلة صافي الأجر النهائي"* — it does not appear in the final net-pay formula.

**Step 8 — employer cost**
```
cash gross                               40,000.00
+ employer social insurance               3,131.25
+ Training & Rehabilitation Fund levy         0.83   (EGP 10/year ÷ 12; see §8.1)
= TOTAL EMPLOYER COST                    43,132.08
```

**Effective rates on this package:** salary tax 17.73% of cash gross; employee total deductions 22.77%; employer on-cost 7.83%.

## 2.2 The same employee in February 2026 with a bonus — why cumulation matters

Same base package, plus an annual bonus of EGP 60,000 paid in February (field `DTE250` — *مبالغ خاضعة منصرفة بصورة سنوية*).

```
period                                   Jan          Feb
cash entitlements                     40,000.00   100,000.00
in-kind car benefit                    1,000.00     1,000.00
employee SI (11% × 16,700)             1,837.00     1,837.00
personal exemption (1/12)              1,666.67     1,666.67
net income of the period              37,496.33    97,496.33
cumulative net income                 37,496.33   134,992.66
days worked cumulative                       30           60
annual pool = cum × 360 ÷ days       449,955.96   809,955.96
rounded down to nearest 10           449,950      809,950
COLUMN                                       A            D   ← changed!
annual tax                            87,237.50   187,487.50
period tax = annual × days ÷ 360       7,269.79    31,247.92
less tax of previous periods                  —     7,269.79
TAX DUE                                7,269.79    23,978.13
```
Annual tax for column **D** (annual pool > 800,000 and ≤ 900,000) at 809,950:
```
  1 – 200,000           @ 20.0%   =   40,000.00
  > 200,000 – 400,000   @ 22.5%   =   45,000.00
  > 400,000 (409,950)   @ 25.0%   =  102,487.50
                                     187,487.50
```
Note what happened: a single bonus pushed the *annualised* pool across two column boundaries, and the 0%, 10% and 15% bands were **withdrawn entirely**. This is not a bug — it is the design of Article 8 (§6.3). It also means a bonus paid early in the year is annualised at 360/days and can massively overstate the pool; the cumulative recomputation self-corrects in later months, but **the cash impact of a January bonus is front-loaded** and the employee's take-home dips for that month.

# 4. Social insurance — Law 148/2019

## 4.1 The contribution wage (أجر الاشتراك) — Article 1(8)

The contribution wage is *"المقابل النقدي الذي يحصل عليه المؤمن عليه … من جهة عمله الأصلية لقاء عمله الأصلي"* — the **cash** consideration the insured receives **from the original employer for the original work**. In-kind benefits are outside it by definition.

Its elements are a **closed list of 16**:

1. الأجر الوظيفي — job wage
2. الأجر الأساسي — basic wage
3. الأجر المكمل — complementary wage
4. الحوافز — incentives
5. العمولات — commissions
6. الوهبة (service charge / tronc) — **only** where three conditions are met: (a) custom is to pay it to establishment workers as a pre-set percentage of amounts due from customers; (b) there is a common fund in the establishment into which it is collected for distribution; (c) there are agreed rules between employer and workers governing how it is distributed
7. البدلات — allowances, **except** the four excluded categories below
8. الأجور الإضافية — overtime pay
9. التعويض عن الجهود غير العادية — compensation for extraordinary effort
10. إعانة غلاء المعيشة — cost-of-living aid
11. العلاوات الاجتماعية — social allowances
12. العلاوات الاجتماعية الإضافية — additional social allowances
13. المنح الجماعية — collective grants
14. المكافآت الجماعية — collective bonuses
15. ما زاد عن الحد الأقصى للأجر الأساسي — the excess over the basic-wage cap
16. العلاوات الخاصة التي لم يتم ضمها للأجر الأساسي — special allowances not merged into the basic wage

**Allowances that are NOT part of the contribution wage** — Article 1(8)(7), verbatim structure:

- (a) **بدل الانتقال، بدل السفر، بدل حضور الجلسات** and other allowances paid against burdens required by the duties of the post — **but بدل التمثيل (representation allowance) is expressly carved back IN**;
- (b) **بدل السكن، بدل الملبس، بدل السيارة** and other allowances paid in lieu of in-kind benefits;
- (c) allowances due because the insured spends time outside the original workplace;
- (d) allowances due to meet cost-of-living burdens **outside the country**.

**Engine consequence.** The SI base and the tax base diverge on exactly these allowances: a cash housing or transport allowance is **outside** the SI base but **inside** the tax base. The ETA monthly calculation form models this with two fields — `EI100` *الأجر الشامل* (comprehensive wage per Form 2 – تأمينات) and `EI105` *بدلات غير خاضعة تأمينيا* — and derives the contribution wage as the difference (ETA FAQ, insurance section, Q2: *"هي الفارق بين الأجر الشامل وأجر الاشتراك (الأجر التأميني) المدرج من واقع استمارة 2 تأمينات"*).

## 4.2 The 2026 ceiling and floor

| Item | 2025 | **2026** | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Minimum contribution wage (monthly) | 2,300 | **2,700** | NOSI announcement 30 Nov 2025 |
| Maximum contribution wage (monthly) | 14,500 | **16,700** | NOSI announcement 30 Nov 2025 |
| Minimum pension in payment | 1,495 | 1,755 | same |
| Maximum pension in payment | 11,600 | 13,360 | same |

Effective **1 January 2026**. The step is the annual uprating contemplated by Law 148/2019 and its Executive Regulations; the Authority's own framing is that the minimum exists mainly to fix a wage for categories whose wage is hard to determine (irregular labour, contracting, construction, agriculture), while for ordinary employees the employer must insure on **actual wages** up to the maximum.

> **Ceiling behaviour.** The maximum is a **monthly** cap on the contribution wage, tested per month against that month's contribution wage. It is **not** a cumulative annual ceiling and there is no year-to-date "remaining room" logic of the Canadian/CPP kind. A 2026 employee earning above EGP 16,700 pays exactly EGP 1,837.00 employee-side every month, and the employer exactly EGP 3,131.25.

## 4.3 Fund-by-fund rates — and how they reconcile to 11% / 18.75%

Egyptian practice quotes **employee 11%** and **employer 18.75%**. Neither number appears as such in the statute; both are sums. Below is the reconciliation, each line traced to its article, for a **private-sector** employee — i.e. Article 2, item First(3):

| Branch | Article | Employee | Employer | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **الشيخوخة والعجز والوفاة** — old age, disability and death | 19(1) | **9.00%** | **12.00%** | Verbatim: employer *"بواقع (12%) من أجر المؤمن عليهم العاملين لديه شهريًا"*; employee *"بواقع (9%) من أجره شهريًا"* |
| **المرض** — sickness (treatment and medical care) | 70(1) | **1.00%** | **3.25%** | Employee 1% of wage. Employer 3% applies to items 1–2 of Article 2 First (government, public sector); **3.25% applies to items 3, 4, 5** — which includes private-sector employees. The extra 0.25% funds *أداء تعويض الأجر ومصاريف الانتقال* (wage compensation and transport costs) |
| **إصابات العمل** — work injury | 46(1) | — | **1.00%** | Paid to the **health-insurance** authority for treatment and medical care, and due only *"بالنسبة لغير الخاضعين لأحكام قانون نظام التأمين الصحي الشامل"* — i.e. for employees **not** covered by universal health insurance |
| **إصابات العمل** — work injury, cash rights | 46(2) | — | **0.50%** | For the financial rights borne by NOSI; **rises up to 1.00% by activity risk** as determined by the Executive Regulations |
| **البطالة** — unemployment | 86 | — | **1.00%** | Employer only. The statute lists **no employee share** |
| **نظام المكافأة** — the Reward System | 36 | **1.00%** | **1.00%** | A mandatory **individual savings account** in the insured's name, credited with investment return, paid as a lump sum when insurance rights fall due (Art. 37) |
| **TOTAL** | | **11.00%** | **18.75%** | Combined **29.75%** |

Three things follow that a payroll engine must not get wrong:

1. **The 1% "unemployment" that practitioners often put on the employee side does not exist.** Article 86 funds unemployment insurance from an **employer-only** 1%. The employee's third percentage point is the **Reward System** (Article 36) — a savings account, not an insurance premium. Any employee-side reporting that labels 1% as "unemployment" is mislabelled.
2. **NOSI's own "28.75%" total is not inconsistent with 29.75%.** NOSI has publicly described Law 148/2019 as cutting the total contribution rate *"من 40% إلى 28.75%"*. That figure is 11.00% + **17.75%**, i.e. it excludes the Article 46(1) **1% work-injury medical contribution**, because that 1% is remitted to the **health-insurance authority**, not to NOSI. The employer's cash outflow is still 18.75%; only 17.75% of it lands at NOSI.
3. **The 0.50% injury-cash contribution is risk-rated up to 1.00%.** For a hazardous-activity establishment the employer rate is therefore up to **19.25%**, not 18.75%. The ETA payroll system does not ask the employer for a rate — it derives the split from the selected **insurance status** code (§4.6), because *"تم تغذية بيانات النظام بجميع حالات حساب اشتراكات التأمينات الاجتماعية المعدة بواسطة الهيئة القومية للتأمين الاجتماعي"* (ETA FAQ, salary-items Q35).

**Escalation clause.** Article 19, final paragraph: the contribution rate rises by **1% every seven years** from the law's entry into force (1 January 2020), **split equally** between employer and insured, with a hard ceiling on the total contribution rate of **26%** (that ceiling is expressed against the old-age/disability/death rate of 21%, not against the all-branch total). **The first step therefore falls due on 1 January 2027 — not in 2026.** For 2026 the 12% / 9% split is unchanged.

## 4.4 Payment date and the late-payment charge — Article 121

- Contributions for a month are due **"في أول الشهر التالي لشهر الاستحقاق"** — at the beginning of the month following the month of accrual.
- Late payment triggers a **monthly additional amount** equal to *"متوسط إصدارات الخزانة من الأذون والسندات في الشهر السابق للشهر الذي تعين فيه سداد المبالغ"* **plus 2%** — the average Treasury bill/bond issuance rate for the month preceding the month in which payment fell due, plus two points.
- **There is a 15-day grace:** *"ويتم الإعفاء من المبلغ الإضافي إذا تم السداد خلال خمسة عشر يومًا من تاريخ وجوب الأداء."*
- The obligor bears the remittance costs; NOSI may charge a collection fee of **1‰** of amounts collected, minimum EGP 10, maximum EGP 100.

**Engine consequence.** The effective operative deadline for both social insurance and salary tax is therefore **the 15th of the following month** — SI by grace (Art. 121) and tax by statute (Law 91/2005 Art. 14). Aligning them is safe; treating the SI date as "the 1st" and panicking on the 2nd is not.

## 4.5 Employer data return — Article 120

The private-sector employer must submit to NOSI, on the forms it prescribes, the data of workers, their wages and their contributions. If the employer does not, contributions are computed **on the basis of the last statement submitted, or on the basis of NOSI's own investigations**, and NOSI notifies the employer of the amount. The employer has **30 days** from receipt to object at the competent office, and the objection is **only admissible on payment of an EGP 100 objection fee**. NOSI must answer within 30 days; refusal opens a route to the dispute-examination committee (Art. 148) and then the competent court within 30 days.

## 4.6 Insurance status — the 28-value mapper

The ETA monthly calculation form's field `EI070` *الحالة التأمينية* is **mandatory** and selects the whole contribution-rate treatment. Its 28 values (ETA SDK v5) are reproduced in full in `05-data-dictionary.md` §3.2. The ones that change the arithmetic most are:

| 04 | Insured, with both of the above decisions | Both reductions |

# 5. Universal health insurance — Law 2/2018

## 5.1 Rates

Article 40 of Law 2/2018 obliges employers *"بواقع (4%) شهريًا من أجر الاشتراك"* and sets insured-person rates by Schedule 1. The Authority's own published operative split:

| Payer | Rate | Base | Note |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Employee (breadwinner) | **1%** | contribution wage | |
| **Non-working spouse** | **3%** | breadwinner's wage | deducted from the **employee** |
| **Each dependant** | **1%** | breadwinner's wage | deducted from the **employee** |
| Employer | **4%** | contribution wage | composed of **3% sickness + 1% work injury**; **minimum EGP 50 per month** |
| Pensioner | 2% | pension | out of payroll scope |

**Engine consequence.** The employee-side UHI deduction is **not a flat 1%** — it is `1% + 3%·(non-working spouses) + 1%·(dependants)`. An employee with a non-working spouse and three children pays **1 + 3 + 3 = 7%**, seven times the headline rate. The ETA monthly form therefore carries two dedicated count fields, `EI120` *عدد الزوجات غير العاملات* and `EI125` *عدد المعالين*, **both mandatory when the UHI flag is 01**, and both rejected when it is 02. ETA FAQ, laws section, Q1: the dependant and spouse data are supplied **by the employee**, the employer passes them through, and **responsibility for their accuracy rests on the employee**.

## 5.2 Which social-insurance components it displaces

The employer's 4% is described by UHIA as 3% sickness + 1% work injury, which maps one-for-one onto Law 148/2019 Article 70 (sickness treatment and care) and Article 46(1) (injury medical care). Article 46(1) itself is drafted to apply only to those **not** subject to the universal-health-insurance law, and Article 70 opens *"مع عدم الإخلال بأحكام قانون نظام التأمين الصحي الشامل"* — without prejudice to that law.

> **CONFIDENCE FLAG.** The *direction* of the substitution is clear from the statutory text on both sides. The **exact residual NOSI employer percentage for a UHI-covered private-sector employee** — in particular whether the 0.25% wage-compensation/transport slice of the 3.25% stays at NOSI — is **not** something I could establish from a primary source in this build. The ETA payroll system resolves it from insurance-status code **06** against NOSI's own rate matrix, which is not published as a document I could read. Treat the combined employer percentage for a UHI-covered employee as **derived** and confirm against the ETA system output before relying on it. See `07-qa-report.md` §3.

## 5.3 Geographic rollout

Article 3 of the enacting law applies the system **gradually to governorates, guided by the phases in Schedule 5**, over roughly 15 years from 2018. Phase 1 comprised **Port Said, Ismailia, Suez, South Sinai, Luxor and Aswan**. Phase 2, in progress during 2026, has been announced for **Minya, Matrouh, Damietta, Kafr El Sheikh and North Sinai**, with Alexandria under study.

> **CONFIDENCE FLAG.** The Phase 1 list is well established. The Phase 2 list and its 2026 activation dates come from press reporting of government statements, not from a gazette or UHIA schedule I could retrieve. **Do not hard-code the governorate list.** The operative input for payroll is the per-employee flag `EI115` *حالة التأمين الصحي الشامل* (01 subject / 02 not subject), which the employer sets from the establishment's actual UHIA registration — drive the engine from that flag, not from a governorate table.

## 5.4 Integration status

ETA FAQ, technical Q22: universal-health-insurance data is entered on the monthly calculation form **manually by the employer** *"لحين إتمام التكامل الكامل مع منظومة التأمينات الاجتماعية ومنظومة التأمين الصحي الشامل"* — pending full integration with the social-insurance and universal-health-insurance systems. As at the March 2026 edition of the FAQ, **that integration is not yet live.**

# 6. Salary tax — ضريبة المرتبات وما فى حكمها

## 6.1 What is charged — Article 9

Verbatim scope (Law 91/2005 Art. 9):

1. **Everything due to the taxpayer as a result of work for another**, with or without a contract, periodically or non-periodically, **whatever the name, form or cause** of the entitlement, and whether for work performed **in Egypt or abroad where the consideration is paid from a source in Egypt** — expressly including wages, bonuses, incentives, commissions, grants, **overtime**, allowances, shares and portions of profits, and **cash and in-kind benefits of all kinds**.
2. What is due to the taxpayer **from a foreign source for work performed in Egypt**.
3. Salaries and remuneration of chairmen and board members in public-sector and public-business-sector companies who are not shareholders.
4. Salaries and remuneration of chairmen, board members and managers in **capital companies** in return for their **administrative** work.

The Executive Regulations determine the bases for valuing in-kind benefits (§7).

ETA FAQ, technical Q99, restates the disclosure scope on the monthly form: everything due for work at the entity, with or without contract, periodic or not, **including daily labour**; entitlements for work performed in Egypt or abroad paid by the entity; entitlements for work performed in Egypt paid by a **foreign source**; cash and in-kind benefits **except exempt collective in-kind benefits**; lump sums such as deferred wage arrears **distributed over their years of accrual**; and — importantly — *"يدخل المقابل النقدي لرصيد الأجازات المنصرف أثناء الخدمة في وعاء الفترة كاملًا بغض النظر عن سنوات هذا الرصيد"*: **cash for accrued leave paid during service falls wholly into the period's base, regardless of how many years the balance accrued over.** That is an express statutory carve-out from the spreading rule (Art. 10, third paragraph, *"عدا مقابل الإجازات"*).

## 6.2 What is not charged, and the exemptions — Articles 12 and 13

**Article 12 — not subject to the tax at all:**
1. **Pensions.**
2. **End-of-service gratuities** (مكافآت نهاية الخدمة).

ETA FAQ Q96 adds the operative condition and it is a trap: for an end-of-service gratuity to be outside the charge *"يشترط … أن تكون انتهت علاقة العامل نهائيا بجهة عمله"* — the employment relationship must have **definitively** ended. **If the worker is re-appointed at the same entity, the gratuity becomes taxable** and must be entered among the entitlement elements (`DTE230` — *مكافأة نهاية الخدمة الخاضعة*). A non-taxable gratuity is disclosed instead on the *إضافات وخصومات* screen (`NAD620`), which does not affect the tax computation.

**Article 13 — exempt from the tax** (amounts as amended):
1. **The personal exemption — EGP 20,000 per year** (Law 7/2024, Art. 13 item 1: *"مبلغ 20,000 جنيه إعفاء شخصي سنوي للممول"*; the original 2005 figure was 4,000).
2. **Social-insurance contributions** and other amounts deducted under the social-insurance laws **or any alternative systems** substituting for them (Law 64/1980 systems).
3. **Employees' contributions to private insurance funds** established under **Law 54/1975**.
4. **Life and health insurance premiums** on the taxpayer for their own benefit or that of the spouse or minor children, and **any premium for entitlement to a pension**.
5. **The following collective in-kind benefits**: (a) meals provided to workers; (b) collective transport of workers, or its cost equivalent; (c) health care; (d) tools and clothing necessary for performing the work; (e) housing the employer makes available to workers **on the occasion of their performing the work**.
6. **The employees' share of profits decided for distribution in accordance with the law.**
7. What diplomatic and consular corps members, international organisations and other foreign diplomatic representatives receive within the scope of their official work, **on a reciprocity basis and within its limits**.

**The cap on items 3 and 4.** The statute's original wording caps the aggregate at *"15% من صافى الإيراد أو ثلاثة آلاف جنيه أيهما أكبر"*. The operative figure ETA applies in 2026 is **the lower of 15% of net income or EGP 10,000 per year, and in all cases limited to the amount actually paid** — ETA FAQ, technical Q41, verbatim: *"باعتماد قيمة الأقساط في حدود نسبة 15% من صافي الدخل بحد أقصي 10000 جم"*, and any excess is **taxable**. FAQ Q42 lists the **four** items that count against that single cap:
1. premiums to private insurance funds established under **Law 54/1975**;
2. **health-insurance premiums**;
3. **life-insurance premiums** on the taxpayer for their own benefit and that of the spouse and minor children;
4. **any insurance premium for entitlement to a pension**.

> **CONFIDENCE FLAG.** The 15% / EGP 10,000 pairing and the four-item basket are stated categorically by ETA in its March 2026 FAQ, which is the operative instruction to employers. I could **not** locate the gazette text of the amendment that moved the alternative floor from EGP 3,000 to a EGP 10,000 ceiling. Use ETA's figure; flag the missing amending instrument. See `07-qa-report.md` §3.

**Law 155/2024 private insurance funds — a separate, uncapped exemption.** ETA FAQ Q43: employees' contributions to private insurance funds established under **Law 155/2024** (or funds that regularise their position under it, from the date of regularisation) are entered in their own field and are **"تعفى بالكامل"** — **fully exempt**, under the final paragraph of **Article 75 of Law 155/2024**, and therefore **outside** the 15% / 10,000 basket. A single employee can therefore have both a capped Law 54/1975 deduction and an uncapped Law 155/2024 deduction. (FAQ, technical Q115, adds a mechanical quirk: in the annual settlement's *شيت المعالجة*, all corrections are negative-only **except** Law 155/2024 premiums, which for the **2025** tax period only may be positive.)

**Employees' profit share — the 100% test.** FAQ Q25 and Q79: the employees' share of profits is entered as an entitlement under Article 9 item 1, and is exempt under Article 13 item 6 **after approval by the general assembly**, *"وذلك في حدود 100% من مرتبات العاملين تطبيقًا لأحكام قانون الشركات"* — **within 100% of the workers' salaries**, per the Companies Law. Only the exempt portion goes in the profit-share field (which auto-carries to the exemption block); **anything above the exempt limit must be entered among the other entitlement elements.** During the year the system exempts the whole amount and verifies the 100% test at annual settlement.

**Special allowances up to 2013.** Two distinct fields exist and they behave differently (SDK `DTE175` / `DTE190`, and FAQ salary-items Q6): *العلاوات الخاصة معفاة* — special allowances up to 2013 **that were approved in tax examinations** — is exempt and appears twice on the Excel sheet (once as a manually-entered entitlement, once system-computed in the deductions block); *العلاوات الخاصة الخاضعة* — special allowances up to 2013 **not** approved in examinations, **plus all special allowances from 2014 onward** — is taxable. Presidential-decree special allowances up to 2013 are accepted as exempt for **both** civil-service and private-sector workers (FAQ Q15).

## 6.3 The rate — Article 8 as amended by Law 7/2024

**This is a matrix, not a band ladder.** The rate row tells you the percentage; the **column** is selected by the size of the **annual net income pool**, and as the pool rises the lower bands are progressively **withdrawn**.

Verbatim structure (*الجريدة الرسمية، العدد 7 مكرر (أ)، 21 فبراير 2024*):

| Tax rate | **A** — net income **≤ 600,000** | **B** — > 600,000 and ≤ 700,000 | **C** — > 700,000 and ≤ 800,000 | **D** — > 800,000 and ≤ 900,000 | **E** — > 900,000 and ≤ 1,200,000 | **F** — **more than 1,200,000** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.00%** | from 1 to **40,000** | — | — | — | — | — |
| **10%** | more than 40,000 to **55,000** | from 1 to **55,000** | — | — | — | — |
| **15%** | more than 55,000 to **70,000** | more than 55,000 to 70,000 | from 1 to **70,000** | — | — | — |
| **20%** | more than 70,000 to **200,000** | more than 70,000 to 200,000 | more than 70,000 to 200,000 | from 1 to **200,000** | — | — |
| **22.5%** | more than 200,000 to **400,000** | more than 200,000 to 400,000 | more than 200,000 to 400,000 | more than 200,000 to 400,000 | from 1 to **400,000** | — |
| **25%** | what exceeds 400,000 | what exceeds 400,000 | what exceeds 400,000 | what exceeds 400,000 | what exceeds 400,000 | from 1 to **1,200,000** |
| **27.5%** | — | — | — | — | — | what exceeds **1,200,000** |

Read the columns as a withdrawal schedule:

- Cross **600,000** → the **0% band is gone**; the first 55,000 is taxed at 10%.
- Cross **700,000** → the **10% band is gone**; the first 70,000 is taxed at 15%.
- Cross **800,000** → the **15% band is gone**; the first 200,000 is taxed at 20%.
- Cross **900,000** → the **20% band is gone**; the first 400,000 is taxed at 22.5%.
- Cross **1,200,000** → the **22.5% band is gone**; the first 1,200,000 is taxed at 25% and only the excess at 27.5%.

**There is no marginal-relief smoothing anywhere in Article 8.** The withdrawal produces genuine **step discontinuities** in the tax charge at each column boundary. Computed from the table above:

| Annual net income crosses | Tax just below | Tax just above (at +10) | **Cliff** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 600,000 | 124,750.00 | 128,752.50 | **+4,002.50** |
| 700,000 | 153,750.00 | 156,502.50 | **+2,752.50** |
| 800,000 | 181,500.00 | 185,002.50 | **+3,502.50** |
| 900,000 | 210,000.00 | 215,002.50 | **+5,002.50** |
| 1,200,000 | 290,000.00 | 300,002.75 | **+10,002.75** |

Each cliff equals the value of the band being withdrawn (e.g. 40,000 × 10% = 4,000 at the 600,000 boundary; 400,000 × 2.5% = 10,000 at the 1,200,000 boundary). A payroll engine must not interpolate them away, and an employer negotiating a package near a boundary should know that EGP 10 of extra annual net income can cost EGP 10,002.75.

**Effective-rate sanity points** (column A): net income 449,950 → 87,237.50 = 19.39%. Net income 600,000 → 124,750 = 20.79%. Column F at 2,000,000 → 300,000 + 27.5% × 800,000 = 520,000 = 26.00%.

**Effective date.** Law 7/2024, second enacting article: for **salary tax and the like**, the law applies *"اعتبارًا من أول الشهر التالى لتاريخ نشره"* — from the first day of the month following publication. Published 21 February 2024 → **in force for salary tax from 1 March 2024**, and unchanged as at the date of this build. (For business, professional and immovable-property income it applies from the tax period ending after publication — a different date, do not conflate.)

**No 2025 or 2026 change.** Laws **5, 6 and 7 of 2025** (*الجريدة الرسمية، العدد 6 مكرر (و)، 21 فبراير 2025*) were read in full for this build: Law 5/2025 settles the position of unregistered taxpayers, Law 6/2025 creates a simplified regime for projects with turnover ≤ EGP 20m, and Law 7/2025 amends the Unified Tax Procedures Law. **None of them touches Article 8, Article 13 or the salary-tax mechanism.** The 2026 amendment activity reported in Egypt concerned **value added tax**, not salary tax.

## 6.4 The annualisation engine — Article 10

Verbatim (Law 91/2005 Art. 10):

> The revenues entering the tax base are determined, for each part of the year in which any taxable revenue was received, **in proportion of its duration to the year and on the basis of the monthly revenue after converting it into annual revenue**. Where the taxable revenue changes, the computation is adjusted from the date of change on the basis of the new revenue **or the old revenue, whichever is lower**, after converting it into annual revenue; and **a settlement is made each year** in accordance with the procedures and rules laid down by the Executive Regulations. Deferred salaries, wages and the like paid in a lump sum in one year are **distributed over the years of accrual, except leave compensation**; the revenue entering the tax base for each year is recomputed and the tax due is settled on that basis.

ETA implements it as (FAQ general Q15, technical Q5, Q29, Q76):

```
annual pool  = cumulative net income × 360 ÷ days worked in the period
annual tax   = Article 8 matrix (annual pool, rounded down to nearest 10)
period tax   = annual tax × days worked in the period ÷ 360
tax due      = period tax − tax computed for previous periods
```

**Day conventions are fixed, not calendar.** ETA FAQ Q39 and Q40:
- The work period (**مدة العمل**) is **mandatory** on the monthly form and is **30 days for every month from January to December** while the worker is on strength — not the calendar length of the month.
- **Maximum 30 days per month; maximum 360 days per year.**
- Leaving during the month → work period runs to the leaving date. Joining during the month → from the joining date to month end. Daily labour → actual period worked.
- Where a salary covers more than one month, what counts is the **months of entitlement**, not the submission date.
- Entering a work period of **fewer than 30 days prorates the personal exemption** by that proportion (FAQ Q93).
- Once a full month's salary has been paid before month end (say on the 25th) and the worker resigns on the 28th, **the monthly form will not let you reduce the work period** — that correction can only be made in the annual settlement (FAQ Q13).

**The tax point is the disbursement, not the entitlement.** FAQ Q91: retroactive increases are entered **in the month of payment**, because *"الواقعة المنشئة لضريبة المرتبات وما في حكمها هي واقعة الصرف"* — the event giving rise to the tax is the act of disbursement. The **only** exception is deferred wage arrears (متجمد أجور), which are spread over their accrual years under Article 10 and handled with tax treatment **8**.

**No negatives.** FAQ Q8 and Q14: the monthly calculation form **does not accept negative figures**. An over-payment or error is corrected either by reducing the following month's salary, by an additional disbursement, or through the pre-settlement processing sheet (*شيت المعالجة*). And FAQ Q102, Q103, Q31: **there is no amended return** — no amended quarterly return, no amended annual settlement, and no amended monthly calculation form, *"وفقًا لقانون الإجراءات الضريبية الموحد"*.

## 6.5 The eight tax treatments (المعاملات الضريبية)

The `EI060` field selects one of **eight** treatments, and the choice determines the entire computation. This is the single most consequential mapper in Egyptian payroll.

| # | When to choose it | Tax effect |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Permanent or temporary staff where **this entity is the original employer** (جهة العمل الأصلية) | Standard Article 10 annualisation. The original employer must **aggregate income from non-original employers** (on their notifications) in the *"المبالغ الواردة من جهات عمل غير أصلية"* field, and record the tax they withheld in the *"الضريبة المخصومة من جهات عمل غير أصلية"* field, so that net income and the pool are correct and there is **no double taxation** |
| **2** | This entity is a **non-original employer** | **Flat 10% under-account withholding on total entitlements** (Art. 72 bis). The **original employer's 9-digit tax registration number is mandatory** and cannot be substituted by its social-insurance number (FAQ Q37, Q47). The non-original employer must notify the original employer of the workers, their total entitlements for the period and the tax withheld |
| **3** | This entity is a non-original employer **but has become the de facto original employer** — the worker draws **more than 50% of income** there, or spends **more than 50% of time** there | Full Article 10 annualisation, exactly as treatment 1 |
| **4** | The worker is a **person with a disability, or cares for one**, evidenced by the **بطاقة الخدمات المتكاملة** issued by Social Solidarity | The **personal exemption is increased by 50%** — Art. 23 item 1 of **Law 10/2018** (Rights of Persons with Disabilities). Everything else as treatment 1 |
| **5** | The worker has a **chronic disease** listed in **Health Minister Decree 259/1995**, **and is not present at the workplace** | The **insurance wage (الأجر التأميني) is exempted from net income** — per **MoF Decree 285/2023**. Everything else as treatment 1 |
| **6** | The entity is a company in a **special-nature economic zone (the Suez Canal Economic Zone)** established **before 24 June 2015** whose validity still runs | The annual pool is taxed at a **flat 5%** instead of the Article 8 matrix, then apportioned back |
| **7** | Treatment 6 **and** the worker is a person with a disability or cares for one | Flat **5%** **and** the personal exemption increased by **50%** |
| **8** | **Deferred wage arrears (متجمد)** relating to earlier periods, paid in the current period — the final paragraph of Article 10 | **The computation rules are frozen.** The entity must recompute the earlier period's tax with the arrears added, derive the difference, and **enter the annual pool and the tax due manually**. Correctness is checked at examination |

Notes with teeth:
- **Foreigners use the same treatments as Egyptians.** FAQ Q52 and Q77: *"المعاملة الضريبية للأجانب نفس المعاملة الضريبية للمصريين"* — what matters is which treatment code you pick, not nationality.
- **Treatments 6 and 7 are hard-coded to a single taxpayer number.** The SDK's dropdown for `EI060` names them as *"الهيئة العامة للمنطقة الاقتصادية برقم تسجيل ضريبي 100693342"* — and the SDK notes 06 and 07 are **not available through the integration channel** documented in that version.
- **More than one treatment for the same worker in one year is allowed.** FAQ Q105: each treatment is called into the annual settlement as a **separate row**, and cumulative net income, the annual pool and the tax are computed **per treatment**.
- **Administrative units are not separate employers** unless they hold their own ETA tax registration number. FAQ Q73: if all units sit under one registration, a bonus paid to an Alexandria employee by the Aswan unit is still treatment 1 — not a non-original-employer event.

## 6.6 Rounding

Article 8, final sentence, in every version from Law 26/2020 onward: *"ويتم تقريب مجموع صافى الدخل السنوى عند حساب الضريبة لأقرب عشرة جنيهات أقل."* — **the total annual net income is rounded DOWN to the nearest EGP 10** when computing the tax. Round **down**, on the **annual pool**, **before** applying the matrix. Do not round the monthly figures, and do not round up.

Article 110 adds a second convention for the delay fee only: *"مع استبعاد كسور الشهر والجنيه"* — fractions of a month and of a pound are disregarded.

## 6.7 Withholding, remittance and returns

| Obligation | Legal basis | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Withhold from the amounts in Article 9 an amount **under account** of the tax due | Art. 14 | At payment |
| **Remit** what was withheld from the previous month's payments | Art. 14 | **Within the first fifteen days of each month** |
| Pay any **difference** of tax due | Art. 14 | With the remittance; the employer keeps a right of recourse against the taxpayer for what the taxpayer owes |
| **Quarterly return** to the competent tax office on the prescribed form | Art. 15(1) | **January, April, July and October** of each year |
| Give the worker, **on request**, a statement showing the worker's **three-part name, the amount and type of income, and the tax withheld** | Art. 15(2) | On request |
| Withholding entities: pay withheld amounts | Art. 86 | **By the end of April, July, October and January** each year, and keep records of amounts paid, recipient, and tax withheld, with a copy sent to ETA |
| Where the employer is **non-resident and has no centre or establishment in Egypt**, the obligation to remit falls on **the recipient of the taxable revenue** | Art. 16 | per the Regulations |

**One claim per disbursement.** ETA FAQ Q9 and Q10: submitting the monthly calculation form generates a **claim (مطالبة) on the Core Taxation system** for each disbursement where the period tax exceeds the tax already computed. The employer may either submit each disbursement separately (a claim each) or aggregate the month's disbursements and get one claim — but in both cases the legal payment window is **the fifteen days following the month of deduction, per Article 14**. Entering a disbursement after the 15th triggers recomputation, a claim, and delay fee (FAQ Q23).

**Two claims per period, not one.** FAQ, salary-items Q12: the claims issued through Core are *"مطالبة تخص ضريبة المرتبات ومطالبة بالمساهمة في صندوق الشهداء"* — one for salary tax and one for the martyrs'-fund contribution (§8.2). Social-insurance and universal-health-insurance claims are **not** issued by this system. And where a single form mixes treatments 1 and 2, integration files the monthly payment form and Form 2 – Salaries **separately**, so **separate claims** are issued (FAQ Q101).

## 6.8 Delay fee and penalties

**Article 110 of Law 91/2005** — a delay fee (مقابل تأخير) is due on:
1. what exceeds **EGP 200** of tax payable and unpaid, **even if an instalment decision was issued**, from the day after the return deadline expires;
2. **what was not remitted** of taxes or amounts the law requires to be withheld at source or collected and remitted to the public treasury, from the day after the remittance deadline.

Rate: *"سعر الائتمان والخصم المعلن من البنك المركزى فى الأول من يناير السابق على ذلك التاريخ مضافًا إليه 2%"* — the **Central Bank of Egypt credit-and-discount rate announced on the 1 January preceding that date, plus 2%**, with fractions of a month and of a pound disregarded. **Objection or judicial appeal does not suspend it.**

**The 100% cap — new.** **Article 45 bis of Law 206/2020**, inserted by **Law 7/2025**: *"لا يجوز أن يتجاوز مقابل التأخير أو الضريبة الإضافية نسبة (100%) من أصل الضريبة المستحق عليها"* — in applying the tax laws, delay fee or additional tax may **not exceed 100% of the tax principal**. This applies across the tax laws, salary tax included, and is a material change to long-running arrears exposure.

Refund side, Article 112: where ETA is late refunding, **the same rate minus 2%** applies, running from 45 days after the refund request.

Payment-application order, Article 111 and the following article: amounts paid to ETA are applied (1) administrative and judicial costs, (2) **delay fee**, (3) taxes withheld at source, (4) taxes due. Partial payment therefore feeds the delay fee first.

> **LIVE-DATA ITEM — never hard-code.** The **CBE credit-and-discount rate** drives both Article 110 and Article 112, and the **average Treasury-bill/bond issuance rate** drives the NOSI additional amount under Article 121 of Law 148/2019. Both must be pulled live at the time of computation. See `07-qa-report.md` §5.

## 6.9 The Law 6/2025 simplified regime — payroll consequences

For employers admitted to the simplified regime for projects with annual turnover not exceeding **EGP 20 million** (ETA FAQ, technical Q117–Q119):

1. Subject entities are **exempted from monthly payment** from the first month of the quarter following entry into the regime;
2. and **exempted from filing the quarterly return (Form 4 – Salaries)** from the quarter following entry;
3. **payment is confined to the annual settlement**;
4. **monthly calculation forms must still be submitted** — not for payment, but because the annual settlement is built by integration from them, so the settlement **cannot be filed** until all monthly forms for the period are in;
5. **no claims are issued** on those monthly forms; the claim is confined to the annual settlement.

# 7. Benefits in kind

## 7.1 The general rule

Executive Regulations Article 11 defines cash and in-kind benefits, for the purposes of Article 9, as *"كل ما يحصل عليه العامل نقدًا أو عينًا دون أن يكون عوضًا عن نفقات يتحملها فى سبيل أداء عمله، وعلى أن تمثل منفعة شخصية له"* — everything the worker receives in cash or in kind **without it being compensation for expenses borne in performing the work**, and **provided it represents a personal benefit to them**. The default valuation is **market value**; the difference between market value and any amount charged to the employee is the benefit (ETA FAQ Q54).

## 7.2 The five prescribed valuations

| Benefit | Valuation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Company cars placed at the employee's personal disposal** | **20% of the cost of fuel, insurance and periodic maintenance** relating to those cars, **whether owned or leased** | Regs art. 11(1) |
| **Mobile phones** | **20% of the phone-related expenses over the year** | Regs art. 11(2) |
| **Employer loans and advances** | Where the employer grants a loan **exceeding the total the employee received during the six months preceding the loan**, interest-free or at a rate **below 7%**: benefit = **7%**, or the **difference** between the loan rate and 7% where the loan rate is lower. "Loan" includes **any form** of it, including amounts paid in advance or appearing in the employer's books charged to the employee's account. **Administrative expenses of obtaining the loan are not deducted from the rate** | Regs art. 11(3); SDK `DTE210` |
| **Life-insurance policies on the employee, their family or their property** | **What the employer bears of the premiums paid during the year** | Regs art. 11(4) |
| **Company shares granted below fair value** | The **difference between the fair value of the share at the date of acquisition and the value charged to the employee**. **Where restrictions on transfer of ownership exist, the benefit does not arise until those restrictions lapse.** The employer must withhold and remit under Article 14 and include all benefits in the annual settlement schedules | Regs art. 11(5), as amended by **MoF Decree 779/2007** (market value → fair value) and **MoF Decree 74/2008** (the restriction rule) |

**Advances against profits** are treated exactly like employee loans and advances — the final paragraph of Regs art. 11(3) (ETA FAQ, salary-items Q40).

**Foreign-currency loans** are valued on the loan balance recorded in the company's accounts, converted to EGP **at the exchange rate announced on the loan's due date** (FAQ Q21).

**Shares and one-off benefits** are entered in the month of release / of enjoying the benefit, and the tax is recomputed with each disbursement entry; if the employee resigns, the tax has already been computed for the period actually worked (FAQ Q107, Q108).

## 7.3 Exempt collective benefits — enter them nowhere

The five collective in-kind benefits exempt under Article 13(5) (meals, collective transport or its cost equivalent, health care, work tools and clothing, employer-provided housing on the occasion of the work) are **not entered on the monthly calculation form at all** — FAQ Q4, Q56, Q59, Q95: *"المزايا الجماعية المعفاة … لا تدرج ضمن بيانات نموذج الاحتساب الشهري."* Do not enter them as an entitlement and then exempt them; omit them.

This is different from **non-taxable end-of-service gratuity** and **non-taxable leave balance**, which *are* disclosed — on the *إضافات وخصومات* screen (`NAD620`, `NAD625`), where they have no effect on the tax computation.

## 7.4 Employer-borne tax and employer-borne employee social insurance are themselves taxable benefits

FAQ Q50 and Q51 are categorical: **the salary tax is a personal tax and its burden cannot be transferred by agreement.** Where the employer bears it, the amount is added to the entitlement elements **as a cash benefit** in the dedicated field *ما تحملته المنشأة من ضريبة المرتبات* (`DTE245`); where the employer bears the employee's social-insurance share, it is added in *ما تحملته المنشأة من التأمينات الاجتماعية* (`DTE240`) and net income is recomputed.

**Engine consequence — a genuine circularity.** A net-of-tax contract requires a **gross-up iteration**: the employer-borne tax is itself in the base that determines the tax. ETA's own instruction (FAQ, salary-items Q21) is the iterative one: enter the employee's data to determine the tax due, then re-enter that value among the entitlements to arrive at the final monthly tax. With Egypt's Article 8 column structure a naive single-pass gross-up can land in the wrong column; iterate to convergence.

# 8. Other payroll levies

## 8.1 Training and Rehabilitation Fund levy — Labour Law 14/2025, Article 21

The Fund (صندوق تمويل التدريب والتأهيل), continued from Law 12/2003 by the second enacting article of Law 14/2025, is financed by, among other things:

> **"نسبة (0,25% ربع فى المائة) من الحد الأدنى للأجر التأمينى** بمنشآت القطاع العام، وقطاع الأعمال العام، والقطاع الخاص، والتى يعمل بها **ثلاثون عاملا فأكثر**، بحد أدنى **عشرة جنيهات**، وبحد أقصى **ثلاثون جنيها عن كل عامل**، تتحملها وتلتزم المنشأة **بسدادها سنويا** مقابل الخدمات المنصوص عليها فى المادة (20)"

Key features, each of which is easy to get wrong:

- The base is **the minimum insurance wage** — not the employee's own wage, not the establishment's payroll. In 2026 the minimum contribution wage is **EGP 2,700 per month**.
- It applies only to establishments with **30 or more workers**.
- It is **employer-borne** and **paid annually**, not withheld monthly.
- **Floor EGP 10 and cap EGP 30 per worker.** These bind at both plausible readings of the base: 0.25% × 2,700 = **6.75**, below the EGP 10 floor → **EGP 10 per worker per year**; 0.25% × (2,700 × 12) = **81.00**, above the EGP 30 cap → **EGP 30 per worker per year**. So the levy is **between EGP 10 and EGP 30 per worker per year** whichever reading applies, and the statutory floor or cap — never the percentage — is what actually determines it at 2026 wage levels.
- A **total exemption** is available: the competent Minister issues the rules and conditions for full exemption from the levy where the establishment **trains its own workers** according to its requirements or its approved internal regulations.

> **CONFIDENCE FLAG.** The statutory text is verbatim and unambiguous, but it is capable of two readings on whether the 0.25% applies to the **monthly** or the **annualised** minimum insurance wage. Because the floor/cap binds under both, the *amount* is safe within the EGP 10–30 band, but I could not find the ministerial decree that fixes the reading, the collection form, or the payment date. Model it as **EGP 10 per worker per year for 2026** (the floor under the monthly reading, and the more conservative of the two), disclose the assumption, and confirm with the Fund. See `07-qa-report.md` §3.

## 8.2 Martyrs' Fund contribution — NOT a private-sector payroll item

The Fund for Honouring the Martyrs, Victims, Missing and Injured of Military, Terrorist and Security Operations and their Families was created by **Law 16/2018** and its resources were widened by a 2021 amendment. The payroll-facing element is a deduction of **EGP 5 per each EGP 10,000** (i.e. **0.05%**) from salaries.

**Its scope is public.** The salary deduction applies to employees of **government agencies, public-sector entities and their companies, and public-business-sector companies and their affiliates**. Private-sector employers are **not** within it.

The ETA payroll system nonetheless computes it, because the system serves all sectors: ETA FAQ Q110 — *"تحسب مساهمة صندوق الشهداء على الصرفيات الأساسية فقط لجميع العمالة فيما عدا المصري العمالة اليومية وتحسب بواقع 5 ج لكل عشرة الاف جنية"* — it is computed on **basic disbursements only**, for all labour **except Egyptian daily labour**, at EGP 5 per EGP 10,000. And FAQ salary-items Q12 confirms it generates its own separate Core claim alongside the salary-tax claim.

> **CONFIDENCE FLAG.** The **rate and mechanics** are from ETA's own March 2026 FAQ and are solid. The **restriction to public-sector payrolls** rests on reporting of the amending law's text rather than on the gazette article itself, which I could not retrieve. If you operate a mixed group with public-business-sector entities, verify per entity. See `07-qa-report.md` §3.

## 8.3 Proportional stamp duty on salaries — NOT a private-sector payroll item

ETA FAQ Q66: *"الدمغة النسبية هي الدمغة المستحقة على الصرفيات الحكومية وفقًا لقانون الدمغة الصادر بالقانون رقم 111 لسنة 1980 وتعديلاته"* — proportional stamp duty is the duty due on **government disbursements**. FAQ Q88 extends it to **public-business-sector companies** under **Article 79 of Law 111/1980** as construed by **State Council Fatwa 475/2/37 of 16 July 1994**, and notes the system provides a basic disbursement type and an additional disbursement type **without** proportional stamp for entities outside that scope. Where it does apply, the company remains obliged to file and pay the proportional salary stamp-duty return on **Form 21-خ**.

FAQ Q66 also defines the base used for it — **"الأساس المجرد"**: *"المرتب الأساسي بدون العلاوات الخاصة المضمومة عليه"*, the bare basic salary without the special allowances merged into it, and it concerns **entities not subject to the Civil Service Law**. The corresponding SDK field is `DTE145` *الراتب الأساسي المجرد*.

Note the interaction that survives even for private employers: **stamp tax legally due is a deduction from the salary-tax base** — Executive Regs art. 12(8) and the ETA exemption block (FAQ Q92 lists *"الدمغة المقررة بالمادة 79 من القانون 111 لسنة 1980"* among the exemption components).

## 8.4 What Egypt does *not* levy on private payroll

For the avoidance of doubt, and because these are commonly mis-assumed: there is **no** employer training levy beyond §8.1, **no** payroll-based development fee (*رسم تنمية الموارد المالية للدولة*, Law 147/1984, has no salary charge), **no** solidarity or emergency levy in force in 2026 (the 1% COVID-era levy under Law 170/2020 was time-limited to twelve months from 1 July 2020 and has expired), **no** local or municipal payroll tax, and **no** separate employer health-fund charge outside §5.

# 9. Minimum wage and the mandatory annual increase

## 9.1 Minimum wage

Labour Law 14/2025 Article 102(1) gives the **National Wages Council** the task of setting the minimum wage **for workers in all sectors at national level**, having regard to workers' and their families' needs, the cost of living and its changes, balance between the parties, and productivity growth. Article 104: establishments subject to the law **must implement the Council's decisions**. Article 108, final paragraph: *"يجب ألا يقل ما يحصل عليه العامل عن الحد الأدنى للأجور، ويحظر احتجاز أجر العامل أو جزء منه دون سند قانوني"*.

**In force for the private sector: EGP 7,000 per month**, decided by the National Wages Council at its meeting of **9 February 2025** and effective **1 March 2025** (Ministry of Planning and Economic Development announcement).

The same decision also, **for the first time**, set a **part-time / temporary-work minimum of EGP 28 net per hour**.

Historical series (from the Ministry's own announcements, useful for retro calculations): 2,400 (Jan 2022) → 2,700 (Jan 2023) → 3,000 (Jul 2023) → 3,500 (Jan 2024) → 6,000 (May 2024) → **7,000 (Mar 2025)**.

> **LIVE-DATA ITEM AND OPEN QUESTION.** The **state administrative apparatus** minimum rises to **EGP 8,000 from July 2026** (Ministry of Finance). As at **27 August 2026** I could **not** find a National Wages Council decision extending EGP 8,000 to the **private** sector, and reporting through mid-2026 states the private-sector date is not officially fixed. **The private-sector floor to apply is EGP 7,000 until a Council decision says otherwise** — but this is precisely the kind of value that must be re-verified at every payroll run, not cached. See `07-qa-report.md` §4.

**Exemption route.** Article 102(3)–(5) and Article 106: an employer facing exceptional economic circumstances may apply for reduction of or exemption from the periodic allowance; the Council sets the criteria for accepting or refusing, must decide within **30 days** of the matter being put to it (Art. 12), and its decisions are appealable to the **labour court** within **30 days** of the date set for paying the allowance or of notification of the decision. The Council's own regulatory rules for exemption applications were issued as **National Wages Council Decision 27/2024**.

## 9.2 The mandatory annual periodic increase — Article 12

> **"يستحق العاملون الذين تسرى فى شأنهم أحكام هذا القانون علاوة سنوية دورية فى تاريخ استحقاقها لا تقل عن (3%) من الأجر التأمينى، وتستحق تلك العلاوة بانقضاء سنة من تاريخ التعيين، أو من تاريخ استحقاق العلاوة الدورية السابقة."**

Three engine-critical features:

1. **The base is the insurance wage (الأجر التأميني), not the basic salary and not total pay.** With the 2026 contribution-wage maximum at EGP 16,700, 3% of the insurance wage is at most **EGP 501 per month** for a capped employee — a very different number from 3% of a EGP 40,000 package.
2. **The rate fell from 7% to 3%.** Law 12/2003 set the periodic increase at 7% of the basic insurance wage. Law 14/2025 sets a floor of **3%**. Any engine or template carrying 7% is running the repealed law.
3. **The anniversary is per employee**, on the elapse of one year **from the date of appointment or from the date the previous periodic allowance fell due** — not on a single company-wide date.

**The Council has set an absolute floor on top of the percentage.** Its 9 February 2025 decision set the periodic allowance at **not less than 3% of the insurance-contribution wage and not less than EGP 250 per month**. So the operative rule is `max(3% × insurance wage, EGP 250)`, and for any employee whose insurance wage is below EGP 8,333.33 the **EGP 250 floor**, not the percentage, is what binds. Article 102(2) expressly empowers the Council to set the minimum periodic allowance *"بما لا يقل عن النسبة المقررة فى المادة (12)"*.

# 10. Working time, overtime and rest

| Rule | Provision | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum actual working time | **8 hours a day** or **48 hours a week**, **excluding** meal and rest periods. The competent Minister may reduce the maximum for certain categories, industries or work | 117 |
| Rest / meal break | One or more periods totalling **not less than one hour**; arranged so the worker does not work **more than five consecutive hours** | 118 |
| Spread of the day | The period between the start and end of working hours may not exceed **10 hours a day**; rest counts as presence if spent at the workplace. Intermittent and special-nature work, as designated by the Minister, may extend presence to **12 hours** | 119 |
| Weekly rest | **Not less than 24 full hours** after at most six consecutive working days, and **in all cases paid**. In remote locations and continuous-operation work, weekly rests may be **aggregated over up to eight weeks** | 120 |
| **Overtime** | Permitted only to meet **extraordinary work necessities or exceptional circumstances**, with **notification to the competent administrative authority within seven days**. The worker is entitled, in addition to normal pay, to pay for overtime hours as agreed in the individual or collective contract, **not less than** normal pay **plus 35% for a daytime hour and plus 70% for a night hour**, computed on the **hourly rate of the worker's original wage**. Work on the **rest day** entitles the worker to **an equal amount of pay as compensation** for that day, **and** the employer must grant a substitute day **during the following week**. In all cases presence at the establishment may not exceed twelve hours | 121 |
| Average daily wage for production / commission workers | Average of what the worker received for **actual working days in the preceding calendar year** (or the shorter period worked), divided by the number of actual working days in that period | 109 |

**Engine consequences.** (a) The premium is **35% / 70%**, expressed as an uplift on the hourly rate of the *original* wage — not a multiplier on total pay and not 1.5×/2×. (b) Rest-day work is **100% extra pay plus a substitute day**, not one or the other. (c) Overtime pay **is part of the social-insurance contribution wage** (Law 148/2019 art. 1(8)(8) — *الأجور الإضافية*), so it feeds SI as well as tax — subject always to the EGP 16,700 cap.

# 11. Leave

## 11.1 Annual leave — Article 124

Paid annual leave, **excluding** public-holiday days and weekly rest days:

| Service / status | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| First year | **15 days** |
| From the second year | **21 days** |
| Completed **ten years** with one or more employers, **or** age over **50** | **30 days** |
| **Persons with disabilities and persons of short stature (الأقزام)** | **45 days** |
| Hazardous or health-damaging work, or remote areas (as designated by ministerial decision) | **+7 days** on top of the above |
| Service under one year | **Pro rata**, provided at least **six months** have been served |

**Article 125** — administration of leave:
- The employer sets the dates according to work requirements and may interrupt leave only for strong reasons of business interest.
- **The worker may not waive leave.** If the worker **refuses in writing** to take the leave the employer has notified, **the right to payment in lieu lapses**.
- In all cases the worker must receive **15 days' annual leave, of which at least six consecutive**.
- **The employer must settle the leave balance, or its cash equivalent, at least every three years.**
- If employment ends before the balance is exhausted, the worker is **entitled to pay for that balance**.
- Leave **may not be split, aggregated or postponed** for children, persons with disabilities and persons of short stature.

**Article 126** — exam leave: the worker may set the annual-leave date if sitting an examination in a stage of education, on 15 days' notice; **and** is entitled to **paid study leave for the actual examination days, not counted against the annual-leave balance**, on 10 days' notice and proof of actually sitting.

**Article 127** — the employer may deny or recover leave pay if the worker is proved to have worked for a third party during the leave, without prejudice to disciplinary action.

## 11.2 Casual and newborn leave — Article 128

- **Casual absence (إجازة عارضة)**: up to **7 days a year**, maximum **2 days at a time**, and **counted against** the annual-leave entitlement.
- **Newborn leave**: a worker whose child is born is entitled to **paid emergency leave on the day of the birth**, **not** counted against annual leave, **maximum three times over the whole period of service**.

## 11.3 Sick leave — Article 131

The worker who proves illness or injury preventing performance of the work is entitled to sick leave as determined by the competent medical authority, during which they receive **wage compensation whose rate and duration are set by the Social Insurance and Pensions Law** — i.e. 75% for 90 days then 85%, capped at 180 days per calendar year, 100% for chronic disease (§4.7).

**One sector is richer.** For workers in **industrial establishments** subject to the Law Facilitating the Grant of Industrial-Establishment Licences (**Law 15/2017**), Article 131 grants, **each three years of service**, sick leave of:
- **3 months at full pay**, then
- **6 months at 85%** of the wage, then
- **3 months at 75%** of the wage,
where the competent medical authority determines recovery is probable.

**What social insurance pays is deducted from what the employer owes.** The worker may use accrued annual leave alongside sick leave, and may ask to convert sick leave into annual leave where the balance allows.

**Article 132**: the medical authority may bar a worker who has been in contact with a household member having a contagious disease from working for an appropriate period **not exceeding three months**.

## 11.4 Maternity and nursing — Articles 54 to 56

- **Maternity leave: four months**, covering the periods before and after birth, with **not less than 45 days after the birth**, on production of a medical certificate stating the probable date of birth. **Paid.** Maximum **three times** over the whole period of service.
- **What social insurance pays under Article 77 of Law 148/2019 (75% of the wage) is deducted from the employer's obligation.**
- From the **sixth month of pregnancy** the pregnant worker's **daily hours are reduced by at least one hour**, and she may **not** be required to work overtime **throughout the pregnancy and until six months after the birth**.
- **Article 55**: right to return to the same or an equivalent post without loss of benefits. **Dismissal or termination during maternity leave is prohibited**, and prohibited on return unless the employer proves a lawful reason. The employer may withhold or recover the leave pay if the worker is proved to have worked for a third party during the leave.
- **Article 56 — nursing**: for the **two years following the birth**, in addition to the ordinary rest period, **two further nursing periods of not less than half an hour each**, which the worker may **combine**. These are **counted as working hours** and no pay reduction may result.

## 11.5 Pilgrimage and holidays

- **Article 130**: a worker who has completed **five consecutive years** with the employer is entitled to **one month's paid leave** to perform the Hajj or to visit Jerusalem, **once** over the whole period of service.
- **Article 129**: the worker is entitled to **paid leave on the holidays, feasts and occasions determined by decision of the competent Minister**, and the provision applies to the religious feasts of non-Muslims.

> **BELOW-FLOOR SECTION — public holidays.** The **mandatory list of public holidays and the precise pay entitlement for working them** is delegated to a ministerial decision that I did **not** retrieve in this build. Article 129 establishes the *entitlement*; the *list* is not in the statute. Confidence on the list: **~35%**. Do not populate a holiday calendar from this pack. See `07-qa-report.md` §3.

# 12. Termination, notice and severance

Egypt has **no general statutory severance/end-of-service gratuity for indefinite contracts** — the pension system under Law 148/2019 (including the Reward System lump sum, §4.3) performs that function. What exists instead is a **notice regime**, a **specific gratuity for employer-terminated long fixed-term contracts**, and a **compensation floor for unlawful termination**.

| Situation | Rule | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed-term contract** | Ends on expiry of its term. If concluded or renewed for **more than five years**, the **worker** may terminate **without compensation** at the end of five years, on **three months' notice**. **If the employer terminates, the worker is entitled to a gratuity equal to one month's wage for each year of service** | 154 |
| **Task contract** | Ends on completion of the task; renewable by express agreement for similar work. If the original or renewed work runs **more than five years**, the worker may **not** terminate before completion | 155 |
| **Indefinite contract — notice** | Either party may terminate on **three months' written notice**. This is a **flat three months**, not a service-tiered scale | 156 |
| **Lawful-reason requirement** | Neither employer nor worker may terminate an indefinite contract **except for a legitimate and sufficient reason**, and termination must be at a time appropriate to the circumstances of the work | 157 |
| Notice mechanics | Notice may not be made conditional; the period runs **from the date of receipt** | 158 |
| Notice during leave | Notice **may not be served during the worker's leave**, and the period runs only from the day after the leave ends. **Sick leave during the notice period suspends** the notice period, which restarts only from the day after that leave ends | 159 |
| Status during notice | The contract **remains in force** throughout the notice period and both parties must perform all obligations; the contract ends on expiry of the period | 160 |
| Waiver | Agreement to **waive or shorten** notice is void; agreement to **lengthen** it is permitted. The employer may release the worker from observing notice where the **worker** is the terminating party | 161 |
| Job-search time | Where the **employer** gives notice, the worker may be absent **one full day a week or eight hours during the week** to look for other work, **with pay**, on at least one day's prior notice | 162 |
| Release from duties | The employer may release the worker from work during the notice period, with **service treated as continuing** to the end of the period and **pay due for it** | 163 |
| **Pay in lieu** | Terminating an indefinite contract **without notice or before the notice period expires** obliges the employer to pay **an amount equal to the wage for the period, or the remaining part of it**; that period **counts as service** and the employer continues to bear the associated burdens. Where the **worker** terminates, the contract ends when they leave | 164 |
| **Unlawful termination** | Where the employer terminates an indefinite contract for an **unlawful reason**, the worker is entitled to compensation for the harm caused of **not less than two months' wage for each year of service**, without prejudice to their other legal rights. Unlawful reasons expressly include union membership or activity, acting (or having acted, or seeking to act) as a workers' delegate, and bringing or joining a complaint or action against the employer | 165 |
| Final settlement | Where employment ends for any reason, the employer pays the wage **and all amounts due** within **not more than seven days from the date the worker claims them** | 108(4) |
| Business transfer | Merger, division, inheritance, bequest, gift, sale (including at public auction), assignment, lease or other disposal **does not terminate** the employment contracts; the successor is **jointly liable** with the previous employers for all obligations arising under them | 11 |

**Engine consequences.** Model three distinct amounts and never conflate them: (i) **pay in lieu of notice** = wage for the unexpired notice, and that period *accrues service and social insurance*; (ii) **Article 154 gratuity** = one month's wage per year of service, but only on employer termination of a fixed-term contract that ran beyond five years; (iii) **Article 165 compensation** = a floor of two months' wage per year of service for unlawful termination. Only (ii) and (iii) sit near the tax question of "end-of-service gratuity"; see §6.2 and `02-wtc.md` for their treatment.

# 13. Wage payment, deductions and attachment

| Rule | Provision | Article |
|---|---|---|
| How the wage is fixed | By the individual contract, the collective agreement or the establishment's approved regulations; failing that, comparable pay; failing that, the custom of the profession in the place of work; failing that, the interim-matters judge at the competent labour court assesses it on equitable grounds | 107 |
| **When and in what** | Wages and other amounts due are paid **on a working day, at the workplace, in legal tender, or into the worker's bank account** | 108 |
| Frequency | **Monthly-paid workers: at least once a month.** Piece/commission work taking more than two weeks: a weekly on-account payment proportionate to work completed, balance within the week following delivery. Otherwise: **at least once a week**, unless otherwise agreed | 108(1)–(3) |
| On termination | Wage and all amounts due **within not more than seven days** of the worker's claim | 108(4) |
| Floor and no withholding | The worker must not receive **less than the minimum wage**; **withholding the wage or part of it without legal basis is prohibited** | 108 final |
| Category change | The employer may **not** move a worker from monthly-paid to daily, weekly, hourly or piece-rate **without the worker's consent**, and the worker keeps all rights acquired during the monthly-paid period | 110 |
| Attendance without work | Worker who attends on time, ready to work, prevented by causes attributable to the **employer** → **treated as having worked, full wage due**. Prevented by **force majeure outside the employer's control** → **half the wage** | 111 |
| Company stores | The employer may not oblige the worker to buy food, goods or services from specified outlets or of the employer's own production | 112 |
| **Employer loans** | The employer may **not deduct more than 10% of the wage** in satisfaction of money lent during the contract, and **may not charge any interest** on such loans. The same applies to wages paid in advance | 113 |
| **Attachment / assignment cap** | In all cases, deduction, attachment or assignment of the wage due for **any debt** is limited to **25% of that wage**, raised to **50% for a maintenance (alimony) debt**. On competition, maintenance ranks first, then amounts owed to the employer for tools or equipment the worker damaged, or recovery of amounts wrongly paid, or penalties imposed on the worker. Assignment within the permitted percentage is valid only with the worker's **written consent** | 114 |
| **The base for the 25% / 50% cap** | *"وتحسب النسبة … بعد استقطاع ضريبة الدخل على الأجر، وقيمة الاشتراكات والمبالغ المستحقة وفقًا لقانون التأمينات الاجتماعية والمعاشات … وما يكون صاحب العمل قد أقرضه للعامل فى حدود النسبة المنصوص عليها فى المادة (113)"* — the percentage is computed **after** deducting income tax on the wage, social-insurance contributions and amounts due under Law 148/2019, **and** the employer loan within the Article 113 10% limit | 114 |
| **Discharge and the wage statement** | The employer is discharged of the wage **only** if the worker signs for receipt in the register kept for that purpose, or on the wage schedules, or on completion of the transfer of wage and entitlements to their bank account; **"ويلتزم صاحب العمل بإعطاء العامل بيانًا بمفردات أجره"** — and **the employer must give the worker a statement of the components of their wage** | **115** |
| Child workers | The employer delivers wages, bonuses and other legal entitlements to child workers directly, and that delivery discharges them | 116 |
| Records | Employers must keep **paper or electronic registers** containing workers' data and the wage due to each worker; the Ministry's inspection bodies conduct periodic inspections to verify implementation of National Wages Council decisions | 105 |

**Engine consequence on the deduction cascade.** The 25%/50% attachment cap has a **defined base**, and it is *not* gross pay: compute net-of-tax, net-of-social-insurance, net of the Article 113 employer-loan slice, and apply 25% (or 50% for maintenance) to that. The Article 113 10% employer-loan limit is a **separate, prior** cap on a different base (the wage), and employer loans carry **zero interest** as a matter of law — which sits interestingly against the tax rule that values an interest-free employer loan as a **7% taxable benefit** (§7.2). Both are true simultaneously: the Labour Law forbids charging the employee interest; the tax law imputes a benefit.
