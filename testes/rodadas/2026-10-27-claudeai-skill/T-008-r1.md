## Resposta

Now the primary text on zakon.rada.gov.ua.

**Reference period: the 12 calendar months immediately preceding the month in which the incapacity began**, counted from the 1st of one month to the 1st of the next, at the employee's last main place of employment. The source is Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1266 of 26.09.2001, the "Procedure for calculating average salary for compulsory state social insurance payments", point 25. The official text on zakon.rada.gov.ua confirms this, as does the Pension Fund of Ukraine: the reference period is 12 calendar months of employment at the insured person's last main place of work, preceding the month in which the insured event occurred.

Shorter employment periods are handled differently:

| Employment before onset | Reference period | Point |
|---|---|---|
| 12 months or more | The last 12 full calendar months before the month of onset | p. 25 |
| Less than 12 months | The full calendar months actually worked, 1st to 1st | p. 26 |
| Less than one month | The calendar days actually worked before onset | p. 27 |
| Incapacity starts on the first day of work, or no earnings in the period | No reference period. The base is the monthly salary or tariff rate in force on the day of onset | p. 28 |

For example, an employee hired in 2023 who falls ill on 5 August 2026 has a reference period of August 2025 to July 2026.

**Average daily salary (p. 3):** take the salary accrued in the reference period on which the unified social contribution (ЄСВ) was charged, and divide it by the number of calendar days in that period. Calendar days not worked for valid reasons, such as unpaid leave or temporary incapacity, are excluded from the reference period. In the p. 28 case, the monthly salary is divided by 30.44, the average number of calendar days per month set by the Procedure.

The most recent amendment is Resolution No. 533 of 29.04.2026, in force from 1 May 2026. It did not change the reference-period rule. Its main changes were to extend the Procedure to sole proprietors (ФОП), independent professionals and farm-household members, and to add a new valid reason for excluding days: periods when a ФОП was exempt from paying ЄСВ.
