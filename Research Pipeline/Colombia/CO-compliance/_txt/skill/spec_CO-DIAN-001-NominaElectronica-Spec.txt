~
R4| Indefinite-term employee (contrato a termino indefinido) | YES | The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.
R5| Fixed-term employee (contrato a termino fijo, max 4 years) | YES | Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.
R6| Specific-work or task employee (contrato por obra o labor) | YES | An employment contract; CST art. 46. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).
R7| Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity) | YES | Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA planilla type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.
R8| Part-time employee | YES | Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
R9| All-inclusive salary employee (salario integral, >= 13 SMMLV) | YES | CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the nomina electronica carries SalarioIntegral = true.
R10| Apprentice (contrato de aprendizaje) | YES | Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decreto 0223 of 5 March 2026. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 4; PILA worker type 12 (lectiva) or 19 (productiva).
R11| Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias) | YES | Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
R12| Teleworkers and remote workers | YES | Ley 1221 de 2008 as amended by Ley 2466 de 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the nomina electronica carries a dedicated Teletrabajo devengado element (NIE158).
R13| Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract | YES | Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
R14| NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract | YES | IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
R15| Employed old-age pensioner in active service | YES | PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
R16| Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract | YES | Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
R17| Independent contractor (contrato de prestacion de servicios) | NO | Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (planilla types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Formulario 350, but under a different casilla.
R18| Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity) | NO | Public-sector only; PILA planilla types T and F are descoped in scope_manifest.
R19| Non-executive director paid honorarios only (junta directiva) | NO | Not employment income. Reported by the payer as honorarios, not as rentas de trabajo of an employee.
R20| Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer | NO | The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
~
R22| Basic salary for the days worked | YES | SueldoTrabajado (NIE070). The single largest input to every downstream filing.
R23| Statutory transport allowance | YES | AuxilioTransporte (NIE071), where the employee earns up to 2 SMMLV. Not salary for contributions, but IS in the base of cesantias, prima and the severance indemnity.
R24| Overtime and premium hours, itemised by type with times and percentages | YES | Seven separate hour-type blocks (table 5.5.5), each with start time, end time, quantity, percentage and amount. A single aggregated 'overtime' figure is NOT admissible.
R25| Vacation taken and vacation compensated in cash, as separate elements | YES | VacacionesComunes and VacacionesCompensadas. They must not be merged: enjoyed vacation is in the contribution base, compensated vacation is not.
R26| Service bonus, severance savings and interest on severance savings | YES | Primas, Cesantias and Cesantias/@PagoIntereses. Reported here even though severance savings are excluded from the monthly withholding base.
R27| Incapacity, maternity / paternity leave and paid leave | YES | Each with its own dates, quantity and amount, and - for incapacity - its type code.
R28| Unpaid leave and lawful strike | YES | Reported as days ONLY. Both blocks deliberately carry no amount element.
R29| Benefits in kind (salary in kind) | YES | Through the paired OtroConcepto S / NS elements or the specific allowance elements. Valued contractually or by expert appraisal; Colombia has no notional valuation scale.
R30| Employer social-security contributions | NO | The document reports what the EMPLOYEE accrued and what was DEDUCTED from the employee. The employer's 12% pension, 8.5% health, occupational-risk premium and 9% levies are an employer cost reported on PILA, never a deduction here.
R31| Employer payroll levies (SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, CCF 4%) | NO | Employer cost, reported on PILA. Never an employee deduction.
R32| Documented business-expense reimbursements | NO | Not remuneration. Excluded from the accrued block; only the permanent portion of viaticos (NIE072) is reportable, and only because it is salary.
R33| Amounts in a currency other than the Colombian peso | NO | Res. 000013 de 2021 art. 26 makes the peso mandatory. A foreign currency may be ADDED to a graphical representation but never substituted; the TRM element (NIE200) exists in the schema but is out of scope for a Colombian payroll.
~
R35| Reported on the accrual (causacion) date | NO | Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
R36| Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date | YES | The universal Colombian rule - pago o abono en cuenta. Applies to the nomina electronica (Res. DIAN 000013 de 2021 art. 3), to Formulario 350, to Formulario 220 and to Formato 2276.
R37| A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month | NO | It does not. The nomina electronica is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (art. 3). PILA and Formulario 350 are likewise monthly.
R38| Nil period still filed | VARIES | Formulario 350: NOT required where no operation subject to withholding occurred in the period (ET art. 606 par. 2). Nomina electronica: no document where there was no payment. PILA: a planilla is still required while the employment relationship subsists.
R39| Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period | VARIES | Nomina electronica: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Reemplazar or Eliminar) referencing the original CUNE. Formulario 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a planilla type N (correcciones) or M (mora).
~
R41| Employee with zero net pay (deductions equal accrued) | YES | A document is still required. ComprobanteTotal (NIE189) is 0.00; the accrued and deducted blocks are fully itemised.
R42| Employee whose withholding falls in the 0% band | YES | The payment is still reported. RetencionFuente (NIE177) is simply omitted rather than zero-filled.
R43| Mid-month hire or mid-month leaver | YES | DiasTrabajados (NIE069) is reduced; FechaIngreso or FechaRetiro is set; TiempoLaborado is computed to the settlement end on the 360/30 convention.
R44| Second employer of a multi-employer employee | YES | Each employer issues its own document for its own payments. There is no aggregation across employers at document level.
R45| Employee paid semi-monthly | YES | ONE monthly document carrying PeriodoNomina = 4 (Quincenal) and TWO FechaPago (NIE203) occurrences. Not two documents.
R46| Correction of a previously validated document | YES | By adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste), never by re-sending the original number - global rule 90 forbids reuse.
R47| Employee holding a PPT (Permiso por Proteccion Temporal) | YES | Reported, but see CO-GAP-06: the annex has no PPT document-type code because the PPT post-dates it. Code 47 (PEP) is the only available proxy.

R4| Version (NIE022) | Configuration | Emit the constant string 'V1.0: Documento Soporte de Pago de Nomina Electronica' exactly, including accents and the colon-space. Validation rule NIE022 compares the whole literal. | A configuration file saved as ASCII will silently strip the accent on 'Nomina' and every document will be rejected.
R5| Numero (NIE012) | Engine sequence + configuration | Concatenate the DIAN-authorised Prefijo (NIE010) with the next unused Consecutivo (NIE011), with no separator. Never re-send a rejected number.
R6| CUNE (NIE024) | Engine (computed) | Compute SHA-384 over the eleven inputs concatenated with no separators, in the published order: Numero, FechaGen, HoraGen (with the -05:00 offset), DevengadosTotal, DeduccionesTotal, ComprobanteTotal, employer NIT (no DV), employee document number, TipoXML, the secret software PIN, and Ambiente. The three money inputs must be TRUNCATED to two decimals - not rounded - and written with a decimal point, no thousand separators and no currency symbol. Emit the result as 96 lower-case hexadecimal characters. | The four classic failures: (1) rounding instead of truncating the money inputs; (2) omitting the -05:00 offset from HoraGen; (3) including the employer DV in NitNE; (4) using the SoftwareID instead of the PIN. Start at 30 (or the number of days from a mid-month hire), then subtract unpaid-leave days (LicenciaNR), suspension days and lawful-strike days. Do NOT subtract incapacity, maternity or vacation days - those are paid and appear in their own devengado blocks. | February always counts 30 days in Colombian payroll, not 28. A 31-day month also counts 30.

R11| DevengadosTotal / DeduccionesTotal / ComprobanteTotal (NIE187-NIE189) | Engine (computed) | DevengadosTotal is the sum of every populated element in the Devengados block; DeduccionesTotal is the sum of every populated element in Deducciones; ComprobanteTotal is DevengadosTotal minus DeduccionesTotal, adjusted for the Redondeo residual. An inference error propagates into PILA and into the employer cost.
R13| Employer contributions must never appear in Deducciones | Wage-type configuration | The Deducciones block reports only what was withheld FROM THE EMPLOYEE. The employer's 12% pension, 8.5% health, occupational-risk premium and the 9% payroll levies (SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, CCF 4%) are employer costs reported on PILA. They are validated independently of FondoPension. Configure the solidarity surcharge as its own wage type from the outset. | This is the single most common Colombian mapping error. Merging produces a 5% 'pension' line that fails reconciliation against PILA fields 47, 51 and 52.
