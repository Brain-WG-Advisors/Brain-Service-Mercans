Title: 2026 Workers' Compensation Legislative Changes (Friday, July 10, 2026)
This is a general overview of workers' compensation legislation passed by the 2026 session of the 114th General Assembly. ...
Maximum & Minimum Benefit Changes
Temporary Benefits - The maximum weekly benefit rate for injuries occurring July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 is $1,488.30 or 110% of the state's average weekly wage.
Permanent Benefits - The maximum weekly benefit rate for injuries occurring July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 is $1,353.00 or 100% of the state's average weekly wage.
Minimum Weekly Benefit - The minimum weekly benefit rate for injuries occurring July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 for both temporary and permanent benefits is $202.95.
Source: US Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics
