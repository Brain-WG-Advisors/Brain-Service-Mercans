You can add or update a POA by submitting a paper POA form or online through your MyTax.DC.gov account. Submit a Paper Power of Attorney Form You can complete and submit the Power of Attorney form (Form D-2848) by mail or in person. Download the Power of Attorney Form (D-2848) Submit a Power of Attorney Online You can also submit a Power of Attorney online through your MyTax web portal. Log in to your MyTax.DC.gov account and follow these steps to add a Power of Attorney: After logging in, select the &ldquo;More&hellip;&rdquo; tab Under the Registration Details section, click on &ldquo;Add Power of Attorney&rdquo; Once your in the power of attorney section, please follow the instructions.&nbsp; &nbsp; Questions?
