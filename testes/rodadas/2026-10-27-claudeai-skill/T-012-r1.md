## Resposta

For January–June 2026 periods, Annex D6 was **J0510610** (version 10). For periods from July 2026 onward, it is **J0510611** (version 11). Version 11 came from **Order No. 243 of the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine (MoF), dated 07.05.2026**, which amends MoF Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015.

**J0510611, "Information on the grounds for recording service of certain categories of persons in accordance with the law" (Annex D6), filed with J0500111**
- MoF Order No. 243 of 07.05.2026 was registered with the Ministry of Justice on 26.05.2026 under No. 751/46145. It updated the Tax Calculation form and its Filling and Submission Procedure.
- The forms took legal effect on 17 July 2026, but the State Tax Service (DPS) said it would start applying them from 01 August 2026.
- The monthly legal-entity form was first used for the July 2026 reporting period. Those returns were filed in August 2026.
- DPS added J0500111 and its annexes, including J0510611 (Annex D6), to the "Single Window" software (release 1.34.7.0, dated 29.07.2026) under MoF Orders No. 243 of 07.05.2026 and No. 284 of 26.05.2026. Order No. 284 only makes technical corrections to Order No. 243.

**Self-employed filers (individual entrepreneurs and persons in independent professional activity)**
- These now have a separate quarterly form, F0500111, and the D6 equivalent is F0510611 (Annex FIZ-D6).
- It was first filed for Q2 2026.

**Previous version, used for January–June 2026 periods**
- The parent form was J0500110 for legal entities and F0500110 for individuals. Annex D6 was J0510610, under MoF Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015 as restated by MoF Order No. 113 of 02.03.2023.

The amended Order No. 4 is at zakon.rada.gov.ua, registration z0751-26. Note that the official 17.07.2026 effective date and the DPS 01.08.2026 application date are different; cite whichever one the reader's question is about.
