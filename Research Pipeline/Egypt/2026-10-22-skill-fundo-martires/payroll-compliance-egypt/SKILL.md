## Design clarifications — confirmed corrections (authoritative, override references/ if conflicting)

These are the things that would silently produce a wrong number if missed. They came out of the research, not out of restatement. Items that affect the calculation engine are marked *(engine)*.

1. **Article 8 is a MATRIX, not a band ladder** *(engine)*. Six income columns × seven rates. The **column is selected by the size of the annualised net-income pool**, and as the pool rises the lower bands are **withdrawn**: cross 600,000 and the 0% band disappears; cross 700,000 and the 10% band disappears; cross 800,000, 900,000 and 1,200,000 and the 15%, 20% and 22.5% bands go in turn. There is **no marginal relief**, so the charge steps discontinuously: **+4,002.50 at 600,000, +2,752.50 at 700,000, +3,502.50 at 800,000, +5,002.50 at 900,000, +10,002.75 at 1,200,000.** A seven-bracket implementation is correct only below EGP 600,000 and understates everyone above it.
2. **Tax is computed by annualising cumulative net income, then apportioning back** *(engine)*. `annual pool = cumulative net income × 360 ÷ days worked` → round the pool **DOWN to the nearest EGP 10** → apply the matrix → `period tax = annual tax × days ÷ 360` → subtract tax computed for prior periods. **Recomputed at every disbursement**, not monthly. Day conventions are **fixed, not calendar**: 30 days per month, 360 per year, and a work period under 30 days **prorates the personal exemption**.
3. **The employee's 11% is 9% pension + 1% sickness + 1% Reward System — NOT unemployment** *(engine)*. Unemployment (Law 148/2019 art. 86) is **employer-only 1%**. The third employee point is the **Reward System** (art. 36), a mandatory individual savings account. And the employer's 18.75% includes a **1% work-injury medical contribution remitted to the health authority, not NOSI** — which is why NOSI's own published total of 28.75% and the 29.75% an employer actually pays are both correct. One limb (art. 46(2), 0.5%) is **risk-rated up to 1%**, so 18.75% is **not universal**.
4. **The social-insurance base and the tax base diverge on allowances** *(engine)*. The contribution wage is a **closed list of 16 elements** (art. 1(8)) and **excludes** transport, travel, session-attendance, housing, clothing, car, out-of-workplace and cost-of-living-abroad allowances — all of which are **fully taxable**. But **بدل التمثيل (representation allowance) is expressly carved back IN** to the contribution wage. Never derive one base from the other.
5. **In-kind benefits never touch net pay** *(engine)*. They enter the tax base only, at their prescribed value: **car 20%** of company-borne fuel + insurance + periodic maintenance, **mobile 20%** of phone expenses, **loans 7%** (or the shortfall from 7%) where the loan exceeds six months' prior income, life premiums borne, shares at fair value less price charged with the benefit **deferred until transfer restrictions lapse**. Where part of an expense is recharged to the employee, the 20% applies to **the part charged to the company's accounts** — a EGP 100 fuel voucher with 20% recovered gives a benefit of **16**.
6. **Exempt collective benefits are entered NOWHERE** *(engine)*. Meals, collective transport, health care, work tools and clothing, and employer-provided work housing are not entered as an entitlement and not entered as an exemption — they are simply omitted. But a **cash allowance in lieu** of any of them (meal allowance, medicine allowance, housing allowance) is **fully taxable**.
7. **Universal health insurance on the employee side is not a flat 1%** *(engine)*. It is **1% self + 3% per non-working spouse + 1% per dependant**. An employee with a non-working spouse and three children pays **7%**. Two count fields are mandatory when the UHI flag is on and rejected when it is off.
8. **The 10% for non-original employers is UNDER ACCOUNT, not final** *(engine)*. Law 30/2023 **repealed Article 11** and inserted **Article 72 bis**, effective January 2024. The ETA system's own dropdown label for tax treatment 2 still reads *"تُحاسب بضريبة قطعية"* — that label is a historical artefact. The original employer must aggregate the income **and** the tax withheld elsewhere, or the employee is taxed twice.
9. **The mandatory annual increase is 3% of the INSURANCE wage with a EGP 250 floor** *(engine)*. Labour Law 14/2025 art. 12 replaced Law 12/2003's 7%. Compute `max(3% × insurance wage, 250)`, on **each employee's own anniversary** of appointment or of the previous increase. With the contribution wage capped at 16,700, 3% is at most EGP 501 — and below an insurance wage of 8,333.33 the **EGP 250 floor binds, not the percentage**.
10. **Employer-borne tax is itself a taxable benefit, so a net-of-tax gross-up must ITERATE** *(engine)*. The salary tax is personal and its burden cannot be transferred by agreement; where the employer bears it, the amount goes into the entitlements. Because Article 8 selects a **column** by pool size, a single-pass gross-up can land in the wrong column. Iterate to convergence. The same applies to an employer-borne employee social-insurance share.
11. **Cash for accrued leave paid during service is NOT spread** *(engine)*. Article 10 spreads lump sums over their accrual years *"عدا مقابل الإجازات"* — leave compensation falls **wholly into the period's base**, however many years it accrued over. Deferred wage arrears, by contrast, **are** spread and require **tax treatment 8** with manual entry of the pool and the tax.
12. **The 25%/50% attachment cap has a defined base — and it is not gross** *(engine)*. Labour Law art. 114: compute it **after** income tax, **after** social insurance and **after** the employer loan within the art. 113 10% limit. Note the deliberate collision: art. 113 forbids charging the employee **any interest** on an employer loan, while the tax regulations impute a **7% benefit** on it. Both are true.
13. **There is no amended return anywhere in Egyptian payroll, and periods must be filed in order.** No amended monthly calculation form, no amended quarterly return, no amended annual settlement. Corrections go into the next month's form, an additional disbursement, or the annual settlement's processing sheet — where entries are **negative-only**. Once the annual settlement is filed, **no disbursement may be filed for that period at all**. Validation must be pre-submission.
14. **Egypt is M2M for tax — but only halfway.** ETA publishes a REST endpoint, a JSON payload and an SDK, which inverts the regional portal-round-trip default. However, **the JSON uploads a draft**: calculation and submission remain portal actions. **No token and no electronic seal are required** — do not port an e-invoicing signing stack into payroll. NOSI and UHIA are **not** integrated.
15. **Always flag for live reverification.** *Never hard-code:* the **CBE credit-and-discount rate** (delay fee = rate + 2%), the **average Treasury-bill issuance rate** (social-insurance additional amount = rate + 2%), the **private-sector minimum wage** (changed six times in four years; an EGP 8,000 step is pending for the private sector as at 27 Aug 2026 — the last positively-decided figure is **EGP 7,000**), and the **contribution-wage floor/ceiling** (steps every 1 January). See `references/07-qa-report.md` §5.

## Scope and limits

- **Egypt, tax year 2026, private-sector employers only.** Civil servants and the state administrative apparatus, the public and public-business sectors, military and police, domestic-service workers, employers and the self-employed, and irregular labour outside a regular employment relationship are **out of scope**. Where a reference describes a public-sector rule (Civil-Service-Law fields, proportional salary stamp duty) it is there to explain **why it does not apply** to a private employer. The **martyrs'-fund contribution (0.05%) does apply** to private-sector employees (`references/01-ccg.md` §8.2).
- **Seven sections are below the 95% confidence floor and are marked as such**: the social-insurance/health-insurance rate boundary for a UHI-covered employee; the UHI governorate rollout list; the Training and Rehabilitation Fund's collection mechanics; the public-holiday list; foreign employees' social-insurance liability; the tax treatment of unlawful-termination compensation; and record-retention periods. **Do not present any of these as settled.** Layouts for Forms 2, 4, 9 and /21خ are **DERIVED** — ETA does not publish them as images or schemas.
- **The `EG-*` wage-type codes are DERIVED** (no Global WTC mapping exists for Egypt). The **ETA field codes** (`EI*`, `DTE*`, `DAE*`, `NAD*`) are **not** derived — they are transcribed from the SDK — but a handful of `DTE`/`NAD` **code-to-label pairings drift** under RTL extraction and are flagged; verify those against a live sheet before wiring an integration.
- **No production API host is published.** Only the pre-production endpoint is documented. Do not guess it.
- For any other country, use that country's compliance skill — do not generalise Egyptian rates or rules.
- The reference files are the source of truth; this SKILL.md only routes to them.

## What good answers look like

**GOOD:** "Per `references/01-ccg.md` §6.3, the tax is **not** a flat band lookup. Article 8 as amended by **Law 7/2024** (*الجريدة الرسمية، العدد 7 مكرر (أ)، 21 فبراير 2024*, in force for salary tax from **1 March 2024**) is a six-column matrix, and an annualised net-income pool of **EGP 809,950** falls in **column D — net income more than 800,000 and not exceeding 900,000**. In that column the 0%, 10% and 15% bands are **withdrawn**: 20% applies from EGP 1 to 200,000 (= 40,000.00), 22.5% from 200,000 to 400,000 (= 45,000.00), and 25% on the excess of 409,950 (= 102,487.50) — **annual tax EGP 187,487.50**. Note the pool is rounded **down to the nearest EGP 10** before the matrix is applied, and that crossing 800,000 costs **EGP 3,502.50** at the boundary with no marginal relief."

**BAD:** "Egypt has progressive rates of 0% up to 40,000, then 10%, 15%, 20%, 22.5%, 25% and 27.5%, so tax on EGP 809,950 is **EGP 177,237.50**." *(No file read. It applies the column-A ladder to a column-D taxpayer, so the withdrawn 0%, 10% and 15% bands are wrongly granted — understating the tax by exactly **EGP 10,250.00**. No gazette citation, no column, no rounding rule, no cliff.)*
