|  | LANGUAGE OF COLUMN B — POSITION AND OUTSTANDING DELIVERABLE | CCG Module 1.1 does **not** document a single national default language. It imposes the language of a personnel document by the region of the employer's seat of operation — Dutch in Flanders, French in Wallonia, German in Ostbelgien, the employee's language in Brussels — and states that bilingual personnel documents are not permitted in Belgium. Column B of this specification carries the **official French** field name exactly as published in the NSSO glossary edition held in 00-official-sources (edition 2026/2, FR). The NSSO publishes the same glossary in Dutch; the Dutch labels were NOT machine-translated from the French, because these are official field identifiers and a translated label is an invented label.
