Hi Lily,

Two corrections to my earlier replies first:
- 701 is not used here. It applies only to multiple employment with exclusions or the Artists scheme (TGSS SLD technical manual, 14/09/2026, code table). The codes are 500 and 601, with 509 instead of 500 when the tramo has peculiaridad 35.
- L03 does not always carry the full increment. Each month is declared "conforme a los topes, bases y tipos y demás condiciones vigentes en los meses a que correspondan los salarios" (RD 2064/1995, art. 16.2.c). So 500/601 only go up to that month's maximum base, and the rest goes to 497–499.

Your queries:
1. Our payroll calculates the bases. The company reports each month's bases in the L03, including 497–499 (RD 2064/1995, art. 72 bis.2); TGSS applies the rates to them (Noticias RED 7/2024). The employee share must be deducted on the payslip that pays the amount, as it cannot be deducted later (LGSS art. 142.2).
2. Scenario 2 (January 2025, already at the 4,909.50 maximum): the L03 carries only 498 = 364.25 and 499 = 469.08, with no 500/601 (Noticias RED 7/2024, point 4). Your 497/498/499 figures are correct.
3. Scenario 3 (February 2026): the L03 carries 500 = 301.20, 601 = 301.20, 497 = 510.12 and 498 = 188.68. 601 has the same 5,101.20 cap as 500 (Orden PJC/297/2026, arts. 1.2 and 2.1). The other 11 months work the same way, each against its own base and maximum (4,909.50 in 2025, Orden PJC/178/2025).
4. 497–499 go in the first tramo only (Noticias RED 7/2024, point 2), and only for periods from 2025 onwards (Noticias RED 1/2025).

Previous tax year:
- Social security: same rule. Each past month gets its own L03 under that month's conditions, so scenario 2 (January 2025) is already the example. A 2024 month has no 497–499.
- Tax: not contradictory. The 15% applies only to arrears, meaning pay that was already due in a prior year and is paid late (LIRPF art. 14.2.b). That pay belongs to the prior year, is left out of the current-year income used for the withholding rate (RIRPF art. 83.2.1ª), and is withheld at 15% (RIRPF art. 80.1.5º). Example: EUR 1,000 that was due in 2025 and is paid in March 2026 → EUR 150 withheld, not added to 2026. Pay that only becomes due in 2026 is 2026 income (LIRPF art. 14.1.a) and goes through the normal 2026 rate, not the 15%. So for the scenario 2 bonus, the 15% depends on when it became due under the bonus terms, not on the social security accrual months.
