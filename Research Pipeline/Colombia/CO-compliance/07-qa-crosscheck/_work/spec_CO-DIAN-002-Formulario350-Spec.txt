R7| - This is a PORTAL ROUND-TRIP, not machine-to-machine. Do not model it on the nomina electronica: DIAN publishes no XSD, no cahier des charges and no import template for Form 350. The engine's deliverable is a reconciled casilla-value set, not a file.
R8| - The DIAN service computes several casillas itself - 55, 56, 57, 58, 109, 110, 111, 112 from the hoja-2 annex; 130, 134, 136 and 138 as totals; 147 as 145 x 146; and 28 from 27. Payroll must not attempt to supply those.
R9| - Signature is by the Instrumento de Firma Electronica (IFE) authorised by DIAN - an authenticated portal credential, not an X.509 document signature as in the nomina electronica.
R10| - The tax portal is NOT the social-security channel. PILA is filed through a licensed private operador de informacion and never touches Muisca.
R12| INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
R13| - integration_type = OUTBOUND (scope_manifest CO-OUT-F350). There is no inbound file: the portal returns an on-screen acknowledgement and a form number (casilla 4), which the engine should capture for the audit trail and for any later corrective return (casilla 26).
R14| - product_mapping_required = true. The engine must accumulate withholding BY COUNTERPARTY TYPE - natural person versus legal person - and, within natural persons, must separate resident employment income (casillas 77 / 93) from payments to non-residents (the hoja-2 annex). The 2024 redesign introduced that separation; the pre-2024 form did not have it.
R15| - Payroll owns four casillas directly - 77, 93 and, for non-residents, the hoja-2 rows that roll into 109 / 111 or 110 / 112 - plus casilla 129 where a prior over-withholding is reversed. Everything else on the form comes from accounts payable or the tax function.
R17| RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT (v2.8 control B1)
R18| - RESIDENT employees are reported on HOJA 1: the depurated ET art. 388 base in casilla 77 and the withholding under the art. 383 progressive table in casilla 93.
R19| - NON-RESIDENT employees are reported on HOJA 2 and roll up to casillas 109 / 111 (no treaty) or 110 / 112 (treaty). The base in casilla 145 is the GROSS payment, because ET art. 408 admits no depuration, and the rate in casilla 146 is 20%.
R20| - The hoja-2 concepto de pago (casilla 142) for a non-resident employee is concepto 5, 'Prestaciones de servicios y compensacion por servicios personales', for a non-treaty country, and concepto 24, 'Rentas de trabajo', for a treaty country. Concepto 24's own definition on the form is 'rentas de trabajo de cualquier naturaleza EN RELACION CON UN EMPLEO a favor de personas naturales no residentes' - it is unambiguously the employee concept.
R21| - The same employee therefore MOVES BETWEEN SHEETS during the year when residence is acquired: hoja 2 while non-resident, hoja 1 from the payment following the date the 183-day test is met. The switch is prospective only; the earlier months are not restated (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026).
R22| - Directors' fees to a non-resident go to concepto 25 (Honorarios de directores) and employer-paid pensions to concepto 27 (Pensiones) - neither is the employee concept, and mis-selecting them changes the treaty article applied.
R24| RELATED FILINGS / CROSS-REFERENCES
R25| - Casilla 93 must reconcile to the sum of RetencionFuente (NIE177) across every nomina electronica document for the same month - CO-DIAN-001 - after the nearest-1,000 rounding.
R26| - Casilla 27 (art. 114-1 exonerated self-withholder) rests on the SAME exoneration test as PILA archivo tipo 1 field 33 and Registro Tipo 2 field 76 - CO-PILA-003.
R27| - The twelve monthly casilla-93 figures do NOT produce Formulario 220. Colombia imposes no employer year-end reconciliation; Formulario 220 (CO-DIAN-004) is a SEPARATE per-employee obligation built from the annual accumulation, not from the monthly returns.
R28| - Casilla 77 is an AGGREGATE base; the per-employee detail behind it is reported annually in Formato 2276 (CO-DIAN-005), which is why the two cannot be derived from one another.
R29| - Formulario 350 aggregates every withholding the employer makes, including on supplier payments and VAT. Payroll is one contributor among several; the return is owned by the client's tax function.

R1| PAYROLL SCOPE SUMMARY - MONTHLY WITHHOLDING TAX RETURN - FORM 350
R2| SCENARIO ~ REPORT? ~ NOTES / REASONING
R3| === WHO GETS REPORTED ===
R4| Indefinite-term employee (contrato a termino indefinido) ~ YES ~ The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.
R5| Fixed-term employee (contrato a termino fijo, max 4 years) ~ YES ~ Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.
R6| Specific-work or task employee (contrato por obra o labor) ~ YES ~ An employment contract; CST art. 46. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).
R7| Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity) ~ YES ~ Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA planilla type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.
R8| Part-time employee ~ YES ~ Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
R9| All-inclusive salary employee (salario integral, >= 13 SMMLV) ~ YES ~ CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the nomina electronica carries SalarioIntegral = true.
R10| Apprentice (contrato de aprendizaje) ~ YES ~ Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decreto 0223 of 5 March 2026. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 4; PILA worker type 12 (lectiva) or 19 (productiva).
R11| Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias) ~ YES ~ Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
R12| Teleworkers and remote workers ~ YES ~ Ley 1221 de 2008 as amended by Ley 2466 de 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the nomina electronica carries a dedicated Teletrabajo devengado element (NIE158).
R13| Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
R14| NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
R15| Employed old-age pensioner in active service ~ YES ~ PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
R16| Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract ~ YES ~ Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
R17| Independent contractor (contrato de prestacion de servicios) ~ NO ~ Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (planilla types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Formulario 350, but under a different casilla.
R18| Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity) ~ NO ~ Public-sector only; PILA planilla types T and F are descoped in scope_manifest.
R19| Non-executive director paid honorarios only (junta directiva) ~ NO ~ Not employment income. Reported by the payer as honorarios, not as rentas de trabajo of an employee.
R20| Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer ~ NO ~ The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
R21| === AMOUNTS AND CALCULATIONS ===
R22| Depurated taxable base of resident employees ~ YES ~ Casilla 77. The ET art. 388 base after non-constitutive income, art. 387 deductions and the 25% exempt income - NOT the gross.
R23| Withholding on resident employees ~ YES ~ Casilla 93. The art. 383 progressive table result, approximated to the nearest 1,000.
R24| Gross payments to non-resident employees ~ YES ~ Hoja 2 casilla 145, rolling up to casilla 109 or 110. The GROSS, because art. 408 admits no depuration.
R25| Withholding on non-resident employees ~ YES ~ Hoja 2 casilla 147, rolling up to casilla 111 or 112. The flat 20% of art. 408, or the treaty rate.
R26| Employer-paid pension income above 1,000 UVT ~ YES ~ Casillas 78 / 94, where the employer itself pays a pension.
R27| Reversal of excess or undue withholding ~ YES ~ Casilla 129, where an employee's withholding is corrected downward in a later month.
R28| Employer social-security contributions ~ NO ~ Not a withholding. Reported on PILA. Formulario 350 is a TAX return only.
R29| Employee social-security contributions ~ NO ~ Not a withholding by the employer in the ET sense. They reduce the casilla-77 base as non-constitutive income, but are never declared as a retencion.
R30| Payments in the 0% band of the art. 383 table ~ PARTIAL ~ The payment IS included in the casilla-77 base; the withholding in casilla 93 is simply nil for that employee. Do not exclude the base.
R31| Withholding on supplier and contractor payments ~ NO ~ Present on the form (casillas 29-54, 79-92, 95-108) but not produced by payroll. Owned by the client's accounts-payable function.
R32| VAT and stamp-duty withholding ~ NO ~ Casillas 131-135. Owned by the client's tax function.
R33| === TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ===
R34| Reported on the accrual (causacion) date ~ NO ~ Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
R35| Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date ~ YES ~ The universal Colombian rule - pago o abono en cuenta. Applies to the nomina electronica (Res. DIAN 000013 de 2021 art. 3), to Formulario 350, to Formulario 220 and to Formato 2276.
R36| A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month ~ NO ~ It does not. The nomina electronica is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (art. 3). PILA and Formulario 350 are likewise monthly.
R37| Nil period still filed ~ VARIES ~ Formulario 350: NOT required where no operation subject to withholding occurred in the period (ET art. 606 par. 2). Nomina electronica: no document where there was no payment. PILA: a planilla is still required while the employment relationship subsists.
R38| Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period ~ VARIES ~ Nomina electronica: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Reemplazar or Eliminar) referencing the original CUNE. Formulario 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a planilla type N (correcciones) or M (mora).
R39| === SPECIAL CASES ===
R40| A month in which no withholding was practised ~ NO ~ ET art. 606 par. 2: the return is NOT obligatory for a period with no operation subject to withholding. This is unusual among Colombian filings - the nomina electronica and PILA do not have an equivalent exemption.
R41| Employee whose withholding is nil because the base is in the 0% band ~ YES ~ The BASE still goes in casilla 77. Only the withholding is nil.
R42| Employee who acquires tax residence mid-year ~ YES ~ Reported on hoja 2 for the months before residence and on hoja 1 from the payment following the date the 183-day test is met. Prospective only - the earlier months are never restated.
R43| Non-resident whose work is performed entirely outside Colombia ~ NO ~ Foreign-source income, not taxable in Colombia, no withholding and nothing to declare (DIAN Oficio 28380 of 2019).
R44| Filing the return without paying it ~ NO ~ ET art. 580-1: a withholding return filed without full payment produces NO legal effect, with no administrative act required. Casilla 980 must equal casilla 138.
R45| Correcting a filed return ~ YES ~ By a corrective return carrying casilla 25 (correction code) and casilla 26 (the form number being corrected). The minimum penalty is 10 UVT of the year the ORIGINAL return was filed.

===== SHEET 'Population Rules'  dims=A1:D29  max_row=29 max_col=4 =====
R1| POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
R2| Field Name ~ Source System ~ Population Logic ~ Edge Cases
R3| === MANDATORY FIELD RULES ===
R4| Casilla 77 - base rentas de trabajo ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ Accumulate, across every employee paid in the month, the taxable base actually used to compute the withholding. For a RESIDENT this is the ET art. 388 DEPURATED base - gross taxable payments less non-constitutive income (mandatory health, pension and solidarity-fund contributions), less the art. 387 deductions elected in writing by the employee, less the 25% exempt income of art. 206 num. 10, subject to the 40% and 1,340-UVT caps. Do NOT report the gross. Round the accumulated total to the nearest 1,000 (ET art. 577), once, at the end - never round each employee and then sum. ~ The casilla also sweeps in honorarios, servicios and comisiones paid to NATURAL persons that are withheld under the art. 383 table (ET art. 383 par. 2, added by Ley 2277 de 2022 art. 8). Payroll supplies only the EMPLOYMENT component; the boundary with casilla 79 / 95 is a client-specific classification that must be agreed and documented at implementation.
R5| Casilla 93 - retencion rentas de trabajo ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ Accumulate the retencion en la fuente actually withheld on work income from natural persons in the month. Each employee's withholding is itself already approximated to the nearest 1,000 under ET art. 868; accumulate those approximated figures and then apply the art. 577 nearest-1,000 rounding to the total. Reconcile the result to the sum of RetencionFuente (NIE177) across every nomina electronica document for the same month BEFORE filing. ~ An employee whose depurated base falls in the 0% band contributes zero here but still contributes their base to casilla 77. Excluding the base is a common and material error.
R6| Casillas 78 / 94 - rentas de pensiones ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ Populate only where the EMPLOYER itself pays a pension. Report the monthly amount EXCEEDING 1,000 UVT (COP 52,374,000 in 2026) under ET art. 206 num. 5, and the withholding on that excess. Where the pension is paid by a pension fund, the fund is the withholding agent and nothing is reported here. ~ Colombia has no employer year-end reconciliation, so an error here is corrected only by a corrective return, not by a December true-up.
R7| Casilla 12 - direccion seccional ~ Company master data ~ Copy the code from casilla 12 of the entity's RUT. It is a static configuration attribute; it does not change with the period and must not be re-derived monthly. ~ A branch structure does not change this casilla - the return is filed for the principal domicile of the legal entity.
R8| === CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ===
R9| Nil period ~ Engine (control) ~ Where NO operation subject to withholding occurred in the month, DO NOT file. ET art. 606 par. 2 makes the return non-obligatory for such a period. This is unusual: the nomina electronica and PILA have no equivalent exemption, so a month with employees but no withholding still produces those two filings while producing no Form 350. ~ Filing an all-zero return is not an error, but it creates a record that must then be corrected if a late payment is discovered. Prefer not filing where the exemption genuinely applies, and document the decision.
R10| Payment must accompany the return ~ Engine (control) ~ Casilla 980 (pago total) must equal casilla 138 (total retenciones mas sanciones). ET art. 580-1: a withholding return filed WITHOUT full payment produces NO legal effect, and no administrative act is needed to say so. Until the taxpayer files again WITH payment, the original filing is treated as never made. ~ This is the single most expensive procedural trap on this return: a filed-but-unpaid return accrues late-filing penalties as though nothing had been filed.
R11| Do not key the portal-derived casillas ~ Engine (control) ~ The DIAN service derives casillas 28 (from 27), 55, 56, 57, 58, 109, 110, 111 and 112 (from the hoja-2 annex totals 148 to 155), 130, 134, 136, 138 and 147 (145 x 146). Supply only the inputs. Attempting to key a derived casilla either fails or, worse, creates an inconsistency the portal silently overwrites. ~ For the non-resident block this means payroll supplies hoja-2 ROWS - casillas 141 to 146 - and never the hoja-1 casillas 109 to 112.
R12| Counterparty-type separation ~ Engine (accumulation) ~ The 2024 form separates withholding on NATURAL persons from withholding on LEGAL persons across the whole grid. The engine must therefore accumulate withholding by counterparty type from the transaction level, not classify it at reporting time. The pre-2024 form did not require this and a migrated accumulation will not carry the flag. ~ Payroll is inherently natural-person only, so its own accumulation is unambiguous. The risk lies in a shared withholding ledger where supplier and employee withholding are commingled.
R13| === AMOUNT FORMAT RULES ===
R14| All money casillas ~ Engine (formatting) ~ Whole pesos, then rounded to the NEAREST 1,000 under ET art. 577. Apply the rounding ONCE, to the accumulated casilla value, not to each contributing record. No decimals, no thousand separators in the transmitted value. ~ Rounding each employee to the nearest 1,000 and then summing produces a total that can differ from the correctly rounded aggregate by several thousand pesos across a large population.
R15| Three conventions must not be blended ~ Engine (formatting) ~ The SAME withholding appears as three different numbers across the Colombian artefacts: nearest-1,000 here; two decimals round-half-to-even in the nomina electronica; and irrelevant in PILA, which carries contributions not tax. Store the unrounded figure once and apply each convention at output. ~ The reconciliation between casilla 93 and SUM(NIE177) must therefore be performed on the UNROUNDED values with a tolerance, not on the rounded ones.
R16| Minimum penalty ~ Engine (control) ~ Where casilla 137 is populated, the amount cannot be below 10 UVT. For 2026 that is 10 x 52,374 = COP 523,740. For a CORRECTIVE return the minimum is 10 UVT of the year in which the ORIGINAL return was filed, not of the current year. ~ A correction of a 2024 return filed in 2026 uses the 2024 UVT for the minimum penalty. Store the original filing year with the correction.
R17| === PAYROLL-SPECIFIC POPULATION RULES ===
R18| Non-resident employees - hoja 2 rows ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ For each non-resident employee, create one hoja-2 row per country and treaty status. Set casilla 141 to SIN CONVENIO or CON CONVENIO; casilla 142 to concepto 5 (Prestaciones de servicios y compensacion por servicios personales) for non-treaty countries or concepto 24 (Rentas de trabajo) for treaty countries; casilla 143 to Persona natural; casilla 144 to the country of residence; casilla 145 to the GROSS payment - not a depurated base, because ET art. 408 admits no depuration; and casilla 146 to 20.00, or to the treaty rate. The portal computes casilla 147 and the four totals. ~ Rows for the same country, concept and person type may be aggregated. Do NOT aggregate across treaty statuses or across concepts - the rate and the treaty article differ. Directors' fees belong in concepto 25 and employer-paid pensions in concepto 27, never in 24.
R19| Residence switch mid-year ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ Carry a residence flag with an effective date on the employee record and re-evaluate it every period against ET art. 10 - presence exceeding 183 calendar days, continuous or not, in any 365-day period. Until the test is met, report the employee on hoja 2 at the flat 20%. From the payment FOLLOWING the date the test is met, report on hoja 1 casillas 77 and 93 under the art. 383 table. Never restate the earlier months. ~ DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026 expressly rejects withholding under art. 383 from day one in anticipation of future residence, even on a contract exceeding 183 days. Withholding is determined at the moment of each payment on the status then held. There is no payroll true-up; the employee reconciles on their own return.
R20| Work performed entirely abroad ~ Payroll engine (tax) ~ Where a NON-RESIDENT performs the work entirely outside Colombia, the income is foreign-source and there is NO Colombian withholding and nothing to report on this return - neither hoja 1 nor hoja 2. Where a RESIDENT performs work abroad, the income IS taxable on the worldwide basis and goes to casillas 77 and 93 as normal. ~ The distinguishing evidence is the place-of-work country carried on the nomina electronica (NIE050). Keep the two records consistent: a document showing LugarTrabajoPais other than CO for a non-resident should produce no Form 350 line.
R21| Casilla 129 - reversing an over-withholding ~ Payroll engine ~ Where an employee's withholding is corrected downward in a later month - a retroactive change to the art. 387 deductions, a cancelled payment, or an arithmetic error - report the reversal in casilla 129 of the month in which the correction is made, rather than correcting the earlier return. Where the month's withholding is insufficient to absorb the credit, the instructivo permits carrying it to the immediately following periods. ~ Casilla 129 also carries reintegros of non-transferable withholding attributable to a resident natural-person beneficiary or a resident foreign investor under DUR 1625 de 2016 art. 1.2.4.37 - not a payroll case, but the same casilla.
R22| Casilla 27 and the art. 114-1 exoneration ~ Company configuration ~ Populate casilla 27 only where the employer is a LEGAL person exonerated from employer contributions under ET art. 114-1. The SAME exoneration test - employees earning less than 10 SMMLV (COP 17,509,050 in 2026) - drives PILA archivo tipo 1 field 33 and PILA Registro Tipo 2 field 76. Configure the test ONCE and consume it in both places. ~ The exoneration is tested MONTHLY and PER EMPLOYEE for PILA purposes, but the self-withholding activity code here is an entity-level, static attribute. Do not conflate the two granularities.
R23| === CROSS-FIELD VALIDATIONS ===
R24| Casilla 25 and casilla 26 ~ Engine (validation) ~ Both blank on an initial return; both populated on a corrective one. Casilla 26 must carry the casilla-4 form number of the return being corrected. ~ A corrective return with casilla 26 blank is treated as a new initial return and produces a duplicate filing for the period.
R25| Casillas 7-10 versus casilla 11 ~ Engine (validation) ~ A LEGAL person populates casilla 11 and leaves 7 to 10 blank; a NATURAL person does the reverse. The DIAN service auto-fills whichever applies from the RUT. ~ Both blocks populated is a RUT inconsistency, not a form error - resolve it in the RUT.
R26| Casilla 27 and casilla 28 ~ Engine (validation) ~ Casilla 28 is derived from casilla 27 by the portal. Supply 27 only. ~ A hard-coded rate in 28 will be overwritten and may mask a wrong CIIU code in 27.
R27| Casillas 982, 983 and 994 ~ Engine (validation) ~ Where casilla 982 is populated, casilla 983 (professional card number) is mandatory and casilla 994 (with qualifications) becomes available. Where 982 is blank, 983 and 994 must be blank. ~ An unsigned return that required an accountant's or auditor's signature is defective.
R28| Casilla 138 and casilla 980 ~ Engine (validation) ~ They must be equal. Block submission where they are not. ~ ET art. 580-1 makes an underpaid return legally ineffective. This is a hard stop, not a warning.
R29| Cross-report reconciliation ~ Engine (reconciliation) ~ Before filing, assert: SUM(NIE177) over all nomina electronica documents for the month, unrounded, equals the unrounded accumulation behind casilla 93, within a tolerance of one peso per employee. Assert also that the employee count behind casilla 77 equals the number of validated nomina electronica documents for the month. ~ A mismatch almost always means either an employee was paid without a nomina electronica document, or a document was rejected and not re-transmitted. Both are deductibility failures, not just reporting failures.
