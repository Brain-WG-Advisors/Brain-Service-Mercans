## Resposta

**Consequence for the GPM313s:** a GPM312 correction does not amend or replace any monthly GPM313. Each GPM313 is corrected on its own, by filing a newly completed GPM313 for the month whose data is being corrected (GPM313 Aprašas, VMI order VA-121 as amended by VA-105, chapter IV, point 18). Whether you need to do that depends on what the GPM312 change touches.

- **Taxable A-class amounts or withheld GPM change:** the affected GPM313s must be corrected too. EDS reconciles the annual and monthly returns, and rejects a GPM312 with the error that the A-class amount declared in fields L8 and U8 does not match the A-class amounts declared in the monthly GPM313s (fields 5 and 8). Practitioners report the totals must match to the cent, and the fix goes into the GPM313 of the month the payment was actually made. That reconciliation evidence comes from the EDS error text as quoted on tax.lt, not from the rules themselves.
- **Only annual-only data changes:** no GPM313 correction is needed. This covers B-class payments with no GPM withheld, non-taxable A-class payments, and recipient identifiers or codes. None of these appear in GPM313, because B-class payments are declared only in the annual GPM312 unless GPM was withheld on them, and GPM313 field 5 covers only taxable employment-related payments (Aprašas, point 9.1).
- **Errors that cannot be corrected after year-end, in either return:**
  - GPM over-withheld during the tax period, such as a rate set too high or tax withheld on a non-taxable payment;
  - GPM under- or over-withheld on a resident's employment income because NPD was applied wrongly or not at all.
  
  These exceptions appear in Aprašas point 19.2.1–19.2.2 for GPM313, and identically in VMI's GPM312 guidance (VMI seminar "Metinės GPM312… pildymo tvarka", updated 2025-01-21, slide 57).

**How the GPM312 correction is submitted:** by filing a newly completed declaration together with its annexes. That means the full GPM312 with the L and U annexes, not a delta. It is filed electronically via EDS. A correction is required on receipt of a VMI notice of errors in the GPM312, or when the payer finds the errors itself, except for errors that cannot be corrected (slide 57).

The governing GPM312 instrument is VMI order of 2018-02-06 No. VA-9. I could not open its consolidated text live, because vmi.lt blocks automated fetches, so the GPM312 procedure above is cited from VMI's own seminar material rather than from the specific VA-9 point number.
