VA-24 makes two structural changes that a build based on the known state would miss entirely. (1) It adds rule 48.5, a FIFTH marker letter 'U' in U7, for payments to a non-resident relating to work or activity performed abroad, in five sub-cases each tied to a U5 code: employment abroad (01), performer or sport activity abroad (52 or 51), immovable property abroad (17 or 18), movable property not requiring registration in Lithuania (16), and foreign-situs land or immovable rent (23). U7 therefore has FIVE valid values (D, M, P, C, U) while L7 has FOUR (D, M, P, C) - VA-24 did not extend rule 29, so the two annexes no longer share a marker domain and any shared validation list would be wrong. (2) It adds rule 36-1, extending the U annex to payments of a non-resident performer's or sportsperson's Lithuanian activity income made not to the individual but to a third party - a foreign or Lithuanian entity acting in their name. A SECOND FINDING NOT IN THE KNOWN STATE: rule 14 second paragraph excludes gambling stakes from declaration box 4 even though they are reported in L8/U8, so box 4 is not simply SUM(L8)+SUM(U8) for a gambling operator; and rule 17 draws box 7 from L13 only, with no U annex component. RESIDUAL 4 %. (1) Column H 'Position(in file)' is notional: rule 11 prescribes completion in EDS and the order publishes no file layout, byte offsets or XML schema; the EDS GPM312 upload schema would close this and is not in the sources folder. (2) Field lengths in column I are inferred from value domains - the order states no lengths, and rules 25 and 44 actually rely on 'if there is not enough room' as a business rule, which makes the true widths material and unknown. (3) The order names no country code standard for L12 and U14. (4) The number of detail lines per annex page is not stated, so 'Lapo numeris' cannot be validated against a row count.

Rules 22 and 41 then COPY both values onto every L and U annex page, so the submission builder must propagate them rather than re-derive them per page: any divergence between the declaration and an annex page is a structural error, not a data difference, and will fail the submission as a whole rather than one line. | Entity re-registered mid-year; branch that also holds its own TIN; part-period return under rules 61 or 62 where the tax period box still carries the full calendar year; group filing where the payroll entity differs from the contracting entity.
R3: Boxes 4, 5, 6 and 7 | Computed from the annexes | Compute box 5 as SUM(L10)+SUM(U10) (rule 15) and box 6 as SUM(L11)+SUM(U11) (rule 16). Box 4 is SUM(L8)+SUM(U8) MINUS the gambling stake rows, because rule 14 second paragraph excludes stakes paid in by residents and non-residents from box 4 even though they are reported in L8/U8 - the excludable rows are exactly those carrying 'C' in L7/U7. Box 7 is SUM(L13) ONLY, with no U annex component at all, because the double-taxation-relief mechanism is defined only for residents working abroad and the U annex has no L13 counterpart. | Gambling operator where a naive SUM(L8)+SUM(U8) overstates box 4; payer with U annex lines only, where box 7 must still be nil; L line where both L10 and L11 carry amounts for the same payment, which must feed boxes 5 and 6 without double-counting the payment in box 4.
R4: L1 / U1 - recipient identifier | Employee master data + AP vendor master | Apply the fallback cascade in the order the rules set, and set L2/U2 to match whichever branch was taken - never source the type code independently. Default to the taxpayer identification number. For a business-certificate payee use the FIRST EIGHT characters of the certificate number and drop the version suffix after the hyphen. Failing that use the VAT payer code with the 'LT' prefix, then the individual-activity certificate number. The final fallback DIFFERS between annexes: L1 allows passport series and number with one blank cell between them, identity card number or residence permit number, while U1 allows ONLY the Lithuanian residence permit number. U1 alone may be left empty where the payer holds no data (rule 42 final paragraph), and U2 is then also left empty (rule 43 final paragraph). Rules 37 and 38 additionally place ENTITY identification numbers in U1 with U2 = 6. | Payee with several business certificates for the same activity; certificate number whose version suffix must be stripped; resident with no TIN and only a passport, requiring the blank-cell composite; non-resident with neither TIN nor permit, leaving U1 and U2 empty; entity routed through the U annex under rules 37-38 taking U2 = 6.
R5: L3 / U3 - names | Employee master data | Assemble from first name and surname with a single blank cell between them - do not map from a display-name field, which risks the wrong separator or a reversed order. Where the individual is registered with several first names and/or surnames, state all of them; where there is not enough room, state one first name and one surname only. Because the order publishes no field width, the 'not enough room' trigger cannot be implemented deterministically until EDS field lengths are confirmed, so a configurable width is required rather than a hard-coded truncation point. | Multiple given names and multiple surnames exceeding the field; Lithuanian diacritics that must not be transliterated; non-Latin source names on the U annex; entity name placed in U3 under rules 37-38; married name change during the tax period.
R6: L4 / U4 - payment class | Derived from payment type and recipient status | Derive from Annex 2, not from a static attribute of the wage type: for codes 02, 16, 17, 18, 27, 55, 56, 58, 59, 64, 65, 66 and 67 the class turns on whether the recipient is resident or non-resident, so the SAME wage type can be class A on the U annex and class B on the L annex. Leave the field EMPTY - the only case in either annex where it is blank - where a gambling operator reports stakes paid in, per rules 26 and 45. | Wage type mapped to a class-dependent code where the employee's residence status changed during the year; small partnership member payments under code 02 or 77; gambling stake row with L4/U4 blank; code 93 which is class A only for the note-5 categories (timber, waste, sport, performer).
R7: L5 / U5 - payment type code | Wage type mapping table | Map every wage type to one of the 63 Annex 2 codes. Two overrides are explicit in rules 27 and 46: employer-paid sickness benefit for the FIRST TWO DAYS of illness goes to code 03, not to the ordinary employment code 01; and a gambling operator reporting stakes paid in uses code 42, the same code as winnings paid out, with the direction distinguished only by the 'C' marker. Note that U5 now also drives the new 'U' marker: rule 48.5 ties each of its five sub-cases to a specific code (01, 52/51, 17/18, 16, 23), so the code-to-marker relationship must be modelled rather than left to manual entry. | First-two-days sickness incorrectly mapped to 01; legacy code 04 for pre-2019 accruals paid later; employer versus non-employer royalties (60 against 61); benefits in kind under codes 73 and 74 that are exempt but still declarable; code 15 waste payments carrying the unique 5 % first band.
R8: L6 / U6 - payment in kind | Wage type in-kind flag | Populate only for payments in kind, using A for the personal use of a car belonging to another person and N for everything else in kind, and leave the field entirely empty for cash. Treat this as a LINE-SPLITTING driver, not just an attribute: rules 28 and 47 require that where part of a payment of the same class and type was made in kind and part in cash during the tax period, the two parts are reported on separate annex lines. A build that stores the flag but does not split will produce the right totals on the wrong number of lines. | Company car benefit alongside cash salary under the same code 01; in-kind gift under code 07 partly settled in cash; employer-paid insurance premiums under codes 73 and 74 which are in kind by nature; benefit granted in one month and cash paid in another within the same tax period.
R9: L7 - special circumstance marker (4 values) | Derived from the payment scenario | Populate only in the four cases of rule 29 and leave empty otherwise. D where the individual-activity payment came from a person who was the payee's employer at the time of payment, splitting the line where those relations began or ended mid-year. M for small partnership payments to its own member under civil services or works contracts, subject to the EUR 100,000 annual ceiling and split at the point membership began or ended. P where the payment is specifically for LAND rent inside code 23. C where a gambling operator reports stakes paid in. Do NOT accept 'U' here: order VA-24 added that value to U7 only and left rule 29 untouched, so L7 and U7 have different domains and a shared validation list would wrongly admit 'U' on resident lines. | Employee who became an individual-activity contractor mid-year, requiring a D split; small partnership member who left during the year, requiring an M split; land rent under code 23 that must carry P while building rent under the same code must not; stake row that must carry C and also be excluded from box 4.
R10: U7 - special circumstance marker (5 values, incl. new 'U') | Derived from the payment scenario | Populate the four L-equivalent cases as above, then apply rule 48.5 letter U - added by order VA-24 of 2026-03-26 - for payments to a non-resident relating to work or activity performed ABROAD, in five sub-cases each tied to a U5 code: employment abroad (01), performer or sport activity abroad (52 or 51), sale or transfer of immovable property abroad (17 or 18), sale or transfer of movable property not subject to compulsory registration in Lithuania (16), and rent of foreign-situs land or other inherently immovable property (23). The purpose is to separate non-Lithuanian-source non-resident income - reportable under rule 36 even though rule 49.3 confirms it may not be an object of tax under GPMI art. 5(4) - from Lithuanian-source income, so the marker must be driven from the place of performance and not from the taxability outcome. | Non-resident employee working partly in Lithuania and partly abroad, requiring a line split with and without U; performer income routed to a third party under new rule 36-1; foreign property rent under code 23 that could arguably take P or U, where 48.5.5 makes it U; first tax period of application, since VA-24 was published after the 2025 filing deadline.
R11: L8 / U8 - payment amount | Payroll results + AP | Aggregate per person per class per payment type per GPM rate, applying the four population cases in rules 30 and 49: taxable class A gross of NPD, GPM, VSD and PSD; non-taxable class A; class B gross of any GPM withheld or borne by the payer; and gambling stakes, which the rules state in EURO rather than euro and cents. Report amounts to VAT-registered payees NET of VAT. For individual-activity payees on the accrual basis - VAT-registered, or using long-term assets in the activity - report in the tax period the invoice was ISSUED rather than when paid, establishing the basis from information the payee supplies. Enter treaty-relieved employment income for work abroad as NON-TAXABLE class A and complete L13. On the U annex, enter payments regardless of whether they are an object of tax under GPMI art. 5(4). | Same person receiving taxable and non-taxable class A plus class B, needing three or more lines; same code taxed at two rates after crossing 36 VDU, needing a rate split; VAT-registered payee where VAT must be stripped; accrual-basis payee invoiced in December and paid in January; treaty-relieved expatriate income that must appear as non-taxable with L13 completed; gambling stakes stated without cents.
R12: L9 / U9 - GPM rate | Payroll results | Enter the rate actually applied to the amount on that line, including where NPD reduced the GPM to zero - a zero in L10/U10 does not license a blank rate. The zero-rate rules DIFFER between annexes and must not be shared: rule 31.2 requires zero in FOUR L-annex cases (non-taxable class A including treaty-relieved expatriate income; class B with no GPM withheld or borne; intermediated dividends, interest or royalties from foreign entities; gambling stakes), while rule 50.2 requires zero in only TWO U-annex cases (non-taxable class A or class B with no GPM; gambling stakes) - the intermediary case has no U-annex counterpart here. | NPD reducing GPM to zero while the rate must still be stated; intermediated foreign dividends requiring zero on the L annex but not covered by the U annex rule; treaty rate below the domestic rate; rate change mid-year requiring separate lines for the same code.
R13: L10 / L11 / U10 / U11 - GPM withheld and GPM borne | Payroll results | Keep withheld GPM (L10/U10) strictly separate from GPM borne by the payer (L11/U11), because they feed different declaration boxes (5 and 6) and have different consequences for the individual. Where BOTH occurred on the same payment, rules 33.1 and 52.1 require ONE annex line carrying amounts in both fields - the line must not be duplicated. Enter zero or leave empty in the four rule 32.2 / 33.2 cases on the L annex and the three rule 51.2 / 52.2 cases on the U annex; note again that the intermediated-dividends case appears in the L rules and not the U rules. | Payer bearing part of the GPM and withholding the rest on one payment, which must stay on a single line; net-pay guarantee arrangements where all GPM is borne by the payer; intermediated dividends requiring empty L10 and L11 but a country code in L12; gambling stake row where both fields are empty.
R14: L12 / L13 - foreign country and foreign tax | Employee master data + payroll results | L12 carries two different meanings and must not be collapsed into one: under rule 34.1 it is the SOURCE state of intermediated dividends, interest or royalties, and under rule 34.2 it is the state to whose budget foreign income tax was paid on employment income for work performed there. The second creates a hard dependency - where L12 is populated under rule 34.2, L13 MUST be completed, and where no foreign tax was actually paid into that budget rule 35 requires ZERO rather than a blank. Where L13 carries an amount, the return must be accompanied by a document proving the foreign tax was paid, having regard to the treaty, so the field carries an attachment obligation as well as a value. L13 alone feeds declaration box 7. | Expatriate assignment to a treaty state where foreign tax is nil, requiring L13 = 0 rather than blank; assignment to a non-treaty state; intermediated dividends populating L12 under 34.1 with L13 left empty; missing foreign tax certificate at the filing deadline; work split across two foreign states in one tax period.
R15: U12 / U13 / U14 / U15 - non-resident identification | Employee master data | U12 date of birth is stated unconditionally by rule 53, with none of the 'may be left empty' escapes that rules 42, 43 and 54 give the other non-resident fields, so it must be treated as a hard requirement in the interface. U13 takes the foreign TIN, falling back to a foreign identity document number or another identification code from a document issued abroad, and may be left empty where the individual has none; rule 54 points to the European Commission TIN portals for structural checks of EU codes. U14 takes the country of residence, but rule 55 overrides it for the rules 37-38 entity cases, where it carries the residence of the DIVIDEND- or INTEREST-RECEIVING ENTITY rather than an individual. U15 takes the foreign residential address as free text. | Non-resident with no foreign TIN and no identity document number, leaving U13 empty while U12 must still be supplied; dual-resident individual where the treaty tie-breaker decides U14; entity in U14 under rules 37-38; address that exceeds the unpublished field width; date of birth held only as a year in the source system.
R16: Whole submission - amendment | Period control | Rule 63: amend by submitting a freshly completed declaration and L and/or U annexes for the tax period being corrected - there is no delta form. Rule 64.2 bars correcting two classes of error: GPM under- or over-withheld on resident employment income where NPD was applied incorrectly or not applied, and GPM over-withheld generally. Rule 65 adds a cross-report obligation that is easy to miss: where the amended payment or GPM amounts were reported in aggregate on a monthly GPM313, that GPM313 must be amended too - so one GPM312 correction can cascade into up to twelve GPM313 amendments. | Error found in one employee's L line that changes a monthly GPM313 total; VMI information notice with its own deadline (rule 66); error falling under rule 64.2 that cannot be corrected at all; amendment that changes the number of annex pages and therefore all 'Lapo numeris' values.
