Title: Withholding for Employers | Department of Taxes (Vermont)
Notice: The Social Security Administration requires electronic reporting of W-2s and 1099s for anyone who files 10 or more returns. Therefore, employers who will submit 10 or more W-2 and/or 1099 forms must file Vermont Form WHT-434 and the accompanying forms electronically.
Vermont Withholding: Employers should request that each employee complete a W-4VT, Employee Withholding Allowance Certificate, in addition to a federal Form W-4 ... Even employees who have recently updated their W-4s should make sure that they have completed the most recent version of the W-4VT.
Hiring Relocated and Remote Workers: Although all income earned in Vermont is considered Vermont income, out-of-state employers are not required to begin withholding Vermont Income Tax until an employee has been working from a Vermont location for thirty days. If an employee lives and works remotely in Vermont, then income earned during the entire period of time that they live in Vermont is subject to Vermont income tax. This is true even if they claim another state as their domicile.
Payment Frequency: Generally, your payment frequency will mirror your federal payment frequency ...
Quarterly: pay when you file Form WHT-436, Quarterly Withholding Reconciliation.
Monthly: You must pay withholding tax for the month by the 25th day of the following month (e.g. June by July 25). (Exception: January payment is due the 23rd day of February but is extended to the following Monday if the 23rd occurs on a weekend.) Third month of the quarter paid with WHT-436.
Semiweekly: Tax withheld for pay dates on Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday is due the following Wednesday. Tax withheld for all other pay dates is due the following Friday.
Quarterly Reconciliation: All taxpayers (Semiweekly, Monthly, and Quarterly) are required to file Form WHT-436 at the close of each quarter.
Annual Reconciliation: All taxpayers are now required to file Form WHT-434, Annual Withholding Reconciliation, due the last day of January. This form serves as the transmittal for forms W-2 or 1099 ...
How to File: myVTax. You must file and pay electronically if: you submit 10 or more W-2 and/or 1099 forms with Form WHT-434, or you pay withholding tax on the semi-weekly schedule, or a payroll filing service is used, or your total withholding remitted for the prior calendar year exceeded $100,000.
Paper allowed only if fewer than 10 W-2/1099, quarterly or monthly schedule, no payroll filing service, and cannot file through myVTax.
