State of Alaska Posters

Some of these posters are in PDF format and may be downloaded and printed at your convenience. If you prefer a printed copy of the posters listed below, write or call the address listed below the poster's name.

9. 2025 SUMMARY OF ALASKA WAGE & HOUR ACT (Effective Jan. 1, 2025)

2025 SUMMARY (Effective Jan. 1, 2026)

These documents print on a single 8 1/2" x 14" legal sheet of paper.

10. EMERGENCY INFORMATION

This poster provides employers with the OSHA-AKOSH requirements for reporting fatalities and serious injuries, and the telephone numbers to make the report.

11. IT'S YOUR RIGHT TO KNOW - Safety and Health Protection on the Job

This download prints landscape on two 8 1/2 x 11 pages with .5" margins. The two pages may be taped together to form an 11x17 poster. The printed poster is also available through the Alaska Labor Standards and Safety Division.Write, call or email:

12. NOTICE TO EMPLOYEES - UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE
Alaska employers are required by law to post this notice.

13. REQUIRED NOTICE TO SEPARATED EMPLOYEES - UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE
Alaska employers are required to provide this notice to employees at the time of separation.

These posters are available through the Division of Employment and Training Services.

14. SUMMARY OF ALASKA CHILD LABOR LAW
This poster provides employers with a summary of the most current work restrictions for hiring minors. It prints landscape on 8 ½ x 14 paper. For further information use the contact numbers or email link listed under Item 9.

15. Recordkeeping Requirements
This poster is not required to be posted but provides Employers with valuable information about the various recordkeeping requirements Alaskan Employers must abide by.

16. State of Alaska ADA Policy Posters (for SOA Internal Departments)
This poster is for State of Alaska agencies. Posting the ADA Policy Poster provides Notice per Administrative Order 129, Section X. This is required to ensure State employees, and the public know their rights and responsibilities under the ADA, where to find access and accommodation, and where to file complaints. Wide distribution of the poster is encouraged to facilitate posting in every State facility and office.
