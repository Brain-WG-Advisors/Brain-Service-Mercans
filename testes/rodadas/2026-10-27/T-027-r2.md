## Resposta

**Consequence for the GPM313s:** they must be corrected too. Where the GPM312 correction changes payment and/or income tax amounts that were declared in aggregate in the monthly GPM313, the GPM313 forms for the relevant months of the same tax period (calendar year) being corrected must also be corrected. This is because VMI cross-checks the two: taxable payment amounts in GPM312 must match the taxable payment amounts declared in the GPM313s (payments are declared in the period according to the date of payment).

**How the GPM312 correction is submitted:** by filing a newly completed declaration together with its annexes (L for residents, U for non-residents), electronically via EDS. It is triggered either on receipt of a VMI notice about errors in the GPM312, or when the payer itself notices errors.

**Errors that cannot be corrected this way:** under- or over-withheld tax on employment payments to a Lithuanian resident caused by NPD being applied incorrectly or not applied, and tax over-withheld during the tax period on payments made to residents in that period.

**Governing instrument:** VMI order No. VA-9 of 2018-02-06 approving the GPM312 form, its GPM312L and GPM312U annexes, and their completion and submission rules (correction chapter). For the monthly side, the GPM313 rules are VMI order No. VA-121 of 2017-12-20, restated by order No. VA-105 of 2025-11-20.

Note: e-TAR blocked live access to the VA-9 consolidated text, so I couldn't confirm the exact point (punktas) number of the correction rule. The wording above comes from VMI's own GPM312 page and its 2025 GPM312 guidance deck ("Deklaracijos GPM312 pateikimas, tikslinimas" section).
