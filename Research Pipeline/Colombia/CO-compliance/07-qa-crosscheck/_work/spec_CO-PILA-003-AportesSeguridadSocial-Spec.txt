R7| - THE SOCIAL-SECURITY CHANNEL IS NOT THE TAX CHANNEL. PILA never touches DIAN Muisca, and Formulario 350 never touches an operador de informacion. Labelling either with the other's portal is a category error.
R8| - Two modalities exist: 1 Electronica (the employer keys or uploads on the operador's site) and 2 Asistida (the operador's call centre keys it). Where Decreto 780 de 2016 art. 3.2.3.9 par. 2 exceptionally permits assisted payment, the modality field must still say ELECTRONICA and the operador reports those employers to the Ministry through SISPRO structure PUB019EXPE by the fifth business day of the following month.
R9| - The layout is byte-exact and published, so this Spec CAN and DOES carry a real fixed-width sample on Sheet 9 - generated programmatically from the position table so that the record lengths are exactly 567, 359 and 686 characters.
R10| - FORMAT TO CONFIRM vs a real filing: each operador wraps the two files in its own upload envelope and may add validations. Confirm the wrapper and any operador-specific rules with the client's chosen operador before go-live.
R12| INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
R13| - integration_type = BOTH (scope_manifest CO-BOTH-PILA). Outbound: the two fixed-width files. Inbound: the operador's settlement response.
R14| - The inbound leg (CO-IN-PILA-SETTLEMENT) is specified here rather than in a separate workbook because it is the response half of one exchange and is keyed on this filing's own planilla number (T2R1-017). The engine must reconcile the settled contributions against its own computation, capture the planilla number and payment date, and detect rejected contributor records. Each operador's response layout is proprietary and must be obtained from the client's chosen operador - it has no published national standard, so no field-level spec can honestly be written for it here.
R15| - product_mapping_required = true. PILA needs FIVE contribution bases per employee (pension, health, occupational risk, CCF, and the separate uncapped parafiscal base), FOUR day counts, and sixteen change-event flags with their date pairs. None of these can be derived at reporting time from a single gross figure.
R16| - Owned upstream (CCG control B7): the operador de informacion's own file wrapper and portal. Mercans produces the two annex-conformant files; the operador owns everything after that.
R18| RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT (v2.8 control B1)
R19| - PILA IS NOT A TAX FILING and carries NO residence concept. A non-resident employee working in Colombia under a Colombian contract contributes to social security on exactly the same terms as a resident - same bases, same rates, same ceiling and floor. The flat 20% of ET art. 408 has no application here.
R20| - The one immigration-related field is 'Foreign national not liable to contribute to pensions' (T2R2-007), and it is about PENSION AFFILIATION, not tax residence: a foreign employee who proves affiliation to a pension system abroad may elect out of Colombian pension affiliation under Decreto 1072 de 2015 art. 2.2.1.2.1.4. Health, occupational risk and the parafiscal levies remain fully payable.
R21| - It follows that the same employee can be simultaneously (a) withheld at 20% on hoja 2 of Formulario 350 as a non-resident, and (b) reported in PILA on ordinary terms. Do not let the tax residence flag leak into the PILA population rules.
R22| - The mirror case - 'Colombian abroad' (T2R2-008) - is also not a tax field. It suppresses the DIVIPOLA work-location fields and enables the foreign filing date in T2R2-097.
R24| RELATED FILINGS / CROSS-REFERENCES
R25| - The employee health, pension and solidarity-fund figures must reconcile to nomina electronica NIE163, NIE166 and NIE168 / NIE170 (CO-DIAN-001), allowing for the different rounding conventions - PILA rounds UP to the next 100, the XML uses two decimals round-half-to-even.
R26| - The ET art. 114-1 exoneration in A1-033 and T2R2-076 rests on the SAME test as Formulario 350 casilla 27 (CO-DIAN-002). Configure it once.
R27| - The employee's mandatory health and pension contributions - the amounts settled here - are the ingresos no constitutivos de renta that reduce the Formulario 350 casilla 77 base and are reported annually at Formulario 220 casillas 53-54 (CO-DIAN-004) and Formato 2276 numerals 23-24 (CO-DIAN-005).
R28| - PILA planilla types T and F (Sistema General de Participaciones) and types I and Y (independent workers) are DESCOPED in scope_manifest - public sector and non-employees respectively.
R29| - The occupational-risk premium (T2R2-063) and the employer's share of pension, health and the levies never appear on the nomina electronica, because that document reports only what the employee accrued and what was deducted from the employee.

===== SHEET 'Payroll Scope Summary'  dims=A1:C47  max_row=47 max_col=3 =====
R1| PAYROLL SCOPE SUMMARY - INTEGRATED CONTRIBUTION SETTLEMENT RETURN (PILA) - SOCIAL SECURITY AND PAYROLL LEVIES
R2| SCENARIO ~ REPORT? ~ NOTES / REASONING
R3| === WHO GETS REPORTED ===
R4| Indefinite-term employee (contrato a termino indefinido) ~ YES ~ The core private-sector population. CST art. 47; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 5.
R5| Fixed-term employee (contrato a termino fijo, max 4 years) ~ YES ~ Identical reporting treatment to an indefinite-term contract. CST art. 46; Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 6.
R6| Specific-work or task employee (contrato por obra o labor) ~ YES ~ An employment contract; CST art. 46. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 3 (Obra o Labor).
R7| Occasional / accidental / transitory worker (< 1 month, non-core activity) ~ YES ~ Still an employee under CST art. 6. Reported on every filing; the PILA planilla type and the day counts differ, not the obligation.
R8| Part-time employee ~ YES ~ Colombia has no separate part-time regime. Reported normally; the PILA contribution base is floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) except for the worker type 51 partial-time regime.
R9| All-inclusive salary employee (salario integral, >= 13 SMMLV) ~ YES ~ CST art. 132 num. 2. Reported with the integral-salary flag set; the contribution base is 70% of the integral salary, and the nomina electronica carries SalarioIntegral = true.
R10| Apprentice (contrato de aprendizaje) ~ YES ~ Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 21 converted the apprenticeship into a special fixed-term LABOUR contract, regulated by Decreto 0223 of 5 March 2026. Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 4; PILA worker type 12 (lectiva) or 19 (productiva).
R11| Intern / practical placement (practicas o pasantias) ~ YES ~ Nomina electronica TipoContrato = 5. Reported where remunerated.
R12| Teleworkers and remote workers ~ YES ~ Ley 1221 de 2008 as amended by Ley 2466 de 2025 arts. 52-54. No reporting difference; the nomina electronica carries a dedicated Teletrabajo devengado element (NIE158).
R13| Resident foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ Full reporting. Withheld under the ET art. 383 progressive table once tax residence is acquired.
R14| NON-RESIDENT foreign national under a Colombian contract ~ YES ~ IN SCOPE and frequently mis-configured. Withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the GROSS, with no art. 388 depuration, until the 183-day residence test is met (DIAN Concepto 011108 of 29 June 2026). See the resident / non-resident block in the Report Matrix.
R15| Employed old-age pensioner in active service ~ YES ~ PILA worker sub-type 01; no pension contribution (PILA pension rate field = 0), health and the levies still apply.
R16| Executive director / statutory legal representative holding an employment contract ~ YES ~ Reported as an ordinary employee where an employment contract exists.
R17| Independent contractor (contrato de prestacion de servicios) ~ NO ~ Not an employee. The contractor self-files PILA on 40% of fees (planilla types I / Y, descoped) and is reported by the payer in the third-party information return, not in this employer filing. Withholding on their fees does pass through Formulario 350, but under a different casilla.
R18| Public-sector employee (contributor type 47, SGP-funded entity) ~ NO ~ Public-sector only; PILA planilla types T and F are descoped in scope_manifest.
R19| Non-executive director paid honorarios only (junta directiva) ~ NO ~ Not employment income. Reported by the payer as honorarios, not as rentas de trabajo of an employee.
R20| Pensioner whose pension is paid by a fund, not by the employer ~ NO ~ The paying fund is the withholding agent and the reporting party, not the employer.
R21| === AMOUNTS AND CALCULATIONS ===
R22| Salary and salary-constituting payments ~ YES ~ The core of all five contribution bases (CST art. 127).
R23| Non-salary payments within the 40% clamp ~ NO ~ Excluded from the contribution base - but only up to 40% of total remuneration. Ley 1393 de 2010 art. 30 adds the EXCESS over 40% back into the base, and that added-back amount must be separately identifiable because it is in the contribution base but out of the statutory-benefit base.
R24| Statutory transport allowance ~ NO ~ Not salary for contribution purposes. It IS in the base of cesantias, prima and the severance indemnity - a different base, and one of the sharpest traps in Colombian payroll.
R25| Paid rest days ~ YES ~ In the PARAFISCAL base (Ley 21 de 1982 art. 17) - campo T2R2-095 - which is why that base differs from the four contribution bases.
R26| Contribution base ceiling of 25 SMMLV ~ YES ~ COP 43,772,625 in 2026. Applies to pension, health, occupational risk and the CCF. Does NOT apply to the parafiscal base in campo 95, which is uncapped.
R27| Contribution base floor of 1 SMMLV ~ YES ~ COP 1,750,905 in 2026, except for the partial-time regime (contributor type 51), which uses hours worked in campo 96.
R28| All-inclusive salary at 70% of the integral salary ~ YES ~ CST art. 132 num. 2. Flagged in campo 41 and applied to all four IBC fields.
R29| Employer contributions ~ YES ~ PILA is the ONLY artefact that reports the employer's cost - pension 12%, health 8.5%, occupational risk 0.348% to 8.7%, CCF 4%, SENA 2%, ICBF 3%.
R30| Employee deductions ~ YES ~ Pension 4%, health 4%, solidarity fund 1% to 2%. Reported as part of the total contribution, not as a separate deduction line.
R31| Income tax withholding ~ NO ~ PILA is a contribution settlement, not a tax return. Withholding goes on Formulario 350.
R32| SENA and ICBF for employees under 10 SMMLV where the employer is exonerated ~ NO ~ ET art. 114-1 exonerates employer health 8.5%, SENA 2% and ICBF 3%. Flagged at employer level in A1-033 and applied per employee per month in T2R2-076.
R33| CCF 4% for the same employees ~ YES ~ The family compensation fund is NEVER exonerated under art. 114-1. The only relief is the Ley 1429 de 2010 art. 5 regime, flagged separately in A1-034.
R34| === TIMING AND RELATIONSHIP ===
R35| Reported on the accrual (causacion) date ~ NO ~ Every Colombian payroll filing is keyed to the PAYMENT or credit-in-account date, not to the accrual date. A December salary paid in January falls in the January filing.
R36| Reported on the payment or credit-in-account date ~ YES ~ The universal Colombian rule - pago o abono en cuenta. Applies to the nomina electronica (Res. DIAN 000013 de 2021 art. 3), to Formulario 350, to Formulario 220 and to Formato 2276.
R37| A semi-monthly payroll produces two filings a month ~ NO ~ It does not. The nomina electronica is expressly ONE monthly document per employee 'irrespective of whether the obliged party makes payments in different or shorter periods' (art. 3). PILA and Formulario 350 are likewise monthly.
R38| Nil period still filed ~ VARIES ~ Formulario 350: NOT required where no operation subject to withholding occurred in the period (ET art. 606 par. 2). Nomina electronica: no document where there was no payment. PILA: a planilla is still required while the employment relationship subsists.
R39| Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period ~ VARIES ~ Nomina electronica: NO - a dedicated adjustment note (NominaIndividualDeAjuste, Reemplazar or Eliminar) referencing the original CUNE. Formulario 350: yes, a corrective return. PILA: a planilla type N (correcciones) or M (mora).
R40| === SPECIAL CASES ===
R41| Health period one month ahead of the other subsystems ~ YES ~ T2R1-016 normally carries the month AFTER T2R1-015: health is paid one month in advance. Setting them equal is the classic first-run defect.
R42| Employee on unpaid leave for the whole month ~ YES ~ Reported with the SLN flag, its date pair, and reduced day counts. The employment relationship subsists, so the planilla is still filed.
R43| Employed old-age pensioner ~ YES ~ Contributor sub-type 01. The annex forces the pension rate in campo 46 to zero for sub-types 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 12.
R44| Foreign employee with proven pension cover abroad ~ YES ~ Flagged in campo 7. Pension is waived; health, occupational risk and the levies remain.
R45| Employee at more than one work centre with different risk classes ~ YES ~ The class attaches to the WORK CENTRE (Decreto 1072 de 2015 art. 2.2.4.2.5.6). Where a person performs several occupations they contribute at the HIGHEST class; a mid-period move is reported through the VCT flag and its date pair.
R46| Contributor type 40 (Beneficiario UPC Adicional) ~ PARTIAL ~ Reported as a detail record but EXCLUDED from the header contributor count in T2R1-019, and with its DIVIPOLA work-location fields blank.
R47| Late payment ~ YES ~ Filed as planilla type M (mora), or N for a correction, with the associated planilla number and date in T2R1-009 and T2R1-010. The operador computes the interest on arrears.

===== SHEET 'Population Rules'  dims=A1:D30  max_row=30 max_col=4 =====
R1| POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
R2| Field Name ~ Source System ~ Population Logic ~ Edge Cases
R3| === MANDATORY FIELD RULES ===
R4| The five contribution bases (T2R2-042 to T2R2-045 and T2R2-095) ~ Payroll engine ~ Compute and STORE five separate bases per employee per month. Four are the IBC for pension, health, occupational risk and the CCF: total salary-constituting payments, plus the excess over the 40% non-salary clamp of Ley 1393 de 2010 art. 30, floored at 1 SMMLV (COP 1,750,905) and capped at 25 SMMLV (COP 43,772,625). The fifth is the PARAFISCAL base of campo 95, defined by Ley 21 de 1982 art. 17: it INCLUDES paid rest days and it is UNCAPPED. Never populate all five from one variable. ~ The four IBC values legitimately differ from one another once a change event enters - for example health may remain payable during an unpaid leave where pension does not. The header total in T2R1-020 is the sum of campo 95, NOT of campo 45; using the CCF base there is a silent, systematic understatement for any employer whose employees earn above the ceiling or who has significant paid rest days.
R5| The four day counts (T2R2-036 to T2R2-039) ~ Payroll engine ~ Maintain a contributory-day counter PER SUBSYSTEM on the 30-day commercial month. Start at 30 (or the proportion from a mid-month hire or to a mid-month leave), then apply the change events: SLN and lawful strike reduce all four; IGE and IRL follow the subsystem-specific rules; vacation and remunerated leave do not reduce them at all because they are paid. ~ February is 30 days and a 31-day month is 30 days. The counts must reconcile to the novedad date pairs: a five-day SLN in a full month gives 25 contributory days and dates in T2R2-083 and T2R2-084 that span exactly five days.
R6| Health period one month ahead (T2R1-016) ~ Engine (control) ~ Set the health payment period to the month AFTER the period in T2R1-015. Health is paid one month in advance; the other subsystems are paid in arrears. A March salary settlement carries period 2026-03 in T2R1-015 and 2026-04 in T2R1-016. ~ Setting the two equal is the single most common first-run defect. Note also the split of responsibility the annex records: T2R1-015 is CALCULATED BY THE OPERADOR, while T2R1-016 is SUPPLIED BY THE EMPLOYER.
R7| Exoneration flags (A1-033 and T2R2-076) ~ Engine (control) ~ A1-033 is an EMPLOYER-LEVEL, standing declaration that the employer qualifies under ET art. 114-1. T2R2-076 is a PER-EMPLOYEE, PER-MONTH application of it, and may be S only where A1-033 is S. The employee test is that the health base (T2R2-043) is BELOW 10 SMMLV = COP 17,509,050 in 2026; at or above that it MUST be N. Where a NATURAL-person employer employs two or more workers and the base reaches 10 SMMLV it must also be N. Where T2R2-076 is S, set T2R2-054 to the EMPLOYEE rate alone (4.0000%), and set the SENA and ICBF rates and values (T2R2-066 to T2R2-069) to zero. ~ Because the test is MONTHLY, an employee who crosses 10 SMMLV in a bonus month loses the exoneration for that month alone and regains it the next. Evaluating it annually, or at hire, systematically under-contributes. The same test drives Formulario 350 casilla 27.
R8| === CRITICAL VALIDATION RULES ===
R9| Header-to-detail consistency ~ Engine (validation) ~ Before submission assert: T2R1-004, 005, 006 and 007 equal Archivo Tipo 1 campos 1, 2, 3 and 4 byte for byte; T2R1-011, 012 and 013 equal Archivo Tipo 1 campos 10, 5 and 6; T2R1-021 equals Archivo Tipo 1 campo 30; T2R1-019 equals the count of UNIQUE contributors in the detail EXCLUDING those with contributor type 40; and T2R1-020 equals the sum of T2R2-095 across the detail. ~ The type-40 exclusion from the contributor count is easy to miss and produces a header-to-detail mismatch the operador rejects outright.
R10| Pension rate forced to zero ~ Engine (validation) ~ Where the contributor sub-type (T2R2-006) is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 or 12, the pension rate (T2R2-046) MUST be 0 and no pension contribution may be settled. ~ The nomina electronica equivalent is narrower - only SubTipoTrabajador 01 forces the rate to zero there. Map between the two lists; do not assume they agree.
R11| Health rate may be zero only in two cases ~ Engine (validation) ~ The annex is explicit: T2R2-054 may be reported as zero ONLY where (a) SLN (T2R2-024) is X and the exoneration flag (T2R2-076) is S, or (b) SLN is C and the employer's legal nature is public. And where the health contribution (T2R2-055) is zero, the health days (T2R2-037), the health base (T2R2-043) and the health rate (T2R2-054) must ALL be zero - except for those same SLN-plus-exoneration contributors. ~ This is a four-field interlock. Implement it as one validation, not four independent ones.
R12| Novedad flag to date-pair interlock ~ Engine (validation) ~ Every marked novedad flag must have its corresponding date field or pair populated, and every populated date field must have its flag marked: ING to 080, RET to 081, VSP to 082, SLN to 083 and 084, IGE to 085 and 086, LMA to 087 and 088, VAC-LR to 089 and 090, VCT to 091 and 092, IRL days to 093 and 094, TAE to 034, TAP to 032, AVP to 048 and 049. ~ Build the interlock as a table, not as a chain of conditionals - there are twelve pairs and a missed one produces a rejection at the operador rather than at Mercans.
R13| Contribution calculation checks published in the annex ~ Engine (validation) ~ The annex prints the arithmetic the operador will apply: campo 55 = campo 43 x campo 54; campo 65 = campo 45 x campo 64; campo 67 = campo 95 x campo 66; campo 69 = campo 95 x campo 68; campo 71 = campo 95 x campo 70; campo 73 = campo 95 x campo 72. Note that the CCF uses the CCF base while SENA, ICBF, ESAP and MEN all use the PARAFISCAL base. ~ Reproduce these checks internally before submission. Each must then be rounded UP to the next multiple of 100.
R14| === AMOUNT FORMAT RULES ===
R15| Rounding is UP, not to nearest ~ Engine (formatting) ~ Round the contribution base UP to the whole peso, and round EACH contribution value UP to the next multiple of 100 (Decreto 1990 de 2016 art. 1). Worked illustration: occupational risk at class I on a base of 12,000,000 is 0.522% = 62,640, which becomes 62,700. ~ This is neither the nomina electronica convention (2 decimals, round-half-to-even) nor the Formulario 350 convention (nearest 1,000). Blending them produces reconciliation differences that look like errors but are not - the reconciliation must be run on the UNROUNDED values with a tolerance.
R16| Numeric and alphanumeric justification ~ Engine (formatting) ~ Numeric fields are right-justified and ZERO-filled to the left; alphanumeric fields are left-justified and SPACE-filled to the right. The annex states this at s.0 and it applies without exception across all three records. ~ An empty numeric field is a run of zeros, not a run of spaces. This catches optional numeric fields such as A1-016 (Fax) and the unused parafiscal rate and value fields.
R17| Rate fields carry implied decimals ~ Engine (formatting) ~ The rate fields are numeric with implied decimals in their published width - seven positions for T2R2-046, 054, 064, 066, 068, 070 and 072, and nine for T2R2-061. Confirm the implied decimal placement with the chosen operador before go-live; the annex does not print it unambiguously for every rate field. ~ FORMAT TO CONFIRM. This is the one presentation detail of the layout that the annex leaves under-specified, and it differs between the seven-position and nine-position rate fields.
R18| === PAYROLL-SPECIFIC POPULATION RULES ===
R19| The 40% non-salary clamp ~ Payroll engine ~ Total the salary and the non-salary payments for the period. Where non-salary payments exceed 40% of total remuneration, add the EXCESS back into the four contribution bases (Ley 1393 de 2010 art. 30). Store the added-back amount as its own quantity: it is IN the contribution base but OUT of the statutory-benefit base for cesantias, prima and the severance indemnity. ~ The clamp is computed on the period's totals, so a single large non-salary bonus can push an otherwise compliant employee over the line for one month. It also interacts with the exoneration test, because the added-back amount raises the health base toward the 10-SMMLV threshold.
R20| All-inclusive salary at 70% ~ Payroll engine ~ Where the employee is on salario integral (CST art. 132 num. 2, minimum 13 SMMLV = COP 22,761,765 in 2026), mark T2R2-041 and set the four IBC fields to 70% of the integral salary. The remaining 30% is the statutory prestacional factor and is not a contribution base. ~ Note the interaction with the ceiling: 70% of a large integral salary can still exceed 25 SMMLV, in which case the ceiling applies to the 70% figure, not to the full salary.
R21| Work centre and risk class ~ Employee master data ~ Carry the work centre on the employee record and derive the risk class from it, not from the employer. Where an employee performs several occupations, contribute at the HIGHEST class (Decreto 1072 de 2015 art. 2.2.4.2.5.6). Where the employee moves work centre mid-period, mark VCT (T2R2-029) and populate T2R2-091 and T2R2-092. ~ A mid-period move can change the ARL rate. Confirm with the operador whether the period is settled at one rate or split - the annex does not prescribe a split, so market practice governs.
R22| Foreign employee electing out of pension ~ Employee master data ~ Mark T2R2-007 where the employee is a foreign national who has proved affiliation to a pension system abroad and elected out under Decreto 1072 de 2015 art. 2.2.1.2.1.4. Health, occupational risk and the parafiscal levies remain fully payable. The election narrows the valid document types to CE, PA, CD, SC and PE. ~ THIS IS NOT A TAX FIELD. It says nothing about tax residence: the same employee may be withheld at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on hoja 2 of Formulario 350 while contributing to Colombian health and occupational risk on ordinary terms.
R23| Incapacity and leave advanced by the employer ~ Payroll engine ~ Colombian employers universally ADVANCE the EPS and ARL benefits through payroll and recover from the institution. Report the IGE and LMA authorisation numbers (T2R2-057, T2R2-059) and values (T2R2-058, T2R2-060), and model the benefit as PAID to the employee and RECEIVABLE from the institution. ~ Without the authorisation number the institution will not reimburse. CO-GAP-03 and CO-GAP-04: the employer's first-two-days rate for general illness (Mercans default 66.67%) and the one-minimum-wage floor on incapacity pay are configurable and need the client's documented election.
