# T-018

**Pergunta:** Ireland, Mercans payroll engine regulation design: what reg structure should be used to compute progressive income tax (PAYE bands at 20%/40%)?

**Resposta:**
Use a single regulation with Taxation Type `Differential`, with the bands (20% up to the cut-off, 40% above). The engine sums the bands.

Do not split the tax into separate atomic Threshold / Base / Amount regulations. That split was "Learning #2" from the IE design comparison (2026-06-24). It was revoked on 2026-07-02 because it was specific to the Revenue RPN feed, not a general pattern.

For Ireland, the rates and the cut-off come from Revenue through the RPN, outside the engine.
