Title: Unemployment Tax Rates | Department of Labor (Vermont)
State and Federal Unemployment Taxes
... The state tax is payable on the first $15,400 in wages paid to each employee during a calendar year. ... The federal tax is payable on the first $7,000 ... payment of state unemployment taxes in a timely manner reduces the federal unemployment tax rate from 6.0% to .6% ...
New Employer Rates
Employers pay unemployment taxes at a New Employer rate until such time as they earn a rate based on their "experience" with unemployment. Beginning July 1, 2026, the new employer rate for most employers is one percent (1%). However, if you are an out of state (foreign) corporation classified under NAICS 236, 237 or 238 ... you will be assigned a "New Employer" rate based on the industry average ...
Industry Title | NAICS Code | Tax Rate
Construction of Buildings | 236 | 1.9%
Heavy & Civil Engineering Construction | 237 | 4.0%
Specialty Trade Contractors | 238 | 2.7%
Experience Rating ... at least one complete calendar year of benefit liability ... most employers pay at the new employer rate for at least two years ... after three years, rolling three-year experience.
Benefit Ratio = benefits charged (last 1-3 calendar years) / taxable wages reported for the same period (example 617.34 / 127,381.13 = .00484).
Determining Your Tax Rate: zero benefit ratio -> rate class 0 (lowest rate in each schedule); others placed in 20 tax rate classes ... Your rate depends on (1) benefit ratio relative to other employers and (2) which rate schedule is in effect.
Rate Schedule: Section 1326 of the Vermont Unemployment Compensation Law provides five different rate schedules, each with twenty-one tax rates. ... Schedule 3 provides an "equilibrium" ...
