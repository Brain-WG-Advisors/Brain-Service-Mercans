On the voluntary rate (27/10): the rate is the one calculated under RIRPF arts. 82–87. An employee may only ask in writing for a higher rate (RIRPF art. 88.5), so a voluntary rate lower than the calculated one cannot replace it. If the lower rate is applied anyway:
- Reporting: the annual Modelo 190 carries the tax actually withheld ("Retenciones practicadas"), so the shortfall is not declared.
- Year end: when less was withheld than due because of the payer alone, the employee deducts in their return the amount that should have been withheld (LIRPF art. 99.5).
- Penalties: not paying amounts that should have been withheld is classified as a serious infraction (LGT art. 191.2.c and 191.3.c), with a fine of 50% to 100% of the amount not paid, plus late-payment interest (LGT art. 26). If the employer corrects it with a late return before any AEAT request, the art. 27 surcharge applies instead of the penalty (1% plus 1% per full month, 15% after 12 months).

On the rate display (27/10): the rate is expressed with two decimals (RIRPF arts. 86.1 and 87.3.c) and the AEAT algorithm truncates it at the second decimal. In your screenshot, 58115 ÷ 58117 = 48,879.93 ÷ 123,883.63 = 39.45%, so the cell should show 39.45, not 39.50: 58118 holds 0.395, a rate rounded to 39.5% instead of truncated to 39.45%.
