@asaber @rfusana How the floor works in Romano's example (taxable income 40,000, CEA 20,000, 2025 brackets):
1. Annual tax with the CEA deduction (base 20,000): 3,250.000.
2. Annual tax without it (base 40,000): 9,550.000; 45% of it = 4,297.500.
3. Tax due is the higher of the two: 4,297.500 (art. 12 bis).
4. Withholding on each pay is the annual tax divided by the number of pays (art. 53-I). So where payroll grants the CEA deduction, it must withhold on 4,297.500, not 3,250.000: with 12 pays, 358.125 a month instead of 270.833.
