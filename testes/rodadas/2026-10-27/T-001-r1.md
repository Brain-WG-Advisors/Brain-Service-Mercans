## Resposta

For the termination month, the TGSS Manual CRA (version updated 1 July 2026) gives the following.

**Severance (indemnización por despido/cese): CRA 0054, with both E and I records.**
- The E record carries the portion up to the exempt limit set by the ET or applicable collective agreement. It must be reported even when nothing is included in the base. The I record carries any excess. Indemnities for death, transfers, suspensions and dismissals are exempt from contribution up to the maximum set by sectoral rules, the collective agreement or the Estatuto de los Trabajadores, and the CRA records must carry both the excluded part (E), even when there is no included amount, and any included part (I).
- Both parts are prorated over the "doce meses anteriores a aquel en que tenga lugar la circunstancia" (Manual CRA §2, "CRA 0051 a 0054 – Indemnizaciones").
- Mercans wage types: 58198 Prorated Severance Exempt → 0054/E and 58197 Prorated Severance Taxable → 0054/I. Each gets one record per prior settlement period. The lump-sum wage types 58184, 58186 and 58188 are the source amounts, so reporting them as well would double count.

**Employer's notice pay (falta de preaviso por parte del empresario): CRA 0054/I.** Because it is more salary than indemnity in nature, it must be declared as INCLUIDO "I" under 0054.

**Unused vacation paid at termination: CRA 0006/I.**
- If the SAA 001 period caused by the unused vacation runs into the following month, the amounts must be broken down by each month affected.
- If the unused vacation is only a fraction of a day, that pay goes under CRA 0001.
- The full amount is reported only in the settlement deadline for the month it is paid, since it is liquidated through L13.
- Use 0060 instead when the company prorates vacation through the contract. Use 0059 when the vacation is paid after the worker's death.

**Pagas extra settled in the finiquito: CRA 0004/I** (0005/I for other items paid less often than monthly). In the month the prorated payment is actually made, the paid amount is not reported, only the prorated monthly amount.

**Employee failed to give notice:** report the amounts the worker actually receives, reduced by the deductions applied.

The April reply to Lily on CT-4346 contradicts this manual. That reply said 58184, 58186 and 58188 are all 0054/E in the termination month only, and that 58197 and 58198 stay out of CRA. The manual differs on two points: the excess is I, not E, and both parts are prorated across the prior 12 months.

The January example to Lily (Apr 2025 → Mar 2026 for a March termination) also includes the termination month. Read literally, the manual's window ends the month before (Mar 2025 → Feb 2026).

Source: [Manual CRA, TGSS, v. 01/07/2026](https://www.seg-social.es/descarga/es/188863), §2 and Anexo 1 Tabla 84.
