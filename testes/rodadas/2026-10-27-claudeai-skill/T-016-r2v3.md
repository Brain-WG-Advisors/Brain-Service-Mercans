## Resposta

This is the same question you asked in the "Diseño de liquidación IUSC en Mercans" chat earlier today. The answer below follows the same structure and adds four points that answer didn't cover: zona extrema, the social-security side of multi-month bonuses, prior-year spillover, and why 58121 helps retro.

I'm working from the Chile decisions recorded up to today, not from the v1.1 design file itself. Accounts 58121, 58122 and 58123 are real; any other 581xx, 582xx or 585xx number below is a placeholder.

## 1. Monthly IUSC (LRE 3161 → F29 cód. 48)

The design works in UTM units. The SII brackets are fixed in UTM, while its CLP table changes every month. Converting the base once means the bands never change; only `$legal_entity_hr.utm` is entered each month (decision of 3 June).

| Step | Account | Type / Tax Rule Group | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flag | 582xx regime | flag | 1 = IUSC, 2 = Impuesto Adicional 35% (non-resident, F50). Route with GREATER/LESS, never EQUALS 0 |
| a | 581xx BTM CLP | Flat | Matrix of the tributable series, **excluding the Art. 46 lumps** so they aren't taxed twice. The credit side carries, as negatives: AFP 10% + comisión, Salud 7% legal (the Isapre excess stays in 9152081), AFC 0.6% for indefinido, and APV-B up to 50 UF. The 600 UF annual APV cap needs a `_ytd` tail |
| b | 58121 BTM in UTM | Flat, `_post_formula` | `DIVIDE($band_amount_raw;$legal_entity_hr.utm)` |
| c | Tax in UTM | v1.1: lookup 58122/58123 · alternative: one Differential `simple_tax_monthly` | 0 / 4 / 8 / 13.5 / 23 / 30.4 / 35 / 40% on 13.5 / 30 / 50 / 70 / 90 / 120 / 310 UTM |
| d | IUSC CLP → 3161 | Flat, `_post_formula` | `FLOOR(ADD(MULTIPLY($band_amount_raw;$legal_entity_hr.utm);0.5))`, GL 2610/2552 |

Notes on these steps:

- **Lookup vs Differential gives the same numbers.** The SII rebajas (0.54 / 1.74 / 4.49 / 11.14 / 17.80 / 23.32 / 38.82 UTM) are exactly the cumulative slab sums. I re-ran this: the CCG examples match (21,507 / 44,417 / 296,500). Across CLP 0–40M the largest gap is 1 peso, from rounding. So the dev guidance from June (lookup, "without differential") and the rulebook rule from 2 July (one Differential) don't conflict numerically. It's a choice to confirm with Ruchi. If you go with Differential, 58122 and 58123 disappear.
- **Band edges.** Don't close UTM bands at ,99: a 0.01 UTM gap is about CLP 692. Use contiguous edges.
- **Mayor retención (Art. 88)** comes from an HR input and goes to its own element, 3163, with GL 2552. F29 cód. 48 must equal 3161 + 3162 + 3163 + 3164 + 3165.
- **Outside the engine** (boundary decisions): Art. 55 bis, and the zona extrema rebate computed on SII Form 1902. On zona extrema, see research gap 3 below.

## 2. Art. 46 reliquidación (LRE 3164 / 3165)

This should be its own family, ordered after the monthly IUSC. It needs three inputs:

- **The lump, in a 581xx intermediate.** It holds 62200–62209 when the bonus covers more than one month, 62108 (sueldo retroactivo), and the arrears/adjustment codes. 62160 aguinaldo is excluded. 62182 (indemnización voluntaria sobre tope) needs its own path, because it is spread over years of service and posts to 3162.
- **n, the number of attributable months.** It is a non-monetary selector, so it goes in a flag, never in a band.
- **Each attributable month's original base in UTM.**

**Calculation:** Δᵢ = T(bᵢ + L/n) − T(bᵢ). Post the current month's Δ to **3165** and the sum of the other months' Δ to **3164**, both with GL 2610/2552.

**The single YTM step recorded in v1.1 is only exact when all attributable months are equal.** A YTM accumulator sees an average, not each month. Results with a 6M bonus:

| Scenario | Exact Art. 46 | YTM average |
|---|---|---|
| 12 equal months (CCG example) | 240,000 | 240,000 |
| Raise in July (1,472,760 → 3,200,000) | 438,126 | 480,000 |
| 8 zero months, then 4 at 2.5M | 160,000 | 191,163 |

**Calendar problem.** YTM resets on 1 January, but the CCG says bonos anuales are usually paid January–March of the following year. Tax on prior-year accessory income paid January–April also has to feed DJ 1887 Box 2 (C-03), which needs a `_period` base crediting 99998.

**Options for the dev team.** This is rulebook open item §8.3.

1. **`retro_` on the IUSC family**, attributing L/n to each month as a dated arrears slice. This is literally Art. 46, and it fits the 10 July decision ("retro: SS partial, tax 100%"), which is still untested. The 58121 design helps here. Because IUSC(B, U) = U × T(B/U), recomputing each past month at its own UTM is automatic once the base is in UTM. The catch: a bonus isn't a retro event, so someone has to create the slices at input, and the social-security side must not reopen unless the research says it should (gap 4).
2. **Keep the YTM step with a documented restriction**: same calendar year, homogeneous months only, everything else handled manually.
3. **Per-month accumulators.** This was the v1.0 approach, already rejected as too heavy.

I'd recommend option 1 and putting it to Ruchi and Mohit together with the retro test they still owe.

## Research gaps for compliance

1. **No field for n or the accrual period.** The DD and WTC have nothing for it. Slots 62200–62209 and the 6214x additions mix monthly and multi-month bonuses under the same codes. The LRE codes 2116–2123 do encode the accrual length.
2. **UTM and IPC convention for the lump.** CCG §6.3 doesn't say whether the slices are converted at each month's UTM or the payment month's, or whether IPC is applied.
3. **Zona extrema.** The CCG and DD call it a "tax credit". But DJ 1887 B-10 is defined as renta neta + rebaja, which only makes sense if the rebate reduces the base. If it reduces the base, the engine has to take the Form 1902 amount as an input on the credit side of the BTM, not just report it.
4. **Social security on multi-month bonuses.** The CCG is silent on whether they are spread across months for the 89.9 UF ceiling. Option 1 above depends on the answer.
5. **Non-resident scope.** The DD calls NON_RESIDENT "outside scope", while the CCG and LRE COD-1170 include the 35% Adicional.
6. **DD Income Tax Type `IUSC_RELIQUIDADO`.** It is modelled as a worker-level regime, but reliquidación is a per-payment event.
7. **"Bono navidad" contradiction.** F29 Scenario 2 reliquidates a "bono navidad" over 12 months, which contradicts the CCG and WTC (aguinaldos don't qualify).
8. **LRE 2313.** Feriado indemnización sits under "no tributables" in the LRE spec, but its description and the CCG say it is tributable.

**What I validated:** I ran the formulas in Python. The CCG monthly examples and the Art. 46 example (240,000) match. **Not validated:** loading into the engine, the 10-scenario matrix, special categories, or a UTM that varies month to month. Not production-ready.
