Pittsburgh City Code Chapter 626 (Paid Sick Days Act), transcription of pp.2-4:
626.02(f) EMPLOYER: any entity situated or doing business in the City that employs one (1) or more persons ...; excludes the United States Government and the State of Pennsylvania.
626.02(i) PAID SICK TIME: compensated at the same base rate of pay and with the same benefits, including health care benefits, as the employee would have earned at the time of use. (1) In no case shall the hourly wage ... be less than that provided under 43 P.S. 333.104(a). (a) Employees using sick time are not entitled to compensation for lost tips or commissions; compensation only for hours the employee was scheduled to have worked.
626.02(l) SEASONAL EMPLOYEE: hired for a temporary period of not more than sixteen (16) weeks during a calendar year, notified in writing at hire.
626.03 Accrual of Sick Time [Ord. No. 27-2015, 3, eff. 8-13-2015; Ord. No. 11-2025, eff. 6-12-2025 (Editor's note: amendments take effect 1-1-2026)]
(b) All employees of employers with fifteen (15) or more employees shall accrue a minimum of one (1) hour of paid sick time for every thirty (30) hours worked in Pittsburgh unless the employer provides a faster accrual rate. (1) ... accrue no more than seventy-two (72) hours of paid sick time in a calendar year, unless the employer designates a higher amount. (a) At no point ... access to more than seventy-two (72) hours ...
(c) All employees of employers with fewer than fifteen (15) employees shall accrue a minimum of one (1) hour of sick time for every thirty (30) hours worked in Pittsburgh ... (1) ... no more than forty-eight (48) hours of paid sick time ... (a) At no point ... access to more than forty-eight (48) hours ...
(d) Employees exempt under 29 U.S.C. 213(a)(1) are assumed to work forty (40) hours in each workweek for accrual, unless their normal workweek is less than forty hours.
(e) Accrual begins at the commencement of employment.
(f) All employees shall be entitled to use accrued sick time beginning on the ninetieth calendar day following the commencement of their employment.
(g) Accrued sick time shall be carried over to the following calendar year, except when: (1) an employer with 15 or more employees provides at least 72 hours of paid sick time at the beginning of each calendar year; or (2) an employer with fewer than 15 employees provides at least 48 hours at the beginning of each calendar year.
(h) No payout of unused accrued sick time on termination, resignation, retirement or other separation.
(i)(1) Rehire within six (6) months: previously accrued unused paid sick time reinstated.
(j) Employer may advance sick time. (k) Successor employer: accrued time carries over. (l) PTO policy meeting the accrual requirements satisfies the chapter. (m) CBA providing sufficient paid leave satisfies the chapter.
