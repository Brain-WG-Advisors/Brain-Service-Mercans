## Resposta

For private-sector employers, the basic employer contribution (*cotisation patronale de base / basisbijdrage*) for **categories 1 and 3** of the structural reduction is **19.88%**, applicable **from Q1 2018**. Including the wage moderation contribution, the total is **about 25.00%**. These are the rates for 2026.

**The basic rate.** The 19.88% is the *pourcentage de cotisation globalisé*. It is split across the branches: pensions 8.86%, medical care 3.80%, sickness and disability 2.35%, unemployment 1.46%, occupational diseases 1.00%, work accidents 0.30%, and the remaining branches. The rate came from the tax shift, under the Law of 26 December 2015 and the Law of 16 May 2016. The older figure of **24.92%** was the basic rate from Q1 2015, before the tax shift, so it should not be used for 2026.

**Wage moderation (*cotisation de modération salariale*).** The general rule is 5.67% of the wage plus 5.67% of the employer contributions due, capped at 7.48%. The tax-shift categories get a reduced rate of 4.27% of the wage plus 4.27% of the employer contributions, capped at **5.12%**:

(19.88 × 0.0427) + 4.27 = **5.12%**

19.88% + 5.12% = **≈ 25.00%** (the white-collar "facial rate")

**Points that affect the calculation:**
- **Blue-collar workers:** the base is the gross at 108%, which is why their facial rate is higher.
- **High-wage exemption:** from 1 January 2026, the basic and wage moderation contributions are not due on the part of the quarterly wage above **EUR 86,700**. The 1 July 2026 change to this measure did not raise the threshold. It only excludes paid athletes and professional cyclists, under the Programme Law of 30 May 2026, arts. 109–110.
- **Double holiday pay:** employer contributions are not due on it.
- **Separate items:** the asbestos fund (0.01%) and the structural reduction are not part of the 25.00%. The CCG describes the facial rate as being "less the structural reduction."

**Sources.** I took these figures from WTC v1.3, row 23671 ("Employer global social security contribution", Wage Type Catalogue!F58), and from CCG Module 5.1 and the Module 3 employer on-costs. Both cite the NSSO administrative instructions, "Les cotisations", in the sections "Le pourcentage de cotisation globalisé" and "La cotisation de modération salariale". The 19.88% figure is the correction made on 13 October 2026 in the CCG and on 21 October 2026 in WTC v1.2, and that change log entry is still marked **Under Review**.

The two sources word the scope slightly differently. The CCG names category 1 and refers generally to "the categories covered by the tax shift." The WTC row is the one that names **categories 1 and 3** explicitly.
