 SECTION 2.  Arkansas Code § 26-51-205(a), concerning the income tax 18 
levied on domestic corporations, is amended to add an additional subdivision 19 
to read as follows: 20 
  (6)  For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2027, every 21 
corporation organized under the laws of this state shall pay annually an 22 
income tax with respect to carrying on or doing business on the entire net 23 
income of the corporation, as now defined by the laws of this state, received 24 
by the corporation during the income year, on the following basis: 25 
   (A)  On the first three thousand dollars ($3,000) of net 26 
income or any part thereof, one percent (1%); 27 
   (B)  On the next three thousand dollars ($3,000) of net 28 
income or any part thereof, two percent (2%); 29 
   (C)  On the next five thousand dollars ($5,000) of net 30 
income or any part thereof, three percent (3%); and 31 
   (D)  On net income exceeding eleven thousand dollars 32 
($11,000), four and one-tenths percent (4.1%). 33
