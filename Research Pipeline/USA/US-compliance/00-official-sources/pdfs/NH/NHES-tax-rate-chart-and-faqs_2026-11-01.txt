RSA 282-A:82 fund balance reduction (positive rated): >= $250M -0.5%; >= $350M -1.0%; >= $400M -1.5%.
Inverse minimum rate (Schedules II/III): < $250M +1.5%; < $350M +1.0%; < $400M +0.5%.
RSA 282-A:87: "Effective October 1, 2019 ... the maximum AC rate has changed from 0.2% to 0.4%, the minimum AC rate remains at 0.1%. The remainder is allocated to the UI (Unemployment Trust Fund). The net rate has not changed."
