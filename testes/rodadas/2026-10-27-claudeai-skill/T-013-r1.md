## Resposta

**Short answer:** only the ARL (occupational) incapacity is exempt. Common-illness incapacity and maternity, paternity and shared parental leave are taxable and subject to retención en la fuente.

**1. Incapacidad por enfermedad general: taxable.** This covers both the employer-paid days 1–2 and the EPS-paid period from day 3. DIAN Oficio 023438 de 2015 reads ET art. 206 num. 1 together with Decreto Ley 1295 de 1994 art. 94, in the context of the Sistema de Riesgos Laborales. It concludes that the monetary allowance for non-occupational illness, since it is not expressly listed in num. 1 of art. 206, is a payment taxed under income tax. The payment goes into the monthly retención base as an ordinary labour payment, and the 25% renta exenta of art. 206 num. 10 applies to it.

**2. Incapacidad laboral paid by the ARL: exempt, up to the legal minimum.** Under the same Oficio 023438/2015, the num. 1 exemption covers only work-accident indemnities and the economic benefits for incapacity caused by occupational illness or accident, subject to the limits of parágrafo 1. DIAN Oficio 36846 de 2015 (answer 2) confirms that the temporary-incapacity subsidy of Ley 776 de 2002 art. 3 is to be taken into account in setting the exempt amount under num. 1. That subsidy is 100% of the IBC.
- Under art. 206 par. 1, only the statutory minimum is exempt. Any employer top-up above it is taxable and withheld under arts. 383/384.
- Under art. 206 par. 2, the exempt portion does not also get the 25% of num. 10.
- The exempt portion still counts toward the art. 336 combined 40% / 1.340 UVT limit. This last point is my reading of art. 336 num. 3; I did not find DIAN doctrine on it specifically for this item.

**3. Licencia de maternidad, paternidad and parental compartida: taxable.** Art. 206 num. 2 exempts only *"indemnizaciones que impliquen protección a la maternidad"*, for example the CST art. 239 num. 3 indemnity for dismissal without MinTrabajo authorisation. It does not cover the leave payments themselves. DIAN Oficio 901191 de 2020, reaffirming Oficio 027408 de 2019, states that num. 2 does not include income from maternity and paternity leave, which are therefore not exempt. It adds that, not being exempt, retención en la fuente must be applied to these payments. The 25% of num. 10 applies to them.

- §5.2 and the §9 matrix mark `LicenciaMP` as **exempt** under art. 206 num. 2. Per Oficios 027408/2019 and 901191/2020 it should be **taxable**.
- §5.1 marks every incapacidad as RTF = Y. That is correct for common illness (`Tipo` 1) but misses the ARL exemption for occupational incapacity (`Tipo` 2/3) under Oficios 023438/2015 and 36846/2015.
