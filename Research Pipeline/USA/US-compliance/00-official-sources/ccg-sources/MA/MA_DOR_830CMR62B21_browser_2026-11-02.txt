"(d) Furnish Wage or Payment Statement by January 31st. Every employer and other withholding entity that deducts and withholds a tax from an employee or payee shall furnish a written statement in duplicate to each employee or payee in respect of the wages or other payments paid to that employee or payee during the calendar year. The written statement must show the name of the employer or payer, the name of the employee or payee and his or her social security number, if any, the total amount of wages or payments subject to taxation under M.G.L. c. 62, and the total amount deducted and withheld as tax.
