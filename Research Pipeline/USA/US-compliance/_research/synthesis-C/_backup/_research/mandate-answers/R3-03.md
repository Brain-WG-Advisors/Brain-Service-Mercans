- The DOR Tax Rates page (updated 2025-12-30) lists every Massachusetts tax and the only local options: local room occupancy up to 6% (6.5% Boston), community impact fee up to 3%, local meals tax 0.75%, marijuana up to 3%, jet fuel at local option; wage withholding is the state 5.00% (tax year 2026, plus 4% surtax over 1,107,750). No city, town or county wage or income tax appears [US-MAR3-002]. DLS Local Option Excise Taxes page agrees (meals, rooms, marijuana) [US-MAR3-003].
