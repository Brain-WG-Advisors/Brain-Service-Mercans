## Resposta

**Short answer:** use the **period begin date**, but compare it to the 25th birthday with an inclusive test rather than computing "age < 25". Take the date of birth from **`$person.birth_date`**. Do not use `$employee.years_worked`, which an earlier draft used by mistake.

**Why it's neither "age at begin date" nor "age at end date":** Szja tv. 29/F (3) grants the allowance by **month**, up to and including the month in which the employee turns 25. NAV leaflet no. 73 *Szja adóalap-kedvezmények* (published 2026-03-19, ch. 7.2) says the last eligible month is the month the 25th year is completed.

- **End date** (the dev-team decision of 2026-05-27) is wrong. It drops the birthday month whenever the birthday falls on or before the last day of the period, which is always.
- **Plain age < 25 at begin date** is almost right. It fails only for someone born on the 1st of a month, who is already 25 on `begin_date` and would lose the month they're entitled to.

**Flag that handles both cases** (582xx, values starting at 1, dates as `yyyyMMdd` per the rulebook):

```
IF( LTE( DATE_FORMAT($period.begin_date;'yyyyMMdd') ;
         DATE_FORMAT(ADD_MONTHS($person.birth_date;300);'yyyyMMdd') ) ; 1 ; 2 )
```

Here 1 means eligible and 2 means not eligible. For a monthly calendar period this works because the 1st of the birthday month is always on or before the 25th birthday, and the 1st of the following month is always after it. If you ever run non-monthly frequencies for HU, compare `yyyyMM` instead. This flag sits alongside the existing default-on opt-out flag decided on 2026-05-29; it does not replace it.

**Status as of 2026-10-27:** the 2026-05-27 "end_date" entry is superseded by the 2026-10-27 correction. The HU regulation design still needs to be updated to match.
