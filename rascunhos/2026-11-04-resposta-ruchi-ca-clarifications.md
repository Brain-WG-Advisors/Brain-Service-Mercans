1. Hi Ruchi, answers below.
2. K2R/K2RQ: yes, in scope. They replace K2/K2Q in the basic federal tax (T3); the provincial version is K2RP, which is out of our scope (T4127 Jan 2026, p. 15).
3. F: RPP, RRSP, PRPP/VRSP, RCA and FHSA withheld at source reduce the taxable base for both federal and Quebec (T4127 factor F and T4001; TP-1015.F-V factor F). Only Quebec's F also has CIP, the remote-area travel deduction, the security option deduction and the reserve/foreign-specialist deductions. Union dues are federal only (U1); Quebec's F doesn't include them.
4. WTC: RPP is there (21640–21645, both). RRSP, PRPP/VRSP, RCA and FHSA don't have their own codes yet; I'll add them, tagged for both.
5. F1 and J1 are separate. F1 is what CRA authorizes (letter of authority); J1 is what Revenu Québec authorizes on TP-1016-V. Each applies only to its own tax, so they need separate inputs (T4127 glossary F1; TP-1015.F-V p. 14).
6. J: on TP-1015.3-V (line 19) the employee can claim only two annual deductions: line 14, housing deduction for residents of designated remote areas, and line 15, deductible support payments. Anything else needs Revenu Québec's authorization on TP-1016-V and goes in J1.
