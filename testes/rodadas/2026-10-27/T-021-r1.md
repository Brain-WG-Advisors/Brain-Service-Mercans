## Resposta

**Yes.** Salaried employees in the public and private sectors have the residential occupancy tax (TOL – *taxe d'occupation des locaux à usage d'habitation*) withheld at source by the employer. The amount is a flat **1,000 FCFA per month**, and it is a payment on account against the annual tax. The relevant provision says that for salaried individuals in the public and private sectors, the residential TOL is collected as an advance (acompte) through withholding at source by the employer at a rate of 1,000 FCFA per month.

**Legal basis:**
- **Origin:** Loi n° 34-2013 du 30 décembre 2013 (Loi de finances 2014), Articles 7 to 14, created the TOL. Article 7 established an annual tax called the occupancy tax in the Republic of Congo, and Article 10 placed it on the occupant, whether owner or tenant.
- **Withholding mechanism:** Loi n° 48-2014 du 31 décembre 2014 (Loi de finances 2015), Article 8, Section 6, §6.3, which rewrote **Article 14** of the 2014 law. It is published in JO Édition spéciale n° 1-2015 at page 17.
- **Annual regularisation:** By the 20 April deadline, salaried individuals file a regularisation return with their tax office if they are married employees sharing the same home, employees housed by others, employees taxable at the city-centre rate, or employees occupying exempt premises. Any withholding that the tax administration confirms as erroneous, based on a file the taxpayer submits, gives a tax credit to offset against future withholdings.
- **Beneficiary:** The TOL is collected for local authorities, and its proceeds are distributed under Article 250 of the CGI, Tome 1.

**Not to confuse with:** the professional-premises TOL is a separate charge on the employer, not on employees. Under the 2015 law, it is 60,000 FCFA for small businesses, associations and other non-commercial professions and organisations, 120,000 FCFA for medium-sized businesses, and 500,000 FCFA for large businesses, payable by 20 February.

**Still current:** Practitioner sources still apply the flat 1,000 FCFA in 2026 payroll calculations. For example, one payroll guide deducts the flat 1,000 FCFA TOL after social security and income tax. I checked the 2026 Finance Law (Loi n° 42-2025), but I could only read part of it; in that part I found no change to the TOL.
