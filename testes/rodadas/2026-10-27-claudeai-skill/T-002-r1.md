## Resposta

**TRL = *Tipo de Relación Laboral*** (labour relationship type). In ES-AFI-001, the inbound TGSS → employer *TrabajadoresTramos* file (SR-80), it's the code that tells you which kind of employment relationship applies to the worker in that *tramo* (period segment) of their affiliation record. It's an affiliation attribute, alongside the *DatosTramo* data, not a contribution-rate parameter.

It is **not** *Tipo de Riesgo Laboral* (work/occupational risk type). That was an earlier error on this spec, corrected around May 2026 under SR-80. The original answer said TRL drove the AT/EP rate and included a 000/500/800 scale that doesn't exist in the TGSS schema. The AT/EP rate doesn't come from this field at all. The TGSS assigns it from the CNAE-2025 activity code, and a Cuadro II occupation code can override it (LGSS DA 61.ª, inserted by RDL 3/2026).

One open item to watch: in **ES-AFI-001_TrabajadoresTramos_Spec_v2.0-FINAL** on Drive, row 55 is still inconsistent. A55 and M55 correctly say Labor Relationship Type, but **B55 still reads "Tipo de Riesgo Laboral (TRL)"** and **L55 still reads "Occupational risk level code."** This was spotted in today's accuracy test, and those two cells should be aligned before the spec goes out again.

I don't have the TRL code list itself in the reference pack, so I won't quote values from memory. The domain should be taken from the TGSS AFI schema, in the Sistema RED *Tablas y formatos comunes*.
