Art. 12 bis of Law 89-114 sets a minimum tax of 45% of the IRPP due on global income computed without the exemptions (rate 45% since Law 2017-8, art. 2-6; not amended by LF2025 or LF2026). It covers the deductions granted "sous réserve" of art. 12 bis: life-insurance premiums (Code IRPP/IS art. 39-I-2), CEA deposits (art. 39-VIII) and CEI deposits (art. 39 bis). Deductions granted "nonobstant" art. 12 bis (e.g. art. 39-V, 39 ter) are outside it.

Monthly withholding is the annual tax divided by the number of pays (art. 53-I), so the minimum also applies to withholding when these deductions are taken. The example in this ticket is correct: 45% × 9,550.000 = 4,297.500, above 3,250.000. The minimum needs no additional information from the client.
