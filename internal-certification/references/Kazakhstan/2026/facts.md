# Kazakhstan – payroll facts for October 2026 (source IDs → sources.md)

Read 2026-10-14. Amounts are in KZT. Any value computed from a statutory multiple is marked (calc).

## Base indices (S02 Budget Law 239-VIII, art. 7, from 01.01.2026)
- МРП = **4 325** (art. 7 пп.4)
- МЗП = **85 000** (art. 7 пп.1)

## Legal framework
- New Tax Code: Code of RK 18.07.2025 № 214-VIII ЗРК, **in force 01.01.2026** (S01 art. 848 п.1). It repeals the 2017 Tax Code (art. 848 п.3). Edition checked: 01.07.2026. The only amending law, 308-VIII, does not touch the payroll articles.
- Social Code 20.04.2023 № 224-VII (S03), edition 06.09.2026. OSMS Law 16.11.2015 № 405-V (S04), edition 14.07.2026.

## 1. ИПН (S01)
- Rate: **10%** on annual taxable income up to 8 500 МРП; **15%** on the excess (art. 363 пп.1). The МРП used is the one on 1 January. 8 500 × 4 325 = **36 762 500 per year** (calc).
- Computed **cumulatively YTD from 1 January** (art. 441): YTD taxable income within 8 500 МРП × 10% + excess × 15% − ИПН already computed for earlier months.
- Taxable income (art. 439 п.1) = employee income accrued (art. 426) − exclusions (art. 400 п.1 / art. 429) − personal deductions (art. 401 п.1).
- Deduction order (art. 401 п.3): (1) social-payments deduction, (2) basic deduction, (3) social deductions.
  - Social-payments deduction = **ОПВ + ВОСМС** (+ СО only under civil-law contracts) (arts. 402, 438). **ОПВ and ВОСМС are both deducted from the ИПН base.**
  - Basic deduction = **30 МРП per month = 129 750** (calc), capped at 360 МРП per year = 1 557 000 (calc) (art. 403). It needs the employee's application, can be used with one agent only, and unused amounts do not move to a new employer (art. 437 п.2–3).
  - Social deductions: 5 000 МРП per year (disability gr. I/II); 882 МРП per year (gr. III, child with disability, veterans, parents/guardians of a child with disability, adopters, foster parents). Application and documents required (arts. 404, 437).
- The old 90% income adjustment (корректировка 90%) does not appear in the new Code. INFERENCE: searched for "90 процентов" with no payroll hit.
- Withholding: no later than the day of payment (art. 440 п.3). Transfer: no later than **25 calendar days after the end of the month of payment** (art. 440 п.4). **КБК 101201** (S06). КНП **911** (S07).

## 3. ОПВ – employee pension contribution (S03)
- **10%** of monthly income (art. 249 п.1 пп.1). Base cap **50 МЗП = 4 250 000** per month per agent (calc).
- Minimum: none stated for employees. The 1 МЗП floor applies only to self-employed persons/IPs (art. 249 пп.2). INFERENCE: no minimum for employees.
- Exempt: people of pension age (art. 207 п.1); disability gr. I/II if permanent (may opt in); military and law-enforcement personnel; long-service pensioners (art. 248 п.3).
- Base covers all cash pay and other income (art. 248 п.8). Pay by the **25th of the month after the month of payment** (art. 248 п.9 пп.1). КНП **010** (S07).

## 4. ОПВР – employer pension contribution (S03 art. 251)
- **3.5% from 01.01.2026**. Schedule: 1.5% (2024), 2.5% (2025), 3.5% (2026), 4.5% (2027), 5% (2028).
- Base as for ОПВ, with a **minimum of 1 МЗП = 85 000** and a **maximum of 50 МЗП = 4 250 000** per month (art. 251 п.2).
- Exempt (art. 248 п.6): **born before 01.01.1975**; pension age; disability gr. I/II permanent; military and law-enforcement personnel; long-service pensioners; retired judges.
- Due date: the 25th of the following month (art. 248 п.9). КНП **089** (S07).

## 5. СО – social contributions to GFSS (S03)
- **5%** (art. 244 п.1, from 01.01.2025).
- Base = income taxable at source − **ОПВ** − art. 400 п.1 exclusions, except art. 429 пп.1, 2, 3, 7 (art. 245 п.1).
- **Min 1 МЗП = 85 000; max 7 МЗП = 595 000** per month (calc), so СО runs from 4 250 to 29 750 (calc).
- Not covered: people of pension age (art. 243). Due by the 25th of the following month (art. 246 п.1). КНП **012** (S07).

## 6. СН – social tax (S01)
- **6%** (art. 557 п.1).
- Base = employer expenses on employee income (art. 556 п.1) − **ОПВ** − **ВОСМС** − art. 429 incomes (except пп.2, and пп.3 for KZ citizens) (art. 556 п.2; art. 558 п.1).
- If the monthly base is between 1 теңге and 14 МРП, the base is **14 МРП = 60 550** (calc), using the МРП on the 1st of the month. This does not apply in the month of hire or dismissal (art. 556 п.3).
- **СО is NOT deducted from СН** under the new Code. No such provision exists in arts. 555–562; this "no deduction" finding is a reading of the full СН section. The 2017 Code's 11% rate with an СО offset is background knowledge only and was not re-sourced here (INFERENCE).
- Due no later than the **25th of the following month** (art. 560 п.1). **КБК 103101** (S06). КНП **911** (S07).
- INFERENCE: ООСМС (employer) and ОПВР are not listed as exclusions from the СН base, so they are not deducted.

## 7. ОСМС (S04)
- **ВОСМС (employee) 2%** (art. 28 п.1). Cap **20 МЗП = 1 700 000** per month (art. 28 п.5-2).
- **ООСМС (employer) 3%** (art. 27 п.1, unchanged since 2022). Cap **40 МЗП = 3 400 000** per month (art. 27 п.2-2).
- Base = employee income taxable at source − art. 400 п.1 exclusions, except art. 429 пп.2 and 4 (art. 29 п.1). Minimum base for employees: none stated (INFERENCE).
- Exempt: ВОСМС for all art. 26 п.1 categories (incl. pension recipients, persons with disability, full-time students). ООСМС for art. 26 п.1 пп.1, 5, 7, 11, 12, 13 (incl. pension recipients, persons with disability, students, persons on maternity/childcare leave). Both also exempt military and law-enforcement personnel.
- Due by the 25th of the month after the month of payment (art. 30 п.6 пп.1). КНП **121** (ООСМС), **122** (ВОСМС) (S07).

## Calculation order per employee – monthly (INFERENCE assembled from the articles cited)
1. Gross income accrued (S01 art. 426), less non-taxable / art. 400 п.1 amounts.
2. ОПВ = 10% × min(gross, 50 МЗП); 0 if exempt.
3. ВОСМС = 2% × min(gross, 20 МЗП); 0 if exempt.
4. ИПН base = gross − exclusions − ОПВ − ВОСМС − 30 МРП (only with an application) − social deductions. ИПН is computed YTD per art. 441 (10% / 15% above 8 500 МРП YTD) less ИПН for prior months.
5. Employer side:
   - ОПВР = 3.5% × clamp(gross, 1 МЗП, 50 МЗП).
   - СО = 5% × clamp(gross − ОПВ − exclusions, 1 МЗП, 7 МЗП).
   - ООСМС = 3% × min(gross, 40 МЗП).
   - СН = 6% × (gross − ОПВ − ВОСМС − art. 429 exclusions), with a 14 МРП floor. No СО offset.

## 8. Deadlines and reporting
- Payment of ИПН, СН, ОПВ, ОПВР, СО, ВОСМС, ООСМС: by the **25th of the following month**. For October 2026 that is **25.11.2026**. Sources: S01 arts. 440 п.4, 560 п.1; S03 arts. 246 п.1, 248 п.9; S04 art. 30 п.6.
- Form **200.00** (declaration on ИПН and СН, which also carries ОПВ/ОПВР/СО/ОСМС, S03 art. 254): filed quarterly by the **15th of the 2nd month after the quarter** (S01 arts. 445, 562). Q4 2026 (Oct–Dec) is due **15.02.2027** (calc). The annual per-employee annex goes with the Q4 declaration (art. 445 п.2).
- Form and rules: MinFin Order 20.01.2020 № 39 (reg. 19897), **appendix 121 (form) and 122 (rules)**, applying from 01.01.2023 with no end date. Annexes 200.01–200.06 (S05). **A 2026-specific approval under the new Code was NOT LOCATED** (sources.md N2). KGD site unreachable (N1).
