1. Yes, both can be omitted. ZERO_ZVIT and REASON are optional in XSD S0301016 (minOccurs="0"), and an element absent from DECLARBODY is read as empty (Derzhstat XML Format Standard 3.1, item 4). Derzhstat's technical field list for S0301016 describes only REASON_KOD ("filled automatically according to the selected reason"); ZERO_ZVIT and REASON are not described in it, so the mapping above has no official definition. The no-data reason is sent in REASON_KOD ($legal_entity_hr.1pv_no_data_reason::key). Spec v5.2: X9 updated.

2. No rate formula. Row 11 (r.1030) is the PIT withheld from the wage fund of all workers (row 10, r.1020), excluding temporary disability, without recalculations for the previous year (form 1-PV monthly, line 1030, Derzhstat Order No. 117 of 15.04.2024; Derzhstat Explanation No. 19.1.2-12/24-24, section II, item 2); PIT withheld on income outside r.1020 does not count. Neither the form nor the Explanation distinguishes tax rate or regime; the only rate in the rules is the control r.1030 / r.1020 × 100 ≤ 18 (Explanation, section I, item 8). In the UA WTC, withheld PIT is one element, 2552 "Withheld PIT", with its rate (18 or 5) set by 52552 "Withheld PIT Rate". How to isolate that PIT from 2552 (one element for all taxable income) is a configuration choice; Derzhstat does not prescribe a method.
