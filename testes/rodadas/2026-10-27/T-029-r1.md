## Resposta

**Basic employer contribution, categories 1 and 3: 19.88%.** Under the Law of 26 December 2015 and the Law of 16 May 2016, the rate for category 1 and 3 workers of the structural reduction was cut from 22.65% to 19.88% from Q1 2018. The ONSS still publishes 19.88% in its current instructions for 2026 ("latest" DmfAPPL edition), and I found no 2026 change to the base rate.

**Wage moderation for categories 1 and 3: 4.27%, applied to gross pay plus employer contributions.** The standard wage moderation is 5.67% of the worker's pay, plus 5.67% of the employer contributions due. For category 1 and 3 workers, it was reduced to 4.27% from Q1 2018, and the 0.40% surcharge for workers under the annual holidays legislation no longer applies to them.

**Approximate total: 25.00%.** The calculation is 19.88% + 4.27% × (1 + 0.1988), which is 19.88% + 5.12%. Securex's July 2026 material uses the same figure, citing a 25% employer contribution (32.40% for the non-profit sector).

Things the 25% does not include:
- **The 1.60% contribution.** It applies only to employers that averaged at least 10 workers during the reference period. With wage moderation it comes to about 1.67%.
- **Small special contributions**, such as 0.01% for the asbestos fund (Q1 and Q2 only) and 0.02% for work accidents.
- **Blue-collar workers.** Contributions are calculated on 108% of gross pay. There is also 5.57% collected quarterly for annual holidays, plus 10.27% via an annual debit notice.
- **The new indexation-linked contribution from 1 June 2026.** This is a separate employer contribution that is also called a "wage moderation contribution" (introduced alongside the "cent indexation"). It is not the 4.27% above, so the two shouldn't be confused.
- **Category 3 nuance.** Wage moderation is not due for recognised disabled workers employed in sheltered/adapted work companies (entreprises de travail adapté). For those workers, the total is the basic 19.88% without the 5.12%.

**Where to find it:** ONSS Administrative Instructions (DmfA), "Les cotisations de sécurité sociale > Cotisations." The base rate is under "Conséquence du tax-shift – Exécution à partir de 2018," and the 4.27% is under "La cotisation de modération salariale." The exact rate for each worker code is in the ONSS DmfA rate file.
