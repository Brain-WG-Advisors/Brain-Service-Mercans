## Resposta

For categories 1 and 3 of the structural reduction, the 2026 rates are:

- **Basic employer contribution:** 19.88%. The ONSS says the tax shift lowered the contribution rate for category 1 and 3 workers of the structural reduction from 1 January 2018, to 19.88% from Q1 2018. This replaces the general private-sector rate of 24.92%.
- **Wage moderation:** 4.27% + 4.27% of employer contributions, which caps at 5.12%. The ONSS reduced the rate for these categories from 5.67% to 4.27% from Q1 2018, and a maximum wage moderation of 5.12% is therefore due [(19.88 x 0.0427) + 4.27].
- **Total:** about 25.00% of gross pay. This is the headline rate. Blue-collar workers pay it on 108% of gross.

The 25% does not include several other employer contributions:

- **Blue-collar holiday contributions:** 5.57% each quarter plus 10.27% billed once a year.
- **1.60% contribution (1.69% with wage moderation):** due only by employers that averaged at least 10 workers during the reference period.
- **Smaller special contributions:** asbestos fund 0.01% (levied in Q1–Q3 for 2026) and work accidents 0.02%.
- **Sectoral fund contributions.**
