No. r.1030 is not r.1020 × rate: it is the PIT actually withheld from the r.1020 wage fund (excluding temporary disability), with no distinction by rate or regime (form 1-PV, line 1030; Derzhstat Explanation 19.1.2-12/24-24, II.2). The only rate in the rules is the check r.1030 / r.1020 × 100 ≤ 18 (Explanation, I.8). In the WTC, withheld PIT is a single element (2552, rate from 52552); how to isolate the part for r.1020 is a configuration choice — Derzhstat does not prescribe a method.
