## Resposta

**Short answer (ES-CRA-001 as of 2026-10-27):** in the termination month the CRA carries **58184, 58186 and 58188, all under CRA concept 0054, type E**. **62983** is also reported as **0054/E**. The source for this mapping is ES-CRA-001 Table 84, set in SR-81 and HRBS-10563.

**Wage types that do not go in the CRA:** 58197 and 58198 belong only to the Fichero de Bases (the L03 prorrata). 58198 is not reported anywhere. Our April 2026 answer said "58197 → 0054/I, 58198 → 0054/E". That was wrong, and the BA corrected it in SR-81. Don't reuse it.

**Open conflict to know about before sending this to anyone:** the TGSS *Manual CRA* updated 1 July 2026 (§2, CRA 0051–0054) says two things that clash with the current spec and WTC:

1. The E and I amounts must be **prorated across the 12 months before the termination**, not reported only in the termination month.
2. Severance **above the compulsory statutory amount** is part of the contribution base (LGSS art. 147.2.c). It should therefore be **type I, not E**. That points at **58188** (the severance excess), which WTC V4.6 marks as "non SS subject" and the spec reports as 0054/E.

Deciding which wage type carries 0054 as I versus E, and whether the reporting is prorated, is a design decision that hasn't been made yet. So the spec answer above is what the system does today, but it may not be what the TGSS manual requires. There is a related pending item in ES-BASES-001: it sends the excess (58188) only to base code 701. The SLD manual says 701 is only for pluriempleo with exclusions or for artists, and that the excess goes into 500/601.

**Basis and confidence:** this comes from ES-CRA-001 Table 84 via SR-81 and HRBS-10563. No official TGSS source is cited in those tickets for the 0054/E choice. The current version is v2.4, from the 04/09 ETI-record fix; I can't confirm Table 84 is unchanged in it. My snapshot also doesn't have the wage type names for 58184 and 58186. **Confidence is about 70% that this is the right compliance answer.** What's missing is a check of Table 84 in v2.4 and a design decision on the July 2026 Manual CRA (prorrata, and I versus E for the excess).
