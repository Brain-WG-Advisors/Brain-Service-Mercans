## Resposta

**Salud: 4%** of the salary-based IBC, deducted from the employee. A working old-age pensioner contributes to health on his wages like any other employee: the 12.5% total is split 8.5% employer and 4% worker, paid to the same EPS. The 12% rate (or 4%/10% for lower amounts) applies only to the pension payment itself, under Ley 100 de 1993, art. 204, par. 5, which was added by Ley 2010 de 2019. The pension fund deducts it from the mesada, not the employer from salary.

**FondoPension: not due.** Ley 100 de 1993, art. 17, as amended by Ley 797 de 2003, art. 4, says the obligation to contribute ends once the member meets the requirements for the minimum old-age pension, or is pensioned for disability or early. Neither the employee nor the employer contributes. Resolución 3559 de 2018 of MinSalud says subtype 1, "Dependiente pensionado por vejez, jubilación o invalidez activo", can only be used if the person appears in the Ministry's monthly pensioner file with pension type Vejez, Invalidez or Jubilación. The Fondo de Solidaridad Pensional is also not due, since it is paid together with the pension contribution, so a working pensioner who does not contribute to pension does not pay it either.

**DSNE (XML) mechanics:** In the Anexo Técnico, table 5.5.4, SubTipoTrabajador code 01 is "Dependiente pensionado por vejez activo". The Deducciones/FondoPension group is still a required element in the schema. So it is transmitted with Porcentaje 0.00 and Deduccion 0, not left out. I confirmed this through a provider's integration manual that mirrors the Anexo Técnico, not the DIAN PDF itself.

One exception: a **survivor's pension** (sobrevivientes) recipient who works does still contribute to pension, because that pension doesn't cover his own old age. That person shouldn't be coded 01.
