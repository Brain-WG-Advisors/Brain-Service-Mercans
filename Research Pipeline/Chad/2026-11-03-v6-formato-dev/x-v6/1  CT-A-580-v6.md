# TD Specifications — Compliance v6 (test set)
**Flags**:

**58218**: $hr.employee_type

Status (global flag) — Kept from the current design (`$hr.employee_type`): 0 resident · 1 non-resident · 2 disabled (CGI Art. 45-18°) · 3 young graduate (Art. 3-4°) · 4 diplomatic (Art. 3-2°); routing uses `LESS($58218;1)` for residents, never an equality test against zero.

**58202**: IF(EQUALS($hr.cnps_eligibility_pvid::key;'N');2;1)

CNPS PVID eligible — 1 yes (default, every employee, **non-residents included**: « quelle que soit… leur nationalité », CNPS guide p. 6) · 2 no — only a seconded employee covered by a bilateral or multilateral convention (CIPRES) with a secondment certificate.

**58203**: IF(EQUALS($hr.cnps_eligibility_pfm::key;'N');2;1)

CNPS PFM eligible — 1 yes (default) · 2 no — as 58202.

**58204**: IF(EQUALS($hr.cnps_eligibility_atmp::key;'N');2;1)

CNPS AT/MP eligible — 1 yes (default) · 2 no — as 58202.

**58205**: CEIL(MULTIPLY(YEARS_BETWEEN($hr.young_graduate_employee_exemption_start_date;$entry.period_end_date);12))

Art. 3-4° exemption months (young graduate / worker) — Formula kept from the current design. Months since the Art. 3-4° exemption start (date of recruitment by the real-regime entity), current month included; IRPP exemption while ≤ 60 (« pendant une période de cinq ans »). The same DD date is used for workers. WTC V3.0 names 58205 "Young Graduate Start Date" but its formula (and the current design) also yields months; read by the IRPP-TS report.

**58206**: IF(EQUALS($legal_entity_hr.tfe_tafp_relief::key;'100');1;IF(EQUALS($legal_entity_hr.tfe_tafp_relief::key;'50');2;0))

TFE / TAFP relief (entity) — 0 none · 1 exempt (LF 2020 Art. 14 as amended by LF 2024 Art. 6 / LF 2026 Art. 6) · 2 50% (CGI Art. 158) — **entity field**.

**58207**: IF(EQUALS($legal_entity_hr.transport_uniform::key;'Y');1;2)

Transport allowance granted to all staff — 1 yes · 2 no — **entity field**, default 1 (Art. 45-13°).

**58210**: IF(EQUALS($hr.termination_plan_social::key;'Y');1;2)

Plan social (collective redundancy) — 1 yes · 2 no — **DD field** (Art. 45-10°).

**58212**: $hr.number_of_dependent_children

Dependent children — count of **children** (not dependants) — DAS report and the Art. 45-4° cap (5,000 XAF per child per month).

**58213**: $hr.cnps_family_allowance_monthly

CNPS family allowance received (monthly) — XAF a month paid by the CNPS for the children — **new DD field**, default 0. Art. 45-4°: imputed on the 5,000 XAF per-child cap.

**58215**: IF(EQUALS($legal_entity_hr.tax_regime::key;'REEL');1;2)

Entity under the real regime (régime du réel) — 1 yes · 2 no — **new entity field**, default 1. Condition of Art. 188-10° and Art. 174-3°.

**58216**: FLOOR(DIVIDE(DAYS_BETWEEN($hr.date_of_birth;$period.end_date);365.25))

Employee age (years) — completed years at period end — `under 35` test of Art. 188-10° and Art. 174-3° (TF/TA, read each month).

**58225**: FLOOR(DIVIDE(DAYS_BETWEEN($hr.date_of_birth;$hr.young_graduate_employee_exemption_start_date);365.25))

Age at the Art. 3-4° exemption start — completed years at recruitment — `under 35` test of the young graduate in Art. 3-4° (IRPP), checked at recruitment because the exemption then runs five years.

**58217**: IF(EQUALS($hr.worker_category::key;'OUVRIER');1;2)

Worker (ouvrier) — 1 yes · 2 no — **new DD field**, default 2.

**58219**: IF(AND(EQUALS($58215;1);OR(EQUALS($58218;2);AND(EQUALS($58218;3);LESS($58216;35));AND(EQUALS($58217;1);LESS($58216;35))));1;0)

TFE / TAFP exempt payroll share (youth, disabled, workers) — 1 = the pay of this employee is outside TFE and TAFP: real-regime entity and (disabled, or young graduate under 35, or worker under 35). Art. 188-10° (TF) and Art. 174-3° (TA), CGI 2026 p. 59–61 — no five-year limit in these articles. Derived from other flags in the same run (evaluated before the regulations).

**58226**: IF(AND(EQUALS($58215;1);GREATER($58205;0);LESS($58205;61);LESS($58225;35);OR(EQUALS($58218;3);EQUALS($58217;1)));1;0)

Art. 3-4° IRPP exempt (derived) — 1 = IRPP exempt: CGI Art. 3-4° (p. 15) « Les salaires versés aux jeunes diplômés âgés de moins de 35 ans et aux ouvriers recrutés par les entreprises du régime du réel… pendant une période de cinq ans » — real-regime entity (58215), start date given and within 60 months (0 < 58205 ≤ 60; **the start date is mandatory in the DD when 58218 = 3 or 58217 = 1** — without it there is no exemption), under 35 at recruitment (58225), and young graduate (58218 = 3) or worker (58217 = 1). the under-35 limit is applied to workers too — open point, pending sign-off: Art. 3-4° attaches the age to « jeunes diplômés » only, but does not expressly set it aside for « ouvriers », and Arts. 188-10° / 174-3° of the same policy (LFI 2021) say « les ouvriers âgés de moins de 35 ans ». Derived from other flags in the same run (evaluated before the regulations).

**58223**: $hr.leave_ticket_annual_limit

Leave travel ticket — annual limit — XAF a year: one ticket a year per person (employee, spouse, dependants) at the normal fare from the place of work to the place of origin — **new DD field**, default 0. CGI Art. 45-15°.
