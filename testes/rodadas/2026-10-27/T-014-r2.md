# T-014-r2

- **Pergunta:** Colombia, Nómina Electrónica (DIAN): for an employee who is already a pensioner and keeps working (worker subtype 01), what health (Salud) percentage is deducted from the employee, and is a pension (FondoPension) deduction due?

## Resposta

**Salud: 4% deducted from the employee.** On the salary of a working pensioner, the normal dependent-worker split applies. Under Art. 204 Ley 100/1993, as modified by Art. 10 Ley 1122/2007, the contribution is 12.5% of the IBC, with the employer paying 8.5% and the employee 4%. The reduced 4%/10%/12% pensioner rates apply only to the pension payment (mesada), which the pension fund withholds. They do not apply to the salary.

**FondoPension: no deduction due.** Art. 17 Ley 100/1993, as modified by Art. 4 Ley 797/2003, provides that the obligation to contribute ends when the member meets the requirements for the minimum old-age pension, or is pensioned for disability or early retirement. The MinSalud Abecé on Ley 2381/2024 confirms this: an old-age pensioner cannot contribute to pension again, but must keep contributing to health and occupational risks on income earned as a worker. Ley 2381 does not change this today. It takes effect on 1 April 2027, following the Constitutional Court's decision, so Ley 100 still governs.

**XML reporting.** Code 01 in Table 5.5.4 of the Anexo Técnico (Res. 000013/2021) is "Dependiente pensionado por vejez activo". The Deducciones/FondoPension node is still required by the schema; technology providers' integration manuals mark it as mandatory. You report it at 0. DIAN's own validation notes list NIE164 Porcentaje FondoPension under "Porcentajes sin restricciones", so Porcentaje 0.00 and Deduccion 0.00 pass validation.
