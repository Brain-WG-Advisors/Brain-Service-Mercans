Does Alaska have a withholding tax?

The State of Alaska currently does not have an individual income tax, therefore no employee withholding for state income tax is required.

Note: For information on Alaska’s Unemployment Insurance Tax (also called Employment Security Tax), the Unemployment Insurance Program or Workers' Compensation, contact the Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development at labor.alaska.gov.
