## 0. How to read this pack

- One section per jurisdiction (federal, multistate, 50 states + DC). Each opens with the **report inventory** generated from the facts (report, attribute, value, register_id, fact key) and then carries the stage-5 reporting findings verbatim, which already cite their register ids inline.
- **Channel** is classified in the inventory as `online_form` / `file_upload` / `xml` / `api` / `paper` from the channel/format facts; where several apply they are listed in the order the authority offers them. The **portal is per institution**: the revenue department portal (withholding, W-2) is never the UI agency portal (wage report), and PFML portals are separate again (CT, DE, MA, ME, MN, WA, CO, DC). Mislabelling is check C24.
- **Samples on disk:** FL RT XML package (4 samples + 4 test cases; RT-6 Sample.xml validates against RT_FLTransmission.xsd) [US-FLS5-023]; MI MiUI Wage Report Validator Tool and four 2026 format specs [US-MIS5-022]; CT 276-char layout (positions extracted and summed) [US-CTS5-032]; AK CSV guidelines [US-AKS5-014]; SC SUITS spec v1.6 [US-SCS5-006]; MD BEACON file format 2026-04-20 [US-MDS5-017]; IA IDR W-2 spec with RV record [US-IAS5-018]; WV IT-105 with RV record [US-WVS5-013]; AL EFW2 RS positions [US-ALS5-006]; GA Federal Format Rev 01/18 (partial) [US-GAS5-011]. No real filed sample exists for any other jurisdiction.
- Facts marked `_facts_pending_register_id_pattern` in the JSON are verified against an active row whose id has more than three segments (e.g. `US-ARP51-002`), which the facts schema 1.4.0 `register_id` pattern rejects. They are listed with a (P) mark below. This is the same convention the CCG annex workers used.

## 0.1 Cross-cutting rules (all jurisdictions)

- **Federal EFW2 TY2026 spec is gated** [US-FEDS5-032, gated] - every state RS/RV layout below is read against the TY2025 SSA spec until it is read. Blocking for the W-2 sheets of the Report Spec.
- **New-hire reporting:** federal floor 20 days, fields name/address/SSN/first day of work + employer name/address/FEIN [US-FEDS5-024]; multistate electronic employers may designate one state [US-MULTIS5-006]. States shorter than 20 days: AL 7, ME 7, GA 10, VT 10, IA 15, MS 15, MA 14, RI 14, WV 14 (see table 0.2).
- **W-2 multistate:** boxes 15-20 carry two states and two localities; more need an extra W-2 [US-MULTIS5-011]. UI wage report goes only to the `sui_state` under the localization test [US-MULTIS5-017].
- **Scope traps:** NY files no W-2 with the state (NYS-45 Part C replaces it) [US-NYS5-010]; CA files no W-2 with EDD/FTB (DE 9C carries PIT wages) [US-CAS5-002]; IA has no annual reconciliation and rejects the SSA EFW2 file (IDR-unique format) [US-IAS5-017]; IL and ME have no annual reconciliation [US-ILS5-011, US-MEP52-009]; HI HW-3 dropped [US-HIS5-005]; MS no separate reconciliation [US-MSS5-002]; VA VA-6 is NOT generated by the W-2 upload [US-VAS5-013]; AR forbids the RV record while IA, MD, MO, NE, WV require a state RV [rows in sections]. Contractor new-hire duties (CA DE 542, FL, IL, MA, ME, NH, NJ, OH, OR, VA, IA) exist in law but contractors / 1099-NEC are OUT of scope (owner instruction 2026-10-29, SKELETON-MANIFEST scope line): the Report Spec records them as scope traps, not as reports.
- **Special populations (US-OWN-005):** household employers file Schedule H federally, not 941 [US-FED-004]; state household/agricultural variants are in the sections: CT CT-W3 HHE / CT-941 HHE [US-CTS5-014], NJ-927-H [US-NJP51-001], OR Form OA / WA / OR-STT-A [US-ORS5-010], DC UC-30-H [US-DCS5-018], FL RT-7 [US-FLS5-020], GA DOL-4 [US-GAS5-005], SD 21D/21E [US-SDS5-020], UT annual election [US-UTS5-004], LA domestic annual [US-LAS5-007]. Nonresident aliens: Pub. 515 / Notice 1392, treaty-exempt wages to 1042-S not W-2 [US-FEDS5-016]; FL, NJ refuse ITIN on the wage report [US-FLS5-024, US-NJS5-017].
- **Language seam (US-OWN-006):** authority-facing filings in English; no statute fixing filing language located. Spanish/other-language duties attach to notices and posters only (e.g. CO DR 0995 notice in English + the employer's usual language [US-COS5-006]).

**Stage-5 reporting findings (verbatim, register ids inline).**

- **Deposits:** the 2026 lookback period runs 2024-07-01..2025-06-30. Liability of 50,000 or less in the lookback means a monthly depositor (deposit by the 15th of the following month); more than 50,000 means a semiweekly depositor (Wed/Thu/Fri paydays deposit the following Wednesday; Sat–Tue paydays deposit the following Friday). Any day with 100,000 or more accumulated must be deposited the next business day. Holidays follow DC legal holidays [US-FEDS5-004].
- **Form 941:** quarterly, due the last day of the month after the quarter; amounts in cents.
- **Form 944:** annual, only on written IRS notice, for liability of 1,000 or less.
- **Form 940:** annual, due Jan 31 (Feb 10 if all FUTA was deposited); FUTA is deposited when accumulated liability exceeds 500 [US-FEDS5-031]. The 2026 i940 is not yet published; I read the 2025 edition.
- **W-2/W-3 2026:**
  - Due to SSA and employees **2027-02-01**.
  - New Box 12 codes: **TP** (cash tips), **TT** (qualified overtime), **TA** (Trump-account employer contributions). Box 14 is split into 14a and **14b (Treasury Tipped Occupation Code)**.
  - W-2 is required at 2,000+ wages even with no withholding.
  - SSN may be truncated on employee copies only [US-FEDS5-007].
- **EFW2 TY2026:** **ACCESS gap** [US-FEDS5-032, gated].
- **ACA:** ALE members (50+ FT/FTE) file 1094-C/1095-C. For TY2025 these were due 2026-03-02 on paper and 2026-03-31 electronically, with e-filing required at 10+ returns. Furnishing can be done by website notice. The 2026 affordability percentage is **9.96%** [US-FEDS5-012, US-FEDS5-011]. The TY2026 i1094/1095-C is not yet published.
- **New hire:** report name, address, SSN, first-day-of-work, and employer name, address and FEIN within **20 days** of hire. Electronic filers may instead send two monthly transmissions 12–16 days apart. Multistate employers may designate one state [US-FEDS5-024].
- **Reporting-agent authorization:** Form 8655 [US-FED-008/009/028].
- Confidence: high, except EFW2 (not read).

**Stage-5 reporting findings (verbatim, register ids inline).**

- **W-2 boxes 15-20** carry up to TWO States and TWO localities. For more, issue a second W-2 repeating boxes a-f, never duplicating federal data on Copy A. On the W-3, box 15 = "X" when more than one State; boxes 16-19 are summed [US-MULTIS5-011].
- State copies and formats (EFW2 RS record or state spec) are per State, in the state units.
- **New hire.** A multistate employer that reports electronically may send all new-hire reports to ONE designated State after notifying HHS in writing. Deadline: 20 days, or two monthly transmissions 12-16 days apart [US-MULTIS5-006; CA restatement US-MULTIS5-010]. The HHS/OCSS registration page itself was not reachable (ACCESS gap).
- **UI quarterly wage report** goes to the `sui_state` only (localization, section 3). State income-tax withholding returns go to every State where withholding applies under section 2.
- **Registration / nexus triggers.** No federal rule. The trigger is the first wage subject to that State's withholding (section 2.4 thresholds) or the first localized employee (UI). Examples:
  - VT: out-of-state employers from 30 days [US-VTP52-003];
  - OR: an out-of-state employer withholds for OR residents working in OR, with relief at 300 or less [US-ORP10-001];
  - MI: out-of-state employers with employees working in MI [US-MIP51-001].
- Confidence: 85%.

**Stage-5 reporting findings (verbatim, register ids inline).**

- **No state withholding return, deposit schedule, annual reconciliation or state W-2 filing**, because there is no wage tax [US-AKWT-001].
- **Quarterly UI report: TQ01C (2/26)** with wage schedule [US-AKS5-013, US-AKS5-005]:
  - Due by the last day of the month after the quarter (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31), and within 10 days of ceasing business.
  - A zero report is required. The minimum late penalty is 10 USD.
  - Wages are reported in the quarter **paid**.
  - Per employee: SSN, name, reportable wages, **occupational code (6- or 8-digit) and 2-digit geographic code**. These are Alaska-specific DD fields.
  - Employer level: monthly counts of workers in the pay period that includes the 12th; excess wages; a "wages reported to other states" Y/N flag.
  - Corrections go on Form TADJ.
- **Channel:** `file_upload`, a CSV with no headings [US-AKS5-014, A.2], or online keying through the ES Tax portal, or paper.
  - Online filing is **mandatory** with 50+ employees in a quarter, 1M+ taxable wages in the current or prior year, or when a payroll agent files [US-AKS5-005].
  - The CSV layout (rev. 2016-05-17): a header record of 11 columns and a wage detail record of 12 columns (see the row).
- **Registration:** online, by mail or in person with UI Tax. Form TREG; the new-employer rate depends on the declared industry. The blue "Notice to Employees" placard must be posted (Form 07-1012) [US-AKS5-015]. Agent access: POA [US-AKP51-006].
- **New-hire reporting** (AS 25.27.075) [US-AKS5-007, A.3]:
  - Report within **20 days** of hire, rehire or return to work.
  - Fields: employee name, address and SSN; employer name, address and FEIN.
  - Channel: online through myAlaska. Fax is no longer accepted.
  - Multistate election goes through HHS.
  - Penalty: 10 USD per employee, 100 USD if conspiracy.
  - The child-support agency is now named **CSED**. Its ACSESS system with a Business Portal went live 2025-10-06 [US-AKS5-008, A.2].
  - The statute text itself was not read (ACCESS gap).
