# T-019

**Pergunta:** Côte d'Ivoire 2026: what is the monthly CNPS contribution ceiling for the family benefits, maternity and work-injury (AT/MP) branches, and what is the ceiling for the retirement branch?

**Resposta:**
- Family benefits (PF), maternity (AM) and AT/MP: **XOF 75,000/month** (= SMIG; 900,000/year).
- Retirement (pension): **XOF 3,375,000/month** (45 × SMIG).

Both have applied since 2023-01-01 and are still in force in 2026; no later communiqué was found. The SMIG of 75,000 is also the monthly floor for every branch.

The 70,000 figure is pre-2023 and wrong. It came from CLEISS and also appeared in our earlier HRBS-14996 opinion and in the skill.
