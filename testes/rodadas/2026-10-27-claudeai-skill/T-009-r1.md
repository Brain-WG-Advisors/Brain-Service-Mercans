## Resposta

**Yes.** An employer-paid life insurance premium for an employee is part of the ESV base, including premiums under long-term life insurance contracts.

- **Law No. 2464-VI, Art. 7(1)(1):** The employer's ESV base is the salary accrued to each insured person, covering basic and additional pay and other incentive and compensation payments (including in kind), as defined under Law No. 108/95-VR "On Remuneration of Labour".
- **Instruction on Wage Statistics No. 5 (Derzhkomstat order of 13.01.2004), sub-item 2.3.4:** Employer premiums for employees' voluntary insurance (personal or property) count as other incentive and compensation payments, except for the cases in item 3.5. They enter the wage fund in the month the payment is transferred to the insurer.
- **Item 3.5 of the same Instruction** excludes only employer contributions under voluntary medical and pension insurance contracts for employees and their family members. Life insurance is not on that list.
- **CMU Resolution No. 1170 of 22.12.2010, List section II, item 2:** The ESV exemption covers only payments under voluntary medical and pension insurance contracts for employees and their family members.
- **DPS position (ZIR knowledge base, category 201.04.01):** Employer premiums under long-term life insurance contracts for employees form part of salary and are subject to ESV. Employer contributions under non-state pension insurance contracts are not.
