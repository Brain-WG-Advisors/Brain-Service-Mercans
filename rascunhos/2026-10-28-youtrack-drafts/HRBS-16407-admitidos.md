@nkeer @lli Following up on the chat question about starters and leavers (65630): the EUR 500 per covered person is not prorated. DGT binding ruling V1967-17 (20/07/2017) states that the limit is an annual amount regardless of the employee working less than the full calendar year, so an employee who joins in August keeps the full EUR 500. The payer withholds (ingreso a cuenta) only when the premiums paid in the year exceed the annual exempt limit.
