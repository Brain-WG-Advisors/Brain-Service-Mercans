## 2. Standards (apply to every future change)

**Specs that go to development**
- Issued as the initial version: Version Control has one line only — `1.0 | <date> | Initial version. | Approved | Wallisson Gomes` — until development has received them. After hand-over, normal versioning resumes (one sentence per change).
- Columns T ("Query to RA") and U ("RA Comments") on the spec sheet are for development's questions and our answers only. We never fill them ourselves.
- No review or process language anywhere ("corrected", "revised", "review", "verified", "cross-check", "round", "DRAFT", "previously", "pending", "rule to be set", comparisons with an earlier version, remarks about the client's own files). Each explanation states the rule as it is, with its legal source.
- No internal references (scope_manifest, CO-GAP-xx, CCG control cells).
- Anything without a defined rule stays out of the spec and goes to OPEN-POINTS.
- After every edit: diff, zero formula cells, zero masked tokens, zero review words.

**Client data (PII)**
- Never run `mask_reports.py` over all files again: it would undo manual fixes. Mask only the new files.
- Rename any file whose name contains a person's name before it enters `02-client-reports-raw/`.
- PII audits by statistics only; never print values.

**Scope (agreed 15 Oct)**: only the statutory reports are built — income tax (Formulario 350, Formulario 220, Formato 2276), social security (PILA, cesantías) and Nómina Electrónica. Client operational reports are out of scope.

## 3. Decisions in force (with sources)

**PILA (AT2 v30, 24-07-2026)**
- T2R1-020 total payroll value = sum of campo 45 (IBC CCF).
- IBC CCF has no 25 SMMLV ceiling: base = monthly payroll (Ley 21/1982 art. 17), 70% for integral salary (Ley 789/2002 art. 49); the ceiling applies to pension, health and ARL only.
- Exoneration (ET art. 114-1): total earned in the month < 10 SMMLV (Decreto 1828/2013 art. 7); campo 76 = N whenever campo 43 >= 10 SMMLV.
- Campos 17-20, 32, 34 always blank; 57-60 always blank/zero. Campo 98 (activity code, Decreto 768/2022) closes record type 2 at position 693.
- Campo 38: ARL days add up to 30; lines with SLN, IGE, LMA or VAC-LR report the days of the event; if IRL > 0, same value (AT2 §2.1.2.3.13).
- Campos 45 and 95 may differ; each follows its own population rule.

**Nómina Electrónica**
- AuxilioTransporte only up to 2 SMMLV (Ley 15/1959); the collective-agreement allowance above that goes to AuxilioS/AuxilioNS.
- DiasTrabajados = "Days Worked" from the payroll.
- Prepaid medicine, complementary plan or health policy -> PlanComplementarios; other insurance -> OtrasDeducciones.
- Document types: cédula de extranjería 22, tarjeta de extranjería 21; PEP/PPT per the NE annex table.
- The client's current NE file is not a template (totals, integral flag, place of work, CUNE).

**Formulario 350 (Res. 000031/2024 + ET)**
- Casilla 93 = withholding actually practised, all pay groups and all runs of the month.
- Rounding to the nearest 1,000 at casilla level (ET art. 577).
- Non-resident foreign individuals: "Pagos exterior" section with the sheet-2 concept and country; 20% of the nominal payment, no depuration (ET art. 408).
- Contingent withholding (ET arts. 126-1/126-4) is practised by the fund; excluded from the 350.
- Pension withholding in the pensions row; NE RetencionFuente = casilla 93 + 94. Country codes: DIAN table.

**Formulario 220 (instruction guide, Res. 000022/2024)**
- Values in pesos rounded to the nearest 1,000; zero when no value. Casilla 4 = number assigned by the issuer.
- Pensions -> 50; other payments -> 46; vacation pay -> 42; per diems -> 43; habitual bonuses and salary in kind -> 36; non-habitual bonuses and non-salary in kind -> 46; food for salaries > 310 UVT -> 37, up to 310 UVT the excess over 41 UVT/month -> 38.
- Casilla 49 = cesantías deposited in the tax year (February deposit belongs to the year of the deposit, ET art. 27 num. 3). Casilla 52 = sum of 36-51, computed. Casilla 60 = withholding actually practised.
- Survivor/substitute pension certified to the person receiving it, casilla 50. Document types: 12, 13, 21, 22, 31, 41.

**Formato 2276 v4 (Anexo T3.45, Res. 000227/2025)**
- Amounts = calendar-year sums by payment date; employer-paid pensions included.
- dir/dpto/mun only when the residence country is Colombia; pais always.
- Food: pabop (> 310 UVT), pagahuvt (up to 41 UVT/month), vaex (excess). Per diems pavia; habitual bonuses pasa, others potro; pensions peju, one-off retirement payments potro; pensioner health apos, solidarity apof; ceco = deposits made in the year; vare = withholding practised.
- File: XML, ISO-8859-1, `Dmuisca_ccmmmmmvvaaaacccccccc.xml` (aaaa = year of sending), max 5,000 records per file.

**Cesantías**
- Leavers are excluded from the deposit and paid directly (Ley 50/1990 art. 99 num. 4); the deposit is net of direct payments already made.
- No direct payment of the consolidated balance to an active employee, except authorised cases (CST art. 254).
