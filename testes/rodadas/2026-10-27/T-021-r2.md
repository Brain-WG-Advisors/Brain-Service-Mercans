## Resposta

**Yes.** In the Republic of Congo, the employer withholds the residential TOL (*taxe d'occupation des locaux à usage d'habitation*) from employees' pay.

**Amount:** a flat **XAF 1,000 per employee per month**. It does not depend on salary or on the value of the home.

**Legal basis:** Article 14 of Loi n° 34-2013 (Finance Law 2014), which created the TOL. Article 14 was rewritten by Loi n° 48-2014 (Finance Law 2015), Article 8, Section 6, point 6.3 ("Recouvrement par retenue à la source sur les salaires"). For salaried individuals in the public and private sectors, the residential TOL is collected as an advance (acompte) through withholding at source by the employer, at XAF 1,000 per month. The text was published in the Journal Officiel, Édition spéciale n° 1-2015, p. 17 ([sgg.cg](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2015/congo-jo-2015-01-sp.pdf)).

**Points that matter for payroll configuration:**

- **The XAF 1,000 is an advance, not the final tax.** The annual residential TOL is set by Article 13(a) of the 2014 law. It is XAF 60,000 for the city centre and XAF 12,000 for the periphery, as reproduced in the TOL clauses of petroleum contracts approved by Loi n° 60-2022. Twelve monthly withholdings of XAF 1,000 cover the periphery amount exactly.
- **Some employees must file a regularisation by 20 April.** This applies to married employees occupying the same dwelling, under-housed employees, employees taxable at the city-centre rate, and employees occupying exempt premises. The same article provides that any erroneous withholding confirmed by the tax administration gives rise to a tax credit against future withholdings. The city-centre top-up is therefore settled by the employee, not withheld by the employer.
- **Remittance:** the TOL is remitted monthly, with the same deadline as income tax withholding (between the 10th and the 20th of the following month).

**Caveats:**

- An Exco Cacoges 2023 tax calendar lists XAF 1,000 per employee in the periphery and XAF 5,000 per employee in the city centre. I found no statutory text supporting a XAF 5,000 monthly withholding. The 2015 text sets a single XAF 1,000 rate.
- The 2025 PaySpace/Deel guide still shows XAF 1,000 per month per employee.
- I could not search the full text of Finance Law 2026 (Loi n° 42-2025) or the July 2026 amending finance law to confirm that no change was made. That is the one remaining point to close before treating the position as 100% current.
