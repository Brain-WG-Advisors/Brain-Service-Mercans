Which cities impose an income tax?
Twenty-four Michigan cities levy municipality taxes related to income. These taxes can be individual income tax, business taxes (e.g., withholding tax, corporate income tax, or partnership tax), and/or fiduciary income taxes.
City of Detroit
The Michigan Department of Treasury administers income taxes for the City of Detroit.
