## Resposta

**Yes, it applies.** Private-sector employees are in scope.

**Scope.** Law 16/2018 art. 8(1), as replaced by Law 4/2021 art. 1 (Official Gazette No. 8 bis (هـ), 3 March 2021, in force 4 March 2021), deducts 5 per 10,000 monthly from the salary of employees of public entities *"والعاملين لدى الأشخاص الاعتبارية الخاصة الذين تسرى بشأنهم أحكام القانون الخاص بالعمل"*, meaning employees of private legal persons governed by the Labour Law. Irregular and daily labour (*العمالة غير المنتظمة أو باليومية*) is excluded. Andersen Egypt's summary of the law gives the same reading: the monthly 5/10000 deduction covers employees of private legal persons subject to the labor law, except irregular or daily paid employees.

**Who bears it.** The employee. It is a salary deduction (*تخصم*). The employer withholds it and remits it; it is not an employer cost.

**Base.** There are two sources, and they differ:
- **MoF General Circular 14/2021, item 3:** the total monthly salary (*إجمالي الراتب الشهري*).
- **ETA Payroll-System FAQ v4 (March 2026), Q110:** basic disbursements only (*الصرفيات الأساسية فقط*), at EGP 5 per EGP 10,000. Additional disbursements are excluded.

The ETA system calculates the deduction on the FAQ basis, so that is the operative base in practice.

**Exclusion nuance.** The law excludes all irregular and daily labour. FAQ Q110 excludes only Egyptian daily labour.

**Remittance.** It goes through ETA Core Taxation as its own claim, separate from the salary-tax claim (FAQ salary-items Q12). Under Circular 14/2021, part 2, payment is due monthly by the 10th of the month after the deduction.

The CCG §8.2, Reports Spec EG-RPT-06, DD `martyrs_fund_subject` and QA §3.4 have all been corrected, so `EG-8003` now contradicts them.
