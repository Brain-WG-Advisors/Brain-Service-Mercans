# T-025

**Pergunta:** Canada payroll and payslip: how should annual maximums (e.g. CPP and EI maximum contributions) be applied in each pay period?

**Resposta:**
Per period, deduct the lesser of (a) the remaining annual maximum (annual max minus YTD contributions) and (b) rate x the period's contribution base; never annual maximum / 12. CPP: C = lesser of [CPP max x PM/12 - D] and [5.95% x (PI - 3,500/P)]; once the annual max is reached, deductions stop. EI follows the same lesser-of logic against its annual maximum. 2026 CPP max $3,519.45 (CPP2 max $416).
