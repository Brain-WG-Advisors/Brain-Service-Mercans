## Resposta

**Minimum contribution bases (*bases mínimas de cotización*), Régimen General, from 1 January 2026:**

| Grupo | Categories | Base mínima |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ingenieros y Licenciados; personal de alta dirección not covered by ET art. 1.3.c) | **1.989,30 €/month** |
| 2 | Ingenieros Técnicos, Peritos y Ayudantes Titulados | **1.649,70 €/month** |
| 3 | Jefes Administrativos y de Taller | **1.435,20 €/month** |
| 4–7 | Ayudantes no Titulados; Oficiales Administrativos; Subalternos; Auxiliares Administrativos | **1.424,40 €/month** |
| 8–11 | Oficiales de 1.ª y 2.ª; Oficiales de 3.ª y Especialistas; Peones; workers under 18 | **47,48 €/day** |

The maximum is 5.101,20 €/month (170,04 €/day) for every group.

**Legal source:** **Orden PJC/297/2026, de 30 de marzo**, art. 3 (BOE-A-2026-7296, BOE núm. 79 de 31/03/2026). It applies retroactively from 1 January 2026 and is issued under **Real Decreto-ley 3/2026, de 3 de febrero**, art. 3 (BOE-A-2026-2548). Following parliament's repeal of RDL 16/2025, the Order rests on RDL 3/2026, whose article 3 sets the references for minimum and maximum bases, ceilings, MEI and the solidarity contribution. The floor for groups 4–11 is tied to the SMI 2026 set by **RD 126/2026, de 18 de febrero** (BOE-A-2026-3815). The Order states that minimum bases for the contribution groups are fixed automatically at the amount set in RD 126/2026, increased by one sixth. TGSS's Boletín Noticias RED 5/2026 confirms the range runs from 1.989,30 €/month for group 1 down to 1.424,40 €/month in several groups, with 47,48 €/day for groups 8 to 11.

**Points to watch:**

- **The floor is 1.424,40 €, not 1.424,50 €.** Applying 7/6 to the monthly SMI of 1.221,00 € gives 1.424,50 €, but that is not the published figure. The floor comes from the rounded daily figure: 40,70 € × 7/6 = 47,48 €/day, and × 30 = 1.424,40 €. Use the published amounts as they stand.
- **This group table applies only to the *contingencias comunes* (common contingencies) base.** The AT/EP base (work accidents and occupational disease), which is also used for desempleo, FOGASA and Formación Profesional, applies the single global floor of 1.424,40 € and cap of 5.101,20 € under Orden art. 2. It does not use the per-group minimums.
- **Do not cite RDL 16/2025 or RDL 2/2026.** Both were repealed after publication. There is also no 2026 budget law, so if one is enacted mid-year these figures could be revised retroactively.
