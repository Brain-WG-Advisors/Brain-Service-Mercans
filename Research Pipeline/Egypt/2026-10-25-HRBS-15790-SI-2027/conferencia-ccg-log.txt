  ANTES : SIL 148/2019 provides that changes to SS contribution rates or the insurable wage ceiling take effect from the date the Minister of Finance issues the relevant gazette notification. Mid-period changes (within a contribution month) are handled by applying the new rates from the first day of the month following the effective date unless the effective date is the first of a month, in which case the new rates apply to that month's full contribution. NOSI administrative circulars confirm this approach. The change in the ceiling (e.g., from EGP 16,700 to a higher amount in a future year) is applied prospectively from the effective month.
  DEPOIS: The floor and ceiling of the contribution wage change automatically every 1 January under Article 53 of the Executive Regulations (PM Decree 2437/2021): +15% per year for seven years from 2021 (the last step is 1 January 2027: EGP 3,200 / 19,300), then by the inflation rate, rounded up to the nearest EGP 100; NOSI announces the amounts (2026: notice of 30 November 2025). The contribution rate rises by 1% every seven years from 1 January 2020, split equally between employer and employee, up to a total of 26% (Law 148/2019 Art. 19, last paragraph; Executive Regulations Art. 57): the first step is 1 January 2027 (OAD 12% / 9% → 12.5% / 9.5%), the next is 1 January 2034. Both changes apply from the January contribution month. Worked examples in this guide use the 2026 parameters (EGP 2,700 / 16,700; EE 11%, ER 18.75%).
