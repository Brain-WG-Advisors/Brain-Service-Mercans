Posters in the Workplace

Federal and Kansas State law requires that certain posters be displayed in the workplace. All required posters are FREE to download from the appropriate agencies.

REQUIRED STATE POSTERS
REQUIRED FEDERAL POSTERS

The following posters are required by the Kansas Department of Labor (KDOL) and may be downloaded and printed from this site:

Unemployment Insurance Poster
Attorney General of Kansas Human Trafficking Resources poster
Workers Compensation Rights and Responsibilities
KDOL Human Trafficking Poster (English)
Publicación sobre trata de personas del KDOL (Español)
Child Labor Poster (English)
Aviso sobre la ley de trabajo infantil (Español)

Note: The Child Labor Poster is only required if you are employing youth under 16 years of age and are not covered under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Please contact the Kansas Department of Labor with questions on the above postings.
