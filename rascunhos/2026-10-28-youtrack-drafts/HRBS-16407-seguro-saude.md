@nkeer @lli On the health insurance (65630), to clarify: I did not confirm a monthly threshold. The exemption is EUR 500 per year for each covered person (the employee, spouse and descendants; EUR 1,500 for each person with a disability), LIRPF art. 42.3.c and RIRPF art. 46. A fixed 41.67 a month does not follow this: it ignores the spouse and descendants covered, and it can tax premiums that are still within the annual limit, for example when the premium is paid once a year. The limit is annual and per covered person, so the exempt amount must follow the number of persons covered and the premiums paid in the year.
