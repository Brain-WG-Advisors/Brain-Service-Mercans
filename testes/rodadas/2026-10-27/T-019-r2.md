## Resposta

**Family benefits, maternity and AT/MP:** 75,000 FCFA per month (same as the contribution floor for all branches)

**Retirement:** 3,375,000 FCFA per month, which is 45 × the SMIG.

**Source:** CNPS Direction Générale, *Communiqué à l'attention des employeurs* (January 2023). It applies from 1 January 2023 and follows Decree No. 2022-986 of 21 December 2022, which raised the SMIG from 60,000 to 75,000 FCFA. I found no newer CNPS communiqué or SMIG decree, so these figures still apply in 2026.
https://www.cnps.ci/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/NOUVEAU-PLAFOND-DES-COTISATIONS-SOCIALES-DE-LA-CNPS.pdf

**Watch out:** Some secondary sources still show the old 70,000 FCFA ceiling for these three branches. These include CLEISS, whose table shows 70,000 FCFA for maternity, family benefits and AT/MP as of 1 January 2025, and Mercans' own DSR page, which lists per-branch ceilings of 70k / 3.375M. The 70,000 figure applied before the 2023 SMIG increase. It no longer works, because the ceiling would sit below the 75,000 FCFA floor.
