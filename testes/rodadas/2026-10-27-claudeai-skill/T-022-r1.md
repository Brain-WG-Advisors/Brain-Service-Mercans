## Resposta

**Employee deductions: Global 21100–21900. Employer contributions: Global 23640–23673.** Together that is 59 codes, and the Gabon WTC must carry all of them.

These are the two blocks missing from GA-WTC v3.0. They were added in **GA-WTC-001 v3.1 DRAFT** (HRBS-14978, 23 Sep 2026), alongside clearing the saved filter on column A and adding the 3.1 line to Version Control. The ticket decision was that Global deductions are mandatory for Gabon, including a non-cash deduction for BIK.

The 59 Global codes (54 + 5) are:

- **Deductions (54):** 21100, 21102, 21103, 21108–21125 (except 21126), 21127, 21140–21152, 21190, 21210–21213, 21400, 21520, 21610, 21611, 21640, 21641, 21642, 21645, 21670, 21800, 21810, 21820, 21890, 21900
- **Employer contributions (5):** 23640, 23670, 23671, 23672, 23673

The country-specific CNSS employer split (PVID 11% / PF 5% / AT-MP 2%) sits on top of these as separate elements and isn't part of the Global block.
