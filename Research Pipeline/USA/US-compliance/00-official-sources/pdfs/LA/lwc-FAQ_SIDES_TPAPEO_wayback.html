Unemployment Insurance

- Frequently Asked Questions -
From Employers Concerning State Information Data Exchange System (SIDES)
(Third-Party Administrator (TPA) and Professional Employer Organization (PEO) Information)

  1. If I am a TPA or a PEO, how do I connect my client to my SIDES broker?
  2. If I complete the POA upload on LAWATS, how are my employer clients connected to my SIDES broker?
  3. My Clients are linked to my agent account in LAWATS. Is that the same thing as SIDES?
  4. My client has stopped doing business with my PEO or TPA. How do I disconnect my former client from SIDES?
  1. If I am a TPA or a PEO, how do I connect my client to my SIDES broker?

    You must make sure you have completed the benefits Power of Attorney upload on the Louisiana Wage and Tax System (LAWATS).
    You are not actually uploading a signed POA document; you are just completing an online form to show that you represent a client.

    On that page, you must specify your broker number. If you are using the POA bulk upload page, you must specify your broker number in the broker number column (second-to-last column) for each client. If you do not specify your broker number, we cannot add your employer client to your SIDES broker.

    When entering your broker number, use only numbers and do not add leading zeroes (example: if you are broker BR00001, enter 1).
    For more information on uploading POAs to LAWATS, see https://www.laworks.net/UnemploymentInsurance/UI_Employers.asp.

    Return To Questions

  2. If I complete the POA upload on LAWATS, how are my employer clients connected to my SIDES broker?

    At this time, LAWATS does not automatically connect employers to SIDES. LA's SIDES team regularly runs a report of recently added POAs and if a broker number is indicated, we add the client to that SIDES broker. We then send a confirmation email to the email address on file for that POA.

    Return To Questions

  3. My Clients are linked to my agent account in LAWATS. Is that the same thing as SIDES?

    No. Linking between TPAs and employer clients in LAWATS is not related to SIDES. An employer client must have a benefits POA on file in LAWATS with the TPA or PEO's name in order to be connected to SIDES.

    Return To Questions

  4. My client has stopped doing business with my PEO or TPA. How do I disconnect my former client from SIDES?

    Use the POA Revoke page in LAWATS to remove the Power of Attorney from your client. Afterwards, please email SIDEShelp@lwc.la.gov to revoke the POA.

    Return To Questions

Who can be contacted for any other SIDES-related concerns? Please contact SIDEShelp@lwc.la.gov.
