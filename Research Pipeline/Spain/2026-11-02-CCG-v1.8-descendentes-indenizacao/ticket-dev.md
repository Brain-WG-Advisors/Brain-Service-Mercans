**Summary:** Spain: CCG v1.8 — IRPF rate truncation (58118), health insurance (65630), descendants counted by half in withholding, severance excess in the SS base

Spain is live. CCG v1.8 (includes the v1.7 changes from HRBS-16407, whose change ticket was not opened yet) changes the configuration rules below; please review them against the production setup.

1. IRPF withholding rate (6.1.2, 58118): the final rate has two decimals and is truncated, not rounded (RIRPF arts. 86.1 and 87.3.c; AEAT algorithm, TRUNCAR (TIPO)). In HRBS-16407, 58118 holds 0.395 for 48,879.93 / 123,883.63 = 39.45%; the expected value is 0.3945.
2. Health insurance (65630): the exemption is EUR 500 per year for each covered person (employee, spouse and descendants; EUR 1,500 with a disability), LIRPF art. 42.3.c and RIRPF art. 46, applied on the premiums paid in the year and not prorated for starters or leavers (DGT V1967-17). Please confirm the setup multiplies the limit by the number of persons covered. STG's 41.67 monthly cap is a separate CR, not part of this change.
3. Descendant minimum in withholding (6.1.1 and 7.1): each descendant counts as half, unless the employee is exclusively entitled to the whole minimum for that descendant (RIRPF art. 84.2.º).
4. Severance (9.1, Treatment of Excess, ETP-002): the dismissal indemnity is exempt from Social Security only up to the compulsory amount; if the contract ends before conciliation, or in collective (ET art. 51) or objective (ET art. 52.c) dismissals for economic, technical, organisational, production or force majeure reasons, up to the unfair-dismissal amount (LGSS art. 147.2.c). The excess is included in the base and prorated over the 12 months before the month of the termination (RD 2064/1995 art. 23.2.B). ES-CRA-001 v2.5: 58188 → 0054/I (was E), and 58184/58186/58188 prorated over the 12 months before the termination month (TGSS CRA manual, 1 Jul 2026, "CRA 0051 a 0054"). WTC V4.9: 58188 flags unchanged; please confirm the engine reports the excess only through the prorated L03 settlements (ES-BASES-001 v3.4) and not in the termination-month base.
