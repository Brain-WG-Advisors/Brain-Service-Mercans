## Resposta

**IRPP:** An indemnité de transport is exempt up to **30% of base monthly salary**, provided it is granted to all employees (CGI Art. 45-13°). Only the excess goes into the IRPP base. If the allowance is not granted to all staff, the whole amount is taxable. The 30% cap is independently confirmed: since the 2019 Finance Law, the transport allowance deductible from the PIT base is limited to 30% of each employee's basic salary, where previously there was no limit.

**CNPS:** The portion within the same 30% limit is excluded from the insurable salary. The basis is Art. 7 of the CNPS guide, which lists "Indemnité mensuelle de transport" among expense-reimbursement items outside the base. Any excess is included in the base and is subject to the 500,000 XAF/month ceiling.

**Example:** With a base salary of 300,000 XAF and a transport allowance of 100,000 XAF, 90,000 XAF is exempt from both IRPP and CNPS. The remaining 10,000 XAF is taxable and contributable. In the reports, the exempt part is excluded from IRPP-TS R10 and CNPS Appel Mensuel R18. In the DAS, it is split between R30/FI-016 (exempt portion) and R31/FI-017 (taxable portion).

**One caveat on CNPS:** The 30% limit comes from the tax code. The CNPS guide's Art. 7, as cited in the CCG, names the item without stating a percentage. Our artifacts apply the same 30% ceiling to CNPS for consistency, and I couldn't locate the CNPS guide text online to confirm whether it sets its own limit.

**Inconsistency in the Chad artifacts (TFE, not part of your question):** The Report Specs' TFE section (TFE-001) puts the transport allowance in the TFE base even when it is IRPP-exempt. That contradicts confirmed design clarification #4 and the CCG, both of which say TFE and TAFP follow the IRPP base, so the exempt portion is outside TFE and TAFP too.
