Wage & Hour
The Bureau of Wage and Hour Administration enforces Ohio laws that set minimum wage requirements, protect minors in the workplace, and ensure fair pay on public improvement projects. These laws include the Minimum Wage Law (Ohio Revised Code 4111), the Minor Labor Law (Ohio Revised Code 4109), and the Prevailing Wage Law (Ohio Revised Code 4115).
