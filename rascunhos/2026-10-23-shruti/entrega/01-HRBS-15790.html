Texto para entrega

Hi Mustafa,

We confirm all four changes from 1 January 2027:

- Contribution wage floor/ceiling: EGP 3,200 / 19,300 per month (from 2,700 / 16,700). Executive Regulations (PM Decree 2437/2021), Art. 53: both limits rise by 15% every 1 January for seven years (2021–2027), rounded up to the nearest EGP 100 (2,700 × 1.15 → 3,200; 16,700 × 1.15 → 19,300). NOSI's 2027 notice is not yet published.
- Rates: employee 11% → 11.5%, employer 18.75% → 19.25%. Law 148/2019, Art. 19 (last paragraph): the contribution rate increases by 1% every seven years from 1 January 2020, split equally between employer and employee; Executive Regulations, Art. 57 applies it to the old-age, disability and death branch (9% → 9.5%; 12% → 12.5%).

EG-CCG-001 updated to v1.5 with the 2027 values: [link]

@mjain For Reg. 23 (EG Social Security), from 01-01-2027: floor/ceiling 38,400 / 231,600 per year in all bands; under-60 base band employee 10% → 10.5% and employer 14.75% → 15.25%. We expect the 1%/4% layer and the 60+ band to stay unchanged, as they do not include the old-age branch; please confirm against the band composition. Would you like us to raise a Regulation Update task, or will you handle it from here?

Best regards,
