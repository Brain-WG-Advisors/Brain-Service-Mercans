P11: Residency:
P12: Who is a resident:
P13: The following individuals will be treated as tax-resident in Lithuania.
P14: An individual whose permanent place of residence during the tax period is in Lithuania.
P15: An individual whose personal, social, or economic interests during the tax period may be considered to be in Lithuania rather than in a foreign country.
P16: An individual who stays in Lithuania, continuously or intermittently, for 183 or more days during the tax period.
P17: An individual who stays in Lithuania, continuously or intermittently, for 280 or more days during a number of successive tax periods and who, during one of such periods, stayed in Lithuania, continuously or intermittently, for 90 or more days.
P18: An individual who is a Lithuanian citizen and does not satisfy the criteria set out in (3) or (4) above where such individual receives one’s remuneration for work under an employment contract or any other substantially similar contract and has the costs of living in another country covered from the state or municipal budgets of Lithuania.
P19: Who is subject to Income tax in Lithuania
P20: Resident:
P21: Residents are chargeable to tax in Lithuania on their worldwide income. 
P22: Non-residents:
P23: Non-residents are only chargeable to tax Lithuania on income sourced in Lithuania. Income from employment from a fixed base in Lithuania is deemed to be sourced in Lithuania.
P24: Tax calculation methods:
P25: Monthly calculation – The pay for each pay period is dealt with separately. No refunds may be made by the employer in such cases. There is no year-end reconciliation in Lithuania.
P26: Minimum Wage:
P27: The Minimum wage is € 1153 per month. It is used in the tax exemption amount calculation and employer social security calculation – See relevant sections below.
P28: Income Tax
P29: Classes of income:
P30: A Class includes:
P31: income from employment or from similar relations income from sports activities, performer ‘s activities, interest and royalties received.
P32: B Class includes:
P33: income from gambling and lottery winnings, income received as remuneration in providing services under the service receipt received and  income not classified under class A.
P34: Employment relations or corresponding relations: 
P35: shall mean relations when work is carried out under employment contracts, as well as any other activity performed on the basis of legal ties corresponding (by agreement as regards remuneration for work, workplace and duties, work discipline, etc.) to relations between an employer and employee created under the employment contract.

P37: In paying out amounts attributable to category A income of an individual under the procedure for tax payment, a resident of Lithuania, a Lithuanian entity, a permanent establishment or a non-resident of Lithuania engaged in individual activities through his fixed base, in the capacity of tax withholders, must calculate, withhold and pay into the budget income tax.

P39: Taxable Pay
P40: Taxable pay is pay that is subject to income tax.
P41: Pay is subject to income tax in the month that is paid to the employee and not in the month that it was earned in case of backdated and arrear payments.
P42: Taxable earnings are all earnings received in cash and in kind from employment this include:
P43: Wages,
P44: Salaries,
P45: Overtime,
P46: Bonuses,
P47: Employee stock option benefits,
P48: Incentive payments, 
P49: Allowances,
P50: Sick leave pay,
P51: Leave pay out,
P52: Termination/Severance pay,
P53: Gratuities, 
P54: Prizes/Gifts,
P55: Note: When an employee was supposed to receive pay in a particular month and by error on the employer’s behalf the employee was not paid, income tax should be calculated in the month the employee received the payment and not the month the payment was supposed to be received had there not been an error.

P59: The employer does not resubmit the January and February tax reports.

P65: Non-Taxable Pay
P66: Non-taxable pay is pay that is not subject to income tax. It includes the following:
P67: Benefit for a death in a family (in case of close relative ‘s death).
P68: Daily allowances paid during Business Trip (proved by invoices or receipts)
P69: Travel expenses reimbursement (proved by invoices or receipts)
P70: Gifts/prize up to maximum of € 200 per year.
P71: Employee stock options exercised not earlier than 3 years following the grant date and exercised at no cost or for lower than their fair market value.
P72: Railway or public transport tickets for the employee’s to/from one's workplace provided by the employer.
P73: Life insurance contributions, health insurance contributions, and pension contributions paid by an employer on behalf of an employee provided that the total amount of such contributions does not exceed 25% of the employee's annual employment income.

P78: Tax Exemption Amount of Income (TEAI)/NPD:
P79: Is amount of income treated as exempt for tax purpose, it is determined based on the employee’s incapacity (disability) to work or monthly income:
P80: Employees with incapacity to work (disability):
P81: Incapacity between 0% to 25%, TEAI = € 1 127
P82: Incapacity between 30% to 55%, TEAI =€ 1 057 
P83: Employees not earning more than the minimum wage:
P84: TEAI = € 747.
P85: Employees earning above minimum wage but below € 2 387.27: 
P86: TEAI = € 747 minus 0.49 x (monthly earnings minus minimum wage)
P87: Employees earning above € 2 387.29:
P88: TEAI = € 400 minus 0.18 x (monthly earnings minus € 642)
P89: Article 20 of The Law on Personal Income Tax
P90: Note: Where TEAI calculated is negative, TEAI is equal to zero. Unused TEAI may not be carried forward to the next period.
P91: Monthly income
P92: This is the taxable pay minus non-taxable income. 
P93: Note: Monthly income is the amount paid to the employee in the current month, regardless of when the amount was earned.
P94: Example:
P95: an employee started working from 1 January earning € 2 000 per month, however the employer only put the employee on the payroll in March and paid them € 6 000.
P96: In March TEAI will be calculated on the € 6 000 (TEAI will be nil) 
P97: The employer does not resubmit the January and February tax reports.
P98: The employee will be able to get the overpaid taxes when submitting their tax return.
P99: Calculation steps:
P100: Determine whether employee is resident of Lithuania:
P101: If no, do not calculate TEAI.
P102: If yes, move to next step.
P103: Determine if they have an incapacity to work:
P104: Yes, determine the incapacity
P105: 0 – 25%, TEAI = € 1 127.
P106: 30 – 55%, TEAI = € 1 057.
P107: No, move to next step
P108: Determine the employee’s monthly income,
P109: Determine if it is below the minimum wage:
P110: If yes, TEAI = 747
P111: If no, move to next step.
P112: Determine if it is below or abovel € 2 387.29:
P113: If yes: TEAI = € 747 minus 0.49 x (monthly earnings minus minimum wage)
P114: If no: TEAI = € 400 minus 0.18 x (monthly earnings minus € 642)
P115: Note: TEAI is only applicable to employees who are tax residents of Lithuania (non-residents may claim the exemption upon filing their annual returns). For TEAI to apply on payroll, the employee must provide the employer with a written consent for the employer to apply TEAI on the payroll. 
P116: Detailed Example:
P117: Example 1:  Below OR Equal Minimum Wage 
P118: An Employee is paid € 840, which is less than the minimum wage of €1,153 They did not provide consent to apply the TEAI exemption on the payroll.
P119: TEAI = 0 
P120: If they had provided consent:
P121: TEAI = 747
P122: Example 2: Below or Equal € 2 387.29 
P123: An Employee is paid € 2 000, which is more than the minimum wage of € 1153. They provided consent to apply the TEAI exemption on the payroll.
P124: TEAI = 747 – 0.49 x (2 000 – 1153)
P125:  = 331,97
P126: Example 3: Above € 2 387.29  
P127: An Employee is paid € 3 000, which is more than the minimum wage of € 1153. They provided consent to apply the TEAI exemption on the payroll.
P128: TEAI = 747 – 0.49 x (3 000 – 1153)
P129:  = 0
P130: Example 4: 0 to 25% incapacity
P131: An Employee is paid € 5 000, they have an incapacity to work of 25% and have provided consent to apply the TEAI exemption on the payroll.
P132: TEAI = 1 127
P133: Example 5: 30 to 55% incapacity
P134: An Employee is paid € 3 000, they have an incapacity to work of 30% and have provided consent to apply the TEAI exemption on the payroll.
P135: TEAI = 1 057

P176: Social Security
P177: Lithuania has a comprehensive social security system that offers a range of benefits and social securities, including maternity support.If an employee is employed through an employment contract, they are covered by State Social Insurance, with a certain percentage of their salary each month being deducted directly as a contribution. Social security related services in Lithuania are administrated by SODRA – the State Social Insurance Fund Board under the Ministry of Social Security and Labour.
P178: Employees contribute to Pension social insurance, illness, maternity and health insurance contribution while the employer contributes to unemployment insurance, accidents and occupational diseases and guaranteed funds

P192: Social security Base
P193: Social security base is pay that is subject to social security.
P194: Pay is subject to social security in the month that is earned by the employee and not in the month that payment is made to the employee in case of backdated and arrear payments.
P195: Social security base is calculated on gross salary (same as taxable pay) of the employee excluding the following:
P196: Benefit for a death in a family (in case of close relative ‘s death).
P197: Daily allowances paid during Business Trip (proved by invoices or receipts)
P198: Travel expenses reimbursement (proved by invoices or receipts)
P199: Gifts/prize up to maximum of € 200 per year.
P200: Sick leave compensation.
P201: Note: When an employee was supposed receive pay in a particular month and by error on the employer’s behalf the employee was not paid, social security should be calculated in the month the employee was supposed to have been paid had there not been an error.
P202: Example 1:
P203: An employee started working from 1 January earning € 2 000 per month, however the employer only put the employee on the payroll in March and paid them € 6 000.
P204: In March, social security will be calculated on the € 2 000 pay received for March. 
P205: The employer must resubmit January and February social security declaration to declare the social security calculated on the € 2 000 earned for January and February respectively.  This will cause interest for late payment of social security contributions as well as penalties.  
P206: Note: It is not allowed to enrol employees to Payroll so late, it is controlled by Sodra as employers are obligated to submit a new hire report (1SD) 1 day before EE start date. As of then, Sodra will be expecting monthly reports (until the 15th of the next month) and SSC payments from the Employer. 
P207: Example 2:
P208: On 1 March, an employee receives a backdated increase of €200 per month starting from 1 January. Their normal salary was €2000, there in March they received €2 600.
P209: In March, social security will be calculated on the € 2 200 pay received for March additionally for € 200 (January and February). Social security contributions should be calculated based on January/February/March month’s data (in case of change). 
P210: Employer has to resubmit the January and February social security declarations.
P211: Example 3:
P212: In the March 2024 payroll, an employee is paid €5 000 as overtime due to them from December 2023.
P213: The €5 000 is not subject to social security in March 2024.
P214: The employer will have to resubmit the December 2023 social security declaration.
P215: Note: 
P216: There is a limit of 5 years in which the employer may make social security corrections, but that will be a lot of interests. A common practice in Lithuania is to reimburse what was missed in the current month paying a bonus to employee. 
P217: A salary change can have an influence on an average salary, so if you resubmit Dec 2023 or Jan and Feb 2024, the employee’s salary for past periods will change, this means that you will have to recalculate all later payments that are based on average salary if any, such as holiday pay, sick leave. 
P218: It is not common change salary backdated, employee must sign an addendum of an agreement 1 day before the change.

P230: Unemployment social insurance 
P231:  The contribution rates depend on the employment contract type of the employee:
P232: Open-ended employment contract - 1.31% 
P233: Fixed-term employment contract - 2.03%
P234: Note: When an employee’s employment contract changes during the month from Open-ended to fixed-term employment or vis versa, this should be reported to Sodra, and a new employment record should be opened for the employee. For that month the employee with have two records, for the part of the month where the employee was in open-ended employment, the employer’s contribution is calculated using the rate of 1.31% and the other part where the employee is in a fixed-term employment contract the employer’s contribution is calculated using the rate of 2.03%.

P237: Group I - 0.14%
P238: Group II - 0.43%
P239: Group III - 0.7%
P240: Group IV - 1.4%
P241: Contribution to the Guarantee Fund: 0.16%. The Bank of Lithuania, Budget institutions, political parties, trade unions, religious communities and communities are excluded from making contributions.
P242: Contribution to the Long-Term Employment Benefits Fund: 0.16%. The Bank of Lithuania and Budget institutions are excluded from making contributions.

P246: Social Security Calculation Process
P247: Processing for Social Security 
P248: Every month an employer must pull a statement from the Sodra portal:
P249: Statement S47: the statement will indicate social security rates for the month,
P250: Statement S50: this must be pulled only on occasion (after receiving Sodra message),It will indicate: 
P251: whether an employee has reached their contribution limit,
P252: refunds due to the employee,
P253: additional contributions due by the employer (in case employee earned less than minimum wage) see note below:
P254: Note 1: Monthly earnings less than minimum wage:
P255: Where the employees’ earnings for social security are below the minimum wage amount, currently € 1153 for 2026, the employer’s contribution shall be based on the minimum wage, Unless if:
P256: The employee 24 years of age or younger,
P257: The employee by more than one employer, or
P258: The employee is a pension recipient.
P259: Then the contribution will be calculated based on the actual earnings paid.
P260: Sodra at the end of the month will issue a report notifying the employer if they have overpaid or underpaid their social security contributions. If the employer underpaid contributions, they will have to make additional payments as indicated in the S50 statement.
P261: In case where an employee was on unpaid leave Sodra will prorate the minimum wage provided it was reported to Sodra (report 12 SD) using the formula: MMA (minimum wage) / official working days of the month x actual working days.

P370: Statutory Reports
P371: Statutory reports in Lithuania are the compulsory submission of payroll-related data to government authorities, ensuring adherence to legal and regulatory obligations. Employers must accurately report employee earnings, tax deductions, social security contributions and other relevant information.
P372: Payroll submissions are done through either of the following channels:
P373: Online submissions
P374: Manual capture – This allows the employer to manually update reports by manually capturing reports in the system.
P375: File uploads - This allows employers to use files created by payroll software and upload them to authority portals.
P376: Manual/in-person submission – This allows the employer to hand over the report and supporting documentation to the authorities in person.
P377: Monthly Reports:
P378: GPM 313
P379: GPM313 is a report to the tax authority, it provides information on Employer level on earnings and benefits paid to the employee and tax deducted for the month. 
P380: For each payroll month, you must provide the following information:
P381: Total earnings and benefits paid to your employees,
P382: Tax withheld for the month split between tax withheld from employee pay before the 15th and tax withheld after the 15th of the month.
P383: Due Date:
P384: Submitted online, due by the 15th of the following month.
P385: SAM
P386: SAM is a report to the social security authority (SODRA), it provides information on employee level on earnings and benefits paid and the social security tax deducted/contributed for the month. 
P387: For each payroll month, you must provide the following information for each employee:
P388: Employee social security number
P389: Total earnings and benefits paid to your employees,
P390: Total social security deducted from each employee,
P391: Total social security contributions made by the employer for each employee.
P392: Due Date:
P393: Submitted online, due by the 15th of the following month.
P394: Annual Reports
P395: GPM 312
P396: GPM312 is a report to the tax authority, it provides information on employee level on earnings and benefits paid and the tax deducted for the full tax year.
P397: For each tax year, you must provide the following information for each employee:
P398: Employee tax identification number or other forms of identification.
P399: Total earnings and benefits paid to the employees, separated by the tax code and the tax rate applied to each tax code earning or benefit,
P400: Total deducted 
P401: Due Date:
P402: Submitted online, due by the 15th of the February of the following year.
