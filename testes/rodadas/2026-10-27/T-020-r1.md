## Resposta

**1.45%**, made up of WGA 0.91% and ZW-flex 0.54%.

Sector 42 is Groothandel II (wholesale II). The governing instrument is the Besluit gedifferentieerde premie Whk 2026, Bijlage "Sectorale premies 2026", which lists sector 42 Groothandel II at WGA 0,91 and ZW 0,54. In the Handboek Loonheffingen 2026 (March 2026 edition), the table is Tabel 10, "Sectorale premies voor de gedifferentieerde premie Whk (voor kleine werkgevers)", on p. 518, and §7.6.1 "U bent een kleine werkgever" is on p. 152.

A small employer for 2026 is one whose 2024 wage bill (loonsom) was at most €1,082,500. That threshold is set in Article 1 of the Besluit.

I couldn't open the Handboek PDF itself to read the row in Tabel 10. The rates above come from the Besluit published on wetten.overheid.nl (BWBR0051245), which is the source Tabel 10 reproduces.
