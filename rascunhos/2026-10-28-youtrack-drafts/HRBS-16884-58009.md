# HRBS-16884 — pergunta da Lily sobre 58009 na 58163 (06/11)

Draft gravado com ok do usuário em 06/11, não publicado.

> @lli Yes. Per CCG 6.1.2 (PROJECT_ANNUAL_GROSS), the current month counts the fixed pay plus the variable paid in the month, so 58009 goes into 58163. Variable paid in earlier months sits in the YTD part, and only MAX(0, prior-year variable − YTD variable) is projected for the remaining months (item 2 of our 05/11 comment).

Fontes: CCG v1.8 §6.1.2 (parágrafos 852, 857, 972–1001); WTC V4.9; CONFERENCIA STG agosto B1(a). [[Espanha]]

> @lli Yes. Per CCG 6.1.2, the variable paid in the month is part of the annual gross, so 58009 should be added to 58163.
