## Resposta

**WT 79432 – Parental Leave Payout:** Clave L, Subclave 27. The "Prestación por nacimiento y cuidado del menor" must be reported in the paying entity's Modelo 190 under Clave L, Subclave 27 (Prestaciones públicas por maternidad o paternidad exentas del IRPF), since it is simply a formal renaming of the former maternity/paternity benefits. The exempt amount is capped at the maximum benefit recognised by Social Security; any excess is taxed as employment income under Clave A.

**WT 79430 – Pregnancy Risk Payout:** Clave B, Subclave 03. The pregnancy-risk benefit is not exempt because it falls under neither letter h) nor letter z) of Ley 35/2006 (IRPF), and is reported under Clave B, Subclave 03; it is also distinct from the temporary incapacity (ILT) benefit.

**WT 79429 – Breastfeeding Risk Payout:** Clave B, Subclave 03. The AEAT guidance does not name this benefit explicitly. It is treated the same way as pregnancy risk because it is likewise a non-exempt Social Security subsidy outside art. 7.h) and 7.z) LIRPF. It should not be confused with the benefit for co-responsible care of an infant. That benefit is exempt under art. 7.z) LIRPF and goes to Clave L.20.

**Source:** AEAT, Modelo 190, "Preguntas frecuentes" (page updated 08/09/2026). The relevant headings are "¿Cómo deben declararse las prestaciones de pago directo de maternidad, paternidad o riesgo durante el embarazo?" and "¿Qué es la 'Prestación por nacimiento y cuidado del menor'? ¿Dónde ha de consignarse en el modelo 190?":

One point affects which of these keys the employer actually uses. These benefits are normally paid directly by the INSS or the Mutua, and the paying entity is the one that reports them. If the employer pays the maternity benefit itself, it also reports those amounts under L.27, within the limits of art. 7.h) paragraphs 2 and 3 LIRPF. A voluntary employer top-up is a different concept. Voluntary benefit complements, like other salary supplements, are a different pay item from the benefit and go under Clave A, in the "not derived from incapacity" field.
