## Resposta

Under **Article 17, Part 1 of Law of Ukraine No. 1105-XIV "On Compulsory State Social Insurance"** (dated 23.09.1999), the sick leave benefit is paid as a share of average salary (income), depending on the insurance record (stazh):

| Insurance record (stazh) | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 years | 50% |
| 3 to 5 years | 60% |
| 5 to 8 years | 70% |
| Over 8 years | 100% |

The Pension Fund of Ukraine (PFU) confirms this scale under Article 17 of the Law. A PFU letter of 14.10.2024 (No. 2800-030401-8/62121) sets out the same four bands under Article 17 of Law No. 1105.

**100% regardless of stazh** applies to these groups:
- Chornobyl categories 1–3
- War veterans, injured participants of the Revolution of Dignity, and families of fallen defenders
- Victims of Nazi persecution
- Rehabilitated victims of repression
- Blood donors: this provision was repealed on 26 January 2026, but donors who qualified before 25 January 2026 keep the benefit for one year after their donation

**Other rules:**
- **Diia City residents' employees and gig specialists:** they get the full salary on which contributions were actually paid, regardless of stazh.
- **Monthly cap for 2026:** the benefit cannot exceed the maximum contribution base, which is 8,647 × 20 = UAH 172,940.
- **Less than 6 months of stazh in the 12 months before the illness:** the benefit cannot exceed the amount calculated from the minimum wage.

**Citation note:** many older sources still cite these rates as Article 24 of Law 1105. That was the numbering before the law was restructured. The current article is **Article 17**.

**2026 changes:** the amendments that took effect on 1 April 2026 (Law No. 4683-IX) did not change these rates. They mainly expanded the PFU's powers to verify whether sick-leave certificates are justified.
