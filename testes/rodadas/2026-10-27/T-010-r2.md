## Resposta

**4DF income code: 157.** Under the Directory of Income Codes in Appendix 2 to the Procedure (MoF Order No. 4 of 13.01.2015), income paid to a self-employed person is reported by the tax agent in Annex 4DF under code "157", and it applies to income paid to a FOP or a person carrying out independent professional activity. The STS position is set out in STS letter of 24.02.2025 No. 162/2/99-00-24-01-03-02. In the row, columns 3a (accrued income), 3 (paid income) and 6 (income code, "157") are filled, while columns 4a/4 (PIT accrued/transferred) and 5a/5 (military levy accrued/transferred) are left blank.

**PIT: not withheld.** If the FOP provides a copy of the document confirming state registration as an entrepreneur (an extract from the Unified State Register), PIT/ML is not withheld from the income paid (TCU Art. 177.8).

**Military levy: not withheld.** This rests on the same basis. The STS confirms that the payer does not withhold military levy on income paid to a FOP, provided the FOP supplies a copy of its registration document (TCU Art. 177.8, Section IV).

**ESV: not due from the payer.** ESV is not payable if the civil-law contract is with a FOP and the works or services match the activity types recorded in the Unified State Register (Law No. 2464-VI, Art. 4(1)(1)). The FOP therefore gets no row in Annex D1. If the services fall outside the FOP's registered KVEDs, this exemption no longer applies.

**Mercans wage type:** I can't confirm the element number without the current Ukraine WTC in front of me, and I won't guess one. To be compliant, the element needs 4DF = 157, PIT = No, ML = No, ESV = No, and no D1 category. It also has to be separate from the non-FOP CPC element (4DF 102, KZO 21), which does carry PIT, ML and ESV. If you send the WTC, I'll point to the exact row.

One thing to flag: one of the earlier Ukraine DD/spec drafts labelled code 157 as "non-resident income – general." That label is wrong, and it's worth checking whether it made it into the released DD.
