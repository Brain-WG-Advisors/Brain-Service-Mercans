## Findings
1. **Pensioners paid by the employer (PENDING #41).** The current provider codes them TipoTrabajador 01, SubTipoTrabajador 01, TipoContrato 2, Sueldo 0.00, and reports the pension as OtroConcepto (ConceptoNS). This is practice evidence, not a legal source: Anexo Tecnico v1.0 table 5.5.4 defines 01 as 'Dependiente pensionado por vejez activo'. The spec (M54) maps 01 only to an employed old-age pensioner and has no rule for a pensioner who does not work.
2. **Health of sub-type 01 - CONFLICT with the spec.** In the files, every 01/01 record carries a health deduction (amount = 4% / 10% / 12% of the pension for 394 / 51 / 8 pensioners - the bands BAU described), while the Porcentaje field says 4 in 453 of 454. The spec (M183) says that for an employed old-age pensioner, sub-type 01, Salud is emitted with Porcentaje 0.00 and Deduccion 0.00. The spec statement must be checked against the primary sources (health contribution of pensioners and of working pensioners: Ley 100 de 1993 arts. 157, 204; Decreto 780 de 2016) before anything changes.
3. **Vacation paid on termination.** In the 2 termination files it appears as OtroConcepto 'Vacaciones Definitivas', not as VacacionesCompensadas (spec M131). The spec follows the DIAN annex; this is a provider practice to record, not a spec change. Q16 (fractional days) cannot be verified here: the provider layout carries no day quantity.
4. **Voluntary retirement plan (WT 62151).** Reported as OtroConcepto 'Suma Transaccional-PRV' (16) within ordinary employees 01/00 - consistent with BAU's answer (bonus, not a pension); no spec conflict.
5. **Apprentices** (19 / 4 with the support record): consistent with the spec bidirectional rule (G177 / M67).
6. **37 employees with Sueldo 0.00** (01/00): observation only; the spec requires the contract salary. Not a rule issue unless the cause is known.
