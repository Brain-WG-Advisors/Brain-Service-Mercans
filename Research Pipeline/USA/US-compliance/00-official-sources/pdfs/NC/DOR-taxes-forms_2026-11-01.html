Renew Your 2027 IFTA/Intrastate License and Decals

Important Notice Published Regarding Dyed Diesel Penalties

The Department will not impose a penalty when dyed diesel fuel is sold or used from bulk storage for highway use associated with farming and agricultural purposes. Review for guidance.
