## Resposta

**Salud: 4% of the IBC, deducted from the employee.** Being a working pensioner doesn't change the salary-side health contribution. The total stays at 12,5% (8,5% employer / 4% employee) under **Ley 100 de 1993 art. 204**, as amended by **Ley 1122 de 2007 art. 10**. The employer's 8,5% can still be exonerated under **ET art. 114-1** if the employee earns under 10 SMMLV. The separate health contribution on the pension *mesada* is withheld by the pension payer, not by this payroll.

**FondoPension: not due.** **Ley 100 art. 17**, as modified by **Ley 797 de 2003 art. 4**, provides that the obligation to contribute ceases once the affiliate meets the requirements for the minimum old-age pension, or is pensioned for invalidity or early retirement. Colpensiones doctrine confirms the obligation ends for the General Pension System, but not for the Health and Occupational Risk systems. Since the Fondo de Solidaridad Pensional rides on the pension contribution, **FondoSP** doesn't apply either.

**In the XML:**
- `Trabajador/@SubTipoTrabajador` = `01` (*Dependiente pensionado por vejez activo*), per Anexo Técnico §5.5.4.
- `Deducciones/Salud`: `Porcentaje="4.00"`, with `Deduccion` = 4% of the IBC.
- `Deducciones/FondoPension` is mandatory in the XSD (1–1), so it can't be left out. Send it with `Porcentaje="0.00"` and `Deduccion="0.00"`. Our reference doesn't record DIAN explicitly confirming that zero values pass validation, so it's worth a test send before production.
- `Deducciones/FondoSP`: omit it, since it's optional (0–1).
