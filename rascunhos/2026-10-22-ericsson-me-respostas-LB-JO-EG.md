## #4 1026 Housing Advance e #5 1027 Salary Advance Payment (Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon)

**N:**
Egypt — Tax: an advance does not affect the tax (ETA Payroll FAQ v4, Q7). Exception: an advance/loan above the employee's total pay of the previous 6 months, interest-free or below 7%, creates a taxable benefit of 7% (or the rate gap); "loan" includes amounts paid in advance (Exec. Regulations, art. 11(3)). Social insurance: advances are not among the insurable-wage elements listed in Law 148/2019 art. 1(8) (as published in 2019; later amendments not checked).
Jordan — The law does not address advances. Tax: employment income = salaries, wages, allowances, bonuses and other benefits accruing to the employee from employment (Income Tax Law 34/2014, art. 2); advances are not listed. Social security: wage = consideration received for work (Law 1/2014, art. 2); advances are not in the exclusions list (Coverage Regulation 14/2015, art. 11(a)). Treating the advance as a loan (not wage) is an interpretation, not a statutory rule.
Lebanon — The law does not address advances. NSSF base = total income arising from work (Social Security Code, art. 68(1)); salary tax = salaries, wages, indemnities and benefits (Income Tax Law DL 144/1959, arts. 46 and 49). Neither names advances.

**O:** EG: ETA Payroll FAQ v4 Q7 (p.11) and Q41 (p.38); Decree 991/2005 art. 11(3); Law 148/2019 art. 1(8). JO: Law 34/2014 art. 2; Law 1/2014 art. 2; Coverage Reg. 14/2015 art. 11(a). LB: NSSF Code (Decree 13955/1963) art. 68(1); DL 144/1959 arts. 46, 49.

## #6 1043 Notice Period Payment (Egypt, Lebanon)

**N:**
Egypt — Tax: taxable salary income at the normal progressive rates, not a flat 20%. Art. 9(1) taxes everything due for work for another, periodic or not, whatever its name or cause; art. 9(1) has no flat rate; the only flat rate in the 2005 salary chapter (art. 11, 10%) covers non-residents and payments from a non-original employer, not notice pay; art. 12(2) exempts only end-of-service gratuities. Arts. 9 and 12 are not amended by Laws 30/2023, 7/2024 or 151/2026 (other amendments not checked). Martyr Fund: 0.05% of the employee's total monthly salary, deducted from employees of private entities under the Labour Law (except irregular/daily workers), borne by the employee (MoF General Circular 14/2021, item 3; copy obtained from a non-official host). The ETA FAQ (Q110) computes it on the basic monthly disbursement only; no text says whether notice pay is included.
Lebanon — Social security: the NSSF base is the total income arising from work, including all its elements and accessories (art. 68(1)). No NSSF text names notice pay, and none excludes it.

**O:** EG: Law 91/2005 arts. 8, 9(1), 12(2); MoF General Circular 14/2021 item 3; ETA Payroll FAQ v4 Q110 (p.29). LB: 62921 (per Mohit); NSSF Code art. 68(1).

## #22 1041 Severance Payment (Lebanon)

**N:** The NSSF Code does not expressly exclude it. Art. 68(1) sets the base as the total income arising from work; arts. 68(3) and 51(1)(a) only say that the art. 68(1) wage is the basis for computing the end-of-service indemnity. We found no NSSF memo or circular on this in the 2017-2026 index (titles reviewed). Our earlier note ("outside the NSSF base") is not supported by a primary source and is withdrawn.

**O:** NSSF Code (Decree 13955/1963) arts. 68(1), 68(3), 51(1)(a).

## #23 1042 Gratuity (Lebanon)

**N:** Depends on what the payment is — please confirm with Ericsson. If it is tips customarily paid by third parties, it is in the NSSF base (art. 68(1) names "sums customarily paid by third parties (gratuities)"). If it is an end-of-service gratuity, same answer as #22.

**O:** NSSF Code art. 68(1).

## #39 1125 Leave Payment (LTA) (Egypt)

**N:**
Tax: no provision allows it to be left out of Egyptian payroll tax. Amounts from a foreign source for work performed in Egypt are taxable (Law 91/2005, art. 9(2)), whether paid in Egypt or abroad (ETA FAQ Q6). We did not review double-tax treaties.
Social insurance: the insurable wage is defined by the list in Law 148/2019, art. 1(8) (as published in 2019; later amendments not checked). It excludes travel allowances and allowances for living costs abroad (item 7(a) and 7(d)), and includes only collective grants and bonuses (items 13–14). Whether the LTA is a travel allowance or an individual bonus depends on the nature of the payment — please confirm with Ericsson.

**O:** EG: Law 91/2005 art. 9(2); ETA Payroll FAQ v4 Q6 (p.10); Law 148/2019 art. 1(8) items 7, 13, 14.

## #45 1044 Leave Payment (Lebanon)

**N:** The NSSF Code does not address it. The base is the total income arising from work, including all its elements and accessories (art. 68(1)); no NSSF text excludes leave pay, and we found no NSSF memo or circular on it in the 2017-2026 index (titles reviewed). We found no primary source for Ericsson's exclusion.

**O:** NSSF Code art. 68(1).
