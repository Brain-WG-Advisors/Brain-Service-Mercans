Fonte do art. 23: JORCI de 02/09/1999, p. 705 (Loi n° 99-477 du 2 août 1999) e p. 708 (art. 23, §§ 2–3), em raw/docs/oficiais/CI/2026-10-16-Loi-99-477-Code-Prevoyance-Sociale.pdf; ver 2026-10-16-VERIFICACAO-complemento-teto-CNPS.md.

> Hi Sivaranjani,
>
> The ceiling in force since 1 January 2023 is XOF 75,000/month for Prestations Familiales, Assurance Maternité and AT/MP, and XOF 3,375,000/month for pensions. The floor for all branches is also XOF 75,000 (the SMIG).
>
> Source: CNPS communiqué "À l'attention des employeurs", published on cnps.ci on 11 January 2023 (attached), issued under Décret n° 2022-986 of 21 December 2022, which raised the SMIG from XOF 60,000 to XOF 75,000.
>
> Legal basis: art. 23 of Law n° 99-477 of 2 August 1999 (Social Security Code): earnings above an amount set by decree count only up to that amount, and the contribution base can never be lower than the SMIG.
>
> XOF 70,000 is the pre-2023 ceiling: the CNPS "Employeur" web page still shows it, next to the old SMIG of XOF 60,000.
>
> So the XOF 75,000 base currently used by BH is correct.
>
> Kind regards,
> Wallisson
