## Resposta

**Yes, it's subject to ESV.** Employer-paid premiums under long-term life insurance contracts for employees form part of the salary and are part of the ESV base.

**Legal basis:**
- **Law No. 2464-VI, Art. 7(1)(1):** The employer's ESV base is the salary accrued to each insured person, covering basic and additional pay plus other incentive and compensation payments, including in-kind payments, as defined under the Law "On Remuneration of Labour" No. 108/95-VR.
- **Derzhstat Instruction No. 5 (13.01.2004), Section 2, para 2.3 / sub-para 2.3.4:** Other incentive and compensation payments include enterprises' insurance contributions for employees under voluntary (personal, property) insurance, except the cases in para 3.5. These amounts go into the payroll fund in the month the transfer to the insurer is made.
- **CMU Resolution No. 1170 (22.12.2010), Section II, para 2:** The exclusion only covers payments under voluntary medical and pension insurance contracts for employees and their family members. Life insurance is not on that list.

**Exception:** Contributions the employer pays for an employee under a non-state pension insurance contract are not part of the ESV base. Voluntary medical insurance is also excluded under the same para 2 of Resolution 1170.

**Source:** State Tax Service (Main Department of the DPS in Ternopil Oblast), April 2024 clarification: https://tr.tax.gov.ua/data/material/000/652/774460/CHi_bazoyu_15.04.pdf. The same position appears in the DPS ZIR knowledge base, category 201.04.01.
