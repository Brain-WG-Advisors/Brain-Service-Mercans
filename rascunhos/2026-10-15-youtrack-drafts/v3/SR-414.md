1. Yes, both can be omitted. ZERO_ZVIT and REASON are optional elements of DECLARBODY (XSD S0301016, minOccurs="0"), and an element absent from DECLARBODY is read as empty (Derzhstat XML Format Standard 3.1, empty values). Derzhstat's technical field list for S0301016 describes only REASON_KOD ("filled automatically according to the selected reason"); no Derzhstat source defines the values of ZERO_ZVIT or REASON, so there is no rule to support the mapping above, and omitting both is valid.

2. No rate formula. Row 11 (r.1030) takes the PIT actually withheld from the row-10 (r.1020) wage fund, excluding temporary disability, without recalculations for the previous year (form 1-PV monthly, line 1030, Derzhstat Order No. 117 of 15.04.2024; Derzhstat Explanation No. 19.1.2-12/24-24, section II, item 2); PIT withheld on income outside r.1020 does not count. Neither the form nor the Explanation distinguishes tax rate or regime; the only rate in the rules is the control r.1030 / r.1020 × 100 ≤ 18 (Explanation, section I, item 8). In the UA WTC, withheld PIT is one element, 2552 "Withheld PIT", with its rate (18 or 5) set by 52552 "Withheld PIT Rate".
