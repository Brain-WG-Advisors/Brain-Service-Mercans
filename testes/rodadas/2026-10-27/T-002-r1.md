## Resposta

**TRL = Tipo de Relación Laboral** (type of employment relationship).

It's a mandatory child of the `InformacionAfiliacion` node inside each `Tramo`, and identifies the worker's type of employment relationship. It is a 3-digit numeric string (`\d\d\d`). The TGSS sends it as part of the affiliation data that drives the contribution calculation for each worker's tramo. In practice, `000` means an ordinary employment relationship with no special regime, and TGSS's own examples show both `000` and `930`.

Source: TGSS, *SLD: Fichero de Trabajadores y Tramos – Manual de Usuario* v2.0.0 (Dec 2021), section "Nivel 3.9.3.1.4.1.4.8 – Nodo TRL", p. 30: https://www.seg-social.es/descarga/es/187663
