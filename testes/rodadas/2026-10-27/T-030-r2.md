## Resposta

For 2025, the deductible base depends on the type of policy. For a savings life insurance policy covering the employee, spouse or minor children, 50% of the premiums paid by the employee are deductible. For personal insurance policies such as death, accident, health, illness, disability, unemployment, maternity, childbirth and education, 100% is deductible. In either case the policy must be taken out with a pension or insurance company that is resident and headquartered in Turkey (GVK Art. 63(1)(3)).

Two limits then apply together:

1. **Monthly limit (not cumulative):** the deduction cannot exceed 15% of the gross wage earned in the month the premium is paid. The wage used for this is the total gross amount of regular taxable payments made by the employer, such as salary, premiums, bonuses, social benefits and raises. Each month is assessed on its own. In the GİB example, the portion above 15% is simply lost for that month.

2. **Annual limit (cumulative):** total deductions over the calendar year cannot exceed the annual gross minimum wage, which is TRY 312,066 for 2025. Any change in the minimum wage during the year is taken into account. In practice this is checked month by month: the year-to-date deductions plus the current month's deduction must stay within the cap.

Points that affect both limits:

- **Shared with employer pension contributions:** the employer's private pension (BES) contributions and the insurance premiums deducted under Art. 63(1)(3) together cannot exceed 15% of the monthly wage and the annual minimum wage. If the total goes over the limit, the parties can choose whether the deduction is taken from the wage tax base or from the employer's commercial income, as long as nothing is deducted twice. The employee's own BES contributions can never be deducted from the taxable wage.
- **Premiums paid by the employer:** these count as a benefit to the employee. They must be grossed up and included in the monthly payroll as wage, and they are then deductible under the same 15% and annual limits.
- **Timing:** premiums paid in advance for later months cannot be deducted in the month they are paid, even if they fall within the 15% limit. If a premium is paid as a lump sum, it is split evenly over the remaining months, starting with the month the receipt is given to the employer.
- **No double deduction:** premiums already deducted through payroll cannot be deducted again on an annual income tax return.

The source is the GİB guide "Gelir Vergisi Matrahından İndirilecek Hayat/Şahıs Sigorta Primleri" (Publication No. 597, February 2026), sections 1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10 and 11, which apply GVK Art. 63(1)(3).
