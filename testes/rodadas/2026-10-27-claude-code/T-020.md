# T-020

**Pergunta:** Netherlands 2026: what is the total Whk premium (WGA + ZW-flex) for a small employer in sector 42, according to the Handboek Loonheffingen 2026?

**Resposta:**
**1.45%** for sector 42 (Groothandel II): WGA 0.91% + ZW-flex 0.54%.

1.25% is the figure for sectors 41 and 45, not 42. Reading it for sector 42 is a known error; see the correction of 2026-10-16.
