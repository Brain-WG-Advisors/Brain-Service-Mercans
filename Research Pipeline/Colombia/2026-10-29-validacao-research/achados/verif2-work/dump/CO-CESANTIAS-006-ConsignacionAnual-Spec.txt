Report Matrix!A23	SUBMISSION CHANNEL
Report Matrix!A24	- Channel: online_form AND file_upload (fixed-width TXT / XLS 97-2003 / CSV) | Portal: a licensed information operator - ACH Colombia S.A. (SOI), www.nuevosoi.com.co, and Simple S.A., www.pagosimple.com; Aportes en Linea, Asopagos, Enlace Operativo and MiPlanilla offer the same service | Source: SOI instruction guide DDS-DES-INS-008 v2 and Simple S.A. 'Manual Severance savings' ss. 3.1-3.2 | The 258-position layout is the one published by Simple S.A. and is the only detail layout defined in this Spec; the information operator used is a configuration setting, and this Spec defines no other operator's variant.
Report Matrix!A25	- THE RECIPIENT IS NOT A GOVERNMENT AUTHORITY. The money goes to a private fondo de cesantias, or to the state-owned FNA, in an individual account in the employee's name. There is no authority portal, no fiscal receipt and no tax-authority validation - which is exactly why no XSD exists and why this filing must never be labelled XML.
Report Matrix!A26	- THE OPERATOR IS THE SAME KIND OF INTERMEDIARY AS FOR PILA, BUT THE SERVICE IS NOT PILA. Severance savings is a Payment Supplementary to social security Social (PCSS) and is selected as a distinct service inside the operator's portal, with its own return number series and its own period concept - a YEAR, not a month. Simple S.A. notes that the login is the same one used for PILA, which is the source of the frequent error of building a PILA return and expecting the severance savings to be in it.
Report Matrix!A27	- The DIAN portals play no part in this filing. Form 350, Form 220 and Format 2276 go to DIAN; the severance savings deposit never does. What DIAN sees of severance savings is the reporting of the amounts on Form 220 casillas 48-49 and Format 2276 numerals 19-20, which are separate filings with their own Specs (CO-DIAN-004 and CO-DIAN-005).
Report Matrix!A28	- File-size and structure constraints from the published instructions: no header row; the data start on the first line or row; all records on one Excel sheet; a file above 1 MB must be compressed before upload (SOI); the fixed-width record is 258 characters and the sequence must start at 00001 and be consecutive.
Report Matrix!A29	INTEGRATION TYPE AND DIRECTION
Report Matrix!A30	- Integration: outbound file generation and inbound response handling. OUTBOUND: the annual return, keyed or uploaded. INBOUND: CO-IN-SEVERANCE SAVINGS-CONFIRMATION, the deposit confirmation evidencing compliance with the February deadline. Both directions are modelled on Sheet 4 - the outbound record as T01-T20, the inbound values as E10, E11, E90, E91 and E93.
Report Matrix!A31	- product_mapping_required = true. The engine must hold the fund code as EMPLOYEE master data (it is the employee's statutory choice, not a company default), the 360-day service count, the CST art. 253 base including the transport allowance, the accrual-year period concept, the per-employee deposit-compliance status and, when the decree is enacted, the monthly-advance regime flag per contract.
Report Matrix!A32	- NOT DERIVABLE FROM THE MONTHLY FILINGS. The population is not the PILA population: an employee who left during the accrual year is EXCLUDED from the deposit and is paid directly in the final settlement (Ley 50 de 1990 art. 99 num. 4; Population Rules Step 0 exclusion (2)), while an all-inclusive salary employee is present in PILA and owed nothing here. The population must be generated from the Population Rules, never copied from a PILA return. Do not use the operator's seed-from-last-PILA convenience (E08).
Report Matrix!A33	- THE ONE-TO-MANY TRAP ON THE RETURN. Where the medio de pago is Pago Credito, the operator issues ONE RETURN NUMBER PER FONDO, so the reconciliation store must accept many return numbers for one payroll run (E05, E10).
Report Matrix!A34	- The engine must also produce two things that are NOT in this file and are commonly conflated with it: the 12% interest paid to the EMPLOYEE in January (IN01-IN03), and the tax figures that appear on Form 220 and Format 2276. Different payees, different deadlines, different penalties.
Report Matrix!A35	RESIDENT / NON-RESIDENT TREATMENT
Report Matrix!A36	- RESIDENT AND NON-RESIDENT ARE TREATED IDENTICALLY IN THIS FILING, AND THAT IS THE POINT. Severance savings is a LABOUR entitlement under the CST, not a tax concept, so it attaches to the employment relationship and not to tax residence. A non-resident foreign national employed under a Colombian contract accrues severance savings on the same base, at the same 360-day rate, is deposited to a fund of their own choosing and is reported in this return on exactly the same record as a resident. There is no non-resident variant of the layout and no withholding on the deposit.
Report Matrix!A37	- WHERE RESIDENCE DOES BITE IS THE TAX TREATMENT, WHICH IS NOT THIS FILING. For a RESIDENT, ET art. 206 num. 4 exempts the severance savings and their interest in full where the average monthly income of the last six months is at or below 350 UVT (COP 18,330,900 in 2026), then tapers 90 / 80 / 60 / 40 / 20 per cent across the 350-410, 410-470, 470-530, 530-590 and 590-650 UVT bands, with nothing exempt above 650 UVT. For a NON-RESIDENT the art. 206 num. 4 exemption is unavailable - it belongs to the resident ID card general - and the payment is taxed at the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the gross with no art. 388 depuration. Neither outcome changes a single character of the record sent to the fund.
Report Matrix!A38	- THE DOCUMENT TYPE IS WHERE THE FOREIGN EMPLOYEE ACTUALLY BREAKS. The layout admits CE, PA, CD, SC, PE and (in the XLS variant) PT, and SOI records that a Special permanence permit number must be exactly 15 digits. A regularisation that converts a PEP or PPT into a Foreign resident ID card changes the join key (T03 + T04) that the fund holds, so the change must reach the fund or the following February's deposit lands on an orphan account. See T03 and T04.
Report Matrix!A39	- A Colombian employee posted abroad but retained on a Colombian contract remains in scope; an employee of a foreign entity with no Colombian employment contract is out of scope entirely, because there is no CST relationship to accrue cesantias on.

Payroll Scope Summary!A42	Nil period still filed
Payroll Scope Summary!B42	NO
Payroll Scope Summary!C42	There is no nil return. Where no employee meets the population rule for the accrual year, no return is built and nothing is deposited.
Payroll Scope Summary!A43	Corrections handled by re-filing the whole period
Payroll Scope Summary!B43	VARIES

A return paid after its deadline is filed on type E; type M (mora) applies only to periods before health period March 2014 with a total absence of payment (AT2 v30 §2.1.1.2.2 campo 8).

File Structure!A2	═══ SUBMISSION CHANNEL ═══
File Structure!A3	Channel
File Structure!B3	TWO channels, both through a licensed information operator, NOT through a government portal. (A) online_form - 'Planilla En Linea' (SOI) / 'Elaborar planilla' (Simple S.A.), each employee keyed into the operator's form. (B) file_upload - 'Liquidacion Por Archivo' (SOI) / 'Envio de Archivo Unificado de Cesantias' (Simple S.A.). Both channels are documented in both operators' published instructions.
File Structure!A4	Portals
File Structure!B4	ACH Colombia S.A. (SOI) - www.nuevosoi.com.co, instruction guide DDS-DES-INS-008 v2. Simple S.A. - www.pagosimple.com, 'Manual Severance savings'. The same service is offered by Aportes en Linea, Asopagos, Enlace Operativo and MiPlanilla, each of which may impose an operator-specific layout. This Spec is written to the SOI channel and the Simple S.A. layouts; the information operator used is a configuration setting, and this Spec defines no other operator's variant.
File Structure!A5	Authentication
File Structure!B5	Portal session with (company document type + number), (user document type + number) and a password. Simple S.A. states the credentials are the SAME ones used for PILA. There is NO certificate, NO electronic signature and NO machine-to-machine service - contrast CO-DIAN-001, which has a SOAP contract, a XAdES signature and a synchronous ApplicationResponse.
File Structure!A6	What comes back
File Structure!B6	A return number (one per fondo where the medio de pago is Pago Credito), a pre-payment voucher in PDF, and after payment the informe por fondo / por afiliado / completo in PDF or Excel.
File Structure!A7	NOT XML
File Structure!B7	No XSD, no cahier des charges and no XML of any kind exists for this filing. The word XML must not appear against CO-CESANTIAS-006. CSV is accepted by SOI as an upload format, but the two layouts published at field level are the fixed-width TXT and the XLS 97-2003, and this Spec is written to those.

The only defence is to compute campo 14 in the engine.

Population Rules!A1	POPULATION RULES - HOW TO FILL EACH FIELD FROM PAYROLL
Population Rules!A2	Field Name
Population Rules!B2	Source System
Population Rules!C2	Population Logic
Population Rules!D2	Edge Cases
Population Rules!A3	═══ STEP 0 - BUILD THE POPULATION (THIS IS WHERE THE FILING IS USUALLY GOT WRONG) ═══
Population Rules!A4	Population of the return
Population Rules!B4	Payroll master data + service history
Population Rules!C4	Select every employee IN SERVICE AT 31 DECEMBER of the accrual year who is on the annual severance savings regime (Law 50 of 1990 art. 99 - all employees hired from 1 January 1991, plus pre-1991 hires who migrated). EXCLUDE, in this order: (1) all-inclusive salary employees, whose severance savings is absorbed by CST art. 132 num. 2; (2) employees who LEFT during the year, whose severance savings was settled and paid in the final settlement; (3) pre-1991 non-migrated employees on the retrospective regime, for whom there is no fund deposit at all; (4) employees discharged by proven direct payment under CST art. 57 para. 4; (5) records whose liquidation falls below one day of one SMMLV, which the channel rejects. Do NOT build the population from the December PILA return, however convenient the portal makes it (E08): PILA is the population PAID in a month, not the population ENTITLED for the year - it contains every all-inclusive salary employee and omits every mid-year leaver.
Population Rules!D4	A leaver included = double payment, and the money is then in a fund account the ex-employee must claim. A all-inclusive salary employee included = an unrecoverable deposit. A missed employee = one day's salary per day of delay, uncapped, running until it is found.
Population Rules!A5	Fund per employee
Population Rules!B5	Employee master data
Population Rules!C5	Read Employee.Cesantias_Fund_Code. It is the EMPLOYEE'S statutory choice under Law 50 of 1990 art. 99 num. 3 ('en el fondo de cesantia que el mismo elija'), so it is employee master data and never a company default. Where a mid-year change of fund has been notified, use the fund in force at the date of the deposit. Where the field is empty the record cannot be built - escalate, because affiliation is compulsory even where the severance savings is paid directly (CST art. 57 para. 4 in fine). Set E07 to N so that this per-employee value governs.
Population Rules!D5	An empty fund code is not a payroll defect to be defaulted - it is an unfulfilled affiliation obligation. Answering YES to the portal's single-fund question to get past it deposits other people's money into the wrong fund and does not discharge the obligation.
Population Rules!A6	═══ STEP 1 - COMPUTE THE BASE AND THE AMOUNT (CST ARTS. 249 AND 253) ═══
Population Rules!A7	T14 Valor de las cesantias
Population Rules!B7	Payroll engine - annual liquidation
Population Rules!C7	Compute, do NOT let the portal compute. (a) Determine the BASE under CST art. 253: the LAST monthly salary if it has not varied in the last three months; otherwise, and for every variable salary, the AVERAGE of the last year of service, or of the whole period served if shorter. (b) ADD THE TRANSPORT ALLOWANCE where it is payable - CST art. 253 requires it and it is payable where the salary does not exceed 2 SMMLV (COP 3,501,810 in 2026). (c) Multiply by days of service over 360. (d) Round to the whole peso - not up to the next 100 as PILA requires, and not to the nearest 1,000 as Form 350 requires. (e) Validate against the floor of one day of one SMMLV (COP 58,363.50 for 2026). (f) Where a lawful partial settlement was made during the year under CST art. 254, deduct it. Write the result into positions 128-136, right-aligned and zero-filled, with '0000' in positions 124-127.
Population Rules!D7	THE TRAP: the layout's own formula is 'Basic salary * (dias worked / 360)' and campo 12 is named 'basic salary'. Follow the portal and a minimum-wage employee is deposited COP 1,750,905 instead of COP 2,000,000 - a COP 249,095 short deposit per employee, penalised as non-deposit at one day's salary per day of delay. The floor also bites in reverse: a residue below COP 58,363.50 cannot be deposited and must be settled with the employee instead.
Population Rules!A8	T12 Salario basico reportado en el ultimo mes
Population Rules!B8	Payroll - December of the accrual year
Population Rules!C8	Report the DECEMBER basic salary of the accrual year, exactly as the layout specifies ('Corresponde al ultimo salario basico reportado en el mes de diciembre del ano causado de cesantias'), in 9 integer positions, zero-filled, with '0000' in positions 111-114. This is a REPORTING field for the fund's records - it is NOT the statutory base, and the two will legitimately differ for a variable-salary employee whose base is a twelve-month average. Do not reverse-engineer T12 from T14 to make the portal's calculator agree.
Population Rules!D8	For an employee who was on unpaid leave in December, or who was hired on 20 December, the December basic is still the contractual monthly salary, not the amount actually earned that month. For an employee whose salary changed in November, T12 carries the December figure while the CST art. 253 base is the twelve-month average - so T14 will not equal T12 * T10/360, and that is correct.
Population Rules!A9	T10 Numero de dias worked
Population Rules!B9	Payroll - service calendar
Population Rules!C9	Count the days of service in the accrual year on the 360-day COMMERCIAL convention: each complete month is 30 days, a full year is 360. Include paid leave, paid holidays and recognised incapacity, all of which count as service. Exclude unpaid leave (unpaid leave) and suspension. For an employee in service the whole year write 360, rather than 365 and rather than 366. The field admits 1 to 360 only, so any computation that can produce 361 or more is wrong before it reaches the file.
Population Rules!D9	A leap year does not change the divisor. An employee hired on 31 January accrues 330 days, rather than 335. Where unpaid leave straddles a month end, count the calendar days of the leave on the 30-day convention and subtract - do not subtract whole months.
Population Rules!A10	═══ STEP 2 - IDENTIFICATION AND CONTACT FIELDS ═══
Population Rules!A11	T03 + T04 Tipo y numero de documento
Population Rules!B11	Employee master data
Population Rules!C11	Map the document type to the two-letter severance savings code (CC/CE/TI/PA/CD/SC/PE, plus PT in the XLS route only) - this is a DIFFERENT mapper from the DIAN numeric codes used on Form 220 and Format 2276 and from the PILA list. Strip dots, commas, spaces and hyphens from the number, then RIGHT-ALIGN and ZERO-FILL to 16 positions for the fixed-width route, or leave it plain to a 15-character maximum for the XLS route. Where the type is PE the number must be exactly 15 digits.
Population Rules!D11	A regularisation that converts a PEP or PPT into a Foreign resident ID card changes the join key the fund holds; unless the change reaches the fund, the February deposit lands on an orphan account and the employee cannot see it. A TI holder reaching majority is the same problem. Where the workforce includes PPT holders and the fixed-width route is used, the document-type list printed for that layout has no PT value - confirm with the operator rather than substituting CE.
Population Rules!A12	T05-T08 Surnames and given names
Population Rules!B12	Employee master data
Population Rules!C12	Write the four name components into their own 20-position fields, left-aligned and space-filled: first surname (mandatory), second surname, first given name (mandatory), segundo nombre. Strip accents and every special character the operators reject. Where a component is absent, SPACE-FILL THE WHOLE FIELD - never shift the following fields up, because the record is positional and the fund code at 106-107 would then be read out of the middle of a name. Space-fill rather than truncate; a truncated surname mismatches the Asofondos base every year. Answer NO to the auto-adjust question (E09) and fix differences at source.
Population Rules!D12	Compound surnames longer than 20 characters have to be truncated somewhere - do it deliberately and record the rule, because the same truncation must be applied consistently year on year or the mismatch report churns. A person with one name and one surname produces two space-filled fields, which is valid. Foreign employees whose passport shows a single name field need a documented split rule; the fund's record follows whatever was first sent.
Population Rules!A13	T15-T19 Direccion, DANE, telefono, correo
Population Rules!B13	Employee master data
Population Rules!C13	Write the RESIDENTIAL address (not the work address - the fund communicates with its member at home), 50 positions space-filled. Split the DANE divipola code into its two-digit department half at 187-188 and its three-digit municipality half at 189-191. The landline field admits EXACTLY seven digits, so a 10-digit mobile cannot go in it; zero- or space-fill instead. The e-mail field is 50 positions and the operator validates the local part, the @ and the domain separately, with the hyphen barred from the first and last position of the domain.
Population Rules!D13	The single most common defect here is sending the WORK municipality, because that is what PILA and the electronic payroll want; the affiliate then never receives a fund statement. Truncating a mobile number to seven digits produces a wrong-number record on the fund's file, which is worse than an empty one. Addresses longer than 50 characters must be abbreviated by a documented rule, not cut mid-token.
Population Rules!A14	T20 Hire date
Population Rules!B14	Contract master data
Population Rules!C14	Write the CONTRACT HIRE DATE in AAAA-MM-DD, ten positions including the hyphens, at 249-258. For an employee hired in 2019 and reported for accrual year 2026 the value is 2019-03-01 - rather than 2026-01-01, and not the start of the accrual period.
Population Rules!D14	Sending the accrual-year start corrupts the fund's seniority record, which the fund uses for its own member-benefit rules. For a rehired employee, use the hire date of the CURRENT contract, which is the contract the cesantias accrued under. Note the layout types this field N while the content contains hyphens; a strict numeric writer will emit 8 characters and fail validation even though nothing shifts, because the field is last in the record.
Population Rules!A15	═══ STEP 3 - ENVELOPE, PAYMENT AND RECONCILIATION ═══
Population Rules!A16	E01-E06 Return envelope
Population Rules!B16	Company master data + run parameters
Population Rules!C16	Authenticate with the company document pair, select the service CESANTIAS (not PILA), enter the ACCRUAL YEAR (not a month), select afiliacion type DEPENDIENTE, select the medio de pago and label the return with the run identifier so the returned return number can be tied back. Answer NO to the seed-from-PILA question and NO to the single-fund question.
Population Rules!D16	The period concept is the trap: every other Colombian payroll filing is monthly and keyed to a payment date, and this one is keyed to the accrual year that closed on 31 December. A return built in February 2027 carries ano accrued 2026.
Population Rules!A17	E10-E11, E90 Return values
Population Rules!B17	Operator portal - inbound
Population Rules!C17	Store the return number, the total payable and the PAYMENT date. Where the medio de pago is Pago Credito, expect and store MANY return numbers - one per fondo. Reconcile the returned total against the engine's own sum of T14 before paying, because the file carries no header, no trailer, no record count and no declared total, so a truncated upload is not self-detecting. Download and archive the voucher PDF and the per-fondo report.
Population Rules!D17	Liquidating the return is not paying it. A return liquidated on 13 February and paid on 17 February is LATE - the deadline attaches to the deposit, so the compliance evidence is the payment date, not the liquidation date.
Population Rules!A18	E91-E93 Compliance status and evidence
Population Rules!B18	Engine-derived + fund certificate
Population Rules!C18	Derive the per-employee status (ON TIME / LATE with a day count / SHORT with a shortfall / DISCHARGED BY DIRECT PAYMENT) from E90 against the deadline, and hold it with the fund's deposit certificate reference for at least the employee's three-year limitation period under CST art. 488 as substituted by Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 62. Quantify the exposure: the penalty is one day's salary per day of delay, per employee, uncapped - for 100 minimum-wage employees a fortnight is of the order of COP 81.7 million.
Population Rules!D18	The certificate is issued by the FUND, but CCG Module 10 records that the EMPLOYER must be able to evidence it - so the reference must be stored employer-side, not left on a portal. A SHORT deposit made on time is not a lesser problem than a late one: the shortfall is treated as non-deposit and accrues the same daily penalty.
Population Rules!A19	═══ STEP 4 - THE TWO THINGS THAT ARE NOT IN THIS FILE ═══
Population Rules!A20	IN01-IN03 Interest on severance savings
Population Rules!B20	Payroll engine - paid to the EMPLOYEE
Population Rules!C20	Compute 12% per annum on the 31 December balance, pro-rated on days over 360, and PAY IT TO THE EMPLOYEE DIRECTLY DURING JANUARY of the following year - never to the fund and never in February. Liquidate no interest on interest. Where a WRITTEN agreement exists under Ley 2466 de 2025 art. 64 par. 2, pay instead 1% monthly of the monthly base (salary + transport allowance). Evidence the payment - failure costs an indemnity equal to the interest owed, once only, under Ley 52 de 1975 art. 1.
Population Rules!D20	Two obligations, two payees, two deadlines, two penalties, and they are routinely conflated. Depositing the interest to the fund discharges nothing and leaves the January obligation open. Note also that the monthly 1% option and the annual 12% do NOT arrive at the same figure, because the monthly base is the monthly salary while the annual base is the accumulated balance.
Population Rules!A21	Tax treatment of the cesantias
Population Rules!B21	CO-DIAN-004 and CO-DIAN-005, not here
Population Rules!C21	No withholding is practised on the DEPOSIT. When the money is eventually paid to the employee, a RESIDENT gets the ET art. 206 num. 4 exemption - full below a six-month average of 350 UVT (COP 18,330,900 in 2026), then tapering 90/80/60/40/20 per cent across the 350-410, 410-470, 470-530, 530-590 and 590-650 UVT bands - while a NON-RESIDENT gets no exemption and the flat 20% of ET art. 408 on the gross. The figures are reported on Form 220 casillas 48-49 and Format 2276 numerals 19-20.
Population Rules!D21	Format 2276 numerals 19 and 20 are TRANSPOSED against Form 220 casillas 48 and 49 - map by MEANING, per the CO-DIAN-005 Spec. Nothing about the tax treatment changes a character of the record sent to the fund, which is why a non-resident's record is identical to a resident's.
