**Pergunta:** UK workplace pension auto-enrolment: in the GB Country Data Dictionary, what default employee and employer contribution percentages should be set?

**Resposta:**
Employee 5%, employer 3% (statutory minimums on qualifying earnings, 8% total). GB DD v3 had them inverted (EE 3 / ER 5); corrected.
