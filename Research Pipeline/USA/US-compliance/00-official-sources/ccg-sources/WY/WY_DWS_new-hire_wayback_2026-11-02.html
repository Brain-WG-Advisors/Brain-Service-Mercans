Unemployment Insurance

Wyoming New Hire Reporting

Employers are required to report new hires. We utilize the State Directory of New Hires (SDNH) and the National Directory of New Hires (NDNH) databases to audit recent employment. This audit process ensures that any earnings reported by claimants upon being hired are accurate and correctly documented.
