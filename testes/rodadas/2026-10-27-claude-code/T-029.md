# T-029

**Pergunta:** Belgium 2026: what is the basic employer social security contribution rate (ONSS) for private-sector worker categories 1 and 3, and what is the approximate total rate including the wage moderation contribution?

**Resposta:**
Basic rate 19.88% (since Q1 2018, tax shift) for categories 1 and 3, plus wage moderation contribution of up to 5.12% [(19.88 x 0.0427) + 4.27]: total approx. 25.00%. 24.92% (still in older CCG/WTC versions) was the basic rate from Q1 2015 and is outdated.

**Fontes:**
- ONSS administrative instructions, "Les cotisations" > "Le pourcentage de cotisation globalise" and "La cotisation de moderation salariale" (edition 2025/4); legal basis Law of 26/12/2015 and Law of 16/05/2016
